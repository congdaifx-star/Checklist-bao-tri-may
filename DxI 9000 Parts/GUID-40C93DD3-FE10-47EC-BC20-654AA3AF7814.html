Reagent Pipettors

Illustrated Parts List

This topic contains part numbers and descriptions for the DxI 9000 Reagent Pipettor subsystems and their components, including but not limited to:
  • Motion/drive components
  • Wash buffer tubing/sensors
  • Ultrasonics mixing and level sense
