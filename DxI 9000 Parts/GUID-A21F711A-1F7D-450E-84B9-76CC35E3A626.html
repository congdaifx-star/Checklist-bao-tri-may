Sample Storage Wheel

Illustrated Parts List

This topic contains part numbers and descriptions for the DxI 9000 Sample Storage Wheel subsystem and components, including but not limited to:

  • Wheel plate and covers
  • PCB and sensors
  • Motor, belt, drive components
  • Thermal components (peltier, thermistor)
