Illustrated Parts List

This topic contains part numbers and descriptions for the DxI 9000 Disposable Tip (DispoTip) Stocker subsystem and components, including but not limited to:

  • Tray Loader
  • Tray Gripper
  • Tray Carrier
  • Tray Waste and Stockyard
