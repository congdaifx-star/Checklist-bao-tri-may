1 D00490 D00490 PCB, SPU RACK IF SPU Rack Detect Sensor PCB

Two per system (SPU end of onload and SPU end of offload). Replaces C36946.

6 B77713 B77713 ASSY, BUTTON SPU Onload Button

Button fastens into C23492 SW PLATE bracket.

7 C19743 C19743 PCB, SLIB SPU Status Light PCB

Small PCB with five LED's for displaying SPU locked/unlocked status. PCB is mounted to a bracket beneath the cover and not directly visible from the image above.

8 C38010 C38010 SENSOR ASSY, COVER SWITCH, MK04-1A66E SPU Cover Sensor Onload/Offload

Two per assembly (SPU end of onload and SPU end of offload). Sensor does not include C38036 MAGNET which fixes to the cover to trigger the sensor.

10 C51449 C51449 ASSY, IDLER, ONLOAD SPU Onload Tensioner/Idler Assembly

Idler assembly does not include tensioner springs, order qty. 2 B80574 SPRING, TENSIONER separately if needed.

11 B80599 B80599 BELT, ONLOAD, SPU SPU Onload Drive Belt
12 C19494 C19494 ASSY, LINEAR RAIL, SPU, X SPU Onload Linear Slide

Includes truck that rides on rail. Fixes to chassis with qty. 12 M3x8 screws.

13 B77768 B77768 ASSY, SENSOR, ANGLED SPU Home Sensor

Sensor used in multiple locations throughout system.

SPU Presentation Components



Item Order Number Mfg. Number Oracle Description Service Description Notes
1 C18883 C18883 PLATE, BCR BACKGROUND SPU Camera Background Reflector

Metal plate with reflective tape for SPU camera background. Ensure no scratches or scuffs on the reflective tape before installing.

2 C35769 C35769 ASSY, CAMERA, SPU SPU Camera Assembly
Note: This is the camera fixed to the mounting bracket. The camera should not be removed from the bracket as this may alter the camera position in relation to the rack/sample containers and lead to issues with detection/calibration.
3 B80541 B80541 BLOCK, GUIDE, RACK SPU Presentation Rack Guide Block

Guide blocks which secure the rack at the pipetting position. Two per assembly, does not include B80609 SPRING, RACK GUIDE BLOCK (qty. 2) which fix to B80607 PLATE, SPRING MOUNT to provide tension to the guide blocks.

4 C57658 C57658 TRANSFER CARRIER, RACK, PU SPU Rack Presentation Tray/Carrier

This is what carries the sample racks. Does not include C80528 ASSY, PU COUPLER that attaches the tray to the linear slide truck, order separately if needed.

5 B77768 B77768 ASSY, SENSOR, ANGLED, SPU Presentation Home Sensor Sensor used in multiple locations throughout system.
6 C49240 C49240 ASSY, MOTOR, PU SPU Presentation Motor

Motor/encoder/pulley are one assembly.

7 B80540 B80540 BELT, COMMON RACK, PU SPU Presentation Drive Belt

Attaches to the ASSY, PU COUPLER.

8 C19019 C19019 ASSY, LINEAR RAIL, SPU, Y. SPU Presentation Linear Slide

Includes truck that rides on rail. Fixes to chassis with qty. 8 M3x8 screws.

9 C51448 C51448 ASSY, IDLER, SPU SPU Presentation Tensioner/Idler Assembly

Idler assembly does not include tensioner springs, order qty. 2 B80574 SPRING, TENSIONER separately if needed.

Not Shown D01860 D01860 TUBE, 13 x 100 mm, 9 mL TUBE, 13 x 100 mm, 9 mL QTY 1 = one 13x100 tube. These are 13x100 glass tubes for performing SPU camera calibration. Order at least QTY 7 in order to fill up a tube rack.
