Pick and Place

Illustrated Parts List

This topic contains part numbers and descriptions for the DxI 9000 Pick and Place (PnP) subsystems and components, including but not limited to:

  • Drive components (motors, belts, linear slides, pulleys)
  • Pneumatics (valves, tubing, ejectors)
  • Electrical components (PCBs, sensors)
Note: There are three PnP assemblies in the system (sample, incubate, and analytic), which share many components. This parts list has been organized to identify the generic components of each PnP, and details for variations betwen the three are called out in the tables below.
