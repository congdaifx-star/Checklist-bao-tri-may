Reagent Storage

Illustrated Parts List

This document contains parts contained within the DxI 9000 Reagent Storage assembly

  • Gripper and X/Z-axis Drive Components
  • Reagent pack nests
  • Reagent cooler assemblies
  • Reagent load tray
