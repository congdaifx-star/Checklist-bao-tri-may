Reagent Carousel Thermistor Troubleshooting

If Then
1 Reagent temperature is out-of-specification high and the duty cycle of the Peltiers is 1.0 when viewed in the Analog Devices screen of the user interface Ensure that:
  • Peltiers are functioning correctly.
  • Peltiers blower is functioning correctly.
  • All other fans in the instrument are functioning correctly.
  • Air intakes are not clogged with dust.
  • Peltiers heat sink is not excessively dusty.
2 Temporarily connect a replacement thermistor to reagent carousel thermistor connector J15 on the reagent carousel PCB. If the temperature changes from -21 °C or +50 °C to a reading in the +20 °C range (ambient temperature) the reagent carousel thermistor is defective.
Note: Replace the reagent carousel thermistor only with a sealed thermistor PN 973023. Other thermistors that are not sealed can fail prematurely because of condensation.
3 Temporarily connect a replacement thermistor to reagent carousel thermistor connector J15 on the reagent carousel PCB. If the temperature does NOT change from -21 °C or +50 °C to a reading in the +20 °C range (ambient temperature) then troubleshoot connections at:
  • J16 (Sensor Out) on the reagent carousel interface PCB.
  • J19 pin-4 and pin-5 on the backplane (temperature harness connection).
  • The I/O PCB to backplane connection at J17 pin-B3 and pin-C3.
4 Measure the reagent carousel temperature using a handheld thermocouple. If the temperature is greater than 5 °C from what it should be, replace the thermistor.
Note: Refer to the Replacing Reagent Carousel Thermistor procedure.
