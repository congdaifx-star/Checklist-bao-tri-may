Replacing the Ultrasonic PCB

This procedure provides instructions for the installation of the Ultrasonic PCB.

Caution:
  • Although the Ultrasonics PCBs for Access and Access 2 are similar, they are not identical and they are not interchangeable. Erratic system behavior and possible system damage could occur if the incorrect PCB is installed.
  • The Instrument power must be off before removing or installing PCBs or connectors.
  • All PCBs in the card cage contain ESD sensitive elements. Use ESD precautions when handling any of them.
  1. Replacing the Ultrasonic PCB
    1. Power off the instrument
    2. Open the card cage and locate the Ultrasonics PCB. (Third PCB from the front)
    3. Remove connectors J9 and J10 from the board.
    4. Remove the Ultrasonics PCB from the Card Cage.
    5. Install the new PCB into its slot and re-connect J9 and J10.
      Note: The Ultrasonics PCBs for Access and Access 2 are not interchangeable. Make sure to use the proper PCB for the instrument being serviced.
  2. Verification
    1. Verify that the transducer temperature is within the limits of 35 to 39 degrees C.
    2. Perform the Ultrasonics Adjustment Procedure. Refer to the "Transducer, Ultrasonics Adjustment" procedure for details.
    3. Perform 20 replicates of the Dry / Wet Level Sense test. Refer to "Level Sense, Troubleshooting, Ultrasonics" for details.
    4. Refer to the "Verification Protocol, Access Service Repair" document for the required system verification procedures.
      Note: Ensure that the cover interlock bypass key is removed from the front cover interlock switch. Do Not leave the key at customer site.
