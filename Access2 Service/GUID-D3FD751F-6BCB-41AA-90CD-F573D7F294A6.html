Ground Leakage Information

This section contains information about ground leakage current specifications and testing.

Information requested: Summary of questions: Information provided:
A site BioMed requests information about a CAP checklist question for electrical safety (GEN.71950). Basically the checklist asks if lab instruments are ensured to be adequately grounded before initial use and after repair. There are exceptions to this requirement for devices that are "Double insulated" or operate at 240V.
  1. Are BCI analyzers (Immunoassay, chemistry, etc.) double insulated?
  2. If not, is there documentation of compliance and are there procedures showing that Field Service Engineers ensure proper grounding after service.
  1. Our products are safety agency certified (UL/CSA/CE) to pass the appropriate grounding and leakage current requirements and each instrument is tested for compliance with the safety standards as part of the manufacturing process.
  2. Service does not perform any modifications or repairs that would affect the instrument grounding or the leakage current.

Ground leakage test: Ground leakage tester:

The CAP question indicates a need for the site to be able to independently verify the ground continuity and the ground leakage current of each installed instrument. The customer should review this question with the CAP auditor and determine if the product safety listing is sufficient or if they are going to need to test it themselves.

  • A special test box is needed for testing the system ground leakage.
  • When checking the system ground leakage current, the Access / Access2 instrument should be tested at the input of the Powervar isolation transformer. The instrument without the isolation transformer may fail the leakage current specification by a small amount.
  • The Power var is not required for 220VAC European models.
An on-line search for AC ground Leakage Current Testers found, as an example, the Short Stop Model 352 AC Leakage Current Tester $1,620.00. It can be used to test products for short circuits, continuity of the ground wire, hi-pot, leakage current, and current draw.

CAP document:GEN.71950 Exceptions to these requirements are as follows: Access Manufacturing tests are:
The relevant section of GEN.71950 states: Is there documentation that both the laboratory director and the institutional safety committee have approved a program to ensure that all laboratory instruments and appliances are adequately grounded and checked for current leakage before initial use, after repair or modification, and when a problem is suspected?
  1. Devices protected by an approved system of double insulation or its equivalent. Such devices must be distinctively marked
  2. Equipment operating at 240 v must be checked for ground integrity only
  • Exemption 1 (double insulated products): Double insulated products do not typically have a ground or any ground leakage current. Our products are not the double insulated type.
  • Exemption 2 (240V products)
  • Hi-pot in startup per SOP A80354
  • Ground leakage in final test per SOP A66523. The manufacturing spec for ground current leakage is less than 3500uA.

Manufacturing does the testing as part of the process to comply with the safety listing. For the safety listing the product and the process are submitted to the safety certification company. The certification Co. tests the sample and then the factory continues to test each new unit made in the factory as part of the contract to keep the certification valid.
