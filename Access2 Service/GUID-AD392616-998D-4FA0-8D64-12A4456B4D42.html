PCB - Backplane (Fuses and Connectors)

Purpose

This document provides information regarding fuses, signals, voltages, and test points on the Backplane PCB.

Backplane PCB

Backplane PCB Fuse Locations

Note:
  • Access Instruments contain 7 replaceable fuses (6 on the Backplane PCB and 1 on the Fluidics Interface PCB).
  • Replacement fuse specifications must be equivalent to the those of the original fuse.
  • Replacement fuses are not stocked in CARES.
  • All fuses are 0.25 inch diameter x 1.25 inch long.
