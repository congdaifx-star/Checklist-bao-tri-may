Aligning Analytical Module and Sample Pipettor

Enter the context of your task here (optional).
Align the analytical module and sample pipettor.
  1. Align the incubator belt.
    Note: Refer to the Aligning Incubator Belt procedure.
  2. Align the sample pipettor to the RV shuttle.
    Note: Refer to the Alignment: Pipettor to RV procedure.
  3. Confirm the shuttle and rake alignments.
    Note: Refer to the Aligning RV Shuttle and Rake procedure.
  4. Check the RV sensor voltage.
    Note: Refer to the Adjusting RV Sensors procedure for details.
