Replacing the Ultrasonic Transducer

The following items are required in addition to a standard tool kit:
  • A calibrated multimeter - certified to read 215 VAC at 36 kHz to an accuracy within +/- 5 volts (Meter Multifunction ).
  • A temperature meter (Fluke 52) or fluke 80TK thermocouple module (Meter Temperature )
  • A set of metric hex wrenches.
  • A CnS wrench. Refer to 'Tool, Click and Seal"
This procedure provides instructions for removing and installing the transducer for the Access 2 Pipettor (3 inch).
  1. Perform these steps to remove the ultrasonics transducer from the pipettor gantry.
    Caution:
    • The transducer is a non-serviceable factory assembled and tested part. Any disassembly of this part will change its characteristics making it impossible to properly adjust.
    • The Instrument must be powered off before breaking or making electrical connectors.
    1. Power off the instrument.
    2. Move the pipettor to the left of the wash tower, then lower the pipettor a few inches to allow clearance during disassembly.
    3. Remove the transducer connector cover by removing the two screws holding it in place, then lifting the cover up about 1/2 inch to allow its left end to clear the housing.
    4. Remove the Service Loop guide by removing the two screws holding it in place. (Photo Step 3)
    5. Disconnect the transducer power and heater connectors.
    6. Slide the service loop cable out of the slot in the left side of the transducer housing and place the cable out of the way.
    7. Note: Be prepared with a paper towel to capture the small amount of buffer that will drain from the probe.
      Remove the buffer line from the transducer.
      If the transducer tubing has a metal fitting, then loosening the fluid fitting at the transducer with a ¼ inch open-end wrench. (Photo Step 3).
      If the transducer tubing has a CnS tubing, then remove the tubing by turning counter clockwise.
      Note: If the tubing is too tight to remove by hand then use the CnS wrench.
    8. Caution:
      • The linear bearing will drop down to the stop pin at the bottom of the slide once the transducer assembly is pulled away from the slide. However, early instruments do not have a stop pin. Inspect the linear slide for a stop pin. If there is no pin, then prevent the slide from dropping before removing the transducer assembly.
      • During this step, hold onto the transducer assembly while removing the last mounting screw.
      Remove the four 2.5mm metric mounting screws which secure the transducer housing to the linear slide.
    9. Move the transducer assembly to the left while sliding the Z Belt out of the belt grip on the right side of the housing.
    10. Remove the old transducer data label from the right side of the carriage assembly.
  2. IPerform these steps to install the ultrasonics transducer from the pipettor gantry.
    Caution:
    • The transducer is a non-serviceable factory assembled and tested part. Any disassembly of this part will change its characteristics making it impossible to properly adjust.
    • The Instrument must be powered off before breaking or making electrical connectors.
    1. Slide the transducer assembly belt grip over the Z Belt with the clip in the general area (not critical) where there are marks on the belt from the removed transducer.
    2. Raise the linear bearing up near the top of the slide, insert 1 of the transducer mounting screws into the transducer, and start the screw but do not tighten it.
    3. Insert the remaining 3 mounting screws one at a time until all screws are started.
    4. Push the transducer to the left to align with the linear slide. Tighten all four screws.
    5. Slide the pipettor tip into the wash tower. Verify the probe tip is centered in the wash tower opening. Adjust as needed.
    6. Attach the transducer's power and heater connections, then place then flat on the housing top.
    7. Attach the buffer supply line and securely tighten the CnS fitting to the transducer housing.
      Note: The connector should make a clicking sounds once it is tightened correctly. Use the CnS wrench if needed.
    8. Install the front service loop cable into the house slot.
      Note:
      • Ensure that the service loop is properly installed by comparing and adjusting the height of the service loop cable to the buffer tubing.
      • The top loops of the tubing and cable should be similar in height and not extend above the REAR service loop guide before installed the FRONT service loop guide.
    9. Install the service loop connector cover and attach its two mounting screws.
    10. Caution:

      Ensure that the guide does not pinch the tubing or wiring during or after attaching the guide.

      Place the front service loop guide into position then route cable and tubing through the guide.
    11. Raise the pipettor Z carriage to its highest position and verify that the service loop cable and buffer tubing do not extend above the rear of the service loop guide.
  3. Perform these steps to set the Pipettor temperature, adjust the Ultrasonics PCB voltages, and adjust the mechanical alignments.
    1. Manually move the Z Carriage up and down several times to ensure that it moves smoothly.
    2. Power on the instrument and allow it to initialize.
    3. Prime the pipettor 1 cycle, then perform the transducer temperature adjustment procedure. Refer to the "Adjusting the Transducer Temperature" procedure.
      Note: The transducer temperature must be within 35 to 39 degrees C to achieve accurate tip displacement. An incorrect temperature setting can result in an incorrect tip displacement even though the ultrasonic voltage settings are on target.
    4. Drain the pipettor fluid line, then adjust the Ultrasonic voltage to accommodate the new transducer. Refer to "Transducer, Ultrasonics Adjustment procedure for details.
    5. Verify/adjust all pipettor alignments. Refer to the "Pipettor, Adjustments / Alignments, Access 2" procedure for details.
      Note: There is no adjustment for the pipettor Z home alignment on the Access 2 system. The pipettor Z-Carriage should move up until the flag attached to the carriage enters the home sensor.
    6. Verify that the pipettor fluid line is connected, then prime the pipettor 1 cycle and observe for proper fluid flow.
  4. Perform these steps to perform system verification after transducer replacement.
    1. Perform 20 replicates of the Dry / Wet Level Sense test. Refer to "Level Sense, Troubleshooting, Ultrasonics" for details.
    2. Refer to the "Verification Protocol, Access Service Repair" and perform the required system verification procedures.
