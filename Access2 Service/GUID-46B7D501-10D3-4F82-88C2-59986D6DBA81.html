Pin-Outs

The sections below provide information on the system power harness connector pin-out wiring.

Note: Wires are color-coded (Gray = Motor, Orange = Control, Black = Ground, Red = 5 V, Yellow = 12 V, Violet = 24 V).

Connector B To Pipettor Interface J11

Connector B mates to the Pipettor Interface PCB at J11. The signals proceed to J5, J6, and J12 to operate the pipettor motors and heaters.

Note: The cover interlock switch operates the relays K1 and K2 through the ribbon cable at J4. The cover interlock relay status can affect continuity measurements.

Connector C To Fluidics Interface J14

Connector C mates to the Fluidics Interface PCB at J14. The signals proceed to J23, J24, J25, J5, and J7 to operate the wash/precision pump/valve motors, the substrate valve and the substrate heater.

Note: Very old rev harnesses may lack connection of pin 21. This connection is required for vacuum pump operation when replacing fluidics PCBs Rev B or C with a newer version.
