Confirming Substrate System Contamination

This procedure describes how to confirm that the substrate system is contaminated before decontaminating the system.
Note: If the system is a new installation or the substrate pump has been replaced or rebuilt, then decontamination is required and confirming contamination is not necessary.
  1. Confirm that stray light entering the system is not an issue by reading the raw RLUs of an empty RV.
    1. In the Luminometer Diagnostics screen, select Load / Read, and then follow the prompts to send the RV in position 1 of the shuttle to the luminometer.
    2. The system displays the raw RLU from the RV loaded in front of the luminometer.
      Note: Confirm that all instrument covers are installed and closed when taking RLU readings.
    3. If the RLU is greater than 100, then troubleshoot stray light or the luminometer.
    4. Select Cancel to end the Load / Read operation and eject the RV.
  2. Read the raw RLUs of substrate that is hand-dispensed from a bottle that is known to be uncontaminated.
    1. Hand-dispense 200 µL of substrate into an RV.
      Note:
      • Hand-dispense the substrate from an unopened bottle that has been equilibrated to room temperature for a minimum of 18 hours.
      • Volume accuracy is important, so use correct technique and a pipettor of known quality.
    2. From the Luminometer Diagnostics screen, select Load / Read. Follow the prompts to place the RV containing substrate into position 1 of the shuttle and send it to the luminometer.
    3. The system displays the raw RLU from the RV that is in front of the luminometer.
      Note:
      • Confirm that all instrument covers are installed and closed when taking RLU readings.
      • The raw RLU is roughly one-fourth of the RLU in a normal system check. The formula for RLU is: Result = raw RLU × 4 × DCF. Example: 7000 = 1750 × 4 × 1.
    4. Allow the system to read the RV for 10 minutes.
      Note: The raw RLU of the substrate starts low, gradually climbs as it warms up, and then stabilizes between 1375 and 1875 after 10 minutes.
    5. Record the mean RLU of the hand-dispensed substrate.
    6. Select Cancel to end the operation and eject the RV.
  3. Read the raw RLUs of that substrate that has been dispensed through the instrument substrate system.
    1. Prime the substrate one cycle.
    2. Perform a substrate volume check to obtain three RVs filled with 200 µL of system-dispensed substrate.
    3. When the three volume check RVs are presented at the RV shuttle, remove them, and select OK. Save one substrate RV and discard the other two.
    4. From the luminometer Diagnostics screen, select Load / Read. Follow the prompts to place the RV containing system-dispensed substrate into position 1 of the shuttle and send it to the luminometer.
    5. The system displays the raw RLU from the RV that is in front of the luminometer.
      Note:
      • Confirm that all instrument covers are installed and closed when taking RLU readings.
      • The raw RLU is roughly one-fourth of the RLU in a normal system check. The formula for RLU is: Result = raw RLU × 4 × DCF. Example: 7000 = 1750 × 4 × 1.
    6. Allow the system to read the RV for 10 minutes.
      Note: The raw RLU of the substrate starts low, gradually climbs as it warms up, and stabilizes after 10 minutes.
    7. Record the mean RLU of the system-dispensed substrate.
    8. Select Cancel to end the operation and eject the RV.
  4. Compare the results of the hand-dispensed substrate RLU to the system-dispensed substrate RLU, and determine if the substrate system is contaminated.
    • If the system-dispensed RLU is greater than the hand-dispensed RLU by 250 or more, then substrate contamination is confirmed. Decontaminate the substrate system.
    • If the system-dispensed RLU is less than the hand-dispensed RLU by 250 or more, then the system-dispensed volume could be low or the substrate system could contain residual Citranox from a recent decontamination. Troubleshoot the substrate volume or prime the substrate to remove residual Citranox.
    • If the hand-dispensed RLU is approximately the same as the system-dispensed RLU and both are above 1875 RLU, then substrate contamination is not confirmed. Troubleshoot other areas. Decontamination is not necessary.
    Note: For troubleshooting RLU issues not caused by contamination, refer to the Adjusting Luminometer Settings procedure.
