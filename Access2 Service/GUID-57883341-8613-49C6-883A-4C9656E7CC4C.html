Tshoot: Substrate Portion of System Check

Use this table for troubleshooting the hardware items pertaining to System Check performance.

Part Failure
Substrate System Checks - 'Substrate' Fails
  • Loose fitting on the substrate bottle cap.
  • Substrate not fully primed.
  • Probe tip damaged or folded over.
  • Nut on the probe has been tightened restricting the tubing.
  • Tubing restricted or leaking anywhere in the substrate system.
  • Substrate not at the correct temperature (cold bottle loaded directly from the refrigerator).
  • Substrate valve defective, intermittent, or wire disconnected / open.
  • Cracked substrate supply line where substrate tubing exits bottle.
  • Substrate pump defective. Belt or seals worn.
  • Substrate heater leaking defective. (rep 7 or 8 of washed portion spike high)
  • Substrate contaminated. (confirm contamination per procedure)
