Remove / Install the X Axis Cable on Non-linear Slide Pipettor Gantry

Perform these steps to remove and install the X Axis Service Loop cable.
  1. Removing the X axis cable
    1. Locate the Pipettor Interface PCB inside the instrument and disconnect the X Axis service cable connector.
    2. From X Interface PCB, disconnect the X & Z Axis cables and the Z home sensor cable (labeled 3rd connector" in the picture).
    3. Caution: Exercise caution when repositioning the bracket so as not to damage the pipettor supply tubing.
      Release the X Interface PCB bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws and slide the pipettor to the left, for the rest of the removal procedure.
    4. Caution: Exercise caution when removing the retainers so as not to damage the pipettor supply tubing.
      Remove the front and rear loop retainer screws and retainers.
    5. Mark the X Axis cable and tubing where they enter the end loop. The cable marking will be transferred to the replacement cable.
    6. Slowly work the cable out of the loop, gently wiggle both ends, until the cable is removed. Slide the pipettor supply tubing out of the end loop.
    7. Detach the service loop chain end from the underside of the X Interface PCB bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws.
    8. Lift the X Axis cable up a couple of inches and remove the end bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws, then remove the end bracket from the rest of the service loop chain.
    9. Caution: Carefully opening the links so as not to damage the pin.
      Use the corner of a slotted blade screwdriver to open all links of the service loop chain.

    10. Remove the X Axis cable from the service loop chain.
    11. Mark a line on the X Axis cable where the cable crosses the end of the bracket near the position of the U-Clips.
      Note: This mark will be transferred to the new cable and will be used for proper positioning of the replacement cable.
    12. Remove the metal U-Clips and the cable from the gantry.
  2. Install the X axis cable
    1. Transfer the positioning marks from the original cable to the replacement cable.
    2. Caution: Improperly installed U-Clips will cause Pipettor motion errors.
      Position new cable on gantry using markings for U-clips. Route and connect to pipettor interface PCB.
    3. Caution: Ensure that the end bracket is parallel with the metal edge next to it. If it is not parallel, or rotated slightly counter clockwise, then the plastic service loop will rub the card cage.
      Attach the service loop end bracket to the top of the main pipettor plate.
    4. Route the cable and tubing into the service loop chain, ensuring that the cable and tubing do not cross paths and that the open link covers of the chain are positioned downward.
    5. Close all link covers and attach the service loop chain end bracket to the chain
    6. Attach the service loop chain end bracket to the X interface PCB bracket using the 2 mounting screws.
    7. Slide the X Axis cable into the metal end loop, then install its retainer and screw.
    8. Slide the Pipettor tubing into the end loop, then install its retainer and screw.
    9. Attach the X Interface PCB bracket to the top of the carriage assembly using its 2 mounting screws while ensuring the cable and tubing are not pinched.
    10. Route the Z home sensor cable and the X Axis cable through the harness clip.
    11. Caution: Ensure the Z home sensor wires are not contacting the main pipettor mounting plate.
      Connect the X Axis cable, the Z Axis cable and the Z home sensor cable to the X interface PCB.
    • If the Z Axis cable or tubing will not be replaced, then proceed to the verification section near the end of this procedure.
    • If the Z Axis cable does need to be replaced, then Remove / Install Z Axis Cable
    • If the tubing needs to be replaced, then Remove / Install the Pipettor Tubing
  3. Perform these verifications to ensure that the ultrasonics is operating properly.
    1. Manually move the gantry up and down and inspect the tubing and cabling for proper movement.
    2. Manually move the gantry left and right and inspect the rear of the carriage for clearance between its plastic harness clip and the metal U-Clips holding the X Axis cable in position. There should be a minimum of 1/8 inch clearance at this location.
    3. Caution: If the service loop chain rubs the card cage (little or no clearance), then adjust the upper and/or lower service loop chain end brackets to improve this clearance.
      Manually move the gantry left and right and inspect the right end of the plastic cable guide for rubbing between it and the front of the card cage.
    4. Refer to the "Module Pipettor Alignments / Adjustments" procedure () Access 2 only.
    5. Verify the transducer's temperature. Refer to "Transducer, Adjusting Temperature" () for specifications and procedures.
    6. Verify all Ultrasonic Transducer adjustments. Refer to the "Transducer, Ultrasonics Adjustment" procedure for details.
    7. Home the Pipettor and verify that the Pipettor is at the Home location.
    8. Perform the Ultrasonics Level Sense Wet/Dry test. (Run 20 reps). Refer to the "Troubleshooting, Level Sense" procedure ().
    9. Refer to the "Verification Protocol, Access Service Repair" () and perform the required system verification procedures.
