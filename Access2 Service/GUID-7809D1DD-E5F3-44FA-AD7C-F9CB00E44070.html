Alignment: Pipettor to Sample Carousel

Perform these steps to align the pipettor to the sample carousel.
  1. Load Sample Tray
    Perform these steps to load a Sample Tray for use during the Pipettor to Sample Carousel alignment.
    1. In the Mechanics screen select Pipettor as the device, choose Sample Carousel as the position, and then Home the Pipettor.
    2. Make the Sample Carousel the "Selected Device", then press Home to home the Sample Carousel.
    3. Load a sample tray with an inverted 2 ml sample cup placed in position 2.
    4. Press Move X to Position to move the tray to the aspirate position.
  2. Sample Aspirate X alignment
    Perform these steps to align the Pipettor to the sample tray.
    1. In the Mechanics screen make the Pipettor the Selected Device, then choose Sample CarouselSample Carousel as the position, Home the Pipettor, and then press Move X to Position.
    2. Remove the interlock switch key to disable the Pipettor motors, rotate the drive gear at the left end of the gantry to manually move the Pipettor down to just above the inverted cup in the Sample Tray, and then reinsert the interlock key.
      Note: Make sure the Pipettor does not move left or right when moving it down.
    3. Use the directional keys to adjust the probe X-Axis into alignment with the pointed end of the sample cup.
    4. Make the Sample Carousel the "Selected Device".
    5. Move the Sample Carousel CW-CCW using to adjust the carousel (pointed end of the sample cup) into alignment with the probe.
    6. Press Save Offset to save the new alignment offset values.
  3. Sample Aspirate Z alignment
    Perform these steps to align the Pipettor to the sample tray.
    1. Invert the sample cup in rack position 2 so that its open end is up.
    2. In the Mechanics screen make the Pipettor the Selected Device, choose: Sample Carousel as the position, Home the Pipettor, and then press: Move Z to Position.
    3. Insert a 0.010 inch feeler gauge between the probe and the top of the sample cup, then use the directional keys to adjust the probe Z-Axis to the correct height.
      Note: When the alignment is correct the gauge should fit between the probe and the sample cup with slight resistance.
    4. Press Save Offset to save the new alignment offset values.
    5. Make the Sample Carousel the "Selected Device", press Home to home the Sample Carousel, and then remove the sample tray.
    6. Make the Pipettor the Selected Device and then press Home to home the pipettor.

Go back to the parent topic to perform any other necessary alignments and for verification steps once all alignments are complete.
