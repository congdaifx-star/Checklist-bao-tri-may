Aligning Wash Carousel

This procedure describes how to align the wash carousel.

Required Materials: The wash carousel to incubator housing alignment tool is required for this procedure.

Use this procedure when:
  • Installing of a new wheel or a new motor drive.
  • Checking for a potential cause of RV jams.
  • Checking for a potential cause of splashing in RVs.
  1. Prepare the system.
    1. Home the incubator belt.
    2. Identify the four vessel holders that are centered at the wash-out location (two to the left of the WO mark, and two to the right).
    3. Disable the motors, and move the incubator belt by hand so that the four vessel holders can be removed near a corner pulley.
    4. Remove the four vessel holders from the incubator belt.
    5. Move the belt by hand back to home. Enable the motors, and home the incubator belt.
    6. Remove the substrate probe from the wash arm. Loosen the wash arm captive screw and move the wash arm out of the way.
    7. To verify the wash carousel alignment without making an adjustment, proceed to the step "Check Clearance for all 55 Positions on the Wheel".
  2. Determine the wash carousel wash-out alignment offset.
    Note:
    • The wash-out alignment offset (when saved) is added to the home position (step count at home position = 400 + wash-out offset).
    • The step count for any position is relative to the home position. Except for the home position, the step count does not change when a new offset is saved.
    • The screen displays offsets for CW and CCW positions, but these offsets are determined by the system automatically and cannot be changed.
    1. Home the wash carousel.
    2. Select Wash Out Align, and then press the Move to Position key.
      Note: Determining the offset can be done at any of the 55 wheel positions. If two positions have significantly different offset calculations, enter an offset that is midway between the two calculations to achieve satisfactory clearance at all 55 wheel positions.
    3. When the wheel stops moving, insert the alignment tool into the wash-out position (marked WO). Center the tool handle between the incubator belt dovetails. If the tool does not drop all the way down, use the arrow keys to step the wheel clockwise or counter-clockwise a few steps until the tool drops all the way down. The tool should be loose in the lowered position.
    4. Step clockwise using the arrow keys, and record the wash-out align offset reading as the CW value.
      The offset reading is recorded as follows: With the tool inserted into the wheel, use the arrow keys to step clockwise while lifting and dropping the tool with each step until you can feel that the tool is held tightly by the wash carousel and incubator track. Back step until the tool is released and drops down.
    5. Step CCW using the arrow keys and record the wash-out align offset reading as the CCW value.
      The offset reading is recorded as follows: With the tool inserted into the wheel, step counter-clockwise using the arrow keys while lifting and dropping the tool with each step until you can feel that the tool is held tightly by the wash carousel and incubator track. Back step until the tool is released and drops down.
    6. Remove the alignment tool from the wheel.
    7. Determine the wash-out alignment offset by calculating the mid-point of the two readings. Offset = (CW + CCW) / 2.

      Example: If the CW value = 18 and the CCW value = - 2, then the offset is calculated as [18 + (-2)] / 2 = 8.

    8. Enter the new offset (or step using the arrow keys until the desired offset is displayed), and then select Save Offset.
  3. Check clearance for all 55 positions on the wheel. Verify the wash carousel wash-out alignment at all positions on the wheel.
    Because of mechanical variability, an alignment offset that is centered at one position might not clear at another position. For this reason, the wheel must be checked for satisfactory clearance at all 55 positions.
    1. Home the wash carousel.
    2. Select Wash Out Align, and press the Move to Position key.
    3. When the wheel stops moving, insert the tool into the wheel at the wash-out position (marked WO).
      Satisfactory clearance is confirmed when the tool drops into place with minimal resistance.
    4. Lift the tool up above the top of the wheel, then press the Advance CW key. When the wheel stops moving, drop the tool into the new wheel position. Again, the tool drops into place with minimal resistance.
    5. Repeat this process until you have checked and confirmed satisfactory clearance at all 55 wheel positions.
      • If satisfactory clearance is not achieved at all 55 positions, then adjust the offset to compensate, and recheck the clearance for all 55 positions. (Suggestion: If two positions have conflicting offset calculations, then try an offset that is the average of the two.)
      • If satisfactory clearance for all 55 positions cannot be achieved after repeated adjustment attempts, then either the wheel or the drive is defective and must be replaced.
  4. Reinstall the wash arm, the substrate probe, and the four incubator belt clips.
  5. Confirm system functionality before allowing the system to resume operation.
    1. Home the incubator belt.
    2. Initialize the system
    3. Calibrate the incubator belt. Print and review the belt calibration report.
      Note: Refer to the Calibrating Incubator Belt procedure.
    4. Remove the light shield.
    5. Perform a minimum of one cycle of the Wash Car. Transfer exerciser, and observe the vessel holders for movement as the RV is transferred at the wash-in and wash-out areas.
      Note: Refer to the Theory of Operation - System Exerciser Routines procedure.
    6. If errors or vessel holder movements occur during cycling, then confirm the alignments of the incubator belt at the wash-in and wash-out positions, calibrate the incubator belt , and run the wash carousel transfer exerciser .
      Note: A defective or misaligned incubator belt or a defective vessel holder can also cause errors.
    7. Reinstall the light shield.
    8. Confirm that the wash arm and substrate probe are installed.
    9. Do one prime each of the substrate and dispense probes, and confirm the correct flow of fluids during the prime cycles.
    10. If the analytical module was removed, then confirm all of the alignments and the functionality of the pipettor and analytical module.
      Note: Refer to the Aligning Analytical Module and Sample Pipettor procedure.
  6. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform the required system verification.
      Note: Refer to the Verification of Preventive Maintenance document.
    2. Contact your local support organization with any questions or concerns.
