Configuring Hardware for CMOS Setup

This procedure describes how to connect the keyboard and monitor to the analytical unit for modifying the CPU CMOS settings.

  1. Connect the keyboard and monitor to the Access 2 instrument.
    1. Remove the keyboard and monitor cables from the back of the PC.
    2. Attach the monitor cable connector to the video port on the instrument.
    3. Connect the keyboard to the instrument.
      Note: For newer CPU PCBs, the keyboard can connect directly to the CPU PCB. Older CPUs can require use of a PS/2-to-AT adapter (973086), which allows a PS2 type keyboard to connect to the connector on the backplane PCB.
    4. Reboot the instrument and press the [Del] key during boot-up to enter the CMOS Setup screen.
    5. Modify the hard drive parameters in the CMOS setup according to the type of hard drive or the label on the hard drive.
      Note: Refer to the Configuring CMOS on AU CPU PCB procedure.
  2. Restore the keyboard and monitor connections to the PC console.
    1. Remove the monitor cable and the keyboard cable from the instrument.
    2. Attach the monitor cable connector to the video connector on the rear of the PC.
      Note: On No Dot DTx PCs, attach the monitor to the video card connector. On other PCs, use the motherboard video connector.
    3. Attach the keyboard cable to the bar code reader cable and the bar code reader to the keyboard port on the rear of the PC.
    4. A PC reboot is not necessary following reconnection.
