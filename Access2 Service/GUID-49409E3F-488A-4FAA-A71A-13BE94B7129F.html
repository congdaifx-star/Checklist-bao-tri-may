Deinstall: Packing

This procedure describes how to pack the instrument for shipping.

Required Materials:
  • Pallet
  • Shrink wrap
  • Laboratory tape
  • Bubble wrap
  • Cardboard
This document describes how to package a secured Access system to limit shipping-related damage of returned instruments.

Ensure the proper RGA paper work is complete before proceeding. Contact the RGA coordinator for more information.

Use this table to determine which series of procedures to perform to remove from service or de-install the Access system.

If the Access will be Then perform these procedures in the order listed
Inactive between one week and one month and not moved
  1. Remove Supplies
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Removing Supplies procedure.
Inactive for more than one month and not moved
  1. Rinse and Prime Dry
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry procedure.
  2. Remove Supplies
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Removing Supplies procedure.
Moved, or packed for shipment
  1. Rinse and Prime Dry
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry procedure.
  2. Remove Supplies
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Removing Supplies procedure.
  3. Disinfecting Fluids Tray and Instrument Exterior
    Note: Refer to the Disinfecting Fluids Tray and Instrument Exterior procedure.
  4. Secure Instrument
    Note: Refer to the Deinstall: Securing Instrument Components procedure.
  5. Packing
Package the instrument and peripherals.
  1. Place the secured instrument onto a pallet.
  2. Place the peripherals on the pallet beside the instrument.
  3. If there is sufficient room beside the instrument, place a protective layer of cardboard or bubble wrap over the top and sides of the instrument.
  4. Place the peripherals on top of the instrument and secure them with laboratory tape or shrink wrap.
  5. Secure the peripherals to the instrument with shrink wrap.
  6. Secure the entire package to the pallet with shrink wrap.
  7. Inspect the final package and confirm all is secured safely to the pallet.
  8. Apply the completed safety sticker and returned address label (supplied with the RGA paperwork).
  9. Contact the RGA coordinator for shipping instructions.
