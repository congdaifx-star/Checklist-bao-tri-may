PCB - Input/Output (I/O)

Purpose

This document provides information regarding functions, signals, voltages and test points on the I/O PCB 7597A.

I/O PCB Functions and Signals

I/O PCB Functions List
  • NVRAM (nonvolatile storage of alignment offsets, etc.).
  • LIS Communications Port.
  • Motor Enable signal to the Stepper Motor Driver PCB (global enable/disable to all stepper motors).
  • Enable signals (to the Power Driver PCB) to activate the Peristaltic Pump, Vacuum Pump, Vacuum Valve, and the Substrate Valve.
  • Pulse Width Modulated signals (to the Power Driver PCB) to drive the Heaters, and the Reagent Cooler (Peltiers)
  • Mixer Motor Direction and Enable signals (to the Power Driver PCB).
  • Power to the Reference LED, and the LED Enable signal (which is also sent to the Power Driver PCB, but only to drive an indicator LED).
  • 5.2 VDC reference for the High Voltage PS on the Luminometer.
  • Interprets whether the system is a "rev A or rev B fluidics" based on the Revision Code inputs from the PCBs.
I/O PCB Input Signals List
  • Mixer Motor Speed Detection.
  • Luminometer Gate and Accumulator.
  • RV sensors.
  • Door position sensors.
  • Sample cup/tube sensors.
  • Wash Buffer and Waste fluid level sensors.
  • Waste bag sensor.
  • Temperature and vacuum sensors.
  • Revision codes for PCBs (Power Driver, Stepper Driver, and Backplane).
Note: Each of these inputs is protected from RF noise and false triggering.
I/O PCB Heater / Cooler Controllers

Heating:

  • Incubator.
  • Wash wheel.
  • Substrate fluid in-line heater.
  • Probe fluid heater.

Cooling:

  • Reagent carousel .
Note: The heating and cooling zones have a Proportional Integral Derivative (PID) algorithm to control the temperature.
