Incubator Belt Calibration Theory Of Operation

This document details the incubator belt calibration process.

Summary

The dovetails on every incubator belt vary slightly in location from one belt to another. The incubator belt calibration allows the system to detect and accommodate variations in incubator belt vessel holder positions. After calibration, the exact measurement for each vessel holder is stored in the I/O PCB. The measurement is used to determine how many steps any vessel holder flag must be moved into the index sensor to locate any other vessel holder precisely at each of the transfer points.

Calibration Process

Table 45: Software-initiated steps at start of incubator belt calibration.
Step Action
1 The incubator belt rotates in a clockwise direction.
2 A vessel holder steps into the index sensor, one step at a time until the vessel holder flag 'trips' the index sensor.
3 The system records the number of steps traveled.
4 The process continues moving every vessel holder on the belt into the index sensor, recording the flag trip point for each vessel holder.
5 The incubator belt moves in the counterclockwise direction, and the vessel holder flag-checking process is repeated for each vessel holder on the incubator belt.
6 The system determines the number of steps traveled from the center of Vessel Holder 36 to the CW and CCW edge of each of the other vessel holders and reports the results on a printout.
7 The offsets are listed under the headers CW Value and CCW Value on the calibration printout.
8 The system determines the number of steps that each vessel holder varied from the theoretical value of 200 steps and lists them under the headers Nom Diff on the calibration report.
9 The system determines if the calibration is acceptable and generates the event log entry.

Specifications

Table 46: Specifications for important aspects of incubator belt calibration
Parameter Specification
Nominal difference between vessel holders 200 steps
Normal vessel holder flag width 28 steps
Vessel holder at shuttle transfer when the incubator belt is at home Vessel Holder 0
Vessel holder next to Vessel Holder 0 in the CW direction Vessel Holder 1
Vessel holder in the index sensor when the incubator belt is at home Vessel Holder 36
One complete incubator belt revolution 12,000 steps
Vessel Holder 0 CW minus Vessel Holder 1 CW is theoretically 200 Steps
Vessel Holder 0 CCW minus Vessel Holder 0 CW is theoretically 28 Steps
All vessel holder locations and offsets are relative to Vessel Holder 36 CW position 0
CW nominal difference for Vessel Holder 36 Always 0 on printout
Incubator belt movement for one motor step 0.004 inch
Vessel holder with the magnet Vessel Holder 35
