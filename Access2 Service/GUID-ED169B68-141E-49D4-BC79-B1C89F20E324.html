Line Conditioner - Powervar

Definition

Line Conditioner: A combination of a voltage regulating transformer with a super isolation transformer which provides smooth, regulated, noise free, AC voltage with no ohmic connection between input and output. A Line Conditioner will solve most problems other than complete power failure.
Note: A line conditioner is not a Universal Power Supply (UPS). A UPS is designed to provide short term power in case of a short term loss of power. A UPS contains batteries which are constantly charged during normal operation. The stored DC battery power is converted to AC power if line power fails.
