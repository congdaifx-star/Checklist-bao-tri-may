Replacing Reagent Carousel Thermistor

This procedure describes how to replace the reagent carousel thermistor.

Required Tools;
  • Thermistor removal tool (SLOTTED 5/16-in NUT DRIVER 3693A)
Perform this procedure when the reagent carousel thermistor fails.
When the reagent carousel thermistor fails, it displays one of three symptoms:
  • A temperature error indicating a temperature slightly higher than the actual temperature —Partially shorted thermistor.
  • 50 °C —Shorted thermistor.
  • -21 °C —Open thermistor.
Note: For troubleshooting information, refer to the Troubleshooting Reagent Carousel Thermistor procedure.
  1. Remove a defective thermistor.
    1. Remove all reagents and sample racks from the sample and reagent carousels module.
    2. Disconnect all wires and cables necessary for removing the module.
    3. Remove the module, and invert the module over a sink to drain any moisture.
    4. Disconnect the reagent carousel thermistor at J15 and free the cabling from the harness clamps.
    5. Remove the (white or blue) insulation that surrounds the thermistor.


    6. Place the thermistor cable into the thermistor removal tool.


    7. Rotate the tool counterclockwise until the thermistor is removed. Lift the thermistor from the module.


    8. Verify that the metal spacer that limits how far the thermistor can be installed is still in place. If the spacer is loose or adheres to the thermistor when the thermistor is removed, separate the spacer from the thermistor and set the thermistor aside for reuse.
    9. Properly dispose of the removed thermistor.
  2. Install a replacement thermistor.
    1. Confirm that the thermistor spacer is in position over the thermistor mounting hole before installing the thermistor.
    2. Lightly coat the threads on the replacement thermistor with thermal compound.
    3. Insert the thermistor cable into the slot in the thermistor tool.


    4. Place the thermistor into the mounting location and rotate the tool clockwise until the thermistor is attached securely.
    5. Install the insulation around the thermistor.
    6. Route the thermistor cable through all the harness clamps, and connect the thermistor to J15 on the reagent carousel interface PCB.
    7. Install the module into position in the instrument.
    8. Attach the module hold-down fasteners.
    9. Connect all the cables and wires to the reagent carousel interface PCB.
      Note: The Sensor Out connector on the lower rear edge of the PCB is often forgotten.
  3. Verify thermistor operation after replacement.
    1. Power on the instrument
    2. View the Analog Devices screen.
    3. Ensure that the reagent temperature is not negative (a negative temperature means that there is an open circuit).
      • If the temperature is positive but less than 40 °C, then allow 30 minutes for the reagent temperature to cool to within the specified temperature range. Monitor the temperature while waiting for it to cool to within the correct temperature range.
  4. If the temperature decreases to within the correct range, then verify all pipettor to sample carousel and reagent carousel alignments.
    Note: Refer to the Pipettor Alignment / Adjustment procedures.
  5. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform the required system verification.
      Note: Refer to the Verification of Preventive Maintenance document.
    2. Contact your local support organization with any questions or concerns.
