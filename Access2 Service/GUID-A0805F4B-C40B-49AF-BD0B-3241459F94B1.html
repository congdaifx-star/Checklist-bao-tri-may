Remove / Install the Z Axis Service Loop on the X Axis Linear Slide Pipettor Gantry

Perform these steps to remove and install the Z Axis Service Loop cable.

Caution: The instrument should be powered off when making or breaking electrical connectors to avoid damaging the instrument's electronics.
Note: Cables and tubing can be replaced separately or all together.
  1. Remove the Z axis service loop cable.
    1. Power off the instrument.
    2. Move the pipettor gantry to the left of the Wash Tower, then manually move it downward a couple of inches.
    3. Remove the Front Service Loop guide by removing the two screws holding it in place (picture A below).

    4. Remove the connector cover by loosening the cover screw, then lifting the cover up out of the slot.
    5. Disconnect the transducer power and heater connectors. Disconnect the fluid tubing.
    6. Slide the service loop cable out of the channel in the left side of the transducer housing (picture B above).
    7. Disconnect the following from the X axis interface PCB (picture below):
      • the Z Axis service loop cable
      • the X Axis service loop cable
      • the Z Axis Home Sensor
    8. Disconnect the Z Motor harness from the Z motor connection and remove the harness from the clamp (picture below).

    9. Remove the two mounting screws which secure the Rear Service Loop Guide assembly (picture above).
    10. Lift the guide upward until the loop retainer screws are accessible.
    11. Mark a line on the Z Axis cable where it enters the retaining loop (picture below).
    12. Remove the Service Loop retainer and screw from the bottom of the rear guide (Cable side only) and remove the Z Axis cable from the cable guide (picture below).

  2. Install the Z axis cable.
    1. Transfer the mark from the removed Z Axis cable onto the corresponding position on the replacement cable, then slide the cable into the retaining loop of the rear Z Axis Guide, placing the mark at the position identified when removing it.
    2. Install the rear Z Axis Guide retainer and screw (see Step 1k).
    3. Reposition the Rear Service Loop Guide and secure it with the 2 screws (Step 1h).
    4. Route the replacement cable into the slot on the rear guide and then into the channel on the transducer (Step 1e).
      Caution: The cable loop may rub on the instrument cover if looped too high. When completed, the cable and tubing should not extend above the rear service loop guide with the pipettor at the Z home position and should be similar in height to each other and to the rear guide.
    5. Connect the transducer heater and power connectors together, then lay them flat on top of the housing.
    6. Install the Connector Cover placing the slot over the securing screw. After installation ensure no wires are pinched or visible outside of the cover. Secure the cover screw.
    7. Align the tubing and cable with the front service loop guide.
    8. Install the front Z Axis Guide using its 2 mounting screws, ensuring that the cable and tubing are properly routed in their slots in the guide.
    9. Reconnect all of the harnesses (Steps 1g and 1h).
  3. Completion Steps.
    • If the X Axis cable or tubing will not be replaced, then proceed to the verification section. Refer to Instrument Verification Protocol for Access Service Repair document and perform the required system verification procedures
    • If the X Axis cable is going to be replaced, then continue to remove or install X axis cable. Refer to Remove / Install an X Axis Service Loop Chain Component on the X Axis Linear Slide Pipettor Gantry for more details.
    • If the tubing is going to be replaced, then remove and install the pipettor tubing. Refer to Remove / Install an X Axis Service Loop Chain Component on the X Axis Linear Slide Pipettor Gantry for more details.
