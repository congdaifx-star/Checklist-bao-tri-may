Decontaminating Substrate Pump Valve Prior to Install

This procedure describes how to manually decontaminate a new Substrate Pump Valve before installing on the system to avoid substrate system contamination. May also be used when substrate system contamination occurs.
Note: This procedure only applies to substrate valve PN D15548.
Caution: FSE should use sterile techniques. Wear gloves and avoid touching any surfaces once it's been decontaminated during this procedure. Perform this procedure on a clean low lint material if possible.
  1. Prepare or locate these supplies for decontamination.
    1. Locate a clean beaker large enough for soaking the valve parts
    2. Fresh mixture of 20% Citranox in wash buffer (1 part Citranox & 4 parts deionized water)
    3. Fresh Contrad
    4. One bottle of unopened equilibrated substrate
    5. Gauze or felt tipped swabs
    6. Transfer Pipette
    7. Alcohol swabs (patches)
  2. Decontaminate the Substrate Pump Valve.
    1. Separate valve from acrylic block (two small Philipps head screws).
    2. Soak the valve diaphragm end, acrylic block and O-rings in a small clean beaker with Contrad for at least 5 minutes.
      Caution: Do not over fill the beaker to avoid damage to the valve electronic components. Recommended depth of fluid is 3/4 inch (19 mm). See images below for more details.

    3. Using a transfer pipette, flush ports of the acrylic block.
    4. Gently wipe down the valve diaphragm with gauze or swab dipped in the Contrad solution.
    5. Repeat steps a. thru d. with freshly made 20% Citranox solution.
    6. Repeat steps a. thru d. with Substrate (use new bottle).
  3. Install the Substrate Pump Valve.
    1. Reassemble the valve and acrylic block parts and insert the 3 O-rings into the acrylic block ports.
    2. Install the Substrate Pump Valve assembly onto the Substrate Pump.
      Note: Before installing the valve assembly, excess residue from the cleaning solutions and substrate may be removed from the mating acrylic surfaces with alcohol swabs (patches).
  4. Verify proper operation.
    1. Prime substrate x30 and observe for leaks.
    2. Perform system verification as stated in the Instrument Verification Protocol for Access Service Repair document.
      Note: If elevated RLU's occur, then perform the Decontaminating Substrate System procedure.
