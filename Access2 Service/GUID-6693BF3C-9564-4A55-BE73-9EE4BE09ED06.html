Adjusting and Aligning Reagent Carousel

This procedure describes how to check and adjust the reagent carousel alignments.

Pre-requisites:
These alignments and adjustments must be performed before performing any of the reagent carousel alignments:
  • Pipettor X-position
  • Pipettor Y-position
  • Pipettor Z-position
  • Reagent carousel tray to reagent carousel drive pulley
  1. Open the instrument front cover and insert the interlock switch bypass key into the front cover interlock switch.
  2. Align the reagent carousel to the main pipettor probe at the aspirate location.
    Note: Before performing this alignment, ensure that the main pipettor probe is not bent or damaged.
    1. In the Diagnostics Mechanics screen, select Reagent Carousel.
    2. Home the reagent carousel.
    3. Select the aspirate location, and then select Move to Position.
      Note:
      • Use the [Tab] key to move the cursor.
      • Access 2 identifies the Aspirate location as Pipettor.
    4. Home the secondary device (Pipettor), and then move the device to the reagent pack aspirate location.
    5. Remove the interlock switch bypass key, manually move the pipettor downward until the probe is just above the alignment pack, and then reinsert the bypass key.
    6. Use the left and right arrow keys to move the reagent carousel clockwise or counter-clockwise, aligning the white cross hairs on the alignment tool to the pipettor tip.


      Note: If the alignment requires more than 15 steps, then the offset can be reduced by adjusting the position of the index sensor. If adjustment of the sensor position is not sufficient, then realign the carousel to the drive pulley. Refer to the Alignment section of Replacing Peltiers (Access 2) procedure.
    7. Save the alignment.
    8. Home the reagent carousel, and then move the reagent carousel to the aspirate location.
    9. Move the pipettor into position and confirm the alignment is correct.
      Note: Leave the alignment tool in the reagent carousel to perform the next step.
  3. Confirm the pipettor y-position alignment to the reagent carousel.
    Note: Ensure that the main pipettor probe is not bent or damaged before performing this alignment.
    1. With the pipettor positioned over the cross hairs on the alignment tool, remove the interlock switch bypass key and manually move the pipettor down the length of the alignment tool. If the line on the pack tool is not straight to the pipettor line of travel, then the module may not be seated fully or the pipettor y-position may not be aligned correctly.


    2. Home the reagent carousel, unload the alignment tool, and remove the interlock switch bypass key.
    3. Press [Escape] to exit the Alignment screen.
    4. Select Save when prompted to save the alignments.
  4. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform the required system verification.
      Note: Refer to the Verification of Preventive Maintenance document.
    2. Contact your local support organization with any questions or concerns.
  5. Ensure that the cover interlock bypass key is removed from the front cover interlock switch. Do not leave the key at customer site.
