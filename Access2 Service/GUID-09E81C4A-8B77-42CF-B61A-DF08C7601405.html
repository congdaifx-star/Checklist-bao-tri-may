Pinch Roller Theory Of Operation

This document describes the theory of operation of the pinch rollers and describes the differences between the solid pinch rollers and the universal pinch rollers.

Pinch Roller Functions

During the mixing and washing process, reaction vessels (RVs) are sandwiched between the mixer pulley and the pinch roller. The pinch rollers perform these functions:
  • The mixer pulley provides the rotational drive via the mixer motor and belt.
  • The pinch roller cradles the RV to allow smooth spinning.
  • The RV is automatically spun alternately clockwise and counterclockwise during the mixing process.
  • The pinch rollers are used in two different process functions:
    • Sample washing
    • Substrate suspension

Sample Washing

After the magnetic particles, conjugate, sample, and any other reagents have been dispensed into an RV, the RVs are washed. This washing process removes all unbound analyte fractions and leaves only magnetic particles with bound analyte fractions and conjugate.
  • The function of the pinch rollers during sample washing is to rotate the reaction vessels smoothly at 2500 rpm while wash buffer is dispensed through the ports in the side dispense probes.

Substrate Mixing

While the reaction vessel is rotated at the substrate dispense station, the reaction vessels are topped off with 200 microliters of substrate from the substrate probe. After the substrate is dispensed, the substrate reacts with the materials that are left in the RV after washing. This reaction creates the light that the luminometer reads.
  • The function of the pinch rollers during substrate mixing is to rotate the RVs smoothly at 2500 rpm while substrate is dispensed through the substrate probe. This mixing suspends the particles evenly in the substrate.

Inspection

Inspect the pinch rollers using the mixer exerciser in the user interface.
  • There should be little or no shaking or bouncing of the RV during this process. If sample is splashed up the walls of the RV during the mixing process, the RLUs can be affected.
