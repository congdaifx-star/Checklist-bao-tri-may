Tshoot: Analytical Module

Use this table for troubleshooting the hardware items in the Analytical module.

Item

Check for

Incubator Belt Errors

  • RV down in incubator track.
  • Damaged or bent vessel holder leg.
  • Incorrect alignment at any of the 3 RV transfer locations.
  • Defective incubator belt pulley bearing.
  • Broken vessel holder leg in track not found or recovered from previous jam.
  • RV Shuttle not retracting fully to its "home" position.
  • RV not cleared from the wash wheel during clean-out.
  • RV inadvertently loaded onto incubator belt or wash wheel after a jam by moving these devices without first cleaning out.
  • Vessel holder hitting or rubbing the Incubator belt index or home sensor, light shield, or bracket
  • Flag (tab) broken off of a vessel holder.
  • Missing vessel holder.
  • Incubator belt 'Home' or 'Index' sensor broken.
  • Incubator belt drive/idler pulleys too high-excessive vertical 'play' within pulleys.
  • Incubator belt drive pulley pin(s) damaged causing incorrect positioning for RV transfers.
  • QSMC PCB not seated correctly.
  • Defective or distorted incubator belt dove tails due to major jams.
  • Mismatched mold parting line around the incubator belt circumference causing dovetail offset.

Incubator Belt Area: Poor washing or flyers

  • Deposits in incubator track causing splashing in RVs.
  • Defective incubator belt pulley bearing.
  • Damaged or bent vessel holder leg.
  • Incorrect alignment at any of the 3 RV transfer locations.

Luminometer:

Zero RLU results

  • Raw RLU less than 750 counts (Event Log message and suppressed results).
  • Luminometer Scale Factor is Zero in nvsetup.csv file.
  • Raw RLU greater than 15 million (Event log message and suppressed results).
  • LED target value set to exactly 1,000,000 (default blank nvsetup file).

Luminometer Dark Count Errors

  • Instrument covers open.
  • Wash wheel contains spilled substrate or sample.
  • Light Shield is not installed or is improperly seated.
  • LED gasket is not installed or is improperly seated.
  • Loose grounding hardware in Luminometer area.
  • Electrical noise.
  • Gold colored connector on PMT H.V. power supply is loose.
  • Luminometer mounting screws loose.
  • Defective Luminometer.
  • Defective I/O PCB.

Mixer Area Errors / Failures

  • Mixer belt teeth worn excessively.
  • Broken wire at mixer motor (often inside shrink tube on motor top).
  • Mixer tensioner pulley bearing defective. (slows motor speed)
  • Mixer pulley bearings worn/seized (symptom >20 RPM difference CW to CCW).
  • Broken wire on mixer speed sensor.
  • Mixer motor drive gear loose on motor shaft.
  • Mixer belt - loose cord strands on edge.
  • Mixer belt rubbing on incubator belt guide block.
  • Mixer belt rubbing on wiring harness.
  • Defective Power Driver PCB.

Rake Failures / Errors

  • RVs not really loaded by operator when load task is performed in the User Interface.
  • RVs improperly loaded behind the rake (loaded with rake not at home position).
  • RV Sensors out of adjustment. Rake will not move toward shuttle from home.
  • Rake to shuttle alignments incorrect.
  • RV count incorrect. One or more rows missing an RV or having an extra RV.
  • Rake Elastomeric strips incorrectly positioned or damaged.
  • Loose Rake drive belt.
  • Damaged rake idler pulley bearing.
  • Loose index sensor wheel.
  • Wire or loose foam insulation obstructing the rake index sensor

RV Shuttle Errors or Failures

  • Waste bag not fully down in its mounting or waste bag filled beyond capacity.
  • RV Shuttle alignment incorrect.
  • End of shuttle walking beam (brown plastic) broken off.
  • Improper software set-up in system configuration screen. (must be Rev 2)
  • RV Shuttle not installed properly with incorrect gap to rake veins or left/right alignment incorrect.
  • Rake alignment incorrect.
  • Incubator belt alignment at shuttle transfer incorrect.
  • Vessel holder leg bent or otherwise deformed.
  • Broken holder leg or RV in incubator track, waste chute, or beside shuttle causing jam.
  • RVs not properly retained in rake, falling into RV Shuttle causing it to jam.
  • Eject gate spring broken, disconnected, or rubbing on another component.
  • Waste chute Velcro strip missing or damaged.
  • RV Shuttle Index/home sensor damaged, broken wire, corroded connector or defective crimp.

Wash Carousel Errors / Failures

  • Corroded wash carousel vertical or horizontal bearing.
  • Incorrect alignment of incubator belt to wash carousel wash-in or wash-out.
  • Incorrect wash carousel wash-out alignment.
  • Loose incubator belt tension.
  • Damaged or distorted incubator belt dovetails and clips.
  • Reaction vessel or vessel holder leg laying down at 'Wash in' or 'Wash out'.
  • Sticky movement of the incubator pulleys.
  • Dirty incubator track or wash carousel.
  • Damaged wash carousel, bent fin.
  • Wash carousel housing flooded and fluid has entered and corroded the drive motor.
  • Wash carousel sensor damaged, broken wire, corroded connector or defective crimp.
  • SMD PCB is defective or dust in backplane connector interferes with connection.
  • Pinch roller hardware loose preventing proper RV release after mixing.
  • Dried fluid spill in wash carousel.

Warning: Do not flatten the RV positioning spring at the Luminometer as this may alter the positioning of the RV in the read area and could potentially cause an incorrect reading.

Wash Arm Errors / Failures

  • Cable, tubing, or wire restricting wash arm movement.
  • Cable, tubing, or wire jammed into wash arm home sensor.
  • Wash arm home sensor flag broken.
  • Wash arm lead screw bearing or linear bearing defective.
  • Aspirate or dispense probe not clearing hole in wash carousel as the arm lowers.
  • Wash arm hold down screw not tight.
  • Wash arm drive pulley set screw loose (backed out and hits wash carousel housing).
  • Hardware on linear bearing clamp loose.
  • Wash arm collar cracked or otherwise loose or defective.
  • Linear slide needs lubrication..
  • Sensor mounting screws loose.
  • SMD PCB is defective or dust in backplane connector interferes with connection.
  • Wash arm drive belt tension too loose or too tight.
