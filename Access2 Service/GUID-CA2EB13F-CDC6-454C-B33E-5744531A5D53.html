PCB - Ultrasonics

Purpose

This document provides information regarding the Ultrasonics PCBs 8382B for Access, and 8808B for Access2.

Access 2 PCB

PCB Revision Inspection

PLL errors can be caused by, incorrect sample cup and tray combination, no fluid in the sample cup, a faulty ultrasonic transducer, or a break in the service loop.

Mod 6164 suggests inspection of all Access 2 instruments with serial numbers less than 500477 that are experiencing random or intermittent PLL (Phase Lock Loop) errors.

The Ultrasonics PCB part number should be 8808B. If the PCB is revision 'A', then replace with the revision 'B' PCB on an as-fail basis. The new board has modified capacitor values and adds two new capacitors to reduce undesired electrical noise spikes that cause the PLL errors. Noise on the Ultrasonic PCB would be a rare cause for PLL errors.

Step Action
1 Remove the existing Ultrasonics PCB from the electronics card cage.
Note: The Ultrasonics PCB is the third circuit board from the front of the card cage.
2 Inspect the PCBs revision information located left and down from center; on the PCBs component side (see Parts Required) after the PCBs part number: 11-008808__.
3 If the part number revision (letter designation after the part number) is revision 'A' then replace with a revision 'B' PCB.
4 If the part number is a revision 'B', return the PCB to its position the card cage.
