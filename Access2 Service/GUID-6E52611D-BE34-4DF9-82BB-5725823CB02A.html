Summary

This document provides information regarding the Analytical Module Interface PCB.

VS1 1.15 V RV Sensor 1

With RV removed adjust to 1.15 V. Vessel present is normally 4.2 V and is not adjusted.

Software thresholds are >2.5 V = RV present. <2.0 V = RV not present

VS2 1.15 V RV Sensor 2

With RV removed adjust to 1.15 V. Vessel present is normally 4.2 V and is not adjusted.

Software thresholds are >2.5 V = RV present. <2.0 V = RV not present

J3 Incubator Thermistor

Incubator Temp In J3 feeds through to J23 Incubator Temp Out

1 = Temp 6H

2 = Temp 6L

3 = Shield

Note: The thermistor connected to J3 is the one that is threaded into the top side of the RV loader area. Old version analytical modules may have a thermistor threaded into the bottom of the incubator housing near the incubator home sensor. The bottom thermistor is no longer used. The thermistor was called the incubator thermistor and connected to J3.
