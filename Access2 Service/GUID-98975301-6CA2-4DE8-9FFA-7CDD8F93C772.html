Instrument Power Supply Theory of Operation

This document provides operational details for the Access instrument power supply.

Power Supply Features

  • The main power supply provides four DC voltages: +5, +12, -12, and +24.
  • The power supply is a self-contained module and is mounted onto a sled to facilitate removal.
  • The power supply can be powered on at any voltage between 110 and 220 volts AC (auto-switching).
  • The power supply incorporates an On/Off switch and AC line filter.
  • A cooling fan is built into the power supply. The fan directs airflow across the power supply components, from left to right (as viewed from the front of the instrument).
  • The power supply output is delivered via a power harness to the Backplane PCB J7 and distributed throughout the instrument.
  • Each voltage is fused on the Backplane PCB.
Note:
  • For fuse information, refer to the PCB - Backplane (Fuses & Connectors) document.
  • The 5 V supply voltage can still be present through connections on the backplane, even if the 5 V fuse is open.

Power Supply Actions

Use this table to determine which action to take when encountering power supply issues.

If Then
One of the DC voltages is outside of specification. Adjust the voltage.
Note: Refer to the Adjusting Power Supply Voltage procedure.
Any of the DC voltages are not present on the power supply or the voltage cannot be adjusted within specification. Replace the power supply.
