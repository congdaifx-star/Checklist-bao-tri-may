Adjusting Wash Arm

This procedure describes how to align/adjust the wash arm pinch roller actuator guide brackets and the aspirate probe height.

Required Tools:
  • 0.050-in feeler gauge
  • 0.010-in feeler gauge
  1. Adjust the pinch roller actuator brackets.
    1. Home the wash arm.
    2. Loosen the mounting screws on both of the pinch roller actuator guide brackets.
    3. Place a 0.050-in feeler gauge between the pinch roller actuator arm and the guide bracket.
    4. Push the bracket toward the wash carousel until snug, then tighten the bracket mounting screws.
    5. Disable the motors and push the wash arm down.
    6. Verify that the pinch roller actuator arms are free and allow the pinch rollers to deploy fully.
    7. Enable the motors and home the wash arm.
  2. Adjust the aspirate probe height.
    1. Ensure the aspirate probes are installed correctly, are straight, and are centered over their respective holes in the wash carousel housing.
    2. Home the wash arm.
    3. Move the wash arm to position. Use the arrow keys to adjust the wash arm up or down until a 0.010-in feeler gauge fits snugly between the center aspirate probe and the wash carousel housing.
      Note:
      • Software allows a maximum offset of +20 steps. Adjust the home flag position, if the maximum offset is exceeded.
      • The aspirate probes are spring-loaded. If a probe rises when lowered to the feeler gauge, then the wash arm is adjusted too far down.
    4. Save the offsets, home the wash arm, and move the wash arm to position.
    5. Check the gap between the wash carousel housing and aspirate probes 2 and 3. If the gap is not 0.010 inch, repeat the steps to adjust the aspirate probe height.
      Note: If necessary, the wash arm can be adjusted for level after loosening the eight mounting screws on the wash arm linear slide (a metric hex wrench is required). When the wash arm is level, tighten the screws and repeat the steps to adjust the aspirate probe height.
    6. Save the alignment values.
  3. Verify the wash arm is functioning properly.
    1. Disable the motors.
    2. Manually move the wash arm down and verify the aspirate, dispense, and substrate probes enter the wash carousel. Verify that wash arm motion is not restricted by tubing, wires, etc.
    3. Move the wash arm up into the home position and enable the motors.
    4. Initialize the system.
    5. Exercise the wash arm. Allow at least two cycles to complete before stopping the exerciser. If errors occur during the exerciser test, troubleshoot as necessary and repeat wash arm verification steps.
      Note: Refer to the System Exerciser Routines Theory of Operation procedure.
  4. Verify the pinch rollers function properly.
    1. Run the Mixer Exerciser test.
      Note: Refer to the System Exerciser Routines Theory of Operation procedure.
    2. Confirm that the retracted pinch rollers do not bump the RVs when the RVs are being moved into position. Confirm that the pinch rollers deploy fully when the wash arm moves down and that the RVs rotate smoothly during mixing.
