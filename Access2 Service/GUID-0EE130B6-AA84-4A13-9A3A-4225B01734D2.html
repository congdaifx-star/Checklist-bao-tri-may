Tshoot: Fluids Tray

Use this table for troubleshooting the hardware items in the fluids tray (also see Fluids tray documents in the peripherals chapter).

Item

Check for

Fluids Tray Sensor Errors/ Failures

Cable connector from instrument to tray corroded, open, shorted, or crimped.

Fluids Tray Substrate Area Failures

  • Substrate bottle cap fittings loose (air or insufficient substrate dispensed).
  • Substrate bottle internal draw tube is too short or missing.
  • Check valve on the substrate air vent line may be connected backwards or to the wash buffer vent.

Fluids Tray Reservoir Area Failures

An overflowing buffer tank is normally caused by a leak in the rubber seal between the bottle cap (called a valve) and the bottle. Air leaks into the bottle and allows fluid to flow out, overfilling the tank. Overflow can also be caused by not allowing the bottle to completely empty before loading a new one.

Common issues:

  • Wash buffer bottle cap (valve) seal failure.
  • Wash buffer bottle and valve assembly have poor thread match. (Use valve assembly with black dot.)
  • Wash buffer reservoir outlet fitting is loose and leaking (needs Teflon tape to seal threads).
  • Wash buffer reservoir supply tank air vent tubing siphoning fluid (only if tank over fills).
  • Wash buffer reservoir cracked or seam defective.
