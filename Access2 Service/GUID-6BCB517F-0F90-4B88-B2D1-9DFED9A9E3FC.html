Customer Training After Instrument Installation

Summary

The Training Checklist and Log:
  • Lists the skills and knowledge objectives for the specific training session
  • Is a guide for the instructor and trainee(s) to ensure coverage and comprehension of the desired subjects
  • Provides a record of training
  • May be completed manually or electronically

Using the templates

Note:

Training Certificates are provided only at the customer's request. If desired, complete the appropriate section of the training checklist and e-mail or mail to:

Beckman Coulter Inc.

1000 Lake Hazeltine Dr.

Chaska, MN 55318

Attn: Rosemary Willett MS R530-A

Certificates will be mailed to the customer using the information provided.

  1. If the checklist is to be completed manually, then launch the document, print it, and enter all information as requested. Proceed to step 5.

    If the document is to be completed electronically, then proceed to step 2.

  2. Launch the document. The document will open as a "Read Only", with the cursor in the first field.

  3. Enter the requested information, then tab to the next field. Continue until all fields are completed.

    For check-boxes, enter X as appropriate.

    For text-fields, enter the requested information.

  4. Save the document with the desired filename, to the desired location.
  5. Print a copy to leave with the customer.
