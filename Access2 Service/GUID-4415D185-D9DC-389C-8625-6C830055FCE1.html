Troubleshooting Temperature Wash Carousel and Incubator

This procedure provides information regarding troubleshooting and repair of the Wash Carousel and Incubator heaters.

Symptoms

Use this table to analyze thermistor and heater problems.

Symptom

Troubleshooting notes

Temperature readings which are slightly above or below the limits.

Note:
  • The temperatures may overshoot the limits when the instrument is first powered on, or if a gust of cold air blows across the instrument.
  • Check that the internal case temperature displayed is not higher than the maximum specified analytical module temperature.

The associated heater or thermistor may be:

  • connected incorrectly
  • interchanged
  • disconnected
  • have a broken wire

The "Duty Cycle" of one heater is abnormally high (> 0.4), and the duty cycle of the adjacent heater is abnormally low (< 0.2).

Note:
  • The analytical module heaters and thermistors are all attached to the same housing, so one failed device can affect the behavior of the other devices by thermal conduction.
  • The duty cycle is viewed in the Analog Devices or Temperature/Voltages screen in Diagnostics.
  • A constant 24VDC is applied to all heaters. The Power Driver PCB provides a pulsed ground (duty cycle) to control the heaters.

Check for:

  • Interchanged heater connectors or interchanged thermistor connectors.
  • High resistance connection to a thermistor.
  • A defective thermistor.
Note: If a misreporting thermistor requests too much heat, then the adjacent thermistor may report an over temperature error while showing a low duty cycle on its associated heater.

The "Duty Cycle" is abnormally high (1.0), but the heater does not become warm.

The associated heater may be misconnected, disconnected, or have a broken or corroded wire.

Note: The Analytical module heaters are wired in series. A single disconnection or broken wire will affect all elements in the associated circuit.

Temperature errors that are widely out of specification or intermittent and may post a hardware error event.

Note:
  • < -21C = Open thermistor or connection.
  • > 50C = Shorted thermistor or shorted wire.
  • An open or shorted thermistor will post a hardware error.

A poor connection, faulty thermistor, misconnected thermistor, or damaged wire.

Note:
  • Reseat the "Temp/Voltage/Vacuum" connector J19 on the backplane.
  • Check the I/O PCB connector pins on the backplane.
  • Check the thermistor connections, swap in a spare thermistor or borrow the case temperature thermistor.

Determining the area of failure

Use these stepsto determine which area is causing the temperature problem.

  1. Check the on screen duty cycle of the Wash Carousel heater.

    Status

    Action

    If the Wash Carousel heater duty cycle is near 1.0 and the Wash Carousel temperature is out of range low, then the Wash Carousel heater circuit may be open

    Inspect the wash carousel top front heater connections for corrosion. Corrosion on the connections may cause an open circuit disabling all the Wash Carousel heaters.

    If the heater has high resistance with broken or corroded wires, then Proceed to the "Heater Repair and Replacement" procedure.

    If the Wash Carousel heater duty cycle is near 1.0 and the Incubator temperature is out of range high, then there is a Wash Carousel thermistor problem.

    Confirm that the thermistor connectors have not been misconnected (swapped).

  2. Check the on screen duty cycle of the Incubator heater.

    Status

    Action

    If the Incubator heater duty cycle is near 1.0 and the Incubator temperature is out of range low, then the Incubator heater circuit may be open.

    Inspect the Incubator heater connections for damage that may cause an open circuit disabling the Incubator heaters.

    If the Incubator heater duty cycle is near 1.0 and the Wash Carousel temperature is out of range high, then there is a Incubator thermistor problem.

    Confirm that the Inc Temp (J23) and WW Temp (J26) connectors have not been misconnected at the bottom edge of the Interface PCB.

  3. Use a temperature probe to measure the temperature of the wash carousel. If the temperature is only slightly lower than 36.4 degrees and the screen displays a temperature which is much lower than 36.4 degrees, then reseat connector J19 on the backplane and reseat the I/O PCB. Check the I/O PCB for bent pins.
  4. Measure the DC volts from J8 pin-8 to ground (TP3) on the Analytical Interface PCB. If the displayed duty cycle is near 1.0 and the meter reads a voltage near zero, then the Wash carousel heater drive circuit is functioning properly.

Specifications

This table contains reference specification values for use in troubleshooting the heater components.
Note:
  • The Wash Carousel heater is made up of 3 sections connected in series. If any of the sections fails there will be no heat on the wash carousel.
  • The heater resistance should be between 0.5 and 10 ohms. What is most important in measuring heater resistance is that the resistance value is not extremely high (open) or low (shorted).
