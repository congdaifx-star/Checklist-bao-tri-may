Troubleshooting Error Shuttle RV

This procedure provides troubleshooting information for RV Shuttle errors.

Common Errors

Use this information to identify and correct the most common shuttle errors.

Error

Action

Shuttle Home Error when initializing

(no visible jam)

If all alignments in mechanics are noted to be zero, then refer to the "NVRAM Restoration" procedure for alignment file recovery.

Note: If the software has lost the alignments in the nvsetup.csv file and I/O PCB, then the system will default to a state where the Shuttle does not home correctly. It stops with the flag behind the home sensor.

RV Cleanout Error

Check the adjustment of the vessel sensors VS1 and VS2. Refer to the RV Sensor Adjustment procedure for details.

Motion Errors / Jams

  • Check that the finger on the right end of the shuttle stay beam has not broken off.
  • Check that the Waste Chute is not clogged. Replace the waste bag and seat the bag all the way down into its mounting slot. Check the gate and return spring operation.
  • Check that the Rake alignment to the shuttle is not pressing the RVs to tightly into the shuttle.

Areas to Check

This table provides a list of RV Shuttle problem areas to check and suggestions for correcting the problem.

Incubator Belt

Action

Incubator belt incorrectly aligned at shuttle transfer.

Perform shuttle transfer alignment.

The incubator belt dovetails may be torn or deformed.

Replace the incubator belt.

The incubator belt index sensor may be damaged causing erratic incubator belt positioning.

Replace incubator belt index sensor.

There may be play in the incubator belt drive system The drive shaft pulley or incubator belt driven pulley may be damaged or the drive belt may be loose.

Replace the incubator belt drive pulley assembly with the latest version drive pulley assembly.

The incubator belt tension may be too low.

Tension the incubator belt to the proper tension using the tensioning tool.

The incubator belt may be torn/stretched/cut between any two dovetails. Carefully inspect the entire incubator belt.

Inspect the incubator belt for cracks or other damage and replace the belt if damage is discovered.

The incubator belt dovetails may be offset by a mold parting line that runs around the circumference of the belt. (the vendor should discard these)

Replace the incubator belt.

The incubator belt guides may be incorrectly positioned.

With the incubator belt properly tensioned the belt guides should just lightly touch the incubator belt.

Vessel Holders

Action

The vessel holder legs may be bent (caused by a previous jam or new vessel holders may have gotten damaged during storage).

Evaluate the holders using the vessel holder exerciser test. Replace suspect vessel holders.

Rake Area

Action

The RVs in the rake may be too loose or too tight to each other because the rake is not adjusted properly.

Perform the rake to RV Shuttle alignment procedure.

RVs (Reaction Vessels) Related

Action

RVs with excessive width at their tops may be catching on vessel holders as they are loaded into the vessel holders.

Send samples of any questionable RVs to STS.

RV Shuttle Area :

Action

The RV Shuttle to rake vane gap may be incorrect.

Home the shuttle prior to performing this adjustment. Also ensure that all 3 of the RV Shuttle mounting screws are tightened securely even if this alignment is correct.

The RV Shuttle left/right alignment may be incorrect (possibly due to an interference problem at the outer wall of left rake vane). Check for this interference problem by looking for a gap between the left side of the far left rake vane and the portion of the RV Shuttle just below and to the right of where the Shuttle Home sensor is mounted. The RV Shuttle "pusher" (far right end) must be flush with or no more than 0.010" away from the incubator track inner wall with the shuttle in its home position.

Realign the RV Shuttle

Debris in the rake, shuttle or incubator track mechanism, such as a broken vessel holder leg or a fluid spill that has become gummy or dry.

Remove debris and clean the all related areas.

Far right end of the upper (stay) beam of the RV Shuttle is broken or missing.

Install replacement shuttle (or stay beam) and align the shuttle.

The shuttle flag may be interfering with the index/home sensor. Starting 8/30/2006 washers were added under the shuttle home sensor to center the flag between the sensor.

Verify that the flag is not rubbing on the sensor. Adjust if needed.

The shuttle index/home sensor may be loose or its alignment may be incorrect.

Ensure that the sensor is not loose or broken. Realign if the sensor is moved.

The RV Shuttle index/home sensor may be damaged. (fluid spillage, cracked, poor electrical connection)

Replace and align the Shuttle index/home sensor.

Error at End of Run

RVs are returned into the RV Shuttle where Vessel Sensor #1 verifies their presence before they are ejected. If any RVs are missing or fail to be detected, then an error is generated.

Example:

SHUT device failed at index XXX in Motion Sequence ZZZZZ. Seq

1997/ 5 /24 15:00:02 7c000c uiaccess (011) [2757]

XXX = a three digit number which indicates which RV the error occurred on

(most of the maintenance functions use more than one RV).

ZZZZZ = the Sequencer file name of the maintenance function being performed.

Causes of an RV Sensor not detecting the presence of an RV:

  • Dirty RV Sensors.
  • Incorrectly adjusted RV Sensor voltage.
  • Defective RV Sensor.
  • Incorrectly aligned Shuttle.
  • Defective Shuttle.
  • The RV is actually not present.
