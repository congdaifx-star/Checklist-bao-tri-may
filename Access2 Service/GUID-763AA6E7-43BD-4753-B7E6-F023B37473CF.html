Replacing Heaters in Analytical Module

This procedure describes how to replace analytical module heaters.

Before replacing a heater, troubleshoot heater failure symptoms to determine the source or cause of the failure. Because the heaters and thermistors are attached to the same housing, one device can affect the behavior of the other devices. In some cases, the connections can be repaired without replacing the heater.

Note: Refer to the Troubleshoot Analytical Module Heaters procedure.
  1. If the heater cannot be repaired, replace the heater element.
    Note:
    • Heater under the mixer motor area (Heater, Wash Carousel Top) — The heater is fairly accessible and can be replaced in the field with little disassembly of the analytical module.
    • Heaters for the incubator and the RV loader — Access to the heaters requires extensive disassembly of the analytical module. Replacement of these heaters in the field is not recommended.
    • Heater by the luminometer (Heater, Wash Carousel Housing, Outside) — Replacement of this heater is not recommended because of the time involved and concerns with reapplication.
    1. Before you begin, confirm that the heater is defective by measuring the resistance with an ohm meter.
      • A defective heater gives an extremely high or infinite reading.
      • A good heater typically reads 2 to 20 Ohms.
    2. Carefully pry and peal the heater away from the surface of the analytical module.
      The heater is glued down with a strong, but pliable adhesive.
    3. Use a single-edge razor blade or other suitable tool to scrap all of the residual adhesive from the surface of the analytical module.
    4. Wipe the surface clean with alcohol (alcohol does not dissolve the glue).
    5. Before removing the paper backing from the new heater, rehearse placing the heater into position.
    6. Choose the first heater section to be mounted. Remove the adhesive backing from the section and press the section into place.
      Note: Remove the adhesive backing from only one section at a time. Center the section and avoid air pockets and unwanted folds or wrinkles.
    7. Choose the next heater section to be mounted. Remove the adhesive backing from the section and press the section into place. Repeat the process one section at a time. Maintain good alignment, center each section, and avoid air pockets and folds in the material.
  2. Power up and initialize the system.
  3. Verify the heater is repaired.
    1. From the Voltage/Temperatures screen in Diagnostics, observe the temperature readings.
    2. Functionality of the heater is verified if the temperatures rises above the lower limit within several minutes and then remains above the lower limit with normal duty cycles.
      • A typical system settles into a duty cycle range between 0.10 and 0.50 (it varies with the ambient temperature).
      • The temperatures can overshoot (too hot) during the warm-up after powering up the system. If the temperatures settle into range after a few minutes, then the temperature overshoot is not a problem.
  4. Verify system performance.
    1. Check the pipettor and wash arm alignments, adjust as needed.
      Note: Refer to the Pipettor Alignment / Adjustment procedure.
    2. Check mixer speed, adjust as needed.
      Note: Refer to the Adjusting Mixer Motor Speed procedure.
    3. Check fluid line connections, and Prime All one cycle each. Observe and verify fluid flow.
    4. Run controls. All control results must be within acceptable limits.
  5. If you have questions or concerns, contact your local support office.
