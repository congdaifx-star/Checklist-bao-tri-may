Line Conditioner Recommendation

Definition: A line conditioner is a device which isolates the instrument from direct power line noise (voltage and current spikes) by use of two large windings, similar to those in a transformer.

UPS Recommendation

Definition: A UPS (universal power supply) is a type of power supply that maintains power to a device for a short period of time. It contains DC batteries that continually charge, and when power is interrupted the battery power is converted to the AC voltage necessary to operate only the console PC.

Parameter Specification
Maximum output capacity 1300 V·A
Output voltage 120 or 240 V AC
Output frequency 50 or 60 Hz, single phase
Output wave form True Sine wave (<5% distortion)
Standby run time Minimum 15 minutes at 1000 W output (low battery indicator and shutdown recommended)
Approvals UL 1778, CSA C22.2 107.1 (UL 544, if desired)
Recommended Model Powervar ACE 1500S: 1440 V·A
