Adjusting Luminometer Output After Luminometer Replacement

This procedure describes how to adjust luminometer output.

Perform this procedure after replacement of the luminometer or the analytical module (that includes a new luminometer and LED).

Note: Assay recalibration is required after replacing the luminometer.
Caution: Do not expose the luminometer lens to direct light that may temporarily affect the output (dark counts) or damage the photomultiplier tube in the luminometer.

To adjust the luminometer LED reading after replacement of the LED only, refer to the Adjusting Luminometer After LED Replacement procedure.

  1. Prepare for luminometer assembly adjustment.
    1. Print an Alignment Report.
    2. Obtain a printout of the most recent System Check.
  2. Adjust the luminometer output.
    1. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Luminometer > Read.
    2. Confirm that the dark counts are less than 100 with the LED off and all covers installed and closed.
    3. Select Enable LED.
    4. Place the cursor in the High Voltage Control window. Use the up and down arrow keys to adjust the voltage until the Current RLU matches the LED Target Value within 2%.
      Note: The voltage can be typed into the High Voltage Control window. After typing the voltage, select [Enter].
    5. Allow the Current Mean to stabilize until the Current %CV is less than 0.2%.
    6. Select Cancel.
    7. Select Save High Voltage Control.
    8. At the Save new H. V. Control value warning, select Yes.
    9. Select Save LED Reading.
    10. At the Save new LED Reading warning, select Yes.
      Note: All customer assays must be recalibrated after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED Target Value.
    11. Select Enable LED to power off the LED.
    12. Press the [Esc] key to exit the luminometer diagnostics.
  3. Verify the instrument and luminometer meet specification.
    1. Verify the dark counts with covers closed are <100 RLUs
    2. Perform a System Check procedure to verify luminometer operation, the DCF, and the Substrate Mean RLU.
      Note: If the Substrate Mean RLU is not within specification (5000 to 9000), evaluate the system for substrate contamination. Refer to the Confirming Substrate System Contamination procedure. If the substrate is not contaminated, adjust the substrate RLUs. Refer to the Adjusting Substrate RLUs procedure.
    3. Print a new Alignment Report.
    4. If a new LED value has been saved, recalibrate all customer assays.
    5. Run QC on all customer assays with results within customer limits.
