Troubleshooting DilTest results

This procedure details the process of troubleshooting DilTest results.

Assay details

Review these details to enhance interpretations of test results:

  • DilTest (932) dilutes 10 µL of sample plus 470 µL buffer into the first RV, then dispenses 6 µL of the diluted sample from the first RV plus 44 µL buffer into a second RV. The total sample dilution is 1:400.
  • The RLU response is not linear, so the LumiAP / DilTest RLU ratio will be 205 to 300 not 400.
  • DilTest uses high-res and low-res dispense from the precision pump to perform the dilutions.
  • Level sensing is not used when drawing diluted sample from the first RV; the probe goes to a fixed depth based on the Pipettor Z alignment at the RV position.
  • DilTest (932) and LumiAP (907) do not wash or incubate the RV before adding substrate.
  • The LumiAP assay makes no dilution.

Troubleshooting ratio problems

Use this table to troubleshoot if the DilTest ratio is out of range.

If the Ratio is Then Troubleshooting / Repair
Greater than 300 (over diluted) Possible low sample delivery from the sample cup to the dilution RV or from the dilution RV to the assay RV. Possible excessive diluent volume.
  • Check / adjust the pipettor "Z" alignments.
  • Replace both precision pump seals.
  • Inspect / replace the precision pump return spring. Check that the washer is present on top of the plunger.
  • Inspect for a loose belt or broken teeth on the high-res location of the precision pump belt.
  • Poor mixing in the dilution RV. (Mixing is assisted by sonication on Access systems, but not on DxI)
Less than 205 (under diluted) Possible low diluent delivery.
  • Verify the precision pump low-res dispense volume (350 µL Volume Check)
  • Inspect the precision pump motor, belt and drive for a possible backlash problem, loose belt coupling screw, or broken teeth on the low-res location of the belt.
  • Poor mixing in the dilution RV. (Mixing is assisted by sonication on Access systems, but not on DxI)

Use this table to troubleshoot means or CVs that are out of range.

If Then Troubleshooting / Repair
The LumiAP CV is high Sample pipetting precision is poor
  • Precision pump / seals
  • Precision valve
  • Primary probe
  • Pipettor Alignments
  • Ultrasonics
The DilTest CV is high

Dilution pipetting is poor

or

  • Luminometer
Either mean is high or low

  • The Luminometer response may be outside the norm.
