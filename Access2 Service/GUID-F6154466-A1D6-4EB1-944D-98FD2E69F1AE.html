Luminometer

Table 25: DCF and High Voltage
Luminometer Adjustment Drift Correction Factor Limit Notes
Drift Correction Factor (DCF) At Install: 0.90 to 1.1 If the DCF has decreased, decrease the control voltage. The maximum high voltage limit is -985 volts. If more than -985 volts is required to correct the DCF, a luminometer replacement is necessary.
LED Target / New Mean = DCF 0.6 to 1.7 Message is posted in the Event Log when the DCF is out of range. Adjust the HV control to the original manufacturing LED reading and save the HV control, but DO NOT save a new LED value.
