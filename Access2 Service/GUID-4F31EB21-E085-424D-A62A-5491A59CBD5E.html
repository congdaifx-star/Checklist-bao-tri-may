Installing the Molded RV Load Door_Incubator Cover Kit

This procedure describes how to install the molded plastic RV load door on instruments that previously contained a metal load door and crossbar.

Required Parts, Tools, and Materials: The following parts, tools, and materials are necessary for installation of this kit.
  • Parts

    Molded RV Load Door/Incubator Cover Service Kit (P/N 386142) contains these parts:

    • RV Load Door Assembly
    • SHCS 6-32 x 1 inch long
    • Incubator Cover
    • Substrate Bottle Pocket (If onboard substrate is desired)
  • Tools and Materials
    • Tool Kit
    • Hobby Knife with Sharp Blade
    • Diagonal Cutters

This kit was introduced to reduce manufacturing costs and improve the reliability of RV pack loading.

  • Purpose - The RV load door and incubator cover have been redesigned to improve performance and lower cost. The molded RV load door and support are better aligned with the RV pack, and the alignment is independent of the incubator cover. The incubator cover can be removed easily and is semi-transparent to allow viewing of the RV shuttle and incubator belt.
  • Configuration Requirements - This service kit is compatible with all Access instrument configurations.
  • When to Install - Install this kit immediately on any instrument that has a damaged incubator cover, RV ejection problems in the rake area, or unexplained incubator or wash carousel jams. Otherwise, install this kit at the next scheduled service call that involves the analytical module.
  1. Prepare the instrument for kit installation.
    1. Remove the supply cover and the lower front panel (also called the base panel).
    2. If the internal substrate supply position is in use, remove the substrate bottle.
    3. Remove the gray incubator cover and discard it.
    4. Disconnect the RV load door sensor, remove the four screws from the RV load door cross bar, and lift out the RV load door assembly. Discard this assembly.
      Note: If dowel pins remain in the analytical module housing, remove them.
    5. If the system has hard foam insulation on the analytical module, then cut pieces of insulation 1.5 inch long by 1 inch high from both sides of the analytical module to provide clearance for the new incubator cover snaps.
  2. Install the molded RV load door.
    1. Install the molded RV load door included in the service kit so the two alignment pins on the assembly enter the alignment holes in the analytical module.
    2. Ensure that the cross bar is fully seated in the frame, then secure the assembly with the three 6-32 x 1 inch screws provided in the service kit.
    3. Twist the load door sensor wires to keep them in position, and route the sensor wires through the slots in the frame and down through the slot at the left-hand side of the bar. Connect the load door sensor.
    4. Verify that the audible alarm is activated when the RV load door is raised to the full up position.
      Caution: If the alarm does not activate, recheck the sensor connection. If the alarm still fails, order a new load door sensor.
    5. Install the incubator cover included in the service kit. Raise the RV load door slightly, and while moving the load door through the large opening in the cover, slide the cover retaining slots under the load door retainers.
    6. Snap both cover snaps over the incubator frame.
      Note: The motor power wires on some RV shuttles may need loosening and repositioning.
    7. If the substrate is not kept onboard the instrument, then perform only steps 5 and 6 in the next section.
      Note: Perform all of the steps in the next section to allow use of the internal substrate supply pocket.
  3. Perform these steps if the substrate is kept in the storage pocket inside the instrument.
    1. To use the internal substrate supply position, cut five of the six plastic runners with a pair of diagonal cutters. Lift the knockout slightly, and while holding it, cut the last plastic runner and remove the knockout.
    2. Ensure that the keying tab is oriented correctly, then lower the substrate bottle pocket into position. Press down on the bottle pocket until it is fully seated.
    3. Reinstall the substrate bottle.
    4. Replace the supply cover and the lower front panel.
  4. Verify operation of the molded RV load door assembly.
    1. Initialize the instrument.
    2. Load a minimum of one pack of RVs.
    3. Perform a System Check.
