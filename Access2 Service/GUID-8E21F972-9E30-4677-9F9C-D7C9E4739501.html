Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry

This procedure describes how to rinse and prime dry the Access 2 instrument.

If the Access will be Then perform the steps in the order listed
RGA'd by a Field Service Engineer and is functional

OR

not active for more than one month,

  • Prime an Air Gap
  • Rinse The Wash Buffer Reservoir
  • Rinse The Fluidics System
  • Prepare To Prime Dry
  • Prime the System Dry
RGA'd by a Field Service Engineer and is nonfunctional, Remove and discard dispense probes, aspirate probes, the substrate probe, the sample probe, and peristaltic pump tubing. Remove any existing fluid in the vacuum jar or remaining tubing.
decontaminated and shipped by a third-party company,
Note:
  • Some instrument returns have the decontamination, packaging, and shipping performed by a third-party company. The NACO procedure includes a flowchart that explains how the process is decided and implemented.
  • Engineers unable to access the NACO procedure can contact Chaska Technical Support for a copy of the procedure.
moved (not an RGA)
  • Prepare to Prime Dry
  • Prime the System Dry
  1. Prime an air gap.
    1. Remove the substrate cap from the substrate bottle.
    2. Discard any remaining substrate and rinse the bottle with distilled water.
    3. Reconnect the substrate cap to the empty substrate bottle.
    4. Change the number of substrate primes to one and initiate a substrate prime.
    5. Allow the priming cycle to complete.
  2. Rinse the wash buffer reservoir.
    1. Remove the wash buffer bottle from the reservoir.
    2. Disconnect the wash buffer reservoir level sense wire located beneath the substrate holder, disconnect the quick connect from the bottom of the reservoir and remove the reservoir.
    3. Discard the wash buffer from the reservoir into the sink and rinse a minimum of three times with distilled water.
    4. Fill the reservoir with distilled water and reconnect the quick connect fitting and level sense wire.
  3. Rinse the fluidics system.
    1. Remove the substrate cap from the substrate bottle and rinse the bottle a minimum of three times with distilled water.
    2. Fill the rinsed substrate bottle with distilled water and reconnect the substrate cap.
    3. Change the number of prime cycles to:
      • Pipettor = 16
      • Dispense Probes = 8
      • Substrate = 15
  4. Prepare to prime dry.
    1. Remove the substrate cap from the substrate bottle and empty any remaining distilled water into the sink.
    2. Reconnect the substrate cap to the empty substrate bottle.
    3. Remove the wash buffer supply tubing using the quick disconnect fitting located beneath the reservoir.
    4. Disconnect the wash buffer reservoir level sense wire located beneath the substrate holder, then remove the reservoir.
    5. Discard the distilled water from the reservoir into the sink.
    6. Reconnect the wash buffer reservoir and fluid level sensor wires.
    7. On the Unibase tray, invert the empty reservoir to defeat the level sensor. On the original tray, place an opaque piece of tape over the sensor window to defeat the level sensor.
  5. Prime the system dry.
    1. Change the number of prime cycles to:
      • Pipettor = 16
      • Dispense Probes = 8
      • Substrate = 15
    2. Allow the prime cycle to complete.
    3. Reconnect the wash buffer reservoir and fluid level sensor wires.
    4. On the Unibase tray, invert the empty reservoir to defeat the level sensor. On the original tray, place an opaque piece of tape over the sensor window to defeat the level sensor.
