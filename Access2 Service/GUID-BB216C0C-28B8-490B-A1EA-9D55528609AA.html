Remove / Install the Pipettor Tubing on Non-linear Slide Pipettor Gantry

Perform these steps to remove and install the pipettor tubing.
  1. Removing the tubing
    1. Lower the Pipettor probe fully into the Wash tower.
    2. Enable the Vacuum Pump in Diagnostics >> Digital Devices so that fluid does not overflow the wash tower.
    3. Disconnect the service loop fitting at the pressure sensor block.
    4. Drain for 30 seconds or until fluid is removed from the transducer.
    5. Disable the Vacuum Pump and move the Pipettor to the Home position.
    6. Close the Digital Devices screen and return to the Main UI.
    7. Power off the instrument.
    8. Caution: To help prevent hardware from falling into the instrument during the removal and installation procedure, place a paper towel (10 inch square minimum) under the area being serviced.
      Move the pipettor gantry to the left of the Wash Tower, then manually move it downward a couple of inches using the Z pulley at the left end of the upper gantry shaft.
    9. Move the pipettor gantry to the left of the Wash Tower, then manually move it downward a couple of inches using the Z pulley at the left end of the upper gantry shaft.
    10. Remove the connector cover by removing the two screws holding it in place, then lifting the cover up about 1/2 inch to allow its left end to clear the housing.
    11. Disconnect the transducer power and heater connectors.
      Note: Might need to move the gantry down to access the connectors.
    12. Slide the service loop cable out of the slot in the left side of the transducer housing and place the cable end out of the way.
      Note: This cable may fit tightly into the slot. Slowly work the cable out of the loop, gently wiggle both ends, until the cable is removed.
    13. Disconnect the all cables from the X Interface PCB and remove the cable from the harness clamp.
    14. Remove the two mounting screws which secure the Rear Service Loop Guide.
    15. Lift the guide upward until the loop retainer screws are accessible.
    16. Remove the Service Loop tube retainer screw from the bottom of the rear guide (tubing side only) and remove the tubing from the cable guide.
    17. Caution: Exercise caution when repositioning the bracket so as not to damage the service loop cable.
      Release the X Interface PCB bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws and slide the pipettor to the left, for the rest of the removal procedure.
    18. Caution: Exercise caution when removing the retainers so as not to damage the pipettor supply tubing.
      Remove the front and rear loop retainer screws and retainers.
    19. Mark the X Axis cable where they enter the end loop. The cable marking will be needed when cable is reinstalled. Slowly work the cable out of the loop, gently wiggle both ends, until the cable is removed. Slide the pipettor supply tubing out of the end loop. Detach the service loop chain end from the underside of the X Interface PCB bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws.
    20. Lift the X Axis cable up a couple of inches and remove the end bracket by removing its 2 mounting screws, then remove the end bracket from the rest of the service loop chain.
    21. Caution: Exercise caution when opening the links so as not to damage the pin.
      Use the corner of a slotted blade screwdriver to open all links of the service loop chain.

    22. Remove the tubing from the service loop chain.
  2. Warning: When completed, the cable and tubing should not extend above the rear service loop guide with the pipettor at the Z home position (manually move pipettor to top) and should be similar in height to each other and to the rear guide.
    Perform these steps to install the CnS tubing.
    1. Route the straight end new CnS connector end through service loop chain so it can be connect to the pressure sensor.
      Note:

      The tubing has one end with a bend and one end that is straight. The straight end connects to the pressure sensor.



    2. Caution: Ensure that the end bracket is parallel with the metal edge next to it. If it is not parallel, or rotated slightly counter clockwise, then the plastic service loop will rub the card cage.
      Attach the service loop end bracket to the top of the main pipettor plate.
    3. Route the cable and tubing into the service loop chain, ensuring that the cable and tubing do not cross paths and that the open link covers of the chain are positioned downward.
    4. Close all link covers and attach the service loop chain end bracket to the chain.
    5. Attach the service loop chain end bracket to the X interface PCB bracket using the 2 mounting screws.
    6. Slide the X Axis cable into the metal end loop, then install its retainer and screw.
    7. Slide the Pipettor tubing into the end loop, then install its retainer and screw.
    8. Attach the X Interface PCB bracket to the top of the carriage assembly as follows:
      1. Install the tubing into the bracket.
      2. Check the tubing length by holding the bracket into position.
      3. If the tube is pinched then the tube is too short. Adjust the tubing as needed.
      4. Once the tubing and cable lengths are correct, mounting the bracket using its 2 mounting screws while ensuring the cable and tubing are not pinched.
    9. Route the Z home sensor cable and the X Axis cable through the harness clip.
    10. Caution: Ensure the Z home sensor wires are not contacting the main pipettor mounting plate.
      Connect the X Axis cable, the Z Axis cable and the Z home sensor cable to the X interface PCB.
    11. Install the rear Z Axis Guide retainer and screw.
    12. Insert the lower end of the Z Axis Guide behind the gantry bearing ensuring that the cable and tubing is not pinched, then insert and tighten its two mounting screws.
    13. Route the connector on the P/N labeled end of the Z Axis cable under the pipettor tubing, through the harness clip, then plug it into its connector on the X Interface PCB.
    14. Caution: The cable loop may rub on the instrument cover if looped too high. When completed, the cable and tubing should not extend above the rear service loop guide with the pipettor at the Z home position and should be similar in height to each other and to the rear guide.
      While holding the highest part of the tubing and Z cable side by side, insert the Z Axis cable into the transducer slot.
    15. Connect the transducer heater and power connectors together, then lay them flat on top of the housing.
    16. Install the Connector Cover using its 2 mounting screws, ensuring that during and after installation no wires are pinched or visible outside of the cover.
    17. Align the tubing and cable with the front service loop guide.
    18. Install the front Z Axis Guide using its 2 mounting screws, ensuring that the cable and tubing are properly routed in their slots in the guide.
  3. Perform these verifications to ensure that the ultrasonics is operating properly.
    1. Manually move the gantry up and down and inspect the tubing and cabling for proper movement.
    2. Manually move the gantry left and right and inspect the rear of the carriage for clearance between its plastic harness clip and the metal U-Clips holding the X Axis cable in position. There should be a minimum of 1/8 inch clearance at this location.
    3. Caution: If the service loop chain rubs the card cage (little or no clearance), then adjust the upper and/or lower service loop chain end brackets to improve this clearance.
      Manually move the gantry left and right and inspect the right end of the plastic cable guide for rubbing between it and the front of the card cage.
    4. Refer to the "Module Pipettor Alignments / Adjustments" procedure () Access 2 only.
    5. Verify the transducer's temperature. Refer to "Transducer, Adjusting Temperature" () for specifications and procedures.
    6. Verify all Ultrasonic Transducer adjustments. Refer to the "Transducer, Ultrasonics Adjustment" procedure for details.
    7. Home the Pipettor and verify that the Pipettor is at the Home location.
    8. Perform the Ultrasonics Level Sense Wet/Dry test. (Run 20 reps). Refer to the "Troubleshooting, Level Sense" procedure ().
    9. Refer to the "Verification Protocol, Access Service Repair" () and perform the required system verification procedures.
