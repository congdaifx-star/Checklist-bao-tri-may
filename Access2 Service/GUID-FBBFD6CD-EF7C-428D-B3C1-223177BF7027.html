Deinstall: Securing Instrument Components

This procedure describes how to secure the Access instrument before transportation in a vehicle.

Pre-requisites:
Before beginning this procedure, confirm that the instrument has been primed dry, the supplies have been removed, and the system has been decontaminated. Refer to these procedures:
  • Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry
  • Deinstall: Removing Supplies
  • Deinstall: Disinfecting for Shipping
This document describes how to secure an Access 2 system before transporting the system in a vehicle.
  1. Power down the instrument.
    1. Power down the printer and monitor.
    2. Power down the instrument.
    3. Power down associated line conditioner or UPS.
    4. Power down the PC.
  2. Disconnect the peripherals.
    1. Disconnect the printer signal and power cables. Bundle and tie-wrap the cables. Set the printer and cables aside for final packaging.
    2. Disconnect any LIS (or equivalent) cable. Bundle, tie-wrap, and retain the cable with the interface computer set-up.
    3. Disconnect the monitor signal and power cables. Bundle and tie-wrap the cables. Set the monitor and cables aside for final packaging.
    4. Disconnect the keyboard cable from the bar code reader Y-cable. Set the keyboard aside for final packaging.
    5. Disconnect the mouse cable. Set the mouse aside for final packaging.
    6. Disconnect the bar code reader. Bundle and tie-wrap the cable. Use tape to secure the bar code reader in the holding pocket on the right side of the instrument. Tape the cable to the instrument.
    7. Remove the monitor platform from the articulating arm. Separate the articulating arm into the upper and lower segments. Lift the lower articulating arm segment off the mounting post on the instrument base. Set the monitor platform and articulating arm aside for final packaging.
  3. Disconnect the fluids tray.
    1. Remove the wash buffer supply tubing using the quick-disconnect located beneath the reservoir. Pull the tubing free from the fluids tray.
    2. Disconnect the sensor harness and the substrate supply tubing at the left side of the instrument.
    3. Confirm that the waste bottle tubing has been disconnected.
    4. Tape the buffer bottle valve into the receptacle on the wash buffer reservoir.
    5. Tape the waste bottle, substrate tubing, waste filter tubing, and sensor harness to the fluids tray.
    6. Set the fluids tray aside for final packaging.
  4. Secure the wash arm and main pipettor.
    1. Open the top cover and front panel of the instrument.
    2. If the original pieces of polyfoam are not available, then cut two pieces of foam to the approximate sizes show in the photo, but do not cut the slot shown in the larger piece.


      Note: Do not use Styrofoam. The compressed beads can attach themselves to instrument components because the beads are electrically charged. If beaded foam is all that is available, place tape over any cut edges to contain any loose beads.
    3. Place the larger piece of foam under the wash arm, to the rear and right of the wash arm lead screw. Confirm that the foam does not rest on the mixer belt. Manually rotate the wash arm lead screw counter-clockwise until the wash arm is lowered tightly against the foam.


      Note: The foam should support the wash arm and rest firmly between the wash arm and wash carousel housing.
    4. Place the smaller piece of foam between the PCB and the z-axis bearing block.


      Note: Access 2 instruments have a similar piece of foam inserted between the z-carriage and bearing block, which does not look exactly like the photo.
    5. Slide the main pipettor assembly to the right end of the gantry.
    6. Rotate the pulley on the left end of the upper pipettor gantry shaft until the main pipettor is at the upper end of its travel.
    7. Secure the main pipettor to the z-axis carriage and to the right end of the gantry support with tie-wraps.


      Note: A correctly secured pipettor will not move vertically or horizontally.
  5. Secure the covers.
    1. Close and secure the top cover with the captive screw that is centrally located under the front cover.
    2. Secure the lower cover with three screws: One under the waste bag, and the other two at the ends.
    3. Close the front panel and secure the panel with tape.
  6. Disconnect the peripherals.
    1. Disconnect the printer signal and power cables. Bundle and tie-wrap the cables. Set the printer and its cables aside for final packaging.
    2. Disconnect any LIS (or equivalent) cable. Bundle, tie-wrap and retain the cable with the interface computer set-up.
    3. Disconnect the monitor signal and power cables. Bundle and tie-wrap the cables. Set the monitor and its cables aside for final packaging.
    4. Disconnect the keyboard cable from the bar code reader y-cable. Set the keyboard aside for final packaging.
    5. Disconnect the bar code reader from the instrument. Bundle and tie-wrap the cable. Use tape to secure the bar code reader in its holding pocket on the right side of the instrument. Tape the cable to the instrument.
    6. Remove the monitor platform from the articulating arm. Separate the articulating arm into its upper and lower segments. Lift the lower articulating arm segment off its mounting post on the instrument base. Set the monitor platform and articulating arm aside for final packaging.
