Replacing Rake Strips and Gates

This procedure describes how to replace the hook and loop-style rake strips and gates with the elastomeric-style rake strips and gates.

Required Materials:
Tools, Parts, and Materials Parts
  • Small needle-nose pliers
  • Hex wrench set
  • Large straight-slot screwdriver
  • Straight 5- or 6-inch hemostat
  • Scraper (P/N 60-008315A)
  • 3M Post-it1 Glue Stick
  • Elastomeric rake strip and gate installation tool
  • Elastomeric rake strip and gate spares assembly that contains these parts:
    • 2 elastomeric rake strips
    • 1 elastomeric rake gate
Service Kit 81600077 containing the following:
  • 28 elastomeric rake strips
  • 14 elastomeric rake gates
  • Installation Instructions
Follow this procedure to:
  • Remove Velcro2 rake strips and metal spring fingers.
  • Install elastomeric rake strips and gates.
  • Replace a defective or missing elastomeric rake strip or gate.

Symptoms

RVs that do not move to their correct locations and cause shuttle or rake jams are a symptom of defective elastomeric or Velcro rake strips or damaged elastomeric or metal spring fingers.

  1. Prepare the instrument for installation of the kit.
    1. Remove the analytical module.
    2. Place the analytical module in a well-lit work area with a minimum area of 9 square feet.
    3. Remove the gray incubator cover from the analytical module.
    4. Remove the RV pack ejector by removing the four mounting screws and disconnecting the sensor lead.
    5. Remove all the RVs from the rake and RV shuttle.
    6. Move the rake pusher away from the shuttle as far as mechanically possible by pulling directly on the pusher. Move the shuttle so that the walking beam and stay beam fingers are in-line (one directly above the other).
      Caution: The pusher must not interfere with the installation tool during the installation process. Interference causes incorrect positioning of the tool and misplacement of the elastomeric strips and gates.
  2. Remove the original strips and gates from the instrument.
    1. Insert the scraper under the end of the upper Velcro* strip closest to the rake pusher. Pull a 1/4-in section of Velcro strip loose, and grasp this loose end with the hemostat.
    2. Lock the hemostat onto the Velcro* strip and rotate the hemostat counterclockwise to loosen the Velcro* strip and wrap it around the hemostat.
      Caution: Use only the rotating method to remove the Velcro* strips because it removes the adhesive with the strip. Pulling on the strip to remove it, leaves adhesive on the vane and breaks the strip into pieces.
    3. Repeat the steps using the scraper and hemostat to remove the lower left Velcro* strip.
    4. Repeat the steps to remove the upper and lower Velcro strips for the other 13 rake vanes.
    5. Remove the 14 metal spring gates by grasping the top of each gate with a small needle-nose pliers. Pull the gate toward the waste chute. As the gate begins to loosen, insert the needle-nose pliers farther into the rake vane and under more of the gate until the entire gate has been removed.
    6. Once all the strips and gates are removed, dispose of them.
      Caution: Inspect all the rake vane strip grooves and gate areas of the vanes to confirm that no adhesive is left anywhere. Use the scraper to remove any remaining adhesive.
  3. Install the elastomeric rake strips and gates. Refer to the tool drawing during these steps.
    Caution: While installing the elastomeric rake strips and gates, use only enough adhesive to hold the strips onto the tool during installation. Do not allow adhesive into the small holes for the elastomeric nubbins (protrusions on the strips). If adhesive gets into the small holes in the tool, push a small wire into each hole to force out the adhesive.
    1. Apply a thin coat of Post-it* adhesive to the upper strip, lower strip, and gate areas of the installation tool.
    2. Install two strips and one gate onto the installation tool.
      Note: The elastomeric strips and rake gate are not symmetrical and fit onto the tool only one way. Rake gate 81353 is narrower than the old gate and must be positioned as far to the end of the installation tool gate area as possible. Confirm that the angled flaps on the rake gate are pointing down, towards the bottom strip, when installed on the tool. Before installing each strip onto the tool, inspect the strip to confirm the presence of the nubbins. Dispose of any strips that are defective. Use strips and gates from the strip and gate spares assembly, if needed.
    3. Remove the protective backing from the rake strips and gate. The scraper works well for lifting the corner of this backing.
    4. Carefully insert the tool between the rake vanes, keeping the back of the tool against the nearest wall. Once the tool is down (resting on top of the rake vanes), slide the tool away from the shuttle until the tool stop contacts the front of the vane.
    5. Slide the tool toward the strip grooves in the rake vane, while being sure the tool stop is against the rake vane end (shuttle end).
    6. Once the strips and gate contact the vane, rock the tool clockwise and counter-clockwise to seat the strips against the rake vane.
    7. Move the tool away from the strips until the backside contacts the nearest rake vane. Lift the tool from the vanes.
    8. Using the curved edge of the scraper, seat each strip by rubbing between the nubbins.
    9. Seat the gate by rubbing the curved portion of the scraper against it.
      Note: If the strips and gate are not seated fully, they appear white. If seated correctly, the strips and gate appear black, like the vane to which they are attached. Confirm that all of the new pieces appear black. If there are any pieces of adhesive, Velcro strip, or metal gate under any of the new parts, remove that strip or gate, remove the debris, and then install a new part.
    10. Apply more adhesive to the tool, as required. Normally, more than one set of elastomeric strips and gates can be installed with one application of adhesive.
      Note:
      • If the scraper, hemostat, and installation tool become coated with adhesive, clean them with alcohol or another mildly aggressive cleaner and then wipe them dry.
      • Keep the cover on the Post-it* Glue Stick during the installation process. If the glue is left uncovered, it becomes chunky and dries out.
    11. Repeat these steps to install the remaining 13 rake veins.
    12. When complete, examine all of the strips to confirm that they are positioned correctly. Remove and replace any strips that are not positioned correctly. Confirm that there is no adhesive on the RV side of the strips and gates. Remove any visible adhesive.
  4. Reassemble the instrument.
    1. Replace the RV pack ejector and reconnect the sensor lead.
    2. Place the incubator cover on the incubator.
    3. Close the RV load door.
    4. Install the analytical module into the instrument.
    5. Replace the pipettor gantry.
    6. Install the instrument covers.
  5. Verify the rake is operating properly.
    1. Power up the instrument.
    2. Load three packs of RVs from the Supplies screen.
    3. Use the rake and shuttle commands on the Mechanics screen to load and eject five rows of RVs and test the ability of the rake to move a full load of RVs into the RV shuttle.
    4. Run a System Check.

Expected Outcome

The instrument should meet all of these conditions to ensure it is operating properly.
  • No rake or RV shuttle errors. No RVs fall over during loading.
  • System Check results meet all specifications.
