Tshoot: Pipettor Module

Use this table for troubleshooting the hardware items in the Pipettor module.

Part

Failure

False Level Sense

  • Pipettor probe not properly tightened.
  • Fitting loose between precision valve and primary probe.
  • Air in main pipettor tubing/probe.
  • Service Loop defective.
  • Carriage or cable is rubbing on front cover.
  • Pipettor 'Z' slide linear slide bearing, need lubrication or debris in bearings.
  • Incorrect Pipettor 'Z' to sample cup alignment.
  • Loose hardware on Z-Carriage
  • Tube bearing defective or bearing clamp too tight.
  • Ultrasonics PCB defective.
  • Ultrasonics transducer loose or defective (Pip Probe to Ground = 5 ohms or less).
  • Spring washer on the end of the gantry is installed backwards (top rod).
  • Defective precision pump (unlikely cause).

Pipettor "X" Movement Errors

  • Pipettor rubbing on front cover panel. (Gussets or flat portion).
  • Gantry not cleaned (both shafts) and lubricated (top shaft only) as required.
  • Pipettor 'X' belt retainer clip hitting one of the top two pipettor mounting screws.
  • Pipettor 'X' idler pulley bearings defective.
  • Pipettor gantry mounting thumb screws not fully tightened.

Pipettor "Z" Movement Errors

  • Pipettor rubbing on the front panel cover.
  • Pipettor hitting some tubing, cabling, or wiring during its travel.
  • Pipettor 'Z' home gap incorrect obstructing pipettor 'Z' travel.
  • Pipettor 'Z' belt has jumped one or more teeth (loose belt clip).
  • Pipettor 'Z' linear slide bearings defective or needs lubrication.
  • Pipettor 'Z' idler pulley bearings defective.
  • Pipettor 'Z' encoder wheel loose or rubbing/dragging on sensor.
  • Pipettor alignment. Probe has become bent since last alignment.
  • Pipettor gantry upper rod end spring washers installed incorrectly.
  • Upper guide rod bearing clamp screws are too tight distorting bearings.
  • Pipettor not aligned properly to reagent pack.
  • Defective reagent pack.

Ultrasonics Errors/ Failures/ Level Sense (QNS) or PLL Failures

  • Incorrect pipettor-Z alignment.
  • Main pipettor probe loose, clogged or damaged.
  • Ultrasonics ribbon cable defective or solder joint at PCB bad.
  • Air getting into pipettor tubing.
  • Level sense fail - hardware loose anywhere on pipettor module.
  • Ultrasonics PCB defective.
  • Ultrasonics transducer defective.
  • Pipettor -Y adjustment screws loose.
  • Defective precision pump.
  • Pipettor interface PCB defective.
  • Incorrect sample vessel or Tray ID
  • Incorrect sample volume. (minimum sample volume ignored)
  • Front panel open.
  • Interlock switch circuitry defective. (wiring or backplane PCB)
