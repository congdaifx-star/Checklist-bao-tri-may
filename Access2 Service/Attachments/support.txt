3M TOUCH SYSTEMS SUPPORT SERVICES
=====================================================
3M Touch Systems provides extensive support services through our 
website and technical support organization. Visit the 3M Touch 
Systems website at www.3Mtouch.com, where you can download touch 
screen software and drivers, obtain regularly updated technical 
documentation on 3M Touch Systems products, and learn more about 
our company.

Contacting 3M Touch Systems Technical Support
=====================================================
Whenever you contact Technical Support, please provide the 
following information:

· Part number and serial number from your monitor
· Current driver version
· Operating system used
· Information on additional peripherals

Technical Support is available Monday through Friday 8 am to 8 pm 
US Eastern Standard Time. Technical Support is also available 
normal business hours for each respective country throughout Europe
and Asia. Limited service Saturdays, Sundays and holidays.

You can contact 3M Touch Systems Technical Support by 
calling the hot line or sending a fax.

· Technical Support Hot Line: 978-659-9200
· Technical Support Fax: 978-659-9400
· Email: US-TS-Tech_Support@mmm.com

All offices can be reached through the website: www.3Mtouch.com.
