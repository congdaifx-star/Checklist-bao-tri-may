Adjusting Incubator Belt Index Sensor

This procedure describes how to adjust the incubator belt index sensor.

The P/N A60765 Analytical Interface PCB has a potentiometer available for adjusting the brightness of the LED in the index sensor.

Perform this procedure when:
  • Incubator belt does not home or calibrate correctly.
  • Replacing the index sensor or the analytical module interface PCB.
Adjust the incubator belt index sensor.
  1. Connect a DC voltmeter to test point TP17 and to ground TP3 on the analytical module interface PCB.
  2. Adjust RS3 so that the voltage on TP17 is 1.10 V DC to 1.50 V DC.
    Note:
    • This step adjusts the brightness of the LED in the index sensor. The range is 1.10 V DC to 1.50 V DC.
    • The incubator belt position is not critical. The voltage at TP17 is the same with or without a flag in the index sensor.
  3. Calibrate the incubator belt.
    If the incubator belt calibration fails, adjust the voltage by approximately 0.01 V DC and then repeat the incubator belt calibration.
    Note:
    • Typically, lower voltage works best when adjusting the sensor. If lower voltage does not work, then adjust upward in the range 0.01 V at a time.
    • A slight change in voltage has a significant impact.
    • Typically, adjusting the sensor voltage higher in the range causes more negative readings in the CCW direction only.
    • If you are not able to calibrate the belt after adjusting the sensor, then contact Chaska System Technical Support for assistance.
    Note: Refer to the Calibrating Incubator Belt procedure.
