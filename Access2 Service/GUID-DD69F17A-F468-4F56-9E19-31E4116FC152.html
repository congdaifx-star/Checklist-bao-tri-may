Disinfecting Fluids Tray and Instrument Exterior

This procedure describes how to disinfect the fluids tray and instrument exterior.

This document provides instructions for disinfecting the fluids tray, instrument exterior, and other instrument peripherals.
  1. Disinfect the fluids tray.
    Note: This step applies if an instrument has a waste bottle and waste filter and the instrument is being moved.
    1. Remove the liquid waste and waste air connectors at the waste bottle lid.
    2. If you are moving the instrument a short distance or replacing the fluids tray, then carefully pull the liquid waste and waste air tubing from the fluids tray. Leave the tubing attached to the instrument.
    3. Remove the waste bottle from the fluids tray.
    4. Add enough bleach to the liquid waste container to make a 10% final solution (100 ml for each liter of waste).
    5. Allow the bleached liquid to sit for 10 minutes, then discard into a sink. Rinse the waste bottle and the sink with water.
    6. Discard the waste filter in a biohazardous waste container. If the instrument is being moved a short distance (vehicle transportation not involved), then wipe the waste filter bottle with a 10% bleach solution and reinstall the filter bottle onto the fluids tray.
    7. Wipe the waste bottle quick-disconnect fittings and the waste bottle cover with a 10% bleach solution.
    8. Wipe any spills or dried waste on the fluids tray with a 10% bleach solution.
  2. Disinfect the waste drain.
    Note: This step applies if the instrument is being moved and uses a waste drain
    1. Remove the liquid and air waste tubing from the bulk-head fittings.
    2. Wipe the liquid waste and waste air bulk-head fittings with a 10% bleach solution.
    3. If you are reusing the waste tubing, then submerse and soak the tubing for a minimum of 10 minutes in a 10% bleach solution.
    4. If you are not reusing the waste tubing, then discard the tubing in a biohazardous waste container.
  3. If the instrument is being moved, disinfect the exterior using a 10% bleach solution (or equivalent laboratory disinfectant).
    1. Wipe the keyboard.
    2. If the instrument is an Access 2, wipe the surfaces of the PC.
    3. Wipe the instrument exterior.
    4. Wipe the under surface of the supplies cover.
    5. Wipe the surface under the waste bag.
    6. Wipe the surface under the sample or reagent carousel sliding door.
    7. Wipe the sample trays.
  4. Disinfect the monitor.
    • If the monitor is not a touch screen monitor, wipe the display surface and other areas with the 10% bleach solution.
    • If the monitor is a touch screen monitor, do not clean the touch screen with the disinfecting solution. Instead, use any of the recommended cleaners.
      Caution: Many cleaners have a measured pH of 9 to 10. Do not use cleaners having a measured pH of 9 to 10 for cleaning the touch screen monitor.
      Recommended cleaners:
      • Windex
      • AR Kleener
      • Siemens Antistatic Surface Cleaner
      • Water
      • Rubbing Alcohol
      • Any cleaner with a pH of 6 to 8
