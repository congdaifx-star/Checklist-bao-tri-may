Alignment: Pipettor Y Adjust

Perform these steps to align the Pipettor in the "Y" axis if it is observed that the Pipettor does not align in the front to back axis at the RV or the Reagent Pack alignment steps. The "Y" axis alignment is very rarely needed.
Note:
  • This is a mechanical adjustment and it will affect all other Pipettor alignments.
  • If the Pipettor Y adjustment is performed, then all the other Pipettor alignments will need to be rechecked.
  • Ensure that the Analytical module and Sample Reagent modules are locked into their alignment pins and securely mounted.
  • Ensure that the pipettor probe is not bent and the transducer is seated properly.
  1. Remove the RV in position 1 of the shuttle and install the RV Alignment Tool.
  2. From the Main Menu select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics.
  3. In the Mechanics screen select Reagent Carousel as the device.
  4. PressHome to home the Reagent Carousel, load the reagent pack alignment tool into slot 1 of the Reagent Carousel, then select Pipettor as the position and press Move X to Position to move the tool to the pipettor aspirate position.
  5. Use the directional keys to adjust the reagent pack alignment tool's position so that the Pipettor probe follows parallel to the white line on the tool when it is moved by hand along the tools length.
  6. Slightly loosen the 4 large screws that secure the Pipettor tower to the instrument base plate, and the 2 screws that secure the tower to the upper cross bar.
  7. Rotate or slide the Pipettor gantry/tower to a position where the Pipettor probe tip aligns precisely with the alignment point on the RV alignment tool and the reagent pack alignment tool.
  8. When the alignment at the two positions is correct, securely tighten the 6 tower mounting screws.
  9. Confirm all Pipettor alignments and readjust as necessary.

Go back to the parent topic to perform any other necessary alignments and for verification steps once all alignments are complete.
