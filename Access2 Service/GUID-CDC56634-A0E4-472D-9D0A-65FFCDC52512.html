Performing Exerciser Routines

  1. Run an exerciser routine.
    Note: For more information, refer to the Theory of Operation - System Exerciser Routines document.
    Devices that are available for cycling include:
    • Wash arm
    • Wash carousel transfer
    • Incubator track
    • Vessel holder
    • Mixer
    1. Determine the device that requires movement verification.
    2. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Exerciser.
    3. Select an exerciser.
    4. Select Start/Stop Cycling to start the exerciser.
    5. Observe the motions or actions of the devices being exercised and allow the exerciser to complete all the cycles.
    6. If no errors occur, then the devices are operating correctly (if sufficient repetitions have been run).
      If errors occur, then determine the cause of the failure. Refer to
      Note:
      • Select Cancel to stop cycling, cancel the Exerciser window, and return to Device Diagnostics.
      • Select Start/Stop Cycling to stop cycling after the current cycle is finished.
  2. Evaluate the Wash Carousel Transfer exerciser operation.
    1. Observe the mixing:
      • The mixer belt should not be abnormally worn.
      • There should be no visual fluctuation in speed.
      • Listen for excessive or abnormal noises.
      • The pinch rollers should not visibly bounce while mixing.
    2. While gently touching the top of the pinch roller, only a slight, steady vibration should be felt when the mixer is enabled.
    3. Troubleshoot as required:
      • Abnormal noises could be caused by defective or dirty pinch rollers, mixer pulleys, bearings, or o-rings.
      • Excessive vibration or bouncing of the pinch rollers could be caused by dirty or defective pinch rollers.
      • Mixer belt speed fluctuations could be caused by dirty or defective mixer pulleys, defective or dirty pinch rollers, worn or poorly tensioned mixer belt.
      • Mixer speed errors could be caused by some of the above or loose wires on the mixer motor.
    4. Repeat the exerciser to verify repairs.
