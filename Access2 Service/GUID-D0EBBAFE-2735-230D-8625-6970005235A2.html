Cut-in Log

Summary

This document provides a reference for Service Personnel to determine instrument changes by serial number (where available). Model Type 'N' denotes Nephelometric instruments, but none of these were sold to customers. Due to continuous improvement in the Access design, device and component variations will be seen in field instruments. These design changes are typically invisible to our customers. Being aware of these changes, called Cut Ins, may aid the field engineering group in troubleshooting instrument issues.
