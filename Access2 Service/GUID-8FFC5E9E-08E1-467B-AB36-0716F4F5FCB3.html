Tshoot: Temperature Errors

Use this table for troubleshooting the hardware items pertaining to temperatures issues in Access instruments.

Part

Failure

Substrate

Low

  • Thermistor cable connector to J19 on backplane is defective causing a negative temperature down to -23 degrees. (Replace cable)

Case

High

  • Thermistor on fluidics PCB is defective.
  • Failed internal blower fan at right end panel.

Low

  • Thermistor on fluidics PCB is open causing a reading of -23 degrees.
  • Connection at J19 on backplane is defective causing a negative temperature down to -23 degrees. (Replace cable)

Incubator

High

  • Temperature out of spec high. Incubator or wash carousel heater circuit shorted.

Low

  • Temperature reads -23 degrees. Thermistor circuit open.
  • Cable connector to J19 on backplane has poor connection (reseat or replace cable)

Wash Carousel

High

  • Random, minor, over temperature errors are normally caused by a blast of cool air entering the instrument through the vent on the left end panel. This causes the carousel to go into high duty cycle heating and the temperature will overshoot the control range. Protect the instrument from blasts of cool air.
  • Cable connector to J19 on backplane has poor connection (reseat or replace cable)
  • Thermistor lead is conducting air temp to the carousel sensor. Route 2" of lead under carousel insulation.
  • RV Load door has been left open allowing air to enter through the door.

Low

  • Temperature reads -23 degrees. Thermistor circuit open.
  • Cable connector to J19 on backplane has poor connection (reseat or replace cable)
  • Thermistor WW connection swapped with INC on the interface PCB.
  • Check the duty cycles of both INC and WW heaters in the analog devices screen.
  • Incubator heater defective (open). An open incubator heater affects wash carousel temperature.

Reagent Carousel

(Peltier)

High

  • Temperature slightly high out of range, but the module internal temp is ok or low when checked with a DVOM thermocouple. Partially shorted thermistor. Replace the thermistor.
  • Temperature out of spec high but not higher than room ambient. Peltier open circuit, or the Power Driver PCB is not grounding the 24VDC from the Peltiers.
  • Temperature reads 50 to 59 degrees. Thermistor is internally shorted or short in temp circuit.

Low

  • Temperature reads -23 degrees. Thermistor circuit open or disconnected.
  • Cable connector to J19 on backplane has poor connection (reseat or replace cable)
