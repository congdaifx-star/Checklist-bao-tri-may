Tshoot: Fluidics Module

Use this table for troubleshooting hardware items in the Fluidics module.

Item

Problem Cause / Action

Peristaltic Pump

  • Will not run.
    • Check Fluidics PCB (has motor drive circuit)
    • Check Power Driver and I/O PCB (provide on/off control signal).
    • Check fuses.
  • Runs backwards or runs at incorrect speed.
    • Replace Fluidics PCB.

Precision Pump Errors/ Failures

Note: A "prec vlv / pmp err"or is always a pump problem. The valve has its own specific error message.

  • Check for kinked buffer supply line or an obstruction in the buffer tank. (generally fails at the start of the Utility Assay)
  • Valve rotor not oriented correctly (black line to right rear at home).
  • Valve rotor not securely attached to rotor shaft (pin loose).
  • Primary pipettor probe plugged.
  • Obstruction between precision valve and primary probe.
  • Damaged or defective drive motor, gear, belt, or coupler.
  • Broken upper piston return spring. (the flat washer goes on top of the piston)
  • Pump seals damaged or worn.
  • Home sensor defective, or home flag jamming on sensor.
  • Defective SMD PCB (Older instruments, check for Rev H or higher SMD PCB).

Precision or Wash Valve Errors/ Failures

  • Defective, loose or broken valve drive coupler on motor shaft.
  • Motor seized / corroded due to leaking buffer.
  • Defective home sensor.
  • Stepper Motor Driver or QSMC PCB failure.
  • Ceramic rotor or stator chipped near one of the fluid ports.
  • Dried buffer on valve rotor and/or stator surfaces or outer edges.
  • Valve clogged with debris.

Wash Pump Errors / Failures

  • Home sensor corroded or otherwise damaged.
  • Stepper Motor Driver or QSMC PCB failure.
  • Defective belt, motor, gear or coupler.
  • Clogged passage in pump or fluidics manifold.
  • Defective, loose or broken valve drive coupler on motor shaft.
  • Valve rotor not oriented correctly (black line to right rear at home).
  • Valve rotor not securely attached to rotor shaft (pin loose).

Vacuum / Probe Wash

Over limit errors

  • Vacuum sensor misreading vacuum, zero offset.
  • Clog in the "T" fitting between the wash tower and the vacuum bottle.
  • Wash tower or valve clogged with a clot.

Under limit errors

  • Wash tower vacuum valve not fully closing (defective valve). Close the valve in digital devices or place your gloved finger directly onto the wash tower nozzle to check.
  • Vacuum pump clogged with dried buffer causing slow vacuum increase. Refer to the Flushing Vacuum Pumpprocedure to flush out the residue.
  • Vacuum pump not running. (Pump, Fluidics Interface PCB, or Power Driver PCB)
  • Plugged or restricted waste air filter or waste air / fluids line. (check external drain tube)
  • Incorrect alignment of pipettor probe to wash tower.
  • Vacuum fitting not fully tightened into the wash tower.
  • Intermittent connection of the sensors cable at J19 on the backplane.
  • Vacuum bottle cap or fitting loose, or bottle gasket not seated.
  • Vacuum system tubing leak.
  • Wash tower nozzle not fully tightened into wash tower.
  • Wash tower nozzle ports clogged.
