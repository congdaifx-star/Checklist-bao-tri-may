Tshoot: Noise

Use this table for troubleshooting noises heard while operating the instrument.

Part Failure
Abnormal Noise during Instrument Operation.
  • Mixer pulley corroded or worn pulley bearing.
  • Wash arm squeak - tubing guide arm is not seated correctly on probe plate.
  • Wash arm squeak - slide bearing may be binding.
  • Incubator belt vessel holders breaking due to fallen reaction vessel(s).
  • RV Shuttle plates or delron glides worn.
  • Reagent carousel drive belt loose and slipping on one of its drive pulleys.
  • Stepper motor driver PCB defective (causes shrill squeal in some motors even when they are idle).
  • Incubator belt idler bearing is defective.
  • RV load door sensor is defective. (door open alarm enabled)
  • RV load door is opened with the instrument not in the correct mode for loading reaction vessels.
  • Sample carousel drive pulley defective. (V shaped rubber unglued).
  • Sample carousel attempting to move when jammed.
  • Reagent carousel attempting to move when jammed.
  • Pipettor Gantry mounting screws not seated and tightened causing carriage to stall when moving in a horizontal direction.
