Tshoot: Troponin Fliers (Access 2)

For a collection of Troponin troubleshooting information, go to the Access Systems Applications database/Support Documents and Procedures/General/AccuTnI Support Portfolio

Risks that contribute to Troponin fliers are mechanical and pre-analytical. Rule out any mechanical issues prior to addressing pre-analytical issues with customers. The preferred approach is:

hsTni

Complete the diagnostic testing by running 100 reps of hsTnI using FRESH Sample Diluent A or a control material with a dose recovery of ~18pg/mL. Acceptable results are:

  • SDA - all results RLU are less than 2X S0 mean of active hsTnI calibration curve.
  • Low Control - values within +/- 2SD control limits.

Refer to the hsTnI NRFP Troubleshooting Checklist.pdf document for more details.

AccuTni+3

Complete the diagnostic testing by running 60-100 reps of AccuTnI+3 using FRESH wash buffer or a control with a dose recovery of ~0.04 ng/mL. Acceptable results are:

  • Wash buffer - all results </= 0.03 ng/mL
  • Low control - standard deviation </=0.01 ng/mL

If any of the results are outside limits, troubleshoot hardware similar to that of HS system check failure troubleshooting. See items below listed in decreasing order of importance or likelihood.

Area Check for

Incubator track/analytical module

Debris or bump on the incubator track. Clean thoroughly.

Aspirate Probes

  • Restriction (dirty/clogged/bent probes).
  • Tubing snagged and pulling up on probe when aspirating.
  • Tubing kinked, pinched, or holed.
  • Improper wash arm height adjustment. Refer to the procedure Adjusting Wash Arm.
  • Loose or damaged probe.

Peristaltic Pump and Waste Fluids System

  • Tubing kinked, pinched, or holed.
  • Peri pump cartridge not locked into place.
  • Perform Aspirate probe flow rate, 3 mL/min
  • Blocked waste fluids system.
  • Clogged or restricted waste air filter.

Vessel holder

Defective holder. Run Vessel Holder Exerciser.

Wash Pump / Valve

  • Wash pump seal worn.
  • Damaged pump belt.
  • Stator / rotor / valve cap.
  • Check for air bubbles.

Wash Carousel bearings

Rough movement. Replace as a precaution.

Mixer

  • Bearings - rough movement. Replace as a precaution.
  • Mixer speed, mixer belt, pinch rollers, tensioner wheel.

Mixer actuator arm

  • Actuator bracket adjustment (0.050").

Incubator belt

  • Tension correct.
  • Proper guide block adjustment.
  • Torn dovetail or faulty belt.
  • Belt pulley bearings rough movement. Replace as a precaution.
