Pipettor Alignment / Adjustment

This procedure provides instructions for aligning the main Pipettor. The pipettor X and Z alignments are controlled by software. The Pipettor Y alignment is mechanical.

The Pipettor is aligned in 3 directions:

  • X axis (Left - Right, and carousel CW / CCW rotation)
  • Y axis (Gantry Front - Back)
  • Z axis (Up - Down)

Alignments / Adjustments

Perform alignments on the following interacting subsystems:

Alignment: Pipettor to Reagent Carousel

Alignment: Pipettor to Sample Carousel

Alignment: Pipettor to Wash Tower

Alignment: Pipettor to RV

Alignment: Pipettor Y Adjust

Verification

  1. Enable the Utility Assay.
  2. Refer to the Verification Protocol Access Service Repair document for the required system verification procedures.
Note: Ensure that the cover interlock bypass key is removed from the front cover interlock switch. Do not leave the key at customer site.
