Replacing Mixer Belt

This procedure describes how to replace the mixer belt in Access and Access 2 instruments.

  1. Remove the mixer belt.
    1. Remove the wash arm from the top of the linear slide (one screw).
    2. Remove the lead screw linear nut band from the linear slide (two screws).
    3. Remove the mixer motor and mixer belt tensioner (two screws).
    4. Pull off the mixer belt from the mixer pulleys and remove it over the linear slide.
  2. Install the mixer belt.
    1. Install the mixer belt over the linear slide and onto the mixer pulleys.
    2. Install the mixer motor and mixer belt tensioner while routing the belt over all the pulleys (two screws).
      Note: Do not put extreme tension on the belt during installation. Extreme tension can cause fatigue failure of the internal cords.
    3. Install the lead screw linear nut band onto the linear slide (two screws).
    4. Install the wash arm on top of the linear slide (one screw).
      Note: Be sure the alignment pin is seated into the wash arm.
  3. Verify that the mixer is operating properly.
    1. Confirm the aspirate and dispense probe tubing is routed correctly and is not pulled tight when the wash arm is moved fully down.
    2. Adjust the mixer motor speed.
      Note: Refer to the Adjusting Mixer Motor Speed procedure.
  4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your local support organization.
