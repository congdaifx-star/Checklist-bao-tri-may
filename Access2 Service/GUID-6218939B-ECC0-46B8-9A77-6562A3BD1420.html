Removing Analytical Module

This procedure describes how to remove the Access 2 analytical module.

Use this procedure with other documents that provide detailed information on alignments and specific areas of the module. To install the analytical module, refer to the Installing Analytical Module procedure.

  1. Disconnect the assemblies and connectors in the wash area.
    1. Power off the instrument.
    2. Disconnect the waste bag from the waste chute, and then remove the instrument upper and lower front covers.
    3. Disconnect the substrate probe from the substrate pump and from the wash arm. Place the probe on a lint free tissue.
      Note:
      • Press down and rotate one-quarter turn counter-clockwise to release the substrate probe from the wash arm.
      • Avoid kinking the tubing or touching the substrate probe tip on anything.
    4. Loosen the screw securing the wash arm to the slide carriage. Lift the wash arm from its mount and position the wash arm in a location that allows the analytical module to slide out of the instrument.
      Note: Avoid bending any of the probes.
    5. Disconnect the mixer motor index sensor connector from J33 on the main fluidics PCB.
      Note: Route the cable so that the cable is free of the fluidics manifold.
    6. Remove the screws holding the analytical module support rails to the instrument base.
  2. Disconnect the connectors in the luminometer and interface PCB areas
    1. Disconnect these connectors in the luminometer area:
      • Ground strap
      • Luminometer signal cable connections
      • LED reference connector
    2. Disconnect these connectors from the analytical module interface PCB:
      • Ground strap (mounting screw)
      • Power cable (J11)
      • Ribbon cable (J1)
      • Thermistor cables (INC, WW, and RV)
  3. Remove the analytical module.
    1. Lift the module off the locating pin on the base plate and carefully slide the module a few inches out of the instrument.
    2. Confirm that nothing was missed that could prevent removal of the module.
    3. When the module is half-way out of instrument, stand to one side of the module and place one hand under the front of the module.
    4. Continue sliding the module out until you can place your other hand behind the wash wheel, then carefully lift the module out of the instrument.
    5. Place the module in a location where it cannot slip, drop, or otherwise fall and be damaged.
      Note: If space is limited, the module can be placed backwards in the instrument with the wash wheel toward the front for servicing.
