Installing Front Cover Stops

This procedure describes how to install the front cover stops.

Required Materials:
Quantity Part Name P/N
1 Hex wrench set Not in CARES
1 Alcohol pad Not in CARES
1 Loctite® 2221 Not in CARES
1 Front Cover Stops Service Kit (contains the following):
  • Left stop (one)
  • Right stop (one)
  • Set-screws (four)
  • Clips, retainer (two)
81600065
This procedure describes how to install the front cover stops and low profile c-clips to increase the clearance between a warped front cover and the pipettor carriage. Install this kit only on instruments, as required.

Instruments with front covers that are warped enough to contact the pipettor carriage exhibit x or z pipettor counts out of range errors. Because of the unwanted contact, instruments with warped covers also have scuff marks inside the cover.

Instruments with certified pipettor gantries have either:
  • Stops installed in manufacturing.
  • Gantries with stops that are part of the end brackets.
  1. Install the front cover stops.
    1. Open the upper front cover to access the pipettor gantry.
    2. Place a small amount of Loctite® 222 on each of the four set-screws, and start the set-screws into the left and right stops.
      Note: The set-screws face away from the pipettor carriage.
    3. Slide the left stop over the pipettor gantry left-end bracket so that the bottom edge of the stop block is level with the angle in the bracket.
    4. Tighten the set-screws until the stop is secure.
    5. Slide the right stop over the pipettor gantry right-end bracket so that the bottom edge of the stop block is level with the angle in the bracket.
    6. Tighten the set-screws until the stop is secure.
    7. Remove the clips holding the flex cable and supply tubing.
    8. Replace the clips with the new low-profile retainer clips from the kit.
  2. Verify operation of the pipettor carriage and stops.
    1. Close the upper front cover and open the top cover.
    2. From the Mechanics screen, cycle the pipettor carriage from home to the reaction vessel location ten times, and then cycle the pipettor carriage from home to the reagent pack location ten times.
      Note: If the pipettor carriage rubs on the upper front cover during this verification process, look for the cause. If you can find no cause, other than a warped upper front cover, leave the stops on the pipettor gantry and install a new upper front cover.
Expected Outcome:

During the verification process, observe the pipettor carriage to confirm that the carriage does not rub on the upper front cover.

When the upper front cover of the instrument is closed and the pipettor is operating, there should be no pipettor motion errors, the pipettor carriage should not contact the upper front cover, and there should be no unusual noise.
