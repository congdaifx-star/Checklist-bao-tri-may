Perform Mechanical Check During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to perform a mechanical check when troubleshooting incubator belt calibration.

Perform this procedure when instructed to do to so as part of the troubleshooting process for incubator belt calibration.
Note: Refer to the Tshoot: Incubator Belt Calibration procedure.
Perform a mechanical check.
  1. Check the incubator belt tension.
    Note: Refer to the Replacing Incubator Belt procedure.
  2. Check the two front pulleys to determine if the pulleys are parallel to each other. If the pulleys are not parallel, then refer to Replacing Incubator Belt procedure for instruction.


  3. Check the condition and tension of the incubator belt motor drive belt. If replacement or tensioning is required, refer to the Replacing Incubator Drive Motor Assembly procedure.
  4. Verify that the belt guides are just flush with the incubator belt. Adjust any belt guides that are not aligned correctly.


  5. Check the incubator belt pulley bearings. Refer to Replacing Incubator Belt Bearings procedure.
  6. Ensure that all vessel holders are seated fully on the belt dove tails.
