Disassembling and Reassembling Carousel Module with Frozen Trantorque Nut

Parts required for frozen trantorque nut repair:
  • 3552A - Reagent carousel shaft assembly (Shaft, White Plastic Hub, Roll Pin, C-Clip)
  • 5072B - Pulley, reagent carousel drive
  • 5143A - Trantorque, reagent carousel drive shaft
  • 5138B - Bearings (two) (can break when removing the hub)
Only perform these steps to disassemble and reassemble the carousel module when a frozen trantorque nut is encountered while replacing the Peltiers in an Access 2 instrument.
Note: Refer to the Replacing Peltier (Access 2) procedure.
  1. Disassemble the module when there is a frozen trantorque nut.
    1. Remove the sensor mounting plate (two screws).


    2. Remove the reagent carousel from the white plastic hub (three screws).
    3. Using a small screwdriver and a hammer, split the white plastic hub by positioning the screwdriver blade on top of the hub, to one side of the reagent carousel shaft, and lightly tapping the screwdriver with the hammer until the plastic hub splits.


    4. Split the other side of the plastic hub, and remove the pieces of the hub.
    5. Drive the roll pin out of the reagent carousel shaft using a pin punch or hex wrench and a hammer.
    6. Invert the module, disconnect the reagent carousel drive motor connector, and remove the harnesses from the clamps on the air duct.
    7. Remove the two screws attaching the air duct to the reagent carousel.
    8. Lift up on and remove the air duct, the drive pulley, and the shaft from the reagent carousel.
    9. Remove the C-clip from the drive shaft, and separate the shaft from the air duct.


    10. Remove the home flag from the old carousel drive pulley, and install the flag onto the new drive pulley (two screws).
  2. Reassemble the drive shaft after removing a frozen trantorque nut.
    1. Place the carousel module upright on the work area.
    2. Install the new reagent carousel shaft assembly into the center hole of the reagent carousel.
    3. Mount the reagent carousel onto the white plastic hub (three screws).
    4. Insert the carousel alignment tool into the alignment hole in the reagent carousel at position 3. Rotate the carousel until the tool drops into the alignment hole in the housing.
      Note:
      • When the alignment tool is seated, carousel position 1 is near the pack loading position.
      • Hold or secure the tool in place or it will fall out when the module is inverted.
    5. While holding the carousel and shaft in place, invert the module.
    6. Install the C-clip onto the groove in the shaft where the shaft exists the bottom of the reagent carousel.
  3. Remove the alignment tool from the drive pulley. Rotate the pulley so that the home flag clears the home sensor, and then slide the pulley off the shaft.
    Note: Leave the trantorque nut in the pulley. It falls apart if removed, and then must be reassembled.
  4. Continue with the replacing the Peltiers procedure, at the Remove the Peltiers step.
    Note: Refer to the Replacing Peltier (Access 2) procedure.
