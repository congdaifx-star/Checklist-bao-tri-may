Troubleshooting Assay General

This document provides general troubleshooting tools for assay issues. It contains information regarding classifying a failure and possible causes of specific failures.

Details

Assay Troubleshooting

Use this table to identify causes for various assay performance causes.

Note:
  • Checking test results for both a sandwich and a competitive assay can help to identify the problem cause more clearly because opposite or same-direction changes in RLUs will occur in the different assay formats.
  • The S0 signal for some assays, hTSH and Total IgE for example, are naturally low and come close to approximating those of substrate blanks.
  • Insufficient substrate delivery may have an unexpected effect on assay results either high or low RLUs. As a general rule, signal will increase as the volume of substrate decreases. This is due to a relatively higher proportion of conjugate to a smaller overall volume of fluid. This increase continues until the volume of fluid in the RV is insufficient to reach the read level height of the Luminometer, at which time the signal drops off dramatically.

Outliers or Poor CVs

Note: Often problems are reported simply as "high CVs". An examination of the test results may show that the high CVs are caused mainly by outliers in one direction (high or low RLUs) from the correct value.

Symptom

Causes

Assay Outliers or Poor CVs

Analytical Module

  • Mixer motor speed incorrect, or intermittent. Check for broken mixer motor power leads.
  • Pinch roller retainer loose or improperly adjusted (causes rough reaction vessel mixing).
  • Mixer pulley O-rings worn or idlers not holding RV well for good mixing.
  • Deposits, precipitate, etc. on pinch roller or mixer pulley.
  • Improper alignment between incubator belt and the wash/read carousel at wash in, which causes splashing.
  • Splash caused by uneven movement of the vessel holder through its path of travel (incubator bearings, debris in incubator track).
  • Bent or damaged reaction vessel holder legs causing splashing at transfers.
  • Dirty wash wheel, wash wheel housing.
  • Wash wheel bearings not rotating properly.
  • RV holder spring in front of Luminometer damaged or incorrectly shaped.
  • Luminometer mounting screws loose.
  • Foreign chemical or debris in reaction vessels.

Peristaltic Pump / Aspiration

  • Dirty or plugged aspirate probes.
  • Aspirate probe(s) pulled up due to insufficient tubing slack or incorrect tubing routing.
  • Aspirate or dispense probe retainer broken or loose.
  • Tubing on aspirate or dispense probe(s) is leaking.
  • Peristaltic pump tubing flattened, damaged, or cracked.
  • Waste air filter clogged causing pressure build-up that interferes with aspiration or causes aspirate probes to spit back into the RV.
  • Wash arm not seated properly on its linear slide mount.
  • Wash arm bearing failed or linear nut worn causing Z misalignment.

Sample Pipettor

  • Main pipettor 'X', 'Y', or 'Z' alignment is incorrect causing sample to be dripped or splashed onto the side wall of a reaction vessel.
  • Main pipettor probe dirty, bent, or damaged.
  • Wash tower nozzle dirty or plugged.

Substrate:

  • Substrate probe not centered in the wash/read carousel housing hole (causes substrate deposits on side walls of hole).
  • Substrate probe tubing damaged. (bent crushed tip, kinked tube, probe ferrule is too tight constricting the tube).

Fluidics

  • Wash Valve rotor drive slot worn.
  • Wash buffer line restricted (clogged/kinked/crushed) anywhere between supply reservoir and precision valve.
  • Precision pump or wash pump seals worn and belt stretched or worn.
  • Contamination in wash buffer or substrate and associated tubing/valve/probes.
  • Bubble dispensed with wash buffer into RV bursts and splatters onto RV or probe.

Assay Drift

  • Substrate not equilibrated at room temperature for 18-72 hours before use on instrument (bottle loaded directly from refrigerator).
  • Incorrect ultrasonic mixing of the reagent pack (pipettor alignments, voltage adjustment, service loop broken).
  • Evaporation from the reagent pack due to torn or separated elastomer (pipettor alignments).
  • Reagent pack not stored properly.
  • Residual Contrad suppresses substrate RLUs following a decontamination.

Assay Carryover

  • Pipettor wash tower nozzle holes plugged.
  • Wash tower nozzle lower o-ring missing.
  • Poor pipettor alignment at the wash tower resulting in poor washing.
  • Pipettor 'X', 'Y', or 'Z' alignment incorrect.
  • Vacuum valve, vacuum pump not operating properly.
  • Clogged air filter or waste line.
  • Substrate pump or probe causing splashing in reaction vessels when substrate is dispensed.
  • Reagent pack elastomer seal is loose.

Results Flags

Test Result Flags indicate that something abnormal occurred during a test.

  • More than one flag can be set for any given test.
  • Test results are suppressed when fatal flags occur.

Flag

Description

Fatal / Non-Fatal

CCR

The results could not be calculated because

  • One of the tests included in a derived result formula did not produce a result.
  • A confirmatory test result could not be calculated. Usually this occurs because the samples for the qualitative and confirmatory tests were not aspirated at the same time.
  • Another error prevented the system from calculating a result

Fatal

CEX

The calibration curve or cut-off value used has expired.

Not Fatal

CLT

An obstruction was detected.

Fatal

CRH

The result is above the upper limit of the critical range.

Not Fatal

CRL

The result is below the lower limit of the critical range.

Not Fatal

EXS

The substrate is expired.

Not Fatal

GRY

The result is within the specified gray zone (qualitative assays).

Not Fatal

IND

  • For sandwich assays, the result is at the low end of the analyte concentration curve. The result cannot be distinguished from a system failure because the RLU reading or concentration is too low.
  • For competitive assays, the result is at either the high or low end of the analyte concentration curve. The result cannot be distinguished from a system failure because the RLU reading or the concentration is either too high or too low.

Fatal

LEX

The reagent pack lot used has expired.

Not Fatal

LOW

The result is lower than the minimum reportable result value defined by the assay protocol.

Not Fatal

LRH

The result is above the upper limit of the LIS range. The system does not send results with this flag to the LIS.

  • This flag is displayed only when the Auto-Send to LIS option is set to Verify.
  • This flag is for quantitative , semi-quantitative, and derived assays.

Not Fatal

LRL

The result is below the lower limit of the LIS range. The system does not send results with this flag to the LIS.

  • This flag is displayed only when the Auto-Send to LIS option is set to Verify.
  • This flag is for quantitative , semi-quantitative, and derived assays.

Not Fatal

NCR

No calibration data existed for the reagent lot when the result was processed.

Fatal

NRV

The status of at least one system supply is insufficient for test processing. After the necessary supply status is corrected and Run is pressed, the test will be scheduled for processing.

Not Fatal (not scheduled

for processing)

OLD

The sample has been on-board the analyzer for more than 2 hours. This time is measured from the time the sample is loaded onto the system until the time the sample is pipetted into the reaction vessel. This error only occurs on Base Access.

Not Fatal

ORH

The result is above the upper limit of the reference range.

Not Fatal

ORL

The result is below the lower limit of the reference range.

Not Fatal

OVR

The calculated concentration is above the highest or most concentrated calibrator.

Not Fatal

PEX

The open pack stability time has elapsed for the reagent pack used. This time is measured from the time the pack is first punctured.

Not Fatal

QCF

The quality control sample result has failed to meet the criteria for one or more QC rules. Open the QC Data screen to determine which criteria were not met.

Not Fatal

QEX

The quality control lot has expired.

Not Fatal

QNS

The sample volume is insufficient. If this flag is obtained, no more tests will be scheduled for that sample. However, tests that have already been scheduled will be completed.

Fatal

RFX

The results is from a reflex test.

Not Fatal.

RLU

The relative light unit (RLU) value is outside of the acceptable luminometer measuring range.

Fatal

SYS

A system error occurred during test processing. Refer to the Event Log to review the error.

Fatal

TRI

The temperature of the incubator was outside the acceptable limits. All tests being incubated when the temperature was outside limits are flagged.

Fatal

TRS

The temperature of the substrate was outside the acceptable limits when it was dispensed.

Fatal

TRW

The temperature of the wash/read carousel was outside the acceptable limits. All tests with RVs in the wash/read carousel when the temperature was outside limits are flagged.

Fatal

TMP

The temperature of the pipette fluid heater or reagent carousel was outside the acceptable limits. This error only occurs on Base Access.

Not Fatal

The temperature of the wash/read carousel, incubator, or substrate when it was dispensed was outside the acceptable limits. This error only occurs on Base Access.

Fatal

UNR

The measured sample signal (RLU) is less than an RLU threshold limit for the least concentrated calibrator/calibration control for a positive slope assay, or is greater than the least concentrated calibrator for a negative slope assay, as defined in the assay protocol file.

Fatal
