Alignment: Pipettor to RV

Perform these steps to align the pipettor to the rv shuttle.
  1. Reaction Vessel X alignment
    Perform these steps to align the Pipettor to the RV Shuttle X position.
    1. Remove the Reaction Vessel (RV) from shuttle position 1 and insert the RV alignment tool in its place.
      Note: An RV is slightly taller than the tool and may cause issues with dilution assays if used for the height alignment.
    2. In the Mechanics screen make the Pipettor the Selected Device, choose RV Shuttle as the position, Home the Pipettor, and then select Move X to Position.
    3. Remove the interlock switch key to disable the Pipettor motors, rotate the drive pulley at the left end of the gantry to manually move the Pipettor downward until the probe is just above the RV tool, and then reinsert the interlock switch key.
      Note: Make sure the pipettor does not move left or right when moving it down.
    4. Use the directional keys to adjust the probe X-Axis into alignment with the crosshairs on the RV tool.
    5. Check the alignment of the probe to the RV tool in the front to back "Y" axis.
      If the Pipettor probe does not properly align to the RV Alignment Tool in the front to back "Y" axis, then:
      • Confirm that the Analytical module is locked into position on its alignment pins.
      • Perform the Pipettor "Y" adjustment.
    6. Press Save Offset to save the new offset values.
  2. Reaction Vessel Z alignment
    Perform these steps to align the Pipettor to the RV Shuttle Z position.
    1. In the Mechanics screen make the Pipettor the Selected Device, choose RV Shuttle as the position, Home the Pipettor, and then select Move Z to Position.
    2. Insert a bent 0.010 inch feeler gauge between the probe and the top inside surface of the RV tool, then use the directional keys to adjust the probe Z-Axis to the correct height.
    3. Press Save Offset to save the new offset values.
    4. Press Home to home the Pipettor, remove the RV Alignment Tool, reinstall the Reaction Vessel into shuttle position 1.
    5. Press [ESC] to exit the alignment screen, and select Yes to save alignments if prompted.

Go back to the parent topic to perform any other necessary alignments and for verification steps once all alignments are complete.
