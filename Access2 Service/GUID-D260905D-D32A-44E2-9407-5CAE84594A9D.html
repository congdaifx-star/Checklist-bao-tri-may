Inspecting Mixer Pinch Roller Assemblies

This procedure describes how to inspect the mixer pinch roller assemblies.

This procedure describes how to:

  • Inspect mixer pinch roller assemblies.
  • Verify operation of mixer pinch rollers.
  • Inspect o-rings and bearings.

Defective pinch rollers can cause poor CVs and low RLU issues during calibrations because of poor mixing at the substrate location.

  1. Resolve abnormal mixing motion.
    1. Perform the mixer exerciser found in the More Options screen in Diagnostics.
    2. Observe each pinch roller and verify smooth operation in both directions.
    3. Check that the mixer speed is 2500 ±20 rpm.
  2. Inspect the mixing hardware at the three locations.
    1. Remove the 10-tooth and 14-tooth mixer pulley assemblies and examine the o-rings. The o-rings should be fully seated in their grooves and not twisted or rotated.
    2. Rotate each pulley in its bearing. The bearings should spin freely in both directions.
    3. If a pulley assembly contains twisted or worn o-rings or does not rotate freely, install a new triple o-ring mixer assembly.
    4. Remove each pinch roller assembly.
    5. Ensure that the idler rollers are clean and spin freely in both directions.
    6. Clean or replace the rollers, if necessary.
    7. Repeat the mixer exerciser test after reassembling the parts.
  3. Perform a complete System Check to verify operation of the mixer pulleys and pinch rollers.
  4. If you have questions or concerns, contact your local support office.
