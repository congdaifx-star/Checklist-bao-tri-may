Replacing the Bulk Feeder / Supply Shuttle Subsystem

This topic includes information about how to replace the bulk feeder and supply shuttle subsystem.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for removal/installation of the Bulk Feeder subsystem, including Supply Shuttle:
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)

Removing the Bulk Vessel Feeder / Supply Shuttle Subsystem

Follow these steps to remove the bulk vessel feeder/supply shuttle subsystem.
Note: Removal of the Sample Wheel may be beneficial to gain access to the screws/components. See Replacing the Sample Storage Wheel Subsystem for more information.
  1. Empty all vessels from the vessel hopper and supply chute.
  2. Turn off instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  3. Open the upper left main cover and left rear cover.
  4. Remove the following three cables from the front of the subsystem near the front of the supply shuttle:
    • Serial cable
    • Large connector
    • Small connector (connected to backside of large connector)
  5. Remove the 24V power supply cable from the J6 connection on the DMCC (on the side of the vessel hopper).
  6. Remove the following three cables from the rear of the subsystem by the supply shuttle motor assembly:
    • Motor cable from motor terminal
    • Encoder cable from encoder terminal
    • Home sensor from extension cable
  7. Remove the following four screws (4mm driver) which secure the subsystem to the instrument chassis:
    • Two at the front of the subsystem
    • Two at the rear of the subsystem in front of the sample wheel
  8. Carefully remove the entire subsystem, ensuring that no cables or wires are pinched or stretched.

Installing the Bulk Vessel Feeder / Supply Shuttle Subsystem

Follow these steps to install the bulk vessel feeder/supply shuttle subsystem.
  1. Carefully place the subsystem into the instrument so that it seats on the positioning pins in the instrument chassis.
  2. Secure the subsystem to the instrument chassis with the following four screws (4mm driver):
    • Two at the front of the subsystem
    • Two at the rear of the subsystem in front of the sample wheel
  3. Connect the following three cables at the rear of the subsystem near the supply shuttle motor:
    • Motor cable from motor terminal
    • Encoder cable from encoder terminal
    • Home sensor from extension cable
  4. Connect the 24V power supply cable at the J6 connection on the DMCC (on the side of the vessel hopper).
  5. Connect the following three cables at the front of the subsystem near the front of the supply shuttle:
    • Serial cable
    • Large connector
    • Small connector (connected to backside of large connector)
  6. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.

Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
