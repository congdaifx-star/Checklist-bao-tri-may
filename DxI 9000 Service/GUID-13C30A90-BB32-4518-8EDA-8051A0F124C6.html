Troubleshooting the Ultrasonics Transducer

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot the ultrasonics transducer for the reagent pipettor.

  1. Confirm the ulstrasonics transducer voltage meets specifications. If the voltage does not meet specifications, calibrate the ultrasonics transducer. See Calibrating the Ultrasonics Transducers for more information.
  2. If calibration fails:
    1. Select the SYSTEM RUNNING button and go to Stop > Pause to enter the instrument into a YELLOW (Paused) status.
    2. Inspect all reagent pipettor components for damage, as noted in the table below.
      Note: After you inspect each component or after you inspect all components, calibrate the ultrasonics transducer again. See Calibrating the Ultrasonics Transducers for more information.
      Component Inspection Action

      Pipettor Ground Cable

      Note: The ground cable only appears on instruments with serial numbers 300001 through 300120.

      Inspect the ground cable for damage or corrosion.

      Inspect the ground screws on both ends to be sure they are tight.

      Check continuity to ensure it is reading less than 100 ohms. Refer to the MOD 12792 - Reagent Grounding Cable - Install document for more details.

      If there is damage or corrosion, replace the ground cable, confirm continuity, and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      If the screws are loose, tighten them, confirm continuity, and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      If the continuity is greater than 100 ohms, replace the ground cable, confirm continuity, and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      See Calibrating the Ultrasonics Transducers for more information.

      Pipettor Fluidics Tubing Fitting

      Inspect the tubing fitting for debris and damage or damaged threads.

      If debris is present, clean it, reinstall the fitting to the transdcuer, and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      If there is damage and it is hard to seat the fitting back in place, replace the tubing fitting and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      See Calibrating the Ultrasonics Transducers for more information.

      Pipettor Retaining Nut

      Inspect the retaining nut for rust and damage or damaged threads.

      If there is rust or there is damage to the nut or thread, replace the retaining nut and recalibrate ultrasonics.

      See Replacing the Probe Tip and Retaining Nut for more information.

      See Calibrating the Ultrasonics Transducers for more information.

      Pipettor Probe (Tip)

      Inspect the probe or tip for bends or damage.

      Confirm whether the probe or tip is perpendicular in the X-axis or not hitting the side of the alignment pin or reaction vessel (RV) when it dips down into the RV.

      If the tip is bent or has damage, replace the tip, recalibrate ultrasonics, and perform alignments.

      See Replacing the Probe Tip and Retaining Nut for more inforamation.

      If the probe or tip is not perpendicular in the X-axis or it is hitting the side of the alignment pin or RV, adjust the transducer position. See Adjusting and Aligning the Reagent Pipettor for more information.

      Pipettor Transducer

      Inspect the transducer wires and connectors for damage or signs of corrosion.

      Inspect the transducer's fitting hole for debris or damaged threads.

      If the wires or connectors are damaged or if there are signs of corrosion, replace the transducer, recalibrate ultrasonics, and perform alignments.

      If the transducer hole shows signs of damage or you are not able to connect the tubing fitting onto it, replace the transducer.

      See Replacing the Probe Tip and Retaining Nut for more information.

  3. After you have inspected all components, if ulstrasonics transducer calibration fails, replace the transducer. See Replacing the Probe Tip and Retaining Nut for more information.
