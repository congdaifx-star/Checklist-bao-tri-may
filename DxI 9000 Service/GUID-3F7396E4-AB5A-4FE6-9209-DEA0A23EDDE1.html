Troubleshooting Pressure Fault Events

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot pressure fault events.

Event Description Cause(s)
Failure to confirm condensate purge Expected air supply pressure drop not measured after opening condensate purge valve after 60s timeout
  1. Condensate purge valve failure
  2. Condensate purge line kinked/clogged
Air Supply Pressure Out of Range 32 PSI not achieved after 60 seconds of compressor on time
  1. The Compressor has Failed
  2. A large leak exists in the system
  3. Check valve or filter failure
32-35 PSI Pressure Out of Range Branch pressure is outside the allowable range for a specific period of consecutive time
  1. House air pressure regulator set incorrectly (high or low pressure)
  2. Pneumatic leak (low pressure only)
  3. Check valve or filter failure
20 PSI Pressure Out of Range Branch pressure is outside the allowable range for a specific period of consecutive time
  1. Manifold 20 PSI air pressure regulator set incorrectly (high or low pressure)
  2. Pneumatic leak (low pressure only)
  3. Check valve or filter failure
12 PSI Pressure out of Range Branch pressure is outside the allowable range for a specific period of consecutive time
  1. Manifold 12 PSI air pressure regulator set incorrectly (high or low pressure)
  2. Pneumatic leak (low pressure only)
  3. Check valve or filter failure
Ensure check valve and filter are oriented for downward flow.
