Troubleshooting Process Monitoring

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot process monitoring based on the event message or symptom presented by the system.

The table below includes symptoms (event messages, failed diagnostics, etc.), potential causes, and recovery steps for various process monitoring-related issues.

Symptom

Potential Cause

Recovery Steps

AliquotTipLoadFailure

Event Code: 417040002

The aliquot sample pipettor did not load a new disposable tip.

  1. Perform Collision Sensor Check.

  2. Ensure tip supply has tips.

  3. Check alignments of aliquot sample pipettor to tip supply.

PreciseTipLoadFailure

Event Code: 418040002

The precision sample pipettor did not load a new disposable tip.

  1. Perform Collision Sensor Check.

  2. Ensure tip supply has tips.

  3. Check alignment of precision sample pipettor to tip supply.

PreciseSamplePipettorVolumeFailure

Event Code: 418000101

The precision sample pipettor volume was incorrect three times in a row.

  1. Perform precision sample pipettor camera calibrations.

  2. Check precision sample pipettor pump.

  3. Check alignment of precision sample pipettor to build carriages.

ResidualVolumeFailureinWashWheel

Event Code: 412120001

The wash residual volume was incorrect three times in a row.

  1. Check aspirate tubing connections for damage.

  2. Check peristaltic tubing for damage. Check peristaltic pump.

  3. Perform wash wheel camera residual volume calibration.

  4. Check alignment of wash wheel.

Wash Wheel Substrate Dispense Out of Range Failure

Event Code: 412110001

The substrate dispense volume was incorrect three times in a row.

Refer to Troubleshooting Substrate Loader Issues.
