Replacing the Monitor

This topic includes information about how to replace the monitor.

Important: The instrument must be powered down and disconnected from the power source before you perform this procedure. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • Phillips head screwdriver

Removing the Monitor

Perform this procedure to remove the monitor.

  1. Turn off instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Disconnect the cables from the monitor/USB hub.
    • Power Cable
    • Video Cable
    • USB Cable (to USB hub)
  3. Remove the four screws (Phillips head) securing the monitor and barcode reader bracket to the monitor arm and remove the monitor and barcode reader mount.

Installing the Monitor

Perform this procedure to install the monitor.

  1. Mount the monitor and barcode reader bracket onto the monitor arm and secure with three screws.
  2. Connect the cables to the back of the monitor/USB hub.
    • Power Cable
    • Video Cable
    • USB Cable (to USB hub)
  3. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.

Alignments

No alignments are required after replacement of peripheral components, provided that the alignment values were not lost during service (such as in the replacement of a console or hard drive where a current backup of the database was not recovered).

Verification

To verify operation of peripherals after removal/installation, confirm the system initializes successfully and that all monitor touchscreen functions appear to be working.
