Cleaning and Lubricating the Syringe Pump Drive Assembly

This topic includes information about how to remove, clean, lubricate, and reinstall the syringe pump drive assembly.
Note: Follow this procedure for all eight reagent syringe pumps and two wash syringe pumps. You must typically complete this task during preventive maintenance, but you may also perform this task on an as-needed basis.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Required Materials

The following tools and materials are required to properly clean and lubricate the syringe pumps:
  • 3mm hex driver
  • Lint-free cleaning swabs
  • B10571 - Grease, Losoid 72515 (40g)

Removing the Syringe Pump Assembly

Follow these steps to remove the syringe drive from the system.
  1. Remove the syringe from the drive assembly by loosening the plastic click-and-seal nut at the top of the syringe and the metal thumb screw retaining the plunger.
    Note: To prevent leaks or introduction of air to the system the wash buffer fittings should be left attached.
  2. Remove the top screw and loosen the bottom screw to free the syringe drive assembly from its mounting location.
  3. Disconnect the two cables (motor drive and home sensor) at the rear of the drive assembly.
  4. With the syringe and tubing moved to the side, pull the drive assembly out of the mounting location and free of the instrument.

Cleaning and Lubricating the Drive Components

Follow these steps to clean and lubricate the syringe pump drive components (linear slide and drive gears).
  1. With the drive assembly removed from the system and placed on a workbench, use a lint-free swab to wipe clean the linear slide on the front of the assembly, and the drive gears inside/at the rear of the assembly.
  2. Apply Losoid (Losimol 72515) grease to a clean lint-free swab and use to apply the grease to the linear slide and drive gears.

Installing the Syringe Pump Assembly

Follow these steps to re-install the syringe pump assembly to the system.
  1. Place the syringe pump drive assembly into the mounting location so that the slot at the bottom sits onto the bottom screw.
  2. Pivot the assembly inward so that it rests flush against the mounting location and install the upper screw.
  3. Tighten both upper and lower screws to secure the assembly in place.
  4. Install the syringe to the drive assembly, ensure the syringe is fully seated and that plunger is not binding.
    Note: See Replacing the Syringes for more information.
  5. Connect the two cables (motor drive and home sensor) at the rear of the drive assembly.
