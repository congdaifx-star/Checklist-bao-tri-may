Verification of Preventive Maintenance

This topic includes information about the minimum verification testing required after performing preventive maintenance to return the instrument to the customer for normal operation.

Table 33: Definitions
Term Definition
Service Tasks Tasks to be completed either before or after instrument verification depending on the module serviced and the required verification.
Minimum Verification Required The minimum testing required before releasing the instrument to the customer for its intended use.

Quality Control Statement

Beckman Coulter recommends customers run quality control (QC) samples after any unscheduled or scheduled maintenance or service and after successfully installing new software. Quality control materials simulate the characteristics of patient samples and are essential for monitoring the system performance of immunochemical assays. The verifications recommended in this procedure demonstrate instrument repair and functionality to specification but they do not supersede Beckman Coulter's recommended guidelines for QC as stated in the assay directional inserts, Instrument Instructions for Use (IFU), and Help system. Each laboratory determines the QC rules to apply and the course of action to take when a result violates a rule.
