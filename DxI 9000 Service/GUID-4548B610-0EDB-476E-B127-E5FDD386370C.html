Replacing RV Pusher Components

This topic includes information about how to replace RV pusher components for the bulk vessel feeder, including the motor, home sensor, belt, tensioner, and linear rail.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2mm hex driver (for M2.5 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing RV Pusher Components

Perform this procedure to remove the desired RV pusher components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Move the pusher rod into the eject position (protruding into the vessel scoop chain) to allow access to the tensioner and home sensor screws.
  2. Disconnect the home sensor cable from the extension cable.
  3. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the home sensor to the pusher assembly, and remove the home sensor.
  4. Loosen the two tensioner screws (2.5mm driver) next to the pulley, but do not remove.
  5. Push the tensioner inward to relieve tension from the belt, and secure in place by tightening at least one of the screws.
  6. With tension relieved from the belt, remove the belt from the pulleys and pull the belt downward out of the pusher assembly mount.
  7. Disconnect the motor cable from the motor terminal.
  8. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor to the underside of the pusher assembly and remove the motor.
    Note: The smaller motor under the pusher requires a (2mm) driver.
  9. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the tensioner assembly and remove the tensioner assembly.
  10. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the mount to the linear rail truck.
    Caution: DO NOT allow the truck to come completely off the end of the linear rail, as the internal bearings may fall out.
  11. Remove the five screws (2.5mm driver) securing the linear rail to the pusher chassis and remove the linear rail and truck assembly.

Installing RV Pusher Components

Perform this procedure to install RV pusher components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the linear rail and secure with the five screws (2.5mm driver).
  2. Attach the mount to the linear rail truck with the four screws (2.5mm driver).
  3. Install the tensioner assembly and secure with the two screws (2.5mm driver).
  4. Install the motor from the underside and secure with the four screws (2mm driver).
  5. Connect the motor cable to the motor terminal.
  6. Install the belt by first inserting into the underside of the pusher assembly mount, and then placing around the motor pulley.
    Note: It may be necessary to remove the mount to reinstall the belt
  7. Relieve tension from the tensioner by loosening the two screws (2.5mm driver) and pushing the tensioner inward, then secure at least one of the screws to hold the tensioner in place.
  8. Place the belt over the tensioner pulley, and then set the tension by loosening the tensioner screws (2.5mm driver) and allowing the tensioner to move into place, then secure with the two screws.
    Note: The tension is set automatically by the force of the tensioner springs. Do not apply any force to the tensioner when tightening the tensioner screws after setting the tension.
  9. Install the home sensor and secure with the two screws (2.5mm driver).
  10. Connect the home sensor cable to the extension cable.
