Replacing the Shuttle and Linear Rail

This topic includes information about how to replace the shuttle and linear rail for the supply shuttle.

Note: Before you complete these procedures you must turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Shuttle and Linear Rail

Perform this procedure to remove the RV supply shuttle and linear rail. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Remove the belt from the supply shuttle by sliding it out of the recess that fixes it to the shuttle.
  2. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the shuttle to the linear rail truck and remove the supply shuttle.
  3. Remove the nine screws (2.5mm driver) securing the linear rail to the supply shuttle chassis and remove the linear rail.
    Note: It may be necessary to remove the sample pipettor tip discard chute to remove the linear rail.
    Caution: DO NOT allow the truck to slide off the end of the linear rail as the bearings inside are loose and may be lost.

Installing the Shuttle and Linear Rail

Perform this procedure to install the shuttle and linear rail. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the linear rail and truck onto the supply shuttle chassis and secure with nine screws (2.5mm driver).
    Caution: DO NOT allow the truck to slide off the end of the linear rail as the bearings inside are loose and may be lost.
  2. Install the supply shuttle onto the linear rail truck and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  3. Fix the belt to the supply shuttle by sliding it into the recess.

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
