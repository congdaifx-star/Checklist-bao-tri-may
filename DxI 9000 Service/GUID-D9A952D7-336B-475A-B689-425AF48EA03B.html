Replacing the Z-axis Belt

This topic includes information about how to replace the Z-axis belt for the reagent pipettor.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

You will need the following tools to replace components in the subsystem.
  • 2mm hex driver

Replacing the Z-axis Belt

Perform this procedure to replace the Z-axis belt for the reagent pipettor.
Note: You must turn off the instrument to prevent damaging the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Open the front cover panels and the rear panels.
  3. Gently pull the reagent pipettor assembly to the front of the instrument.
  4. Use a 2mm hex driver to loosen the four screws that attach the Z-axis belt tensioner to the Z-axis frame.
    Note: Do not remove the screws.
  5. Press the Z-axis belt inward to free it from the transducer bracket.
  6. Press and hold the belt tensioner upward to release the belt's tension.
  7. Remove the belt from the tensioner pulley and the Z-axis motor pulley. Discard the belt.
  8. Install the new belt onto the motor pulley.
  9. Press and hold the belt tensioner upward to install the belt onto the tensioner pulley.
  10. Release the belt tensioner and allow it to auto-tension the belt.
  11. Tighten the bottom screws to secure the belt tensioner.
  12. Tighten the top screws to secure the belt tensioner.
  13. Slide the belt into the transducer bracket.
  14. Be sure the belt grooves are aligned to the pulleys.
  15. Rotate the belt to verify the belt is aligned and straight.
  16. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.

Alignments

Align the reagent pipettor. See Adjusting and Aligning the Reagent Pipettor for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
