Software 1.15 Common Failure Troubleshooting

A list of the most commonly observed failure modes and resolution steps for system software versions 1.15.

  • Symptom/Error: Description fo the symptom, or, when applicable, the error/event message observed during the failure.
  • Details/Resolution: Details regarding what may have caused the failure mode and how to recover.
Note: Some failures may be the same or similar to common failures observed in prior SW versions.
Symptom/Error Details/Resolution
Passenger missing / Unsuccessful attempt to keep RV feeder chute full / RV starvation RV starvation occurs when the RV feeder is unable to output vessels at a rate the instrument is requesting them, typically caused by low level of RV's remaining in the feeder "hopper".
  1. Ensure the RV feeder is not "overfilled". Overfilling can actually lead to starvation. Best practice is to load only 1 bag of RV's at a time.
    Note: There is a red "fill line" inside the hopper, but better performance may be observed if the hopper is not filled all the way to this level.
  2. Inspect RV supply counts and replenish if needed.
  3. If issues persist with sufficient vessels in the feeder, check RV feeder components and alignments. Clean/replace/align as needed.
  4. If errors are intermittent, perform vessel exerciser to observe vessel movement and attempt to observe the failure live.
PnP Unexpected Vessel State These errors should be treated as missing RV errors.
  1. Review events to understand which PnP/location the errors occur at.
  2. Check PnP alignments.
  3. Inspect PnP collet/ejector for damage/debris and replace/clean as needed.
  4. If errors are intermittent, perform vessel exerciser to observe vessel movement and attempt to observe the failure live.
Tip Detect Failure Aliquot or Precise Sample Pipettor failed to detect a tip after pickup.
  1. Inspect/clean the mandrel and confirm tips are present in the tip tray.
  2. Check pipettor alignment to tip stocker.
  3. If errors are persistent consider re-calibrating the sample pipettor camera.
Destination Slot Occupied Destination slot occupied error when PnP is trying to place a vessel.
  1. Open covers and inspect the affected PnP, remove vessel from PnP and any fallen vessels/debris in the area.
  2. Initialize system (ideally with "consolidation" selected).
  3. Restart system if issues persist.
  4. Check alignments/PnP hall effect sensor operation.
Lumi Dark Count Out of Range Luminometer dark count reading was above acceptable limits (>300 RLU) prior to sample read. May be associated with LDCH flags.
  1. Inspect Lumi area and analytic PnP for signs of splashing, clean as needed.
  2. Perform dark count reads and if values still exceed acceptable limit then consider cleaning luminometer.
  3. Consider luminometer calibration or replacement.
Precision Sample Pipettor Z-Slippage Precision sample pipettor detected slippage during Z movement.
  1. Inspect for debris/obstruction and clear as needed.
  2. Reboot the system and repeat.
  3. Disable motors and manually move the pipettor to check for binding/snagging.
Reagent Pipettor Z-Slippage Reagent pipettor detected slippage during Z movement.
  1. Inspect for debris/obstruction and clear as needed.
  2. Reboot the system and repeat.
  3. Disable motors and manually move the pipettor to check for binding/snagging.
Reagent Storage (RSM) X Slippage Reagent Pack Gripper detected slippage when moving in the X-axis.
  1. Open and inspect for/remove any debris or fallen packs.
  2. Manually move gripper to home position.
  3. Initialize with remap selected.
Sample PnP Z Slippage Sample Pick and Place (PnP) detected slippage during Z movement.
  1. Inspect for debris/obstruction and clear as needed.
  2. Reboot the system and repeat.
  3. Disable motors and manually move the pipettor to check for binding/snagging.
Substrate Probe Slippage Substrate Probe detected slippage during dispense arm movement.
  1. Inspect for debris/obstruction and clear as needed.
  2. Ensure wash wheel cover is fully seated.
  3. Reboot the system and repeat.
  4. Disable motors and manually move the pipettor to check for binding/snagging.
  5. Perform wash arm alignments.
