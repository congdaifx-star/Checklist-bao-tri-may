Replacing the PC

This topic includes information about how to replace the PC.

Important: The instrument must be powered down and disconnected from the power source before performing this procedure. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • Phillips head screwdriver

Removing the PC

Perform this procedure to remove the PC.

  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Open the lower right door to gain access to the console.
  3. Disconnect all cables from the console.
    Note: Make note of the connection locations and orientation for reference during installation.
    • (1) Power Cable
    • (1) DVI Video Cable
    • (6) USB Cables
    • (2) Network/Ethernet Cables
  4. Remove the two screws (3mm driver) securing the console bracket to the chassis and remove the console with bracket attached.

Installing the PC

Perform this procedure to install the PC.

  1. Insert the console into the instrument, ensure the rear of the console bracket slides into the channel of the partition on the chassis.
  2. Secure the console bracket with two screws (3mm driver).
  3. Connect all required cables to the console.
    • (1) Power Cable
    • (1) DVI Video Cable
    • (6) USB Cables
    • (2) Network/Ethernet Cables
  4. Close the lower right door.
  5. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.

Alignments

No alignments are required after replacement of peripheral components, provided that the alignment values were not lost during service (such as in the replacement of a console or hard drive where a current backup of the database was not recovered).

Verification

To verify operation of peripherals after removal/installation, confirm the system initializes successfully and that all monitor touchscreen functions appear to be working.
