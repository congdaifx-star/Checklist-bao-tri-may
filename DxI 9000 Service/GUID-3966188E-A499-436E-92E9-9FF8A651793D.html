Replacing the Tensioner, Motor, Belt, and Idler Pulley

This topic includes information about how to replace the tensioner, motor, belt, and idler pulley for the reagent shuttles.
Note: Depending on the component you are replacing, not all steps may be required. All four reagent shuttle assemblies are identical in construction, but depending on the carriage, the cable connectors vary to prevent incorrect connections to the dMCC.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Tensioner, Motor, Belt, and Idler Pulley

Perform this procedure to remove the reagent shuttle tensioner, motor, belt, and/or idler pulley.

  1. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables to the motor and encoder.
  2. Loosen the two tensioner assembly screws (3mm driver) and push the tensioner inward to relieve tension.
  3. Remove the belt from the motor pulley.
  4. Fully remove the two screws (3mm driver) securing the tensioner assembly to the carriage frame and remove the tensioner and motor assembly.
    Caution: The tensioner spring will no longer be held in place when the tensioner is removed from the carriage frame, ensure the spring is not lost.
  5. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor to the tensioner assembly and remove the motor.
  6. Remove the belt from the carriage belt clip and idler pulley and remove the belt.
  7. Remove the E-clip retaining the idler pulley and remove the idler pulley from the carriage base.

Installing the Tensioner, Motor, Belt, and Idler Pulley

Perform this procedure to install the reagent shuttle tensioner, motor, belt, and/or idler pulley.

  1. Install the idler pulley onto the shaft on the carriage base and secure with E-clip.
  2. Install the belt around the idler pulley and insert into the carriage belt clip.
  3. Install the motor onto the tensioner and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  4. Insert the tensioner spring into the carriage frame and install the tensioner onto the carriage frame, installing the two screws (3mm driver), but do not completely tighten them.
  5. Push the tensioner and motor assembly inward and install the belt onto the motor pulley.
  6. Tighten the two tensioner screws (3mm driver) to secure the tensioner and motor assembly in place.
  7. Connect the motor and encoder cables to the motor and encoder.

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Aligning the Reaction Build Carriage for more information.

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
