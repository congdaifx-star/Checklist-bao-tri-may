Replacing Circuit Boards

This topic includes information about how to replace circuit boards, including printed circuit boards (PCBs) and dMCCs.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm Hex driver(s) for removal of ground screws
  • Pliers to pinch the standoffs if necessary.

Replacing Circuit Boards (PCB's, dMCC's, etc.)

The following procedures describe how to replace specific circuit boards.
Circuit Board Procedure
Bulk Vessel Feeder DMCC Replacing the Bulk Vessel Feeder DMCC
Incubator Wheel Temperature Control Board Replacing the Incubator Wheel Temperature Control Board
Hall Effect Sensor PCB Replacing the Hall Effect Sensor Printed Circuit Board (PCB), Collet, and Ejector
Pick and Place (PnP) dMCC Replacing the Pick and Place (PnP) dMCC and Flex Cable
Pneumatics Interface PCB Replacing the Pneumatics Interface Printed Circuit Board (PCB)
Sample Pipettor dMCC Replacing the Sample Pipettor dMCC and Flex Cable
Sample Wheel dMCC Replacing the Sample Wheel dMCC Board

Otherwise, follow these generic steps when replacing any circuit boards from the instrument

Important: Keep the circuit board in the protective anti-ESD bag until ready for installation.
  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
    Important: Allow several minutes before proceeding to ensure the instrument is fully de-energized.
  2. Disconnect all cables from the board to be removed. Note the location and orientation of each for installation later.
    Note: Use tape and a marker/pen to mark the cables by their connection point on the board if not already marked on the cable insulation. Taking a picture or creating a quick sketch to note location and orientation may also help during installation.
  3. Remove the grounding screw from the ground standoff if present (typically located at one of the corners of the board).
  4. Remove the board from the remaining nylon standoffs and remove it from the instrument.
    Note: It may be necessary to use pliers to pinch the standoffs to remove a board.
  5. Install the new board onto the nylon standoffs and ensure the "click" into place and secure the board.
  6. Install the ground standoff screw if present.
  7. Connect all cables to the board at their designated terminal.
    Note: Ensure that all cables are properly snapped into place and not loose.
  8. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.

Installing Firmware

View firmware versions for installed printed circuit boards (PCBs). Install or update firmware, as needed. See Installing Firmware for more information.

Verification

At minimum to verify operation of electrical components, the system should be able to power on and initialize successfully.
