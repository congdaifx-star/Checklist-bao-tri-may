Replacing the Aliquot Sample Pipettor Subsystem

This topic includes information about how to replace the aliquot sample pipettor subsystem.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

Use the following tools to remove and install the aliquot sample pipettor subsystem:
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)

Removing the Aliquot Sample Pipettor Subsystem

Perform this procedure to remove the aliquot sample pipettor subsystem.

Note: Before you remove or disconnect anything, take note of the cable routing through the J-clips. To prevent damage, you must use the same routing for the cables during installation.
  1. From the front of the instrument, remove the following cables from the dMCC printed circuit board (PCB) (D4)
    Note: J11 and J12 are wired together and also wired to connector J8 at the Sample Pipettor IF PCB.
    • J1
    • J2
    • J4
    • J5
    • J9
    • J11
    • J12
  2. From the front of the instrument, remove the following cables from the Sample Pipettor IF PCB (S1).
    • J3
    • J8 (also wired to connectors J11 and J12 on the dMCC PCB)
  3. From the rear of the instrument, disconnect the Ethernet cable just below the satellite power distribution board, which is adjacent to the precision sample pipettor dMCC PCB.
    Note: To better access to the aliquot sample pipettor Ethernet cable, you may need to temporarily remove the J13 connector at the satellite power distribution board (W3).
  4. Remove all cabling from J-clips and set it on top of the assembly.
  5. Use a 4mm driver to remove the four screws that secure the aliquot sample pipettor subsystem to the gantry and supports.
  6. Remove the subsystem.
    Caution: Remove the assembly slowly and be sure the cabling does not get caught or damaged.

Installing the Aliquot Sample Pipettor Subsystem

Perform this procedure to install the aliquot sample pipettor subsystem.

  1. Install the assembly onto the gantry and supports. Use a 4mm driver to secure it with four screws.
    Caution: Be sure not to stress or pinch cables.
  2. Connect the Ethernet cable to the connector below the satellite power distribution board. Secure the Ethernet cable with respective J-clips.
  3. If you removed it, reconnect the J13 connector at the satellite power distribution board.
  4. Connect the following cables to the sample pipettor IF PCB (S1)
    • J3
    • J8 (also wired to connectors J11 and J12 on the dMCC PCB)
  5. Connect the following cables to the aliquot sample pipettor dMCC (D4)
    Note: J11 and J12 are wired together and also wired to connector J8 at the Sample Pipettor IF PCB.
    • J1
    • J2
    • J4
    • J5
    • J9
    • J11
    • J12
  6. Secure the loose cabling with the J-clips using the same routing that was in place before you removed the subsystem. To prevent damage from moving components, be sure the connectors are not stressed and that the cabling is not stretched or loose.

Alignments

Align the aliquot sample pipettor. See Aligning the Aliquot Sample Pipettor for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
