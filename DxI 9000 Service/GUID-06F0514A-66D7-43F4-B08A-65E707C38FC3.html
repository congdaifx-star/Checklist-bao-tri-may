Replacing the Substrate Loader Barcode Reader Camera

This topic is about how to replace the barcode reader camera for the substrate loader module.
Note: Turn off the DxI 9000 instrument before you disconnect any camera. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Tools

To replace the substrate loader barcode reader camera, you will need a M2.5 hex screwdriver.

To replace the substrate loader barcode reader camera:

  1. Open the right-side cover on the instrument.
  2. Find the barcode reader camera for the substrate loader. The camera is yellow and includes the Cognex brand name.
  3. Remove the camera cable from the white plastic J-clip.
  4. Disconnect the camera cable connector and remove the cable from behind the substrate syringe enclosure.
    • On new instruments, the camera cable will have a USB-C connector. Older instrument cameras have a Serial connector.
  5. Use the M2.5 hex screwdriver to remove the screws holding the camera to the mounting plate.
  6. Remove all film from the clear plastic on the new camera.
  7. With the lens on the new camera facing the substrate platform, use the M2.5 hex screwdriver and the screws to attach the camera to the mounting plate.
  8. Secure the camera cable to the J-clip.
  9. Move the cable behind the substrate syringe enclosure and reconnect the cable.
  10. Configure auto-detection for the camera. See Configuring Auto-Detection for Barcode Reader Cameras for more information.
