Replacing Internal and Drive Components

This topic includes information about how to disassemble the subsystem and replace the following internal and drive components for the sample wheel.

  • "Starfighter"/Tensioner Bracket
  • Home Sensor
  • Drive Belt
  • Thermistor
  • Driven Pulley
  • Drive Assembly
  • Peltiers
  • Bearing Assembly
Note: Removal of the module from the system is required. See Replacing the Sample Storage Wheel Subsystem for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)
  • 5mm hex driver (for M6 screws)
  • 10mm thermistor driver -or- 10mm wrench

Removing Internal and Drive Components

Perform this procedure to remove internal and drive components.

Note: Removal of the module from the system is required. See Replacing the Sample Storage Wheel Subsystem for more information.
  1. Remove the two screws (3mm driver) securing the tensioner assembly to the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket.
  2. Push the motor/tensioner assembly inward to relieve belt tension and remove the belt from the motor pulley.
  3. Remove the motor and tensioner assembly form the module and set aside.
    Caution: Do not misplace the tensioner spring, as once the tensioner is removed there is nothing retaining it in place.
  4. Remove the sample wheel cover (three thumb screws) and top plate/ground triangle (three screws, 3mm driver).
  5. Rotate the sample wheel by hand so that it is not in the home position.
  6. Remove the two screws (3mm driver) securing "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket to the module baseplate.
  7. Remove the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket by lifting the rear of the bracket upwards to un-seat form the locating pins and pull it out of the subsystem.
  8. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the home sensor to the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket and remove the home sensor.
  9. Invert the module and remove the drive belt from the cut-out on the bottom of the subsystem chassis.
    Caution: The subsystem may leak residual fluid when inverted, place an absorbent pad or material beneath the subsystem to absorb any residual fluid.
  10. Remove the thermistor from the underside of the subsystem (10mm thermistor driver, or 10mm wrench).
  11. Remove the three screws (4mm driver) securing the subsystem baseplate to the risers and remove the baseplate.
  12. Loosen the two set screws (2.5mm driver) securing the driven pulley to the driveshaft, and remove the driven pulley.
  13. Remove the three screws (2.5mm driver) securing the flange plate to the pulley and remove the flange plate.
  14. Remove the screw (2.5mm driver) securing the home flag to the pulley and remove the home flag.
  15. Remove the C-clip from the driveshaft on the underside of the of the subsystem, and remove the drive assembly by pulling it through the topside of the subsystem.
  16. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the duct to the bottom of the subsystem and remove the duct assembly.
  17. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) securing the heat sink to the bottom of the subsystem and remove the heat sink.
  18. Remove the foam insulation pad, and remove the peltiers.
  19. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) securing the bearing housing to the bottom of the subsystem, and remove the bearing assembly by lifting it out of the bottom of the subsystem.

Installing Internal and Drive Components

Perform this procedure to install internal and drive components.

  1. Install the bearing assembly into the bottom of the subsystem and secure the housing with three screws (3mm driver).
  2. Install the peltiers and place the insulation pad on top of them.
    Note: DO NOT use thermal grease when installing the peltiers, as it is not required.
  3. Install the heat sink and secure with four screws (3mm driver).
  4. Install the duct assembly and secure with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  5. Install the drive assembly from the top of the subsystem and attach the C-clip to the driveshaft at the bottom of the subsystem.
  6. Attach the home flag to the driven pulley and secure with one screw (2.5mm driver).
  7. Attach the flange plate to the driven pullet and secure with three screws (2.5mm driver).
  8. Install the driven pulley onto the driveshaft and secure with the two set screws (2.5mm driver).
    Caution: Ensure that the pulley is aligned with the indicator marks on the driveshaft before tightening the screws.
  9. Attach the baseplate to the risers and secure with three screws (4mm driver).
  10. Install the thermistor into the underside of the subsystem (10mm thermistor driver, or 10mm wrench).
    Note: DO NOT use thermal grease when installing the thermistor, as it is not required.
  11. Install the drive belt onto the driven pulley.
  12. Install the home sensor onto the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket and secure with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  13. Install the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket by first inserting the front into the subsystem until it contacts the locating pin, and then placing the rear of the bracket onto the rear locating pins.
    Note: Ensure the sample wheel is not at home position when installing the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket or the home sensor and bracket may not properly fit.
  14. Secure the "Starfighter"/Tensioner bracket to the subsystem baseplate with two screws (3mm driver).
  15. Install the top plate/ground triangle (three screws, 3mm driver), and the sample wheel top cover (three thumb screws).
  16. Install the motor and tensioner assembly, and push the motor inward to relieve tension. Install the belt over the motor pulley and verify smooth movement of the belt.
  17. Let the tensioner spring set the belt tension and secure with the two tensioner screws (3mm driver).
    Caution: Do not apply force to the tensioner when securing it in place.

Alignments

Perform necessary alignments and/or adjustments for affected components and subsystems.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
