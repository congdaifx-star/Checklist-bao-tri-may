Replacing the Bulk Vessel Feeder DMCC

This topic includes information about how to replace the bulk vessel feeder DMCC (circuit board).

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2mm hex driver (for M2.5 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Bulk Vessel Feeder DMCC

Perform this procedure to remove the bulk vessel feeder DMCC (circuit board).

  1. Remove all cables from their connections at the DMCC. Note all locations and orientation for connecting later.
  2. Remove the ground screw (2.5mm driver) securing the DMCC to the ground standoff/post.
  3. Remove the DMCC from the remaining 5 nylon standoffs (push the barb in and pull the board off to remove).

Installing the Bulk Vessel Feeder DMCC

Perform this procedure to install the bulk vessel feeder DMCC (circuit board).

  1. Install the DMCC onto the five nylon standoffs. The DMCC should "lock" into place on the standoff barbs.
  2. Install the ground screw (2.5mm driver) to secure the DMCC to the ground standoff/post (bottom corer closest to the front of the instrument).
  3. Connect all cables to the DMCC, be careful to ensure correct placement and orientation.

Installing Firmware

View firmware versions for installed printed circuit boards (PCBs). Install or update firmware, as needed. See Installing Firmware for more information.

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
