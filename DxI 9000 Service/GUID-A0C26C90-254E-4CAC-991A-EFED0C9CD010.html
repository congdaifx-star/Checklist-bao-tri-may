Troubleshooting Wash Wheel Temperature Errors

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot wash wheel temperature errors.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Symptoms

In the Console App the System Events page displays one of the following errors:
Error Indication Possible Cause
WashWheelTemperatureRead Failure The wash wheel temperature is outside the desired range.
  • Faulty or disconnected thermistor
  • Faulty or disconnected temperature control board (T3)
WashWheelFluidicsTemperature Failure The substrate, QS, D1, or D2 probe temperature is outside the desired range.
  • Disconnected cable between the probe assembly and the temperature control board (T2)
  • Faulty or disconnected temperature control board (T2)
  • Faulty probe assembly

Troubleshooting the WashWheelTemperatureRead Failure Error

Perform this procedure to troubleshoot the WashWheelTemperatureRead Failure error.

  1. Check to be sure the wash wheel thermistor is connected to the extension cable and temperature control board.
  2. Check to be sure the temperature control board (T3) is connected to USB hub 5.
  3. Replace the wash wheel thermistor.
  4. Replace the temperature control board (T3).

Troubleshooting the WashWheelFluidicsTemperature Failure Error

Perform this procedure to troubleshoot the WashWheelFluidicsTemperature Failure error.

  1. Check to be sure the probe assembly is connected to the extension cable and temperature control board (T2).
  2. Check to be sure the temperature control board (T2) is connected to USB hub 1.
  3. Replace the temperature control board (T2).
  4. Replace the impacted probe assembly.
