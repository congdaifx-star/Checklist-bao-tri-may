Camera System Functions Overview

This topic discusses how the camera systems operate in the DxI 9000 instrument.

The DxI 9000 instrument uses cameras to perform multiple functions that include reading barcodes, detecting fluids, detecting disposal tips, detecting tubes and racks, and detecting the presence or absence of sample container caps.

The instrument uses the following types of cameras for different functions in various hardware modules.
Hardware module Camera Type Function More information
Reagent Storage Module (RSM) Cognex Read reagent pack barcodes Operating Principles for the Reagent Storage Module (RSM)
Aliquot Sample Pipettor ADV102
  • Scan the sample pipettor for disposable tips
  • Detect fluids
  • Sample Pipettor Camera Operations
  • Operating Principles for the Sample Pipettors
Precision Sample Pipettor ADV102
  • Scan the sample pipettor for disposable tips
  • Detect fluids
  • Sample Pipettor Camera Operations
  • Operating Principles for the Sample Pipettors
Sample Presentation Unit (SPU) Cognex
  • Read sample rack barcodes
  • Read sample container barcodes
  • Identify sample container types
  • Detect the presence or absence of sample container caps
  • Sample Presentation Unit (SPU) Camera Operations
  • Operating Principles for the Sample Presentation Unit (SPU)
Substrate Loader Cognex Read substrate bottle barcodes Operating Principles for the Substrate Module
Wash Wheel Cognex Capture images of a reaction vessel before and after substrate is dispensed, to detect fluids.
  • Wash Wheel Camera Operations
  • Operating Principles for the Wash Wheel
