Replacing the Cooling Units

This topic includes information about how to replace the cooling units for the reagent storage module (RSM).

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Preparing the Instrument for Replacing Components

  1. Remove all reagent packs from the instrument.
  2. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  3. Open the main covers.

Removing the Cooling Units

Perform this procedure to remove the cooling units.
Note: The RSM Cooling Units can be replaced individually.
  1. Remove the Reagent Storage Module (RSM) to reveal the RSM Cooling Units.

    For instructions on removing the RSM, see Replacing the Reagent Storage Module (RSM).

  2. Disconnect the QTY (3) each Peltier and Thermistor cables.
  3. Unscrew the QTY (6) (3mm hex driver) screw securing the cooling unit to the chassis.
  4. Pull the cooling unit straight out.
  5. Turn the cooling unit assembly over, and unscrew the QTY (4) 2.5mm hex drive screws securing the back panel to the unit, (QTY 2 on each end).
  6. Remove the back panel
  7. Turn the cooling unit assembly over again so the blades are pointing up.
  8. For each cooling unit, unscrew the QTY (4) 2.5mm hex drive screws that secure the unit to the assembly.
    Note: Support the cooling unit from underneath to ensure it doesn't come crashing down to the work surface.
  9. Feed the cooling unit's cables through the large cooler channel holes.
  10. Remove the unit by lifting the frame straight up and away from the unit.

Installing the Cooling Units

Perform this procedure to install the cooling units.
Note: The RSM Cooling Units can be replaced individually.
  1. For each cooling unit, insert the unit from underneath the frame so the blades are pointing up.
    Note: Be sure the cables are protruding from behind the frame.
  2. Screw the QTY (4) 2.5mm hex drive screws into the frame that secures the unit to the assembly.
    Note: Support the cooling unit from underneath to align the screw holes and to ensure the unit doesn't come crashing down to the work surface.
  3. Feed the cooling unit's cables through the large cooler channel holes.
  4. Turn the cooling unit assembly over, and attach the back plate by screwing the QTY (4) 2.5mm hex drive screws securing the back panel to the unit, (QTY 2 on each end).
  5. Screw the QTY (6) (3mm hex driver) screw to secure the cooling unit to the chassis.
  6. Resconnect the QTY (3) each Peltier and Thermistor cables.

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Aligning the Reagent Loader Tray for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
