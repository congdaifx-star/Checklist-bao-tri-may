Replacing Orienter and Dispense Chute Components

This topic includes information about how to replace orienter and dispense chute components for the bulk vessel feeder, including the orienter cover and dispense chute status sensor.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2mm hex driver (for M2.5 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing Orienter and Dispense Chute Components

Perform this procedure to remove orienter and dispense chute components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the orienter cover to the top of the orienter assembly, and remove the orienter cover.
  2. Remove the dispense chute by lifting up on the bottom of the chute assembly and moving it away from the vessel dispenser block.
  3. Remove the two screws (2mm driver) securing the nozzle assembly at the bottom of the orienter and remove the nozzle assembly.
  4. Disconnect the sensor cable from the sensor.
  5. Remove the screw (2.5mm driver) securing the sensor to the nozzle assembly and remove the dispense chute status sensor.

Installing Orienter and Dispense Chute Components

Perform this procedure to install orienter and dispense chute components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the dispense chute status sensor onto the nozzle assembly and secure with the screw (2.5mm driver).
  2. Connect the sensor cable to the sensor.
    Note: Before proceeding, test the sensor status at this time when possible (compare to reference LED on the Bulkfeeder DMCC).
  3. Attach the nozzle assembly at the bottom of the orienter and secure with the two screws (2mm driver).
  4. Install the dispense chute by inserting the top into the nozzle assembly first, then lifting from the bottom and locking the chute into the vessel dispenser block.
  5. Install the orienter cover and secure with the two screws (2.5mm driver).

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
