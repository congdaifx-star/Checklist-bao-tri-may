Turning Off the Instrument

This topic includes information about how to turn off the DxI 9000 instrument.
  1. Be sure the system is not actively processing samples.
  2. In the Console App go to MENU > Tools > Diagnostics > Nonintrusive Diagnostics > Safe Shutdown to open the Safe Shutdown page.
  3. Select Machine Control Shutdown.
  4. Go to Start > Power > Shut down
  5. After the PC has shut down, press the standby switch on the instrument into the OFF position.

    1 Standby switch
    2 Standby switch ON position
    3 Standby switch OFF position
    4 PC power button

    1 Standby switch
    2 Standby switch ON position
    3 Standby switch OFF position

  6. Turn off the main breaker at the rear of the instrument.

  7. Disconnect the power cable from its power source.
