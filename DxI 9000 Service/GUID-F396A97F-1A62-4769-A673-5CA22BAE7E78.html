Replacing the Z-axis Motor and Encoder, Belt, and Tensioner

This topic includes information about how to replace the Z-axis motor and encoder, belt, and tensioner for the aliquot and precision sample pipettors.

Note: The aliquot and precision sample pipettor subsystems are similar. They differ primarily in the size and orientation of the gantry and in range of motion. Any important details for a specific subsystem will be identified in the procedure.
Tip: To more easily access certain components, you may need to remove the subsystem. See the following topics for more information:
  • Replacing the Aliquot Sample Pipettor Subsystem
  • Replacing the Precision Sample Pipettor Subsystem

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for this procedure:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Replacing the Z-axis Motor and Encoder, Belt, and Tensioner

Perform this procedure to replace the z-axis motor and encoder (one piece), belt, and belt tensioner assembly.

1 Belt
2 Motor and Encoder Connectors
3 Gantry
4 Tensioner Assembly
5 Tensioner Pulley

  1. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables from the assembly.
  2. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) that secure the tensioner assembly to the gantry and remove the tensioner assembly.

    1 Tensioner screw and spring
    2 QTY 4 (2.5 mm) ( M3) screws

  3. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) that secure the motor and encoder assembly to the z-carriage.
  4. Remove the belt from the motor pulley, and remove the motor and encoder assembly.
  5. Remove the belt from the z-carriage belt clip and tensioner pulley, and remove the belt.
  6. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables from the assembly.
  7. Loosen the two M3 screws that affix the belt clamp.

    1 Belt Clamp

  8. Remove the belt clamp form the Z axis belt.

  9. Loosen the four M3 screws that affix the tensioner block onto the base.

    Note: Do not completely remove the M3 screws.

    1 M3 screws

  10. Tighten the screw and compress the spring until the pulley loosens so that you can remove the belt.

    1 Tensioner screw

  11. Remove the z-axis belt.

  12. Hook the z-axis belt at both the fixed and driven pulleys.

  13. Loosen the M3x30 screw to apply the spring force to the belt to increase tension.

  14. Tighten the four M3x8 screws.

  15. Tighten the M3x30 screw.
