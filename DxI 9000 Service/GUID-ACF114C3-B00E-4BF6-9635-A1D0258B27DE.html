Replacing Supply Shuttle Drive Components

This topic includes information about how to replace drive components for the supply shuttle, including the idler pulley/tensioner, motor, and drive belt.

Note: Before you complete these procedures you must turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Drive Components (Idler Pulley/Tensioner, Motor, and Drive Belt)

Perform this procedure to remove the supply shuttle drive components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Loosen the two screws (3mm driver) securing the tensioner in place.
  2. Push the tensioner inward to relieve tension on the belt and remove the belt from the idler pulley.
  3. If the tensioner/idler pulley requires replacement, remove the two screws entirely and remove the tensioner/idler pulley from the supply shuttle assembly.
  4. Remove the belt from the motor pulley and supply shuttle, and remove the belt.
    Note: It may be necessary to remove the tensioner to get the belt off.
  5. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables from the motor and encoder.
  6. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor mounting plate to the supply shuttle assembly and remove the mounting plate and motor assembly.
  7. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor to the mounting block, and remove the motor.

Intalling the Drive Components (Idler Pulley/Tensioner, Motor, and Drive Belt)

Perform this procedure to install the drive components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the motor into the motor mounting bracket and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  2. Install the motor and mounting bracket assembly onto the supply shuttle assembly and secure the mounting bracket with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  3. Connect the motor and encoder cables to the motor and encoder.
  4. Install the belt over the motor pulley and insert into the supply shuttle.
    Note: It may be necessary to disconnect the motor encoder, the motor sensor, and home sensor cables to take the belt off and put it back on.
  5. Install the tensioner/idler pulley and install the belt onto the idler pulley, allowing the springs of the tensioner to automatically set the tension for the belt.
  6. Secure the tensioner assembly in place with two screws (3mm driver).

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
