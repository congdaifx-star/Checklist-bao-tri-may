Replacing the Heater, Home Sensor, and Drive Components

This topic includes information about how to replace the heater, home sensor, and drive components for the incubator wheel.

Important: You must turn off and unplug the instrument before you perform this procedure. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm "L" shaped hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Heater, Home Sensor, and Drive Components (Belt, Common Drive)

Perform this procedure to remove the heater, home sensor, and drive component(s). Not all steps may be necessary.
Note: Removal of the module from the system is required. See Replacing the Incubator Wheel Subsystem for more information.
  1. Remove the top cover, ground triangle, and vessel storage plate. See Replacing the Cover, Vessel Storage Plate, and Ground Contact Plate for more information.
  2. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) from the underside of the incubation wheel assembly, and remove the heater (bottom cover) assembly, including the attached home sensor.
  3. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the home sensor to the heater assembly and remove the home sensor.
  4. Loosen the four tensioner screws (3mm driver), but do not remove them.
  5. Push the tensioner assembly, including motor, inward to relieve belt tension.
  6. Tighten at least one of the four tensioner screws to hold the tensioner in place.
  7. Remove the belt from the motor and driver pulley, and remove from the assembly.
  8. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) securing the isolator block to the common wheel drive assembly.
  9. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) securing the common wheel drive and remove the common wheel drive assembly.

Installing the Heater, Home Sensor, and Drive Components (Belt, Common Drive)

Perform this procedure to install the heater, home sensor, and drive component(s). Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the common wheel drive assembly and secure with three screws (3mm driver).
  2. Install the isolator block onto the common wheel drive assembly and secure with three screws (3mm driver).
  3. Install the belt onto the driven pulley first, and then onto the motor pulley (tension must be relieved).
  4. Loosen the four screws (3mm driver) that secure the tensioner in place, and let the tensioner set the belt tension.
  5. Tighten the four screws to secure the tensioner in place.
  6. Install the home sensor onto the heater assembly (bottom cover) and secure with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  7. Install the heater assembly (bottom cover) and secure with three screws (3mm driver).
  8. Install the vessel storage plate, ground triangle, and top cover (see steps above).

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Aligning the Incubator Wheel for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
