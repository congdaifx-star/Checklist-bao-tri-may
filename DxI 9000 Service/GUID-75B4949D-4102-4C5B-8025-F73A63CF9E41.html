Replacing the Analytic Pick and Place (PnP) Subsystem

This topic includes information about how to replace the analytic pick and place (PnP) subsystem.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for removal/installation of the Analytic Pick and Place (PnP) subsystem:
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the Subsystem

Follow these steps to remove the Analytic PnP subsystem.
  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Open the front right cover and rear bi-fold covers.
  3. Disconnect the main power cable (J6) and USB cable (J7) from the analytic PnP dMCC.
  4. Disconnect the pneumatic supply line from the ejector valve fitting.
  5. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) securing the PnP gantry to the tower (from the front below the dMCC), and remove the analytic PnP subsystem/gantry.

Installing the Subsystem

Follow these steps to install the Analytic PnP subsystem.
  1. Install the analytic PnP subsystem/gantry onto the tower (the gantry is pinned into place to maintain alignment and help hold it when securing it with the screws).
  2. Secure the gantry to the tower using four screws (3mm driver).
  3. Connect the pneumatic supply line to the ejector valve fitting.
  4. Connect the main power cable (J6) and USB cable (J7) to the analytic PnP dMCC.

Alignments

Check all alignments of the analytic PnP to interacting subsystems and adjust as needed. See Adjusting and Aligning the Analytic Pick and Place (PnP) for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
