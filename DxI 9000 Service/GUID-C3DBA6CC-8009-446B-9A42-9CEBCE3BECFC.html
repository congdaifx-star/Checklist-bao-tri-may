Replacing the Sample Wheel dMCC Board

This topic includes information about how to replace the dMCC board for the sample wheel.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)
  • 5mm hex driver (for M6 screws)
  • 10mm thermistor driver -or- 10mm wrench

Removing the dMCC Board

Perform this procedure to remove the dMCC board.

  1. Remove the dMCC cover (pull on the tabs securing the cover and pull the cover off of the board).
  2. Remove all cables from the dMCC terminals.
    Caution: Note the orientation and location of the cables for reinstallation.
  3. Remove the ground screw (2.5mm driver) from the ground standoff.
  4. Pinch together the five remaining standoffs and pull the board off of them to remove the board.

Installing the dMCC Board

Perform this procedure to install the dMCC board.

  1. Place the board onto the five standoffs until they snap into place, securing the board.
  2. Secure the board to the ground standoff using the ground standoff screw (2.5mm driver).
  3. Connect the cables to dMCC terminals.
  4. Place the dMCC cover on top of the board and secure it into place so that the tabs snap into position.

Installing Firmware

View firmware versions for installed printed circuit boards (PCBs). Install or update firmware, as needed. See Installing Firmware for more information.

Alignments

Perform necessary alignments and/or adjustments for affected components and subsystems.

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
