Replacing the Reagent Shuttle Subsystems

This topic includes information about how to replace the reagent shuttle subsystems.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for removal/installation of any of the four the Reagent Shuttle subsystems:
  • 4mm driver (for M5 screws)

Removing the subsystem

Follow these steps to remove the reagent shuttle subsystem.
  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Open the rear bi-fold covers.
  3. Disconnect the following cables from the reagent shuttle subsystem to be removed:
    • Motor cable
    • Encoder cable
    • Home sensor cable
  4. Disconnect the flex cable from the temperature control printed circuit board (PCB) for the corresponding reagent shuttle and remove the cable from any J-clips not attached to the reagent shuttle.
    • Reagent Shuttle 1 flex cable connects to J1 (beige connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 2 flex cable connects to J2 (blue connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 3 flex cable connects to J3 (red connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 4 flex cable connects to J4 (yellow connector)
  5. Remove the two screws (4mm driver) securing the reagent shuttle base to the instrument chassis.
  6. Lift the reagent shuttle subsystem off of the locating pins and remove from the rear of the instrument.

Installing the subsystem

Follow these steps to install the reagent shuttle subsystem.
  1. Place the reagent shuttle subsystem into the instrument from the rear and ensure it seats fully on the locating pins.
  2. Secure the subsystem with two screws (4mm driver).
  3. Route the flex cable through any J-clips and connect to the temperature control PCB.
    • Reagent Shuttle 1 flex cable connects to J1 (beige connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 2 flex cable connects to J2 (blue connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 3 flex cable connects to J3 (red connector)
    • Reagent Shuttle 4 flex cable connects to J4 (yellow connector)
  4. Connect the following cables to the reagent shuttle subsystem:
    • Motor cable
    • Encoder cable
    • Home sensor cable
  5. Before powering on the instrument, manually move the carriage and verify that no cables are obstructing motion and that the carriage moves smoothly to both ends of the rail.

Alignments

Check all alignments of the reagent shuttle to interacting subsystems and adjust as needed:
  • Adjusting and Aligning the Sample Pick and Place (PnP)
  • Aligning the Precision Sample Pipettor
  • Adjusting and Aligning the Reagent Pipettor
  • Adjusting and Aligning the Incubate Pick and Place (PnP)

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
