Replacing Dispenser and Pneumatic Components

This topic includes information about how to replace dispenser and pneumatic components for the bulk vessel feeder, including the dispense cylinder and sensor and pneumatic manifold and valve.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2mm hex driver (for M2.5 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing Dispenser and Pneumatic Components

Perform this procedure to remove dispenser and pneumatic components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Purge the pneumatic pressure from the system to ease the removal of tubing/fittings.
  2. Disconnect the dispense cylinder sensor cable.
  3. Disconnect the pneumatic tubing from the dispense cylinder (2 fittings).
    Note: Note where each tube goes so as to be able to reconnect the tubing properly.
  4. Retract the dispense cylinder piston by hand.
  5. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the bracket to the dispense block and remove the dispense cylinder & sensor.
    Note: Make note of the orientation of the cylinder assembly during removal - it must be installed in the same orientation.
    Note: Make note of the clearance gaps at the back where the dispenser protrudes and at the front where the end meets the valve assembly, during installation replicate these gaps as best as possible.
  6. Remove the valve cable from the valve terminal.
  7. Remove the pneumatic tubing from the manifold (3 fittings).
  8. Remove the two screws (1.5mm driver) and remove the pneumatic manifold & valve assembly.

Installing Dispenser and Pneumatic Components

Perform this procedure to install dispenser and pneumatic components. Not all steps may be necessary.

  1. Install the pneumatic manifold & valve assembly and secure with the two screws (1.5mm driver).
  2. Connect the pneumatic tubing to the manifold (3 fittings).
  3. Connect the valve cable to the valve terminal.
  4. Install the dispense cylinder & sensor and secure the bracket to the dispense block with the two screws (2.5mm driver).
    Note: Ensure orientation is the same as when the cylinder wasremoved.
  5. Connect the pneumatic tubing to the dispense cylinder (2 fittings).
  6. Connect the dispense cylinder sensor cable.

Alignments

Perform alignments. See Adjusting and Aligning the Bulk Vessel Feeder for more information.

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
