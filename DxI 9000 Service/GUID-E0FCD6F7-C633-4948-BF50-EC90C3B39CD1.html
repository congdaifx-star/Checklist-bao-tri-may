Utilizing House Air

This topic includes information about utilizing house air for pneumatics lines.

This procedure is for installations where the location has a house air supply that it prefers to use for the instrument's pneumatics lines.
Important: The instrument can accommodate a maximum input pressure of 150 psi from an external source.
Note: Beckman Coulter does not have a dedicated House Air Kit.

Requirements and Specifications

Follow these requirements and specifications for this procedure:

Parameter Requirement / Specification
Location Customer air connection must be within 50 feet of the DxI 9000
Pressure 50 psi (minimum)
Dedicated Air to DxI 9000 - Flow Rate Minimum of 3.2 liters per minute at 50 psi (0.113 cfm)
Shared Air - DxI 9000 and LAS on same air source - Flow Rate Minimum of 85 liters per minute at 90 psi (3.0 cfm)
Mechanical Interface The air source fitting must accept or be adaptable to a 6 mm OD pneumatic tubing

Required Materials

To perform this procedure, the customer must provide the following items:
  • A sufficient amount of 6 mm air line.

Utilizing House Air

  1. Measure and cut a length of tubing to comfortably reach the rear of the instrument from the house air supply.
  2. Attach the air line to the air inlet connection at the left rear of the instrument.
  3. Turn on the pneumatic pressure to the instrument.
  4. Remove the right rear panel of the instrument to gain access to the pneumatic components.
  5. With the house air supply connected and turned on, use the knob on the primary regulator to adjust the primary regulator to 35 PSI (if needed).
  6. Reinstall the right rear panel of the instrument.
  7. Initialize the instrument (with clean out) to confirm consistent pressure.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
