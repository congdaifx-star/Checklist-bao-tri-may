Troubleshooting SYS Flags

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot SYS flags.

When to Perform

A SYS flag is the reporting of a hardware or timing failure error that occurred during a scheduled event for a reaction vessel (RV). The flag may appear on results. The expected error rate during normal operation is five percent or less and the system is capable of recovering on its own.

However, you may notice in results or on the System Events page in the Console App that there is an excessive number of SYS flags related to a subsystem or specific device. In that event, look up the corresponding event message and code in the table below for how to begin troubleshooting the issue.

418020101 Precision Pipettor Gantry Z Slip The z-axis of the precision sample pipettor is out of tolerance at these positions: Motor {0}, Encoder {1}. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Inspect the pipettor tip (damage, bent, tip stuck).

Verify alignment.

Check collision sensor function.

Replace motor.

Replace mcc board.

418040001 PreciseTipDiscardFailure The precision sample pipettor did not discard the used disposable tip. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Inspect mandrel and clean.

Inspect tip discard chute.

Adjust tip waste position sensor and tip waste chute.

418040002 PreciseTipLoadFailure The precision sample pipettor did not load a new disposable tip. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Be sure tip supply has tips.

Check alignment of precision sample pipettor to tip supply.

Perform collision sensor check.

419010001 Wash Buffer Reservoir Failure The wash buffer reservoir is not working. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Inspect the reservoir float sensors for damage (sensors, cable, connector).

Inspect the reservoir container for leaks or damages.

Perform float sensors checks.

419010002 Wash Buffer Intermediate Waste Tank Failure The intermediate liquid waste tank is not working. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Check the functionality of the liquid waste tank float sensor.

Check the functionality of the liquid waste transfer pump.

Check the waste fluid level in the waste tank.

419010102 Wash Buffer Reservoir Critically Low The level of wash buffer in the reservoir is too low. The analyzer cannot begin new tests. Critical

Be sure the wash buffer containers in the wash buffer drawer have wash buffer in them.

Check the operation of the wash buffer reservoir float sensors and associated circuitry.

Check the operation of the wash buffer transfer pump located behind the wash buffer container drawer.

419010402 Wash Buffer Reservoir Critically High The level of wash buffer in the reservoir is too high. The analyzer cannot begin new tests. Critical

Remove fluid from the wash buffer reservoir by priming the reagent pipettors or by manually removing fluid from the wash buffer reservoir.

Verify the operation of the wash buffer reservoir float sensor and associated circuitry.

420020101 Low Res Pump Failure Dispense The low resolution pump for this pipettor is not working: {0} The throughput of the analyzer is reduced. Critical

Check for obstructions in the fluidics line (kinked lines, damaged fittings).

Check the home sensor and motor function and their connections.

Replace the valves.

420020201 Low Res Pump Failure Home The low resolution pump for this pipettor is not working: {0} The throughput of the analyzer is reduced. Critical

Inspect fittings and tubing in the impacted circuit.

Clean and lubricate the low resolution syringe pump.

Replace the syringe.

420030101 Hi Res Pump Failure Dispense The high resolution pump for this pipettor is not working: {0} The throughput of the analyzer is reduced. Critical

If after PM, be sure valves are not swapped.

Prime and observe high res movement.

Replace high res pump.

Replace valve.

420030201 Hi Res Pump Failure Homing The high resolution pump for this pipettor is not working: {0} The throughput of the analyzer is reduced. Critical

If after PM, be sure valves are not swapped.

Prime and observe high res movement.

Replace high res pump.

Replace valve.

421000201 Solid Waste Bin Sensor Broken The waste container sensors are not working. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Check bin flag and optical sensor interaction (e.g. verify no rubbing and make sure the flag reaches sensor).

In the Machine Control App go to GeneralSolidWaste > GeneralSolidWaste.Assembly > GeneralSolidWaste.BinSensor to check the operation of the optical sensor by opening and closing the solid waste drawer with the bin and executing GetState in the Action tab. If the state doesn't change, replace the optical sensor.

421000401 Solid Waste Bag Detection Sensor Broken The waste bag sensor is not working. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Check reflector is clean and unobstructed.

In the Machine Control App go to GeneralSolidWaste > GeneralSolidWaste.Assembly > GeneralSolidWaste.BagSensor to check the operation of the optical sensor by opening and closing the solid waste drawer with the bin and executing GetState in the Action tab. If the state doesn't change, replace the optical sensor.

421010001 Tip Tray Waste Bin Not Set Correctly The tip tray waste bin is not detected. The analyzer cannot begin new tests. Warning

Remove and replace the bin.

Ensure the bin is not bent.

Replace the sensor.

422010301 LiquidWasteCriticalHigh The waste level in the internal waste tank is critically high. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Inspect the waste tank float switch.

Inspect the tubing from the waste tank to the waste pump to the external drain.

Turn off the instrument and disconnect and reconnect the waste pump connector at the fluidics I/F board.

Replace the float switch / waste pump / fluidics I/F board.

422010401 Liquid Waste Discharge Pump Initialization Timeout The liquid waste discharge pump has been running too long during initialization. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Check the level of the intermediate waste tank (it might be very full) and check the connections.

Verify the function of the liquid waste reservoir float sensor and level status. Be sure the float sensor isn't stuck.

Check the tubing for kinks and blockage, especially the external liquid waste line at the back of the instrument.

Disconnect and reconnect the connection to the liquid waste pump.

422010402 Liquid Waste Discharge Pump Running Timeout The liquid waste discharge pump has been running too long when the system is in a running state. The analyzer cannot process samples. Critical

Inspect the waste tank float switch.

Inspect the tubing from the waste tank to the waste pump to the external drain.

Turn off the instrument and reseat the waste pump connector at the fluidics I/F board.
