Troubleshooting the Reagent Storage Module (RSM)

This topic includes information about how to resolve the potential cause of event codes and behaviors related to the reagent storage module (RSM), which includes the following devices:

  • Reagent gripper (movement or open/close errors)
  • Reagent temperature
  • Reagent barcode reader (BCR)
  • Reagent load tray
  • Waste door
Note: Servicing the RSM will require an extended length of time. Therefore, you must unload reagent packs and place them in an external refrigerator to prevent compromising the stability of the reagent packs.
Note: You will be asked to disable the interlock system, which means motors within the Instrument will remain enabled. Devices may move and may potentially cause injury.

Prerequisites

  1. Be sure the following modifications (MODs) have been completed before troubleshooting the RSM.
    • Cat-1 MOD 13059 - TSI RSI PCB Board - Replace
    • Cat -1 MOD 13033 - DXI 9000 USB 2.0 Cables - Replace
    • Cat-1 MOD 13053 - DXI 9000 Reagent Storage Module Tubing - Replace
  2. Log in using the BeckmanAdmin account. See Signing In to Use the BeckmanAdmin Account for more information.
  3. Disable the Interlock System that controls the Instrument cover locks. See Enabling and Disabling the Interlock System for Cover Switch Sensors for more information.
  4. In the Console App go to Menu > Tools > Diagnostics to open the Diagnostics tab. Select Service Diagnostics, select Pause, and select Start Session to change the system status to BLUE (Maintenance).

Possible Interfering Subsystems to Check First

Issues to check first Causes Possible Resolutions
Pneumatics Ensure Pneumatic pressure did not drop below minimum levels.
  • If Instrument is in a non-running state (RED) pneumatic monitoring is turned off, ensure system pneumatic compressor or house air is on.
  • Check compressor or check valve on compressor.
    • If the pressure increases to 36 PSI or more, and after releasing pressure the compressor fails to turn back on, replace the check valve.
    • If the pressure does not increase to 32-35 PSI, check for leaks or replace the compressor.
RSM gripper timing calibration Calibration Timing was not performed or conditions have changed.

After alignments have changed, you must calibrate gripper timing. The calibration determines how far away from pipetting nests specific reagent packs need to be, so that the system can pick and place packs in a timely manner for pipetting reagents. You must allow for approximately 20 minutes to complete the calibration. See Calibrating Gripper Timing for more information.

System was in a non-Running state longer than 3 days. RSI board on RSM will maintain encoder values for 3 days. Powered down for longer than 3 days and stored encoder values may not be available to home gripper properly.
  • Open RSM and manually move gripper to home position in the lower left of the RSM then use machine control to Home Gripper.
  • Ensuring the gripper moves in and out of the home position and opens and closes.
Gripper is not moving to correct location or getting movement errors. It is possible cache files are corrupted. For repeated issues with reagent packs, and only as a last resort, delete the data files. See Deleting the Instrument's Data Files for more information.

Gripper Motion or Homing Errors

Event Log Message or Behavior Causes Possible Resolutions

RSM Gripper X Axis Slip

RSM Gripper Z Axis Slip

RSM Gripper X Axis Movement Failure

RSM Gripper Z Axis Movement Failure

RSM Gripper X Axis Home Failure

RSM Gripper Z Axis Home Failure

Damaged, broken, dirty, or binding parts
  • Home sensor failure
  • X or Z encoder or encoder cable failure
  • Gripper stuck in a nest position with a reagent pack or bad grip
  • pipetting nest slides are dirty
  • Upper and/or lower shafts are corroded or need lubrication
  • Upper guide rod bushing (red) swelled or dirty
  • Loose X or Z belt
  • X or Z idler tensioner assembly damaged
  • Interference from RSI PCB standoff screw
  • Broken X or Z motor coupler
  • Cable carrier (black chain) damaged
  • Bent upper or lower shafts
  • Torque tube bearings or shafts damaged
  • Test home sensor function in Machine Control (Get Named State) while manually moving gripper to and from the sensor
  • Check details of event message for the Motor and Encoder counts
    • Manually move gripper in both X and Z axis and see if encoder counts change in Machine Control (Get Position)
    • Disconnect and reconnect encoder cables and/or replace encoders if values don't change
  • Inspect the pipetting nest slides
    • Push and pull on both front and rear slides to verify they are not binding
    • Clean the nest slides with water as needed
    • Verify the springs are not broken or missing
  • Ensure smooth non-binding movement on upper and lower shafts
    • Clean shafts with a lint free cloth if dirty
    • Lightly lubricate shafts as needed
      • Upper shaft - 3 in 1 oil
      • Lower shaft - Lithium grease
    • Replace upper shaft bushing if it is binding
  • Check the X and Z idler tensioner assemblies
    • Belts can be removed from pulleys to check for pulley damage
    • Replace idlers if bearings are damaged
    • Retention belts if they are loose (belts should have auto-tensioning screws)
  • Inspect the inside top wall of the RSM directly under the RSI PCB for interference from the standoff screw that PCB mounts to
  • Inspect the X and Z motor couplers to ensure they are not broken - replace as needed
  • Replace any damaged black plastic cable carrier chain links
  • Ensure the upper and lower shafts are not bent
    • Manually move the gripper and watch the shafts for bowing
    • Feel for any binding movement
    • The upper shaft can be easily removed from the module and rolled on a flat surface to confirm it is straight
  • Check torque tube bearings and pulleys for damage
    • Feel for binding movement and/or noise from seized pulleys or bearings
    • Belts can be removed from pulleys to confirm the source of binding
Alignments, PCBs, Cables
  • Individual nest offsets out of limits and gripper may not physically move (event message details will indicate expected motor position is beyond the travel limits)
  • PCB Issues
    • Encoder counts of 8192
    • Encoder counts that match motor counts (not out of tolerance)
  • Communication errors
  • Recurring error at same nest location
  • Potential PCB Issues
    • Recommended troubleshooting for all PCB related errors
      • Initialize to recover
      • Restart MC if initialization does not resolve
      • Consider swapping Tip Stocker I/F and Reagent Storage I/F PCBs for troubleshooting
    • Slippage error where motor and encoder counts match (not out of tolerance)
      • Unknown root cause
  • Communication errors - log file or event details may indicate a device communication, invalid encoder reading, irregular communication, or NAK response
    • Disconnect and reconnect the USB cable a minimum of 4 times at USB port on the PCB and on the RSI PCB. (Disconnecting and reconnecting cables provides improved contact between port and cable.)
    • MC may need to be restarted
    • Replace USB cable if errors recur
  • Motion errors recurring at the same X and Z encoder counts
    • Check for a damaged nest and replace as needed
    • Perform alignment at that nest position. Refer to Aligning Individual Reagent Nests

Gripper Pack Open or Close Errors

Event Log Message or Behavior Causes Possible Resolutions
RSM Gripper Failure Open / Close

RSM Gripper Bad Grip

  • Gripper open/close sensor failure
  • Sensor Cable Failure
  • Low Pressure
  • Incorrect Alignments
  • Manually move the gripper and watch the 4 open/close sensor LEDs on top of the gripper to see if the status changes (air pressure should be on so that fingers don't inadvertently change when moving gripper)
    • Status change without toggling gripper indicates a sensor cable or gripper sensor issue
    • Sensors on the gripper cannot be adjusted in the field. Replace gripper if LED status change is not the result of the sensor cable.
  • Toggle the gripper fingers from open to closed by hand to verify each sensor status changes (air pressure off)
    • All four LEDs should toggle from on to off at different points while moving from open to closed)
    • Sensor status can also be viewed in machine control, but polarity is opposite of the LEDs and a check indicates the sensor is off
    • Sensors work in pairs (left and right) and when 1 sensor is on the software interprets both sensors as on
  • Low pneumatics pressure can cause gripper open/close errors
    • Perform Testing Pneumatics Lines for Leaks for the yellow line to check for air leaks and compressor issues
    • Inspect pneumatics tubing and fittings for leaks if leak test fails
    • Confirm gripper valve toggles on and off in machine control
    • Check/replace compressor air filter and check valve if present (tubing should route downward from compressor to air filter/check valve to prevent condensation from draining into compressor
    • Compressor replacement may be needed if issues recur
    • Consider connecting instrument to house air if it is available
  • Poor gripper alignments can cause gripper open/close sensor errors - realign gripper as necessary

Reagent Load Tray, BCR, and Waste Door Errors

Event Log Message or Behavior Causes Possible Resolutions
Unrecognized Reagent Pack

RSM Barcode Read Failure

RSM_Remap Barcode Read Failure

  • Poor reagent pack barcode quality
  • Barcode camera failure
  • Load tray movement errors
  • Poor alignments at load tray or pack nests
  • Unable to read background label in unoccupied load tray slots
  • Inspect reagent pack barcodes
    • Clean labels on reagent packs and remove any condensation
    • Load a different pack of the same reagent lot number
    • Switch to a new reagent lot number
  • Check barcode camera function
    • Confirm barcode camera has power by checking that LED is on at camera
    • Reset camera cables if no power is on at camera
  • Confirm load tray function
    • Verify load tray cover switch is functional
    • Clean and lubricate load tray guide rod and lead screw as necessary
    • Confirm tray in and tray out sensors are functional in MC
  • Verify/perform reagent loader tray alignments
    • Refer to Aligning the Reagent Loader Tray and perform the reagent loader tray alignment section to:
      • Confirm alignments
      • Verify background labels scan
      • Confirm that camera images show pack barcodes
RSM Loader Door Open on Init

RSM Loader Door Open Too Long

Pack Unload/Discard Request Failure

  • Load tray cover is not functional
  • Load tray cover was left in the open position
  • Pack unload failure
  • Waste door errors
  • Load tray cover sensor failure
    • Magnet on door not contacting switch sensor when closed
    • Magnet missing
    • Sensor unplugged
  • Load tray cover was left open for too long or during initialization
  • Pack not able to be unloaded

Temperature Errors

Event Log Message or Behavior Causes Possible Resolutions
RSM air temperature out of range

RSM cooler assembly temperature out of range

  • Thermistor failure
  • Cooler assembly failure
  • RSM fans not functional
  • Blower and instrument case fans not functional
  • Air intake filer is dirty
  • Check RSM air thermistor
    • Confirm it is mounted to the bracket on the back wall of RSM
    • Confirm it is connected to the RSI PCB - an open thermistor will read a negative value of approximately -20 to -40C (replace as needed)
  • Cooler assembly failure
    • One or more cooler failures (currently replace as a unit of 3)
    • Thermistor failure on cooler assembly (thermistor is not replaceable)
  • Check RSM fans
    • Confirm all 3 fans are spinning at the same speed
    • Ensure fans blow air to the rear of the module onto the cooler assemblies
  • Confirm Blower and all 3 instrument case temperature fans are operational
    • Fans should blow air out the rear of the instrument
    • Refer to GUID-060735D9-00D5-4D19-B295-F2F634AB0E3D.html for reference to fan locations
  • Verify air filter under fluidics drawer (lower right front cover) is clean - vacuum as needed

Miscellaneous Errors and Other Troubleshooting

Event Log Message or Behavior Causes Possible Resolutions
Manually moving gripper to home before initializing N/A
  • Always ensure the gripper is in the home position (bottom left corner) in the RSM before initialization or moving to nest positions
  • Starting in the home position will prevent damage to the gripper or nests as a result of the gripper being in an unknown position and crashing into a nest
Initialization with Perform RSM Remap N/A
  • Initialization with "Perform RSM Remap" must be performed after removing the RSM cover
  • Remap with "Perform RSM Remap" must be performed after removing the RSM cover
  • Remap will take inventory of all packs in the RSM and conform locations
RSM gripper timing calibration N/A
  • The RSM gripper timing calibration should be run after changing alignments and takes approximately 20 minutes to complete
  • It calibrates the time to pick and place packs with the RSM
  • The calibration essentially determines how far away certain assay reagent packs can be from the pipetting nests to meet timelines for reagent pipetting

Refer to Calibrating Gripper Timing for details

Reagent Storage Full All reagent pack nests are occupied
  • Unload reagent packs prior to attempting to load additional packs
  • Wait for the instrument to discard packs with 0 tests remaining before loading additional packs
RSM Door Open
  • Door is open
  • Magnet on cover not in close contact with sensor
  • Service switch not enabled
  • Ensure that RSM front cover is secured in place or interlock is bypassed
  • Ensure magnet is mounted in the correct orientation on the cover to interact with sensor
  • Enable the service switch when troubleshooting the RSM module. Refer to Enabling and Disabling the Interlock System for Cover Switch Sensors. Ensure switch is disabled before returning instrument to customer use.
