Instrument Specifications

This topic includes information about the instrument's specifications.

Table 5: Dimensions
Weight approximately 1785 lbs (810 kg)
Length 79 inches (200 cm)
Height

63 inches (159 cm)

82 inches (209 cm) with main cover open

Depth

41 inches (104 cm)

32 inches (81 cm) with SPU removed

30 5/32 inches (76.6 cm) with SPU and covers removed

Table 6: Electrical Requirements
Power Supply

Line Voltage: 220 VAC (180 - 264 VAC)

Frequency: 47 - 63 Hz

Power: No greater than 2700 W

Instrument does not require a dedicated circuit

Power Outlet

AC outlet must be protected by a circuit breaker rated at 20 A (US), 16 A or 20 A (OUS).

NAO only - Must be compatible with NEMA L6-20P twist lock plug on the instrument

Voltage fluctuations – Not to exceed +/- 10 VAC per cycles

Transient over voltages – Installation Category II

Power Cord International Power Cord Currently manufactured by Interpower, specifications can be found on the Interpower website. (insert link https://www.interpower.com/cgi-bin/ic.cgi/p65form.p?w_part=70009110250)
US Power Cord Currently manufactured by Quail Electronics, Inc. P/N: 5084.144AL. Locking NEMA L6-20P to IEC 60320-C19 Auto-Lock.
Locking NEMA L6-20P Plug (NAO only)
Table 7: Liquid Waste Requirements
Height Drain or container must be fewer than 1.5 meters from the floor.
Distance Drain or container must be fewer than 10 meters from the instrument.
