Operating Principles for the Reagent Pipettors

This topic includes information about the theory of operation, or operating principles, for the reagent pipettor module.

Function

The reagent pipettors service five main functions:
  • To provide reagent transfer of a specified amount from a specified well of a reagent pack to the reaction vessel.
  • To provide wash buffer transfer of a specified amount to the reaction vessel.
  • To provide reagent mixing of a specified well of a reagent pack on a pack nest.
  • To provide ultrasonic mixing of a specified vessel on a shuttle of reagent carriage.
  • To provide washing reagent probe.
Note: Reagent pipettors do not perform level sense.

Location

The reagent pipettors are located at the front center of the instrument on the main chassis. See the overhead view drawing of the instrument chassis and components below for location reference:

1 Reagent Pipettors

Operational Overview

General pipettor operation involves the following (in no particular order):

  • Ultrasonic mixing of reagents within the reagent pack.
  • Delivery of reagents from the reagent pack to the reaction vessel.
  • Delivery of wash buffer from the reagent pump through the reagent probe into the reaction vessel.
  • Ultrasonic mixing of reagents within the reaction vessel.
Note: Reagent pipettor operation will vary significantly depending on the assay performed. See Instructions for Use (IFU) and Technical Assay Guide (TAG) documents for specific assays for details.

Component Description and Function
Temperature Control PCB Monitors and maintains temperature of reagent pipettor tape heaters.
Rgt Interface PCB Interface PCB that relays signals and commands to and from the reagent pipettors.
dMCC PCB PCB which drives the motor movement of the reagent pipettors.
Y-axis Motor/Encoder Drives the pipettors in the Y-axis (forward and back) and monitors movement to compare with motor step counts.
Z-axis Motor/Encoder Drives the pipettors in the Z-axis (up and down) and monitors movement to compare with motor step counts.
Y-axis Home Sensor Detects when the Y-axis (forward and back) carriage is at the home position.
Y-axis Index Sensor Detects when the Y-axis (forward and back) carriage is at the index position.
Z-axis Home Sensor Detects when the Z-axis (up and down) carriage is at the home position.
Y-axis Linear Guide Guide to move RP carrier assembly horizontally.
Z-axis Linear Guide Guide to move RP pipettor assembly vertically.
Y-axis Timing Belt/Pulley Transfer Y-motor motion to move reagent pipettor carrier assembly.
Z-axis Timing Belt/Pulley Transfer Z-motor motion to move reagent pipetting assembly.
Y-axis Belt Tensioner CnS fitting which can adjust timing belt tension mechanically.
Z-axis Belt Tensioner CnS fitting which can adjust timing belt tension mechanically.
Carrier Assembly Mounts to the Y-axis linear guide to hold the pipettor assembly and Z-axis components.
Flexible Cable, Leoni Cable Transfers electrical power and signals to the device. (Z-Motor, Z-Home sensor, Pressure Sensor, Probe Heater, US Transducer, US Heater).
Tubing, Protected by Leoni binder Fluid path for reagent pipettor. Tubing is covered by Leoni binder (ePTFE) to prevent wearing of tubing.
Reagent Probe
  • Transfers reagent into reaction vessels (RVs) and dispenses wash buffer to prepare on-board sample dilutions.
  • Punctures the pre-scored elastomer covering and barrier film on reagent packs.
  • Performs ultrasonic mixing of fluid in RVs and magnetic particles in the reagent packs.
Probe is mechanically attached to a transducer. When power is applied to the transducer, the probe moves up and down 23 micro inches at a frequency of 35.5 kHz to 36.1 kHz.
