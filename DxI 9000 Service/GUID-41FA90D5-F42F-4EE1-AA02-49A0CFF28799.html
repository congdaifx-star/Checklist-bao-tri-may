Troubleshooting Temperature Errors

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot temperature errors for the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)).

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Possible Causes

Temperature errors related to the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage [RBC]) might occur for the following reasons:
  • Thermistor failure
  • Heater failure
  • Connector failure
  • Temperature control board failure
  • Temperature control board disconnection

Troubleshooting Temperature Errors

Perform this procedure to troubleshooting issues with temperature errors related to the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)).

  1. Inspect, disconnect, and reconnect temperature cables at the heater connector and temperature control board.
  2. Inspect, disconnect, and reconnect cables between the temperature control board and the satellite power distribution board.
  3. Initialize the system. See Initializing the Instrument for more information.
