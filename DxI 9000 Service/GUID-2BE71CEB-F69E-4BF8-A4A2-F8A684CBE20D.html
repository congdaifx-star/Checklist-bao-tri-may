Replacing the X-axis Motor and Encoder, Belt, and Tensioner Assembly

This topic includes information about how to replace the X-axis motor and encoder, belt, and tensioner assembly in the pick and place (PnP) assemblies.
Note: Not all steps may be required, depending on the component being replaced. All three Pick and Place (PnP) assemblies are very similar, and differ primarily in the size and orientation of the gantry and range of motion. These instructions are written in a generic manner as to work with any PnP subsystem, any specific details for subsystems will be listed in the notes.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 1.5mm hex driver (for M2 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)

Removing the X-axis Motor and Encoder, Belt, and Tensioner Assembly

Perform this procedure to remove the x-axis motor/encoder (one piece), belt, and belt tensioner assembly.
  1. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables from the assembly.
  2. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) securing the motor and encoder assembly to the gantry.
  3. Remove the belt from the motor pulley, and remove the motor and encoder assembly.
    Note: The PnP assembly can now move freely. Exercise appropriate caution.
    Caution: Be sure the assembly on the rail does not slide off either end of the rail.
  4. Remove the belt from the x-carriage belt clip and tensioner pulley, and remove the belt.
  5. Remove the two screws (3mm driver) securing the tensioner assembly to the gantry and remove the tensioner assembly.
    Note: The tensioner may suddenly try to spring out. Use appropriate caution.

Installing the X-axis Motor and Encoder, Belt, and Tensioner Assembly

Perform this procedure to install the x-axis motor/encoder (one piece), belt, and belt tensioner assembly.

  1. Install the tensioner assembly onto the gantry and secure with two screws (3mm driver).
  2. Loosen the two tensioner screws (3mm driver) to allow the tensioner to move.
    Note: These are not the same as the screws used to mount the tensioner assembly to the gantry.
  3. Install the belt into the x-carriage belt clip.
    Note: It may be necessary to move the z axis assembly
  4. Install the motor and route the belt around the motor pulley.
  5. Apply inward force to the loose tensioner and route the belt around the tensioner pulley.
  6. Secure the tensioner in place by tightening the two tensioner screws (3mm driver).
    Caution: Do not apply force to the tensioner while tightening the screws.
