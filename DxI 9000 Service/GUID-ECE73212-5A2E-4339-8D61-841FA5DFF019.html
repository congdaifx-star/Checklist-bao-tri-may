Troubleshooting Motion and Timing Errors

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot motion and timing errors for the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)).

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Error Messages

The following motion and timing errors are related to the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)):
  • Reaction Build Carriage X motion error
  • Reaction Build Carriage X attempt to move when locked error
  • Reaction Build Carriage X failed to rendezvous
  • Reaction Build Carriage X failed to home

Possible Causes

Motion and timing errors related to the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)) might occur for the following reasons:
  • Sensor failure
  • Motor slippage
  • Loose cable connections
  • dMCC failure
  • A misaligned device that intersects or interacts with the reagent shuttle

Troubleshooting Motion and Timing Errors

Perform this procedure to troubleshooting issues with motion and timing errors related to the reagent shuttles (the reaction build carriage (RBC)).

  1. Inspect carriages and rails for obstructions.
  2. Inspect interacting devices, including the sample pick and place (PnP), incubate PnP, precision sample pipettor, and reagent pipettors.
  3. Inspect for clogged waste chutes.
  4. Initialize the system. See Initializing the Instrument for more information.
  5. Disconnect and reconnect the carriage cables for the motor, motor encoder, and sensor.

Verification

Perform the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
