Replacing the Carriage Heater, Carriage, Linear Rail, and E-chain Flex Cable

This topic includes information about how to replace the carriage heater, carriage, linear rail, and e-chain flex cable for the reagent shuttles.
Note: Depending on the component you are replacing, not all steps may be required. All four reagent shuttle assemblies are identical in construction, but depending on the carriage, the cable connectors vary to prevent incorrect connections to the dMCC.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm driver (for M3 screws)

Removing the Carriage Heater, Carriage, Linear Rail, and E-chain Flex Cable

Perform this procedure to remove the Carriage Heater, Carriage, Linear Rail, and E-chain/Flex Cable

  1. Disconnect the flex cable from the carriage heater.
  2. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the heater to the carriage and remove the carriage heater.
    Caution: There is a thermal gasket between the heater and carriage, ensure the gasket is not lost.
  3. Remove the screw (2.5mm driver) securing the cable clamp to the carriage.
  4. Push the E-chain pegs inward to remove the E-chain from the mount on the carriage.
  5. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the carriage to the linear rail truck and remove the carriage.
  6. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the E-chain mount to the carriage and remove the E-chain mount.
  7. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the linear rail to the carriage frame and remove the linear rail.
  8. Disconnect the flex cable from the dMCC.
  9. Remove the two screws securing the E-chain mounting link to the carriage frame and remove the E-chain and flex cable (can be removed as one piece).
  10. Open the E-chain clips and remove the flex cable from the E-chain.

Installing the Carriage Heater, Carriage, Linear Rail, and E-chain Flex Cable

Perform this procedure to install the Carriage Heater, Carriage, Linear Rail, and E-chain/Flex Cable

  1. Install the flex cable into the E-chain and close the clips, ensure the end which connects to the dMCC exits the end of the E-chain with the mounting link, and the end which connects to the heater exits the end of the E-chain which attaches to the mount on the carriage.
  2. Install the E-chain and flex cable onto the carriage frame and secure the mounting link with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  3. Connect the flex cable to the dMCC.
  4. Install the linear rail onto the carriage frame and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  5. Install the E-chain mount onto the carriage and secure with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  6. Install the carriage onto the linear rail truck and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  7. Squeeze the pegs on the free end of the E-chain and attach to the E-chain mount on the carriage.
  8. Place the thermal gasket onto the carriage heater over the two guide pins if it is not present.
  9. Install the carriage heater onto the carriage from the underside and secure with two screws (2.5mm driver).
  10. Install the cable clamp onto the flex cable and secure to the carriage with screw (2.5mm driver).
