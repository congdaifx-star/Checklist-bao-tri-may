Replacing the Sample Wheel Motor and Encoder

This topic includes information about how to replace the motor and encoder for the sample wheel.

Note: Remove the Sample Wheel Assembly from the Unit before replacing the sub-assemblies.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)
  • 5mm hex driver (for M6 screws)
  • 10mm thermistor driver -or- 10mm wrench

Removing the Sample Wheel Motor and Encoder

Perform this procedure to remove the motor and encoder assembly (one piece).

  1. Disconnect the motor and encoder cables.
  2. Remove the two tensioner screws (3mm driver).
  3. Push the tensioner assembly, including motor, inward to relieve belt tension and remove the belt from the motor pulley.
  4. Pull the tensioner asembly, including motor, away form the sample wheel and remove from the module.
    Caution: Do not allow the tensioner spring to fall into the instrument, as there is nothing retaining it once the tensioner is removed.
  5. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) which secure the tensioner assembly to the motor, and remove the motor.

Installing the Sample Wheel Motor and Encoder

Perform this procedure to install the motor and encoder assembly (one piece).

  1. Insert the motor to the tensioner assembly and secure with four screws (2.5mm driver).
  2. Insert the tensioner assembly into the sample wheel subsystem.
    Caution: Ensure the tensioner spring is present and has not fallen out of the assembly.
  3. Push the tensioner assembly inward enough to install the belt over the motor pulley.
  4. Allow the tensioner assembly to set the belt tension, and secure the tensioner in place with two screws (3mm driver).
    Caution: Do not apply force to the tensioner when securing it in place.
  5. Connect the motor and encoder cables.

Alignments

Perform necessary alignments and/or adjustments for affected components and subsystems.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
