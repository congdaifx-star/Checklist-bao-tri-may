Verification of Service Repairs

This topic includes information about the requirements and recommendations for verification of proper instrument operation after a service repair. Internally, this process may also be referred to as "Return to Service" (RTS) testing.

When to Perform

Perform the appropriate section of this procedure after completing a service repair to determine the required verification testing needed.

Table 32: Definitions
Term Definition
Service Tasks Tasks to be completed either before or after instrument verification depending on the module serviced and the required verification.
Minimum Verification Required The minimum testing required before releasing the instrument to the customer for its intended use.

Quality Control Statement

Beckman Coulter recommends customers run quality control (QC) samples after any unscheduled or scheduled maintenance or service and after successfully installing new software. Quality control materials simulate the characteristics of patient samples and are essential for monitoring the system performance of immunochemical assays. The verifications recommended in this procedure demonstrate instrument repair and functionality to specification but they do not supersede Beckman Coulter's recommended guidelines for QC as stated in the assay directional inserts, Instrument Instructions for Use (IFU), and Help system. Each laboratory determines the QC rules to apply and the course of action to take when a result violates a rule.

Hardware Module Serviced

Reference the information below to determine which actions are required for verification after service or repair to each specific module(s) of an instrument
  • As a part of all verification activities, the instrument should be able to power up and initialize successfully with no system messages that may affect system performance. Additionally, all verification testing should complete within passing specifications.
  • As a standard part of any service activity, service should assess/clear all event messages as needed after performing verification.
  • Items in the Minimum Verification Required column are listed in sequential order following a repair (perform these in order from top-down by activity/module if applicable).
  • If multiple modules are affected, verification tasks can be combined in logical order.
  • Important: Ensure the interlocks have been enabled, and the system is logged into the LabUser Windows account after service.

Sample Pipettor (Precise)

Waste Fluidics (Vacuum Pumps, Hi-Cera Pumps)

  • IF float sensors were replaced, THEN check sensor state via Machine Control program.
  • Perform System Carryover ASD (if vacuum or hi-cera pumps repair).
