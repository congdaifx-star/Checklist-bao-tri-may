Sample Pipettor Camera Operations

This topic includes information about how the aliquot and precision sample pipettor cameras operate.

Table of Contents
Overview
Tip Detection
Aliquot
Non-dilution

Overview

The sample pipettor camera has two main functions.
  • Scan the sample pipettor for disposable tips
  • Detect fluids

Tip Detection

The sample pipettor scans the sample pipettor to determine the presence or absence of disposable tips attached to the mandrel during each stage of the sample process including
  • While the pipettor acquires a new disposable tip.
  • Before the pipettor aspirates a sample.
  • After the pipettor aspirates a sample.

Aliquot

Before the aliquot sample pipettor aspirates a sample from the SPU presentation track, the sample pipettor camera will check for the presence or absence of a disposable tip and check for fluids in the sample container.

Table 18: Aliquot Sample Pipettor Camera - Aliquot Operation
Pipettor Motion Camera Function
Before the pipettor aspirates a sample from the Sample Presentation Unit (SPU) presentation track Tip Check
Before the pipettor aspirates a sample from the SPU presentation track Tip Check / Sample Integrity
After the pipettor acquires a new tip at Tip Supply during system initialization Tip Search

Non-dilution

Before the precision sample pipettor aspirates from the sample vessel (SV), the sample pipettor camera will check for the presence or absence of a disposable tip and check for fluids in the SV. Also, after the precision sample pipettor dispenses into the reaction vessel (RV), the sample pipettor camera will check for the presence or absence of a disposable tip and check for fluids in the RV.

Table 19: Precision Sample Pipettor Camera - Non-dilution Operation
Pipettor Motion Camera Function
Before the pipettor aspirates from the SV Tip Check
After the pipettor aspirates from the SV Tip Check / Sample Volume
After the pipettor dispenses into the reaction vessel (RV) Tip Check / Sample Volume

Dilution

Before the precision sample pipettor aspirates from the sample vessel (SV), the sample pipettor camera will check for the presence or absence of a disposable tip. After the precision sample pipettor aspirates from the SV, then aspirates from the dilution vessel (DV), and dispenses into the reaction vessel (RV), the sample pipettor camera will check for the presence or absence of a disposable tip and check for fluids in each vessel.

Table 20: Precision Sample Pipettor Camera - Dilution Operation
Pipettor Motion Camera Function
Before the pipettor aspirates from the sample vessel (SV) Tip Check
After the pipettor aspirates from the SV Tip Check / Sample Volume
After the pipettor aspirates from the Dilution Vessel (DV) Tip Check / Sample Volume
After the pipettor dispenses into the reaction vessel (RV) Tip Check / Sample Volume
