Replacing the Motor and Cap and/or Wheel Flag

This topic includes information about how to replace the luminometer motor and cap assembly, including the wheel flag.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2mm hex driver (for M2.5 screws)
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • 1/4 inch wrench

Removing the Motor and Cap and/or Wheel Flag

Perform this procedure to remove the luminometer motor and cap assembly, including the wheel flag.

  1. Disconnect the motor cable from the motor.
  2. Remove the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor bracket to the top of the luminometer and remove the bracket with the motor assembly attached.
  3. Remove the four screws (2mm driver) securing the bracket to the motor and remove the motor assembly from the bracket.
  4. Remove the screw (2.5mm driver) securing the wheel flag to the motor shaft and remove the wheel flag.

Installing the Motor and Cap and/or Wheel Flag

Perform this procedure to install the luminometer motor and cap assembly, including the wheel flag.

  1. Attach the wheel flag to the end of the motor shaft (one screw, 2.5mm driver).
  2. Attach the motor assembly to the mounting block (four screws, 2mm driver).
  3. Attach the mounting block to the luminometer assembly (two screws, 2.5mm driver).
    Caution: Check for clearance/binding on the wheel/sensors and the cap assembly. Be careful to not damage the sensors.
  4. Connect the motor cable to the motor.

Alignments
