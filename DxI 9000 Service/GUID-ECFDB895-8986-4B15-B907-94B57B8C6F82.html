Replacing the Ethernet Router

This topic includes information about how to replace the Ethernet router.

Important: The instrument must be powered down and disconnected from the power source before you perform this procedure. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • Phillips head screwdriver

Removing the Ethernet Router

Perform this procedure to remove the Ethernet router.

  1. Turn off the instrument. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.
  2. Remove the Ethernet cable.
  3. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) securing the lower right rear panel and remove the panel.
  4. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) securing the power input bracket and let the power input bracket rest out of the way to gain access to the Ethernet router.
    Note: Ensure no cables attached to the backside of the panel are pinched or stretched.
  5. Loosen the two screws securing the network hub bracket to the instrument chassis and slide the bracket out of instrument as far as the cables will allow (the bracket mounting holes are slotted so the screws do not need to be entirely removed).
  6. Disconnect the cables from the Ethernet router and remove the router.

Installing the Ethernet Router

Perform this procedure to install the Ethernet router

  1. Place the router with bracket into the instrument chassis where it was removed from.
  2. Connect all network cables.
  3. Install the bracket onto the screws and tighten to secure in place.
  4. Re-install the power input bracket and secure with four screws.
  5. Reconnect the power cable.
  6. Reconnect the Ethernet cable
  7. Re-install the lower right rear panel and secure with three screws.

Alignments

No alignments are required after replacement of peripheral components, provided that the alignment values were not lost during service (such as in the replacement of a console or hard drive where a current backup of the database was not recovered).

Verification

To verify operation of peripherals after removal/installation, confirm the system initializes successfully and that all monitor touchscreen functions appear to be working.
