Troubleshooting Event Codes for Pick and Place (PnP)

This topic includes information about how to troubleshoot event codes for the pick and place (PnP) system.

The following table provides a list of probable causes, prioritized by probability, for events related to pneumatic device lines and for components associated with the PnP system.
Event Description Probable Causes (prioritized by probability)
PNP failed to pick up alignment pin System will not initialize due to the alignment pin not being picked up on initialization
  • Clean alignment pin with DI water or alcohol
  • Inspect collet
  • Perform auto alignment
Failed to place a vessel The PnP system cannot move vessels and the Instrument is unable to complete tests.
  • Air pressure too low
  • Ejector assembly stuck in up position
  • Air valve failure (includes check valve and filter)
  • Cable failure prevents valve actuation
  • Sticky collet from material buildup
  • Worn seal prevents proper pressure
  • Failure to initialize, home, or move to position
  • Alignments drift/shift
Failed to pick up a vessel
  • Ejector assembly stuck in down position
  • Collet finger failure
  • Failure to initialize, home, or move to position
  • Alignments drift/shift
Failed to transfer vessel
  • Motor or belt failure (slippage)
  • Belt stretching
  • Encoder failure
  • Cable failure
  • Linear rail/bearing wear (motor slippage)
  • Corrosion on linear rail (motor slippage)
  • Vessel ejected/lost during transfer (vessel verification failure)
  • dMCC input to motor incorrect (wrong run/hold current)
  • Failure to initialize, home, or move to position
Failed to correctly verify vessel presence or absence in collet (Hall Effect Sensor)
  • Hall Effect cable failure (High Flex)
  • Hall Effect sensor failure
  • Vessel engagement does not trigger Hall Effect sensor
