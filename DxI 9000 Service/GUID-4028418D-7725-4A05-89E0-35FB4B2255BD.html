Replacing the Pneumatic Manifold Assembly

This topic includes information about how to replace the pneumatic manifold assembly.

Important: The instrument must be powered down and disconnected from the power source before you perform this procedure. See Turning Off the Instrument for more information.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver for M3 screws
  • 3mm hex driver for M4 screws

Removing the Pneumatic Manifold Assembly

Perform this procedure to remove the pneumatic manifold assembly.

  1. Ensure that the instrument is de-energized.
  2. Remove the manifold pivot screw (3mm driver) and swing the manifold to gain access to the rear.
  3. Bleed off air by depressing the red button on the top of the Accumulator Jar.
  4. Disconnect the 14 pneumatic tubes from the rear of the manifold:
    • 4 x 12psi tubing (blue)
    • 2 x 20psi tubing (green)
    • 4 x 32psi tubing (yellow)
    • 2 x supply tubing (red); MANIFOLD IN, MANIFOLD OUT
    • 2 x liquid relief (clear); LIQ VALVE IN, LIQ VALVE OUT
  5. Disconnect the four electrical cables from rear of the manifold:
    • 3 x pressure sensors (12, 20, 32 psi)
    • Liquid relief valve
  6. Remove the four screws (3mm driver) securing the manifold to the hinged mount plate and remove the manifold.

Installing the Pneumatic Manifold Assembly

Perform this procedure to install the pneumatic manifold assembly.

  1. Mount the manifold onto the hinged mount plate and secure with four screws (3mm driver).
  2. Connect the four electrical cables to the rear of the manifold:
    • 3 x pressure sensors (12, 20, 32 psi)
    • Liquid relief valve
  3. Connect the 14 pneumatic tubing to the rear of the manifold:
    • 4 x 12psi tubing (blue)
    • 2 x 20psi tubing (green)
    • 4 x 32psi tubing (yellow)
    • 2 x supply tubing (red); MANIFOLD IN, MANIFOLD OUT
    • 2 x liquid relief (clear); LIQ VALVE IN, LIQ VALVE OUT
  4. Pivot the manifold so that it is entirely inside the instrument and install the pivot screw (3mm driver) to secure the hinge and manifold in place.

Making Adjustments

Make necessary adjustments (12psi, 20psi, and 32psi/H.A.) for affected components and subsystems.

Running the Bulk Feeder Exerciser

  1. Turn on the instrument. See Turning On the Instrument for more information.
  2. Initialize the instrument. See Initializing the Instrument for more information.
  3. In the Console App run the Vessel Exerciser, Bulk Feeder, for 100 reps.
    1. Select the SYSTEM RUNNING button and go to Stop > Pause to enter the instrument into a YELLOW (Paused) status.
    2. In the Console App go to Menu > Tools > Diagnostics > Intrusive Diagnostics and select Start Session.
    3. Select Exerciser to open the Exerciser page.
    4. Select RV Feeder.
    5. Type 100 in the Repetition box.
    6. Select Request.
  4. Confirm that you observe no errors for low system pressure.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
