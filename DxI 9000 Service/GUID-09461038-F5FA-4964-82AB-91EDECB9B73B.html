Troubleshooting Common Installation Failures

This topic includes information about common symptoms observed during prior installations, their causes, and resolutions for those symptoms.

Symptom Cause Resolution
May observe x/z-axis or failed to grip pack errors for RSM. RSM fails initialization as gripper jammed/not at home and cannot recover. Gripper not at home location during shipment or crashes during first initialization and system is unable to recover on its own Gripper may have jammed due to lack of pneumatic pressure to toggle the arms during first initialization, causing it to crash on a reagent nest and then fail homing attempts. Manually Homing the RSM Gripper Assembly
Fluid in the waste bin. Condensation from the RSM was emptied into the solid waste bin and saturated the supply boxes. Condensation in the reagent storage module may escape through the solid waste door into the waste bin, leading to appearance of fluid/spill in the waste bin. Spills should be cleaned up prior to installation of a new waste bag and system initialization. Clean up any fluid present inside the waste bin as well as the reagent storage module.
Substrate loader invalidates bottles at initialization. During position check of substrate loader seals substrate bottles become invalid During initialization the system checks between current and previous positions of the probe and door seals. If more than 4 step difference it can not verify integrity and will invalidate bottles Auto-Aligning the Substrate Loader
Windows desktop displays message of unregistered license of Windows. Windows does not recognize the valid license During first install Windows desktop may display a message indicating an un-licensed copy of Windows OS is installed. Clicking on message and following onscreen instructions will register windows and remove message
Times between system and Remisol do not match. Windows Time zone is not set to UTC Coordinated Universal Time Time zone is set to something other than UTC Coordinated Universal Time Adjusting the System Date and Time
May observe a "Large leak in the system" or "Compressor failure" errors or similar. Leaking PnP collets observed. PnP ejector stuck during shipment and leaking, unable to eject vessels. The PnP ejector was stuck in the "up" position when the instrument was shipped and air pressure was unable to move it resulting in initialization failures and leaking observed at the collet. Remove the collet/ejector assembly and clean with DI water. Reassemble and reinstall. Ensure all seals are properly seated and making good contact with seal surfaces.
May observe x/z-axis or failed to grip pack errors for RSM. RSM fails initialization as gripper jammed/not at home and cannot recover. Gripper not at home location during shipment or crashes during first initialization and system is unable to recover on its own. Gripper may have jammed due to lack of pneumatic pressure to toggle the arms during first initialization, causing it to crash on a reagent nest and then fail homing attempts. Open the RSM and manually home the gripper. May also need to home gripper motors in machine control after homing manually (to reset motor/encoder). Repeat initialization.
Bulk feeder and/or supply carriage motion errors during initialization/sample processing. Bulk Feeder/Supply Carriage screws were found to be loose causing misalignment and motion failures during movement. Screws may not be securely tightened during manufacturing. If motion failures are observed and obvious obstructions are not present, check all components for tightness and remove/reinstall as needed. Remove the Bulk Feeder and Supply Shuttle and reinstall to ensure fasteners are secure and there is no movement in stationary components.
Covers are not securely fastened to the instrument chassis. During shipment, the lugs/tabs that secure the covers in place were broken. Some covers may loosen/dislodge during shipment, extreme cases may lead to damage requiring cover replacement. Check all covers to ensure they are securely fastened to the instrument and adjust/replace as needed. Contact Chaska to obtain new covers and replace as needed.
Covers are not securely fastened to the instrument chassis. During shipment, the fasteners that secure the covers in place had become loose and the cover fitment was out of position. Some covers may loosen/dislodge during shipment, extreme cases may lead to damage requiring cover replacement. Check all covers to ensure they are securely fastened to the instrument and adjust/replace as needed. Remove and re-install the covers ensuring secure fitment and good alignment/minimal gaps.
System would not initialize at first. First or second initialization may fail during initial start-up. Repeat initialization and review errors for detail. If failures are different each time, system may just require multiple initializations. If failures are consistent, investigate root cause of the failure (error message). Sometimes compressor may need to be manually cycled to build pneumatic pressure if pneumatic errors persist. Review errors at every initialization failure, and repeat multiple times. Troubleshoot consistent failure messages as applicable.
Fluid in the waste bin. Condensation from the RSM was emptied into the solid waste bin and saturated the supply boxes. Condensation in the reagent storage module may escape through the solid waste door into the waste bin, leading to appearance of fluid/spill in the waste bin. Spills should be cleaned up prior to installation of a new waste bag and system initialization. Clean up any fluid present inside the waste bin as well as the reagent storage module.
