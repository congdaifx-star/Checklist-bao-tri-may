Replacing the Wash Wheel Subsystem

This topic includes information about how to replace the wash wheel subsystem.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Required Tools

  • 2.5mm hex driver
  • 3mm hex driver
  • 4mm hex driver

Removing the Subsystem

This is the procedure to remove the Wash Wheel Subsystem and its related components.

  1. Remove rear upper bi-fold door if needed to get access
    1. Loosen keeper bolt at the top rail slide to separate door from slide.
    2. Release hasp on door located at side of chassis
    3. Lift door off to remove
  2. Remove side cover
    1. Release the hasp at the side of the chassis.
    2. Lift the door off.
  3. For better access to the Wash Wheel, remove the fan.
    1. Unscrew the (2) 3mm screws.
    2. Unplug the power cable.
    3. Pull the fan out, and set it to the side.
  4. Remove cables
    1. Camera:
      1. RJ45 Ethernet that plugs into the camera
      2. Serial to the port next to D10 on the wash wheel chassis
    2. Spin mixer PCB (N1)
      1. N1-J1(power)
      2. N1-J8(USB)
    3. Temp board (T2)
      1. T2-J9 (power)
      2. T2-J10 (USB)
    4. dMCC
      1. D10-J7 (USB)
      2. D10-J6 (power)
    5. Temp board (T3)
      1. T3-J2(wash wheel heater)
  5. Place absorbent material below substrate, aspirate, and dispense probes.
  6. Disconnect fluidic lines and allow fluid to drain onto absorbent pads:
    Note: Place absorbent material beneath fluidic tubing when disconnecting.
    1. Clear aspirate tubing from the barb fitting which connects it to the cream color aspirate tubing.
    2. Wash buffer clear tubing from manifold above wash syringe pumps (QS, D1, D2, A1, A2, A3)
    3. A1W wash tower waste line from manifold (on the left side of manifold while facing the wash wheel, unthread barb fitting and leave attached to tubing)
  7. Remove Substrate Probe/Heater assembly
    1. Remove two screws (2.5mm driver)
    2. Disconnect the SUB PROBE TEMP wire from the extension on the dispense arm.
    3. Remove assembly and place on an absorbent pad to prevent wash buffer leaking onto system components.
  8. Disconnect pneumatic tubing from pneumatic manifold (yellow wash wheel pneumatic tubing).
  9. Remove the three screws (4mm driver) securing the wash wheel assembly to the instrument chassis.

Installing the Subsystem

  1. Reverse steps above, pay close attention to not pinch or strain any cables or tubing
    Note: See Table below for corresponding tube positions
TOP Manifold Tubing
D3 A2
D4 A3
QS QS
A1 A1W
D1 D1
BOTTOM D2 D2

Removing the Wash Wheel Plate and Housing (Heater)

  1. Remove wash wheel cover
  2. Remove three screws (3mm driver) securing ground plate and wash wheel plate to the wash wheel drive shaft and remove the wash wheel plate and ground plate.
  3. Remove the three screws (3mm driver) securing the base of the wash arm tower to the wash wheel chassis and remove the wash arm tower.
    Note: Due to cables and/or tubing connections, the wash arm tower may be laid next to the wheel as it will not be fully free of the wheel chassis.
  4. Disconnect the following cables:
    1. Backlight camera cable
    2. Wash wheel home sensor cable
    3. Camera serial connector
  5. Remove the three screws (2.5mm driver) securing the wash wheel housing to the chassis and remove the wash wheel housing. Two are located near the alignment pins located by the Wash Tower Assy and one is inside the Wash Wheel Base between the alignment pin and the back-light.
Note: The housing/heater should be replaced as an assembly, do not replace individual heater, insulation, housing, etc.). Remove the camera backlight and camera bracket from the assembly and transfer to new parts if replacing.

Alignment

Perform alignments. See Aligning the Wash Wheel for more information.

Verification

Complete the verification of service repair. See Verification of Service Repairs for more information.
