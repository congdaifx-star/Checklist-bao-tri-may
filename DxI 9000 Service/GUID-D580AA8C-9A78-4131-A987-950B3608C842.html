Replacing the Tensioner / Idler, Belt, and Motor and Encoder

This topic includes information about how to replace the tensioner/idler, belt, and motor and encoder in the sample presentation unit (SPU) tray components.

Safety Reminder

Servicing the DxI 9000 instrument involves potential risks for pinch and puncture, electrical shock, and exposure to hazardous materials. You must mitigate these risks by taking appropriate countermeasures. See Safety for more information.

Recommended Tools

The following tools are recommended for replacement of the components listed in this topic:
  • 2.5mm hex driver (for M3 screws)
  • 3mm hex driver (for M4 screws)
  • 4mm hex driver (for M5 screws)

Removing the Tensioner / Idler, Belt, and Motor and Encoder

Perform this procedure to remove the presentation tray Tensioner/Idler, Belt, and Motor/Encoder.

  1. Open the SPU to gain access to the presentation tray belt tensioner/idler assembly.
  2. Remove the (4) screws (4mm hex driver) on the front right of the Presentation Tray Rack.
  3. Remove tension from the belt by loosening the (2) screws (2.5mm driver) securing the idler assembly in place (2.5mm driver), pushing inward on the idler assembly, and remove the belt from the idler pulley.
    Note: Removing these screws requires using a driver with a 90° angle.
    Caution: Be careful not to drop loose screws into the instrument.
  4. Remove the belt from the motor pulley and coupler channel.
  5. Remove the tensioner/idler assemblies by removing the screws securing it (2.5mm driver).
    Note: There are two screws securing the tensioner block and two screws securing the idler assembly. They are the same size (M3x8).
    Note: These screws are under compression and can be ejected as they are loosened.
  6. Open the rear instrument door(s) to gain access to the rear of the presentation tray subsystem.
  7. Disconnect the cables from the motor and encoder.
  8. Remove the four screws (2.5mm driver) securing the motor to the frame and remove the motor/encoder.
    Note: Make note of the orientation for re-installation.

Installing the Tensioner / Idler, Belt, and Motor and Encoder

Perform this procedure to install the presentation tray Tensioner/Idler, Belt, and Motor/Encoder.

  1. Install the motor/encoder by inserting the pulley through the presentation tray frame and securing it to the frame with four screws (2.5mm driver).
    Note: The motor/encoder should be oriented so that the cable connections point towards the front of the instrument.
  2. Connect the motor and encoder cables.
  3. Install the tensioner/idler assembly and secure with the four screws (2.5mm driver).
    Note: There are two screws securing the tensioner block and two screws securing the idler assembly, they are the same size (M3x8).
  4. Install the belt onto the motor pulley and into the coupler channel.
  5. Loosen the two screws (2.5mm driver) securing the idler assembly and push the idler inward to install the belt onto the idler pulley.
  6. Tighten the two screws (2.5mm driver) to secure the idler assembly in place and set the belt tension.
    Note: Ensure that force is not applied to the belt or tensioner when securing the tensioner/idler assembly in place.
