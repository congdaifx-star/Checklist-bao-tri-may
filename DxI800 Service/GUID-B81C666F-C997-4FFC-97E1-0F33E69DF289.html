Installing Reagent Storage Module

This procedure describes how to install the reagent storage module after replacing a reagent cooler.

Install the reagent storage module after replacing a reagent cooler.

  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Verify that the instrument power cord is disconnected.
    2. Open the upper left and upper right front covers.
    3. Confirm that the reagent storage module mounting area is clean of hardware, parts, and assemblies.
  2. Install the reagent storage module.
    Caution: When removing or installing the reagent storage module, do not damage the bottom of the waste chute door mechanism, the tray motor pulley, the load tray drive pulley, or the load tray home sensor and wires.
    1. Carefully place the reagent storage module into position in the instrument.
    2. Position the reagent storage module for mounting. Force the module into the mounting position by pushing rearward and then downward on its lower front edge until it is seated in the detents.
      Caution: Confirm that no cables are pinched between the module and the base plate before and after mounting the module.
    3. Open the solid waste door, remove the solid waste container, and install the two screws that secure the left end of the reagent storage module to the instrument frame.
      The screws are in the overhead of the solid waste compartment.
    4. Install the solid waste container, and then close the door.
    5. Open the lower right access door, and then install the two screws that secure the right end of the reagent storage module to the instrument frame.
      The screws are in the overhead of the power supply compartment.
  3. Install the power conditioner module (instruments with Magenetek power supplies).
    Note: Instruments with Cosel power supplies do not have power conditioner modules.
    1. Slide the power conditioner module partially into the compartment in the instrument, and install the screw for the green ground wire on the left side.
    2. Mount the power conditioner module to the instrument (one screw).
    3. Connect the two cables (green wires and blue-white wires) to the power conditioner module.
    4. Install the cover on the power conditioner module (two screws on the left side).
    5. Install the cover on the power supply (five screws).
  4. Connect the reagent storage module.
    1. Connect the reagent storage module ground strap to the instrument base plate (one screw).
      The ground screw is located to the right and slightly behind the luminometer.
    2. Connect the air supply line to the correct fitting on the air valve.
    3. Connect these cables to the reagent storage module interface PCB:
      The interface PCB is mounted on top of the reagent storage module.
      • J1 - Ribbon Cable
      • J2 - Motor Drive Harness
      • J3 - Reagent Cooler
      • J5 - 24 V DC Supply
  5. Install the Framus.
    1. Hold the Framus above the reagent storage module interface PCB and reconnect:
      • J25 - Reagent Load Door
      • J30 - Bar Code Reader
      • DxI 800 only: J29 - Ribbon Cable
      • The location of reagent load door connector going to J25 is different between PCB versions.
      • The bar code reader cable must run under the load tray and behind the bar code reader.
    2. Reconnect the two SPU interlock switch wires to the two interlock switch terminals on the underside of the Framus.
    3. Place the Framus on the instrument and mount it to the right end (two screws).
      Note:
      • To disengage the cover latch on the reagent load tray when installing the Framus, operate the release lever located under the reagent load tray inside the lower right access door.
      • DxI 800 only: Early instruments also have two screws that hold the left end of the Framus to the instrument. If the instrument has these screws, install them to mount the Framus on the left end.
    4. DxI 800 only: Move the substrate loader from the hanging position on the front of the instrument, down onto the Framus. Tighten the four captive screws that mount the substrate loader to the Framus.
    5. DxI 800 only: Mount the triangular-shaped support plate at the center of the Framus (four screws).
  6. Close the SPU.
    The SPU is hinged on the left side. The right side is released and can be swung out from the instrument to approximately 45 degrees.
    Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    1. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end toward the instrument.
    2. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin to attach the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    3. Close the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    4. Insert the 3/16-in hex bolt.
    5. Tighten the bolt that holds the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument (locking screw).
    6. Close the upper left cover of the instrument.
      Note: Initialize the instrument after opening and closing the SPU, because a switch is actuated when the SPU is opened.
  7. Perform the final steps.
    1. Close the upper right front cover
    2. Insert the interlock bypass tool to override the open upper left front cover.
    3. Reconnect the power cord and power up the instrument.
    4. Remove the interlock by-pass.
    5. Close the upper left instrument cover.
    6. Initialize the system with Remap selected.
  8. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
  9. Load the removed reagent packs.
  10. Replace the reagent storage cover and any pipetting nest covers that were removed.
