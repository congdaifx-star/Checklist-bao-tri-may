Test Points - PCB - Power Distribution

Note: All the fuses in the Power Distribution PCB are resettable. The fuses reset when the power is cycled off and on.
TP Voltage / Signal Notes

TJ1

0V

Ground.

From J3-2, 5, 8, or 10-15. (TJ1 )

TJ2

24V

+24V_3.

Power input from power supply 3 through J3-1, 4, or 7. (TJ2 )

TJ3

24V

+24V_2.

Power input from power supply 2 through J3-3, 6, or 9. (TJ3 )

TJ4

24V

+24V_1.

Power input from power supply 1 through J3-16, 17, or 18. (TJ4 )

TP1

36V

36V power from regulator U8-4. (36V GATE)

Supplies the gate voltage for the solid state relays.

TP2

12V

12V power from regulator U7-3.

Used to drive transistor gates for Peltier PWM.

TP3

12V

12V power from regulator U9-1.

Used to drive gate of Q1 for main blower fan PWM.

Note: Measure main blower fan PWM % at Q1 gate pin 2. Full speed (0% PWM) = 12 V, Off (100% PWM) = 0 V.

TP4

1V to 6V

+24V_SIG 2.

24V power monitor signal to the DAQC PCB for the INCUBATOR, SAMPLE, REAG_AUX power branches are read on TP4 .

Note: The voltage on this TP varies incrementally based on the number of branches that have power.

TP5

1V to 6V

+24V_SIG 1.

24V power monitor signal to the DAQC PCB for the WASH, ULTRASONIC, PERI PUMP, and MCC power branches are read on TP5 .

Note: The voltage on this TP varies incrementally based on the number of branches that have power.

TP6

1V to 6V

+24V_SIG 3.

24V power monitor signal to the DAQC PCB for the MAIN FAN, and the REAG1, 2, 3 Peltier power branches are read on TP6.

Note: The voltage on this TP varies incrementally based on the number of branches that have power.

TP7

12V or 0V

REAG PWM1+ (reagent cooler 1).

Reagent Peltier 1 enabled: (TP5) = 0 V and D15 LED is on.

Signal from J9-3 to U1-7 to Q12 (TP7) to Q8 grounds 24V coming from the reagent storage module Peltier 1 (pulsed ground).

TP8

12V or 0V

REAG PWM2+ (reagent cooler 2).

Reagent Peltier 2 enabled: (TP7) = 0 V and D17 LED is on.

Signal from J9-5 to U4-7 to Q11 (TP8) to Q4 grounds 24V coming from the reagent storage module Peltier 2 (pulsed ground).

TP9

12V or 0V

REAG PWM3+ (reagent cooler 3).

Reagent Peltier 3 enabled: (TP8) = 0 V and D19 LED is on.

Signal from J9-7 to U4-5 to Q14 (TP9) to Q10 grounds 24V coming from the reagent storage module Peltier 3 (pulsed ground).

TP11

12V or 0V

SAM PWM+ (sample storage cooler ).

Sample Peltier enabled: (TP6) = 0 V and D28 LED is on.

Signal from J9-9 to U1-5 to Q13 (TP11) to Q9 grounds 24V coming from the sample storage module Peltier (pulsed ground).

TP13

1V to 6V

+24V_SIG 4.

24V power monitor signal to the ANALYTICAL MODULE, 24V GATE, LOWER CABINET and the DAQC/LUMI branches are read on TP13 .

Note: The voltage on this TP varies incrementally based on the number of branches that have power.
