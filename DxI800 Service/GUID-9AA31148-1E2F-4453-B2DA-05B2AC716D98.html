Conn. Overview - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Fluidics

  • These are the connectors on the Fluidics Interface PCB.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.

#

Connection Notes

Function / Signals

J1

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, FLUIDICS (P/N 116251) connects J1 to J62 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 5V power, the Sample Wash and Precision Pump/Valve Home sensor, the Peri pump Index sensor, the Sample Pump Solenoid Valve, and the Buffer Reservoir Float Switch signals run through J1.

J2

HARNESS, INTERFACE, FLUIDICS (P/N 116373) Connects Fluidics Interface PCB J2 to MCC Backplane PCB JP42. (MCC4)

The Precision Pump 1 and 2 and Precision Valve 1 and 2 Motor drive signals from MCC4 run through J2.

J3

HARNESS, INTERFACE, FLUIDICS (P/N 116373) Connects Fluidics Interface PCB J3 to MCC Backplane PCB JP52. (MCC5)

The Precision Pump 3 and 4 and Precision Valve 3 and 4 Motor drive signals from MCC5 run through J3.

J4

HARNESS, INTERFACE, FLUIDICS (P/N 116373) Connects Fluidics Interface PCB J4 to MCC Backplane PCB JP61. (MCC6)

The Wash Pump 1 and 2 and Wash Valve 1 and 2 Motor drive signals from MCC6 run through J4.

J5

HARNESS, INTERFACE, FLUIDICS (P/N 116373) Connects Fluidics Interface PCB J5 to MCC Backplane PCB JP62. (MCC6)

The Aspirate Peristaltic Pump and the Sample Pump Motor drive signals from MCC6 run through J5.
