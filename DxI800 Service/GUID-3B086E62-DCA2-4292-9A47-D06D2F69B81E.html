Ref - Sensors - Thermal

Purpose

This document provides reference information for all thermal sensors in a DxI, and the information needed to determine which thermistor can or cannot be substituted for others.

Thermistors

All thermal sensors are pictured in the table. Important facts about these sensors:
  • Two types of thermistors are used in DxI:
    • Threaded stud with shielded cable
    • Flying leads inserted into an Insulation Displacement Connector
  • Ten replaceable thermal sensors are used in the DxI.
  • Three styles of thermal devices are used. Some are also used in Access and Access 2, but might or might not be interchangeable with DxI. Refer to the table.
  • A universal thermister (CARES PN 126011) is available to use as temporary universal replacement for any same-type thermistor. Order the correct thermistor and replace it later.
