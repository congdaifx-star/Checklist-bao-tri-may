J4 - PCB - Data Acquisition (DAQC)

  • Signals from the Temperature Sensor Interface PCB for incubator, wash wheel, substrate, reagent coolers (3), reagent storage air, and sample storage temperatures run through J4.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J4 to J15 at the top of the upper left card cage.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, TEMPERATURE, DSUB connects J15 to J1 on the Temperature Sensor Interface PCB.
  • Temperature Sensor Interface PCB is located near the bottom of the sample pick-and-place gantry tower, slightly to the left of the analytical module.
Note:
  • The normal temperature sensor output is approximately 0.39 V @ 3 °C, 0.64 V @ 27 °C, 0.80 V @ 37 °C.
  • When the sensor is disconnected, the output = 0 V and the temp display = –21 °C.
  • A thermistor resistance table is included in this document.
