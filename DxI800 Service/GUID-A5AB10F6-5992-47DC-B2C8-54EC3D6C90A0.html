Pressure System Diagram

Notes

These notes apply to both the onboard compressors and house air systems.
  • Orange tubing is pressurized to 32 to 35 psi.
  • The Lower Cabinet Interface PCB actuates the condensation release valve.
  • The Pressure Sensor on the Interface PCB signals:
    • A circuit to start the compressor when the pressure drops to 32 psi.
    • The circuit to stop the compressor when the pressure reaches 35 psi.

Diagram - DxI Compressed Air System

Onboard Compressor

The diagram provides details of the onboard compressed air system.
  • Gray tubing is pressurized to 20 psi.
  • Orange tubing is pressurized to 32 to 35 psi.

House Air

The diagram provides details of the compressed air system with house air connected.

Note:
  • The tubing added for connection to house air is shown as yellow in the diagram, but it is actually black. It can be pressurized at 35 psi or 50 psi.
  • Black tubing in the instrument before the house air installation is pressurized to 20 psi.
