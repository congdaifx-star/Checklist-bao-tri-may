Securing Instrument Components

This procedure describes how to prepare a DxI instrument for shipment. Securing Instrument Components is the third of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.

Required Materials
  • Tie-wraps
  • Tape
  • Small piece of polyfoam or alternative packing material approximately 1 in x 1 in x 0.25 in
    Caution: Do not use Styrofoam. The electrically charged beads can attach to instrument components.

This procedure is the third of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI. All four procedures are summarized in the Deinstall Quick Reference. If an instrument is being moved within a building or temporarily stored, not all procedures are necessary. When performing the deinstallation procedures, consider:

  • Moving and shipping conditions
  • Final destination
  • Environmental issues
Perform this procedure when:
  • Preparing an instrument for storage.
  • Deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. If the instrument is on, shutdown the instrument and PC.
    2. Remove the center gray rear cover and place the cover in a location where it is safe from damage.
    3. Remove the upper rear cover and place the cover in a location where it is safe from damage.
    4. Open the upper left and right front covers.
  2. Open the SPU.
    1. Open the upper left cover of the instrument.
    2. Loosen the locking screw.
      The locking screw prevents the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument. It is not necessary to remove the screw because the SPU plate is slotted.
    3. Remove the 3/16-in hex bolt located near the right end of the SPU cover.
    4. Open the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    5. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin that holds the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    6. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end away from the instrument.
      Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    7. (For China and Canada Only.) On the front facing the edge of the Framus assembly, install the Chinese and French labels adjacent to the existing English label.
      Note: The labels are in the DxI Installation Kit.

  3. Secure the lower components of the instrument.
    1. Remove the reagent storage module cover.
    2. Place a small piece of poly foam between the reagent gripper prongs.


      Use a foam piece approximately 1 in x 1 in x 0.25 in. If poly foam is unavailable, then use an alternative packing material, such as bubble wrap or cardboard.
      Caution: Do not use Styrofoam. The electrically charged beads can attach to instrument components.
    3. Use a tie-wrap to attach the reagent gripper and foam to pipettor nest 1.


    4. Install the reagent storage module cover.
  4. Close the SPU.
    The SPU is hinged on the left side. The right side is released and can be swung out from the instrument to approximately 45 degrees.
    Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    1. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end toward the instrument.
    2. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin to attach the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    3. Close the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    4. Insert the 3/16-in hex bolt.
    5. Tighten the bolt that holds the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument (locking screw).
    6. Close the upper left cover of the instrument.
      Note: Initialize the instrument after opening and closing the SPU, because a switch is actuated when the SPU is opened.
  5. Secure the substrate bar code reader in the slot on the side of the SPU.
  6. Secure the upper components of the instrument.
    1. Use tape to secure the RV orienter cover to the bulk feeder.
    2. Use tie-wraps to secure the sample and reagent pipettors in the fully upward position.


    3. Use tie-wraps to secure the three pick-and-place units in the fully upright position.
    4. Use tie-wraps to secure the dispense and aspirate plates in the fully upright position.
    5. Remove any remaining bypass keys and close all the doors.
  7. Complete the securing process.
    1. Remove the seismic mounting brackets.
    2. Rotate the four adjustable feet into the fully upward position to prepare the instrument for movement.
      Caution: Do not raise the rear leveling feet as far as the front feet. Do not force the rear feet beyond the point where restriction is felt.
  8. If the instrument will be shipped, continue to the Deinstall: Crating procedure.
