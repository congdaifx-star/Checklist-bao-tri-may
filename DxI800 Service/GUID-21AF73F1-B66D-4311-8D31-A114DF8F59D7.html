Specs - APC RS1000 UPS

Purpose

This document provides the specifications for the APC RS1000 Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS).

History

This UPS shipped for installations beginning in late 2003.

Other Features

  • Automatic Voltage Regulation (AVR) Technology keeps the utility voltage within safe levels without using the battery, resulting in extended battery life and maximized runtime.
  • USB and serial connectivity
  • Audible Alarms
  • Automatic Self Test
  • Automatic Shutdown
  • Building wiring fault indicator
  • Cold-start, Easy Overload Recovery
  • Hot Swap Batteries
  • Intelligent Battery Management
  • Lightning and Surge Protection
  • Overload Indicator
  • Replace Battery Indicator
  • Resettable Circuit Breaker
  • Replaceable Batteries
