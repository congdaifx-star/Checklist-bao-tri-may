J2 - PCB - Interface - Sample Storage

  • The 24V power and stepper motor drive signals from the MCC0 PCB run through J2.
  • The HARNESS, SAMPLE HANDLING (P/N 116313) connects the Sample Storage Module Interface PCB J2 to JP02 on the MCC Backplane.
  • The Harness also connects SPU I/F PCB J2 & Sample Pipettor I/F J3 to MCC Backplane JP01.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.
