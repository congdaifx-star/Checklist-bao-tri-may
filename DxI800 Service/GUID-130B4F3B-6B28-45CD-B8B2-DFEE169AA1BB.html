Conn. Overview - PCB - Power Distribution

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

HARNESS, INTERFACE POWER (P/N 116473) connects J1 to:
  • Lower Cabinet Interface PCB J2.
  • Analytic Power Interface PCB J3.
  • Sample Storage Peltier.
  • Peri / Vacuum Pump Interface PCB J4.

The Vacuum / Inc / Wash / Sample 24V power run through J1.

Includes 24V for the:
  • Sample storage Peltier
  • Incubator heater
  • Wash carousel heaters
  • Three vacuum pumps
  • Two pressure pumps
  • Select valves for wash buffer and waste bottles
  • Moisture bleed valve
Note: The Vacuum / Incubator / Wash / Sample 24V power runs through Q3 (controlled by the 24V power switch SW1). It is not disabled by the cover switches.

J2

HARNESS, MCC BACKPLANE POWER (P/N 116453) connects J2 to JP1 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 24V power to the MCC PCB runs through J2.

Power to all motors and sensors controlled by the 8 MCC PCBs, including the peristaltic pump motors. Also powers the five MCC card cage fans.

J3

HARNESS, MAIN 24V DC POWER (P/N 116841) connects J3 to the Power Supplies.

The 24V power from the PS1, PS2, and PS3 power supplies runs through J3.

J4

HARNESS, CPU POWER (P/N 110722) connects J4 to J2 on the CPU Power Distribution PCB.

The 24V power to the CPU Power Distribution PCB runs through J4.

Devices powered through the CPU card cage include:
  • AU hard drive
  • Network hub
  • CPU card cage fans (two)
  • CPU Power Distribution PCB.
  • CPU, COMC, NVRAM, and DAQC PCBs
  • Reference LED for luminometer

J5

HARNESS, +24V ULTRA POWER (P/N 116461) connects J5 to JP2 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 24V power to the Ultrasonics PCBs runs through J5.

Note: The ultrasonic power runs through Q6 (controlled by the cover interlock switch J13), and through Q2 (controlled by the 24V power switch SW1) .

J6

Not used.

The 24V spare power output (not used) runs through J6.

Note: The spare power runs through Q6 (controlled by the cover interlock switch J13), through Q2 (controlled by the 24V power switch SW1) and through F6 (3A).

J7

CABLE, POWER DISTRIBUTION (P/N 116561) connects J7 to J14 at the top of the upper left card cage.

CABLE, RIBBON, DSUB9 (P/N 116581) connects J14 to J5 on the DAQC PCB.

The status signals going to the DAQC PCB for all the 24V output branches run through J7.

Note: The luminometer HVPS 24V power runs through fuse F7 (0.9A), through Q6 (controlled by the cover interlock switch J13), and through Q2 (controlled by the 24V power switch SW1) to DACQ PCB J5-9 and then out on DAQC PCB J12 to the luminometer. This cable does not power anything in the CPU card cage.

J8

HARNESS, ANALYTICAL MODULE POWER (P/N 110972) connects J8 to J3 on the Analytical Module Interface PCB.

The 24V power to the analytical module runs through J8. Used for mixer motor and ejector plate air valve.

Note: The analytical module 24V power runs through Q3 (controlled by the 24V power switch SW1). It is not disabled by the cover switches.

J9

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, POWER DISTRIBUTION (P/N 116292) connects J9 to J96 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The pulse width modulation (PWM) control signals from the COMC PCB for the main blower fan, sample storage Peltier, and reagent cooler Peltiers run through J9.

The PWM signal path is COMC PCB to MCC Backplane PCB J1 and J2 then to MCC Backplane PCB J96 and then to Power Distribution PCB J9.

J10

HARNESS, REAGENT PELTIER DRIVE (P/N 116443) connects J10 to Peltiers 1, 2, and 3 (J6, J7 and J8).

The 24V power and PWM grounds to the three reagent Peltiers run through J10.

Note: The reagent Peltier 24V power runs through Q5 (controlled by the reagent coolers power switch SW2). It is not disabled by the cover switches.

J11

Not used.

Not used.

J12

Main blower fan connects to J12.

The FAN_PWM+ signal is used to control the (12V to 0V) output of Q1 for main blower fan speed control (J3-2 = 0 V at fan full speed).

Note:
  • Measure main blower fan PWM % at Q1 gate pin 2. Full Speed (0% PWM) = 12 V, Off (100% PWM) = 0 V.
  • Main blower fan has a 24V continuous input and a 12V PWM input for speed control.

J13

HARNESS, COVER SWITCH (P/N 124374) connects J13 to the SWITCH, DUAL, LINE, INTERRUPT (P/N 7044A) at the upper left and upper right covers (same as Access) and the SWITCH, PANEL MOUNT,

CHERRY EG8-40A (P/N 129531) interlock switch for the SPU assembly that engages when the SPU assembly is swung closed.

The cover interlock switch harness connects to J13.

Note:
  • Opening any one of the interlocks cuts off 24 V DC power from MCC (J2), ultrasonics (J5), luminometer (J7), and spare (J6).
  • Confirm that there is 24V at J13-1 before troubleshooting or replacing any cover interlock switch.

J14

CABLE, REAGENT FAN POWER (P/N 111471) connects J14 to J5 on the Reagent Storage Interface PCB.

The 24V power to the three reagent storage fans and the reagent BCR window heater runs through J14.
