Replacing Pick-and-Place Service Cable

This procedure describes how to replace the service cable on any pick-and-place module.

Removal and installation of the service cable on any pick-and-place module requires gradual removal of the original cable while installing portions of the new cable.

Replace the service cable when:
  • the service cable is cracked.
  • the service cable is suspect in intermittent problems.
  • STS requests replacement.

Reference Graphics:

  1. Replace the service cable.
    1. Remove the pick-and-place module gantry to be serviced.
      Note: Refer to the P&P: Removing Incubator Pick-and-Place, P&P: Removing Sample Pick-and-Place, or P&P: Removing Wash Pick-and-Place procedure.
    2. Cut the tie-wrap that is attached to the upper end of the long guide, and remove the two screws (3/32-in hex) attaching the long guide.
      Caution: The PCB should be resting on the work surface. Be cautious not to damage the PCB or connector pins.
    3. Loosen the screw (3/32-in hex) securing the restrain clip that holds down the connector on the pick-and-place Hall effect PCB, and disconnect the ribbon cable.
    4. Connect the replacement cable to the pick-and-place Hall Effect PCB, and tighten the restrain clip that holds down the connector.
      Note: Confirm that the correct end of the cable is connected to the pick-and-place Hall effect PCB
    5. Route the replacement cable and tubing replacing the old cable.
    6. Install the two screws (3/32-in hex) that attach the long guide.
      Caution: Confirm that the cable and tubing are not pinched or twisted.
      Note: With the guide attached, the cable should slide up and down easily.
    7. Attach a replacement tie-wrap near the top of the long guide, ensuring that the tie-wrap is on the machined slot. Cut off any excess tie-wrap.
    8. Invert the pick-and-place gantry.
      Note: Do not rest the PCB on the work surface.
    9. Remove the two screws (3/32-in hex) attaching the short guide, and route the replacement cable.
    10. Loosen the screw (5/64-in hex) securing the metal retainer to the chain mount.
    11. Remove the old cable, and route the replacement cable around the turn.
      Note: Keep the cable under the air tubing as it routes around the turn. Verify the cabling and tubing are not pinched.
    12. Install the two screws (3/32-in hex) that attach the short guide, and tighten the metal retainer.
      Note: Verify the cabling and tubing is not pinched.
  2. Remove the service cable from the guide chain.
    1. Disconnect the service cable connector from J8 on the pick-and-place interface PCB.
      Note: Notice how the service cable is routed from the PCB to the service chain before removing.
    2. Remove the service cable from the plastic wire bundle clamp, and loosen the screw (5/64-in hex) securing the metal retainer on the main backplate.
    3. Slide the pick-and-place carriage to the end of motion, by the interface PCB.
      Note: If servicing the sample pick-and-place, slide the carriage to the end of motion by the z-motor.
    4. Remove the two screws (3/32-in hex) that attach the service chain to the chain mount on the z-carriage.
      Note: Mark the two holes that the screws are in.
    5. Open all the accessible chain covers.
    6. Remove the service cable from the open chain covers.
  3. Install the service cable into the chain.
    1. Route the replacement cable above the air tubing the entire length of the chain.
      Caution: The chain must be installed in the chain without twists.
    2. Close all the chain covers.
    3. Install the two screws (3/32-in hex) that attach the service chain to the chain mount.
    4. Remove the two screws that attach the other end of the service chain to the backplate.
    5. Open the chain covers, remove the old cable, route the replacement cable, and then close the chain covers.
    6. Install the two screws (3/32-in hex) that attach the service chain to the backplate.
    7. Place the replacement cable into the wire bundle clamp and under the metal retainer on the main backplate.
    8. Tighten the screw (5/64-in hex) securing the metal retainer.
    9. Route the replacement cable between the main backplate and the interface PCB standoff.
    10. Connect the replacement cable to J8 on the interface PCB.
      Note: Confirm that the correct end of the cable is connected to J8.
  4. Verify the proper operation of the pick-and-place.
    1. Install the pick-and-place module in the instrument, and manually verify the vertical and horizontal movement of the carriage. Verify that:
      • No cables or air tubes are pinched.
      • The force required to move the carriage is steady.
      • The carriage movement is smooth and even.
      Note: Refer to the P&P: Installing Incubator Pick-and-Place, P&P: Installing Sample Pick-and-Place, or P&P: Installing Wash Pick-and-Place procedure.
    2. Verify pick-and-place alignments.
    3. Run 100 reps of the vessel exerciser.
      Note: Select the route to run that is the same as the pick-and-place serviced.
