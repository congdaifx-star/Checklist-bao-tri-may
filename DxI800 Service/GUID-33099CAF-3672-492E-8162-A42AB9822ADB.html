J2 - PCB - Interface - SPU

  • The 24V power and SPU motor drive signals from the MCC0 PCB run through J2.
  • HARNESS, SAMPLE HANDLING, (P/N 116313) connects J2 to JP01 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
  • The harness also connects MCC Backplane JP02 to Sam Storage I/F J2 and MCC Backplane JP01 to sample pipettor J2 and J3.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.
