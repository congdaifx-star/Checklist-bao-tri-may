Installing Precision or Sample Pump

This procedure describes how to install the precision pump or the sample pump when either of these pumps is removed or replaced.

Perform this procedure when either of these pumps is removed or replaced.

  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the upper left and right front covers.
    2. Remove power from the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  2. Install the pump.
    1. While holding the pump at an angle, lower it down and toward the back of the reservoir.
      Caution: To prevent damage to the fluidics interface PCB from the pump assembly, tip the valve end down and lower the pump until the valve motor clears the reservoir.
      Caution: If while installing the sample pump it catches on the cables of the upper left card cage, remove precision pump 1 to create more room for installing the sample pump.
    2. Tighten the two hex-screws that hold the pump to the reservoir.
      Caution: Confirm that the o-rings are on the bottom of the pump assembly.
      Note: These screws are at the front of the pump, close to the pressure sensor, and enter the housing vertically.
    3. Make connections to the fluidics interface PCB.
      • If installing a sample pump, reconnect J6 (Pump Power), J22 (Valve Power), and J27 (Pump Sensor) to the fluidics interface PCB.
      • If installing a precision pump, reconnect he pump power, valve power, pump sensor, and valve sensor connectors for any specific pump to the fluidics interface PCB.
    4. Reconnect the pressure sensor cable on the pressure sensor interface PCB (located on the front of the reservoir).
    5. Connect the supply tubing connector to the buffer reservoir for the pump.
  3. Insert the interlock bypass.
  4. Restore power to the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  5. Initialize the system.
  6. Prime the specific pump 10 times and monitor for leaks.
  7. Remove interlock bypasses and close the instrument covers.
  8. Initialize the system.
  9. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
