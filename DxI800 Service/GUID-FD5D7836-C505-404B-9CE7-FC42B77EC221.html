J1 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Vacuum / Pressure Sensor

  • J1 carries the pressure / vacuum sensor and lower case temperature outputs from the Vacuum / Pressure Sensor Interface PCB to the DACQ PCB.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, VACUUM, DSUB connects J1 to J16 at the top of the Upper Left Card Cage.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J16 to J2 on the DAQC PCB.

Note:
  • The pin number assignments going from J1 on the Vacuum / Pressure Interface PCB to J2 on the DAQC PCB are interleaved due to the way pins are numbered on a ribbon cable and a D-sub connector cable.
  • The pressure sensor output is typically 0V in standby, -0.4V at 30mmHg (typical with vacuum and peri pumps running), -2V at 150mmHg (Aspirate probe wash), and -4V at 300mmHg (tubing clamped, vacuum pump running).
