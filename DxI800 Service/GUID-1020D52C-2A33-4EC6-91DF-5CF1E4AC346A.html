J1 - PCB - Interface - Analytical Module (Upper)

  • The 5V power, Eject plate Home, Mixer Tach/Speed/Direction data signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, ANALYTICAL connects J1 to J72 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
J1 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

GND_MCC7

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-1.

Ground from MCC7 PCB. TP11

2

+5V_MCC7

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-2.

5V power from MCC7 PCB.

3

Mixer Eject Arm Up

5V or 0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J72-3 from J7-3.

Home: TP20 = 5V and D2 LED is Off. In the normal Ready state the Eject plate is Up .

Eject: TP20 = 0V and D2 is On. At the Eject position the Eject plate is Down .

4

Aspirate Arm Z Home

5V or 0V

To MCC Backplane J72-4 from J6-3.

Home: TP19 = 0V and D3 LED is On.

5

Dispense Arm Z Home

5V or 0V

To MCC Backplane J72-5 from J5-3.

Home: TP18 = 0V and D4 LED is On.

6

+5V_MCC7

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-6.

5V power from MCC7 PCB. (Not connected on this PCB)

7

Not Used

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-7.

Not Connected

8

Not Used

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-8.

Not Connected

9

Not Used

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-9.

Not Connected

10

+5V_LED7

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-10.

5V power from MCC7 PCB used for indicator LEDs.

11

Mixer Arm Eject

Air Valve

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-11.

Eject: TP17 = 5V and D9 LED is On. 5V control signal to Q1 grounds J4-2. In the normal Ready state the Eject plate is Up. At the Eject position the Eject Plate is Down.

12

Mixer Motor Enable

5V or 0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-12.

Enabled: TP9 = 5V and D1 LED is On.

13

Mixer Motor Direction

5V or 0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-13.

Direction CW: TP7 = 0V. TP8 = 5V. TP8 is TP7 inverted by U7D-8.

14

Mixer Speed Up/Down

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-14.

Digital input for Digital Potentiometer U5-2.

Direction Up (Increase ): TP6 = 5V, Down =0V

15

Mixer Speed Increment

5V or 0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-15.

Digital input for Digital Potentiometer U5-1.

Increment: TP5 = 5V pulse.

16

GND_MCC7

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J72-16.

Ground from MCC7 PCB. TP11

17

PCB Rev 0

0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J72-17.

JP1 grounded when Rev is 0.

18

PCB Rev1

0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J72-18.

JP2 grounded when Rev is 1.

19

Mixer Tach+

0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J72-19 then to COMM PCB J1.

Mixer tachometer signal from U9-5. Home/Index: TP10 = 5V or 0V.

20

Mixer Tach -

5V

To MCC Backplane PCB J72-20 then to COMM PCB J1.

Ground for mixer tach signal.
