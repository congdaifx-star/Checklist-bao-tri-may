Specs - Monitor - DTx

Purpose

This document provides the specifications of the DTx touchscreen color monitor.

Specifications

Parameter Specification
Description 17-in LCD monitor with resistive touchscreen
Brand Name DTx
Brightness and Luminance 300 cd/m² Typical
Color, Cabinet Black
Contents Monitor and these items:
  • Three cables are included with monitor. DVI and audio cable are not included.
  • Printed copy of the Beckman Coulter MSS for installing and calibrating the touch screen on this monitor.
  • Conversion Mounting Plate (100 mm x 100 mm to VESA 75 (75 mm x 75 mm)).
Controls
  • Press for power ON/OFF; OSD Control
  • When pressed, OSD menu displays
  • When pressed for one sec, the power turns off
  • Rotate the rotary switch
  • Adjustments and selections ([+], [-])
Controller, Interface, Location of Touch Controller 3M R-5 SC-502S v3.0 Critical

Serial Interface Cable DB9 P/M - DB9 P/F Semi-Critical

Dimensions, Display Area, Active 335.90 mm ±0.20 mm (W) by 270.40 mm ±0.20 mm (H)

432 mm (17 inch) Diagonal

Dimensions, Monitor 378 mm (W) x 314 mm (H) x 70 mm (D)
Environmental Conditions Operating Temperature: 5 °C to 35 °C

Storage Temperature: -20 °C to 60 °C

Humidity: 20 to 80% RH

Surface Temperature: 50 °C maximum (at Ta=35 °C)

Frequency, Horizontal Video 31.5 to 60 kHz
Frequency, Vertical Video 57 to 75 Hz
Model Number DTx 15790 PC17095R
Pitch, Dot/Stripe 1280 (W) x 1024 (H) RGB vertically stripe with normally white
Power Requirements 100 to 250 V AC (auto-switching), 50/60 Hz
Power Supply Universal input and auto-ranging 100 to 250 V AC ±10% at 50 / 60 Hz
Power Consumption 38 W (Typical) at full brightness; 2.5 W at power-saving.
Regulatory Approvals FCC CE UL/CSA C-Tick
Resolution, Native, Monitor SXGA+ It is possible to display 16.7 million colors (RGB 6 bits + Hi-RFC data)
Timing, Display, Minimum, Monitor Response time

tr+td = 5 ms (Type / at Ta of 25 °C)

Type, Device This LCD device is a TFT active matrix type that is mounted with TFT driver module, a cold-cathode florescent tube back-light.
Type, Display Color TFT LCD monitor
Type, Interface 15-pin mini D-sub SXGA+
Type, Interface, Touchscreen Serial port
Type, Touchscreen 5-wire resistive
Type, Screen LCD device is a TFT active matrix type that is mounted with TFT driver module, a cold-cathode florescent tube back-light
Vendor DTx Inc.
Video Standards Analog Signal Input

Video signal: 0.714Vp-p / 75 Ohm

Sync signal (Separate and Composite): TTL level / 1000 Ohm

Sync on Green

Visual Angle Horizontal / Vertical: 160 degree (±80 degree) / 160 degree (±80 degree) CR=10
Warranty
  • 3-Year Parts and Labor Warranty
  • 3-Year Backlight Warranty
Weight Approximately:

11.5 kg

25.4 pounds
