Installing Wash Pump

This procedure describes how to install either of the two wash pumps onto the fluids reservoir.

  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the upper left and right front covers.
    2. Remove power from the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  2. Install the wash pump.
    1. While holding the pump at an angle, lower it down and toward the back of the reservoir.
      Caution: Do not damage the fluidics interface PCB with the pump assembly.
      Caution: Make sure the three o-rings on the fluidics reservoir where the pump is being placed are in position and seated correctly.
    2. Tighten the two hex-screws that hold the pump assembly to the reservoir.
      Note: The screws are toward the front of the assembly, by the pressure sensor, and enter the housing vertically.
    3. Connect the motor and sensor wires for each pump to the fluidics interface PCB.
      Each pump has four connectors:
      • Valve motor
      • Valve sensor
      • Pump motor
      • Pump sensor
    4. Verify that the fittings for each pump are connected to the fluidics manifold (D1 and D2 for wash pump 1 and D3 and APW for wash pump 2).
    5. Verify that the probes are in the correct position and are seated correctly on the probe plates.
  3. Insert the interlock bypass.
  4. Restore power to the motors using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  5. Initialize the system.
  6. Verify the pump installation.
    1. Prime the specific pump 10 times and monitor for leaks.
    2. Remove interlock bypasses and close the instrument covers.
    3. Initialize the system.
    4. Perform a system check and verify that all specifications are met.
    5. Run QC and verify that all customer specifications are met.
