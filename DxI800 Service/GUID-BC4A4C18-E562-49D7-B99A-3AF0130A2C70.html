Removing Wash Pump

This procedure describes how to remove either of the two wash pumps from the fluids reservoir.

Remove either of the two wash pumps from the fluids reservoir when errors occur on the wash pump or the wash valve.
  1. Prepare the instrument before removing either wash pump.
    Note: Wash pump 1 supplies fluid to dispense probes 1 and 2. Wash pump 2 supplies fluid to dispense probe 3 and aspirate probe 1 wash.
    1. Open the instrument upper left and right covers.
    2. Remove power from the motors by toggling the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)."
    3. Drain the probes.
      • If removing wash pump 1:
        1. Remove dispense probes 1 and 2 from the dispense plate, and place the probes on a paper towel.
        2. Disconnect the fittings labeled D1 and D2 from the fluidics manifold.
        3. Let all the liquid drain from the probes.

          Reconnect the probe fittings to the manifold, and place the dispense probes into position on the probe plate.

      • If removing wash pump 2:
        1. Remove dispense probe 3 and aspirate probe 1 from their respective plates.
        2. Disconnect the fittings labeled D3 and APW from the fluidics manifold.
        3. Drain all the liquid from the probes.
        4. Reconnect the probe fittings to the manifold, and place the probes into position on the probe plates.
    4. Locate the four pump connectors on the fluidics interface PCB (above and behind the pump).
      • Valve motor
      • Valve sensor
      • Pump motor
      • Pump sensor
    5. Disconnect the connectors for the pump being removed.
  2. Remove the wash pump.
    1. Remove the two hex-screws that hold the pump assembly to the reservoir.
      Note: The screws are toward the front of the assembly, by the pressure sensor, and enter the housing vertically.
    2. Remove the pump.
      Caution: Avoid damaging the fluidics interface PCB with the pump assembly.
      Caution: Three o-rings are located on the fluids reservoir for each pump being removed. When removing the pump, verify that the o-rings remain in place in the recessed mounting holes.
