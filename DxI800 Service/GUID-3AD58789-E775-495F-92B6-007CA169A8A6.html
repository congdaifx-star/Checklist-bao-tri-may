Removing Substrate Pump

This procedure describes how to remove the substrate pump when the substrate pump needs service.

  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the upper right cover, or remove the upper rear cover from the instrument.
      Note: The pump can be serviced from the front or from the rear of the instrument.
    2. Use the All DC power switch on the Power Distribution Module to remove power from the pump.
  2. Remove the substrate pump from the instrument.
    1. Remove these items from the substrate pump:
      • Bubble detector clamp
      • Bubble detector cable to the pump bubble detector PCB
      • Substrate supply line
      • Substrate probe
      • Foam insulation
    2. Optional: If servicing the pump from the front of the instrument, then remove the two bolts holding the pump support arm to the instrument frame.
      Removing the pump support arm from the instrument allows more access to the mounting bolts and wiring.
    3. Remove the pump wiring from the cable clamps leading to the substrate interface PCB.
    4. Remove the two screws holding the pump to the pump support arm.
    5. Disconnect the cables to the substrate interface PCB located behind the substrate loader.
      • J13 - Substrate Valve
      • J16 - Substrate Pump Motor
      • J6 - Home Sensor
      • J12 - Substrate Heater
    6. Disconnect the thermistor cable to the temperature interface PCB at J4.
      Note:
      • If you are not removing the pump from the instrument completely, then you might not need to disconnect the cables.
      • To allow additional access to the connectors, remove the substrate loader (four captive screws).
