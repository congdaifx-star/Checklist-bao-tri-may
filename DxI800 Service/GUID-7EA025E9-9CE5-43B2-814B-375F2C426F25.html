J1 - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

  • The logic signals from the Interface PCB to the MCC2 PCB run through J1.
  • The signals are Pick and Place X/Y/Z Home, End of Travel, Eject, Ejector Sense, and PCB Revision.
  • The 24V and 5V power to the Interface PCB from the MCC2 PCB also run through J1.
    • The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SAMPLE P&P (P/N 116191) connects the Sample Pick and Place Interface PCB J1 to J21 on the MCC backplane.
    • The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, INCUBATE P&P (P/N 116201) connects the Incubator Pick and Place Interface PCB J1 to J22 on the MCC backplane.
    • The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, WASH P&P (P/N 116211) connects the Wash Pick and Place Interface PCB J1 to J23 on the MCC backplane.
