J1 - PCB - Interface - Analytical Module

  • The Wash Wheel and Incubator Wheel Heater PWM, Home Sensor, and Encoder Sensor signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, ANALYTIC POWER (P/N 116271) connects J1 to J71 (MCC7) on the MCC Backplane PCB.
J1 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

GND_MCC7

0V

From MCC backplane PCB J71-1.

Ground. TP8.

2

+5V_MCC7

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J71-2.

5V power from MCC7. TP6.

3

Incubator - Home

5V or 0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J71-3 from J13-3.

Home: TP5 = 0V and D7 LED is On.

4

Wash Wheel - Home

5V or 0V

To MCC Backplane PCB J71-4 from J14-3.

Home: TP7 = 0V and D6 LED is On.

5

Wash Wheel Heater PWM +

5V or 0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J71-7 to U3-1.

Heater Active: TP3 = 5V and D2 LED is On.

The Wash Wheel Heater PWM+ signal activates Q1 to ground J7-2.

0V
