J1 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Fluidics

  • The 5V power, the Sample Wash and Precision Pump/Valve Home sensor, the Peri pump Index sensor, the Sample Pump Solenoid Valve, and the Buffer Reservoir Float Switch signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, FLUIDICS (P/N 116251) connects J1 to J62 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
