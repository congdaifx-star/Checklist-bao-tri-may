LEDs - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

  • These are the LEDs provided on the Pick and Place Interface PCB.
Note: The LEDs on this PCB are visible from either side of the PCB.
LED Color Function

D1

Green

X/Y Axis End of Travel.

End of Travel position: TP4 = 0V and D1 LED is on.

D2

Green

X/Y Axis Home.

Home position: TP2 = 0V and D2 LED is on.

D4

Green

RV Ejector.

RV ejector is down: TP7 = 0V and D4 LED is on.

D5

Green

SV Ejector.

SV ejector is down: TP8 = 0V and D5 LED is on.

D6

Green

DV Ejector.

DV ejector is down: TP9 = 0V and D6 LED is on.

D7

Green

Z Axis Home.

Home position: TP5 = 5V and D7 LED is on.

These diagrams show the status of the LEDs and Test Points when the system is in the Ready state.
