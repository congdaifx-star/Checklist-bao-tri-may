Replacing Reagent Storage Pipettor Nest

This procedure describes how to replace a reagent storage pipettor nest.

Caution: This procedure will cause all on board expired packs to become unusable due to the remap.
Two configurations of the reagent storage module affect nest replacement:
  • Instruments with serial numbers 602696 and below or 90567 and below, have pinned nests. These reagent storage pipettor nests have no x-direction adjustment.
  • Instruments serial numbers 602697and higher or 900568 and higher, have slotted nests and slotted holes in the back of the reagent storage module. These reagent storage pipettor nests are adjusted in the x-direction to facilitate the reagent pipettor adjustment to the reagent pack.
  1. Unload and refrigerate the reagent packs.
    1. Select Supplies > Reagent Supplies > Unload all Reagent Packs.
    2. When the load tray opens with packs, remove them.
    3. Place the removed packs into refrigerated storage.
  2. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the main upper right cover.
    2. Use both switches on the Power Distribution PCB to remove power from the motors and reagent coolers.
  3. Open the SPU.
    1. Open the upper left cover of the instrument.
    2. Loosen the locking screw.
      The locking screw prevents the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument. It is not necessary to remove the screw because the SPU plate is slotted.
    3. Remove the 3/16-in hex bolt located near the right end of the SPU cover.
    4. Open the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    5. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin that holds the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    6. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end away from the instrument.
      Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    7. (For China and Canada Only.) On the front facing the edge of the Framus assembly, install the Chinese and French labels adjacent to the existing English label.
      Note: The labels are in the DxI Installation Kit.

  4. Remove the cover on the reagent storage module (six 5/32-in hex screws).
  5. Replace the reagent storage pipettor nest.
    1. Remove the two screws securing the nest to the reagent storage module, and remove the nest.
      Note: Do not lose the spring that is seated behind the alignment slide.
    2. Assemble the L-shaped holder and spring to the front pipettor nest.
    3. Insert the rear holder and spring through the back of the pipettor nest.
    4. Install the nest, and secure the nest into position using the two screws that were removed.
      Note: Verify that the spring is located behind the alignment slide and that the slides are oriented correctly.
  6. Perform alignments and complete service tasks.
    1. Manually move the gripper to the home position (lower left corner).
    2. Bypass the cover interlock switches on the SPU and reagent storage by taping them down.
    3. Power on the MCC boards and reagent storage coolers.
    4. Initialize the instrument with Remap enabled.
      Warning: Do not place your hands inside the storage module during initialization, the gripper and reagent load drawer will be moving.
    5. Confirm that there were no gripper errors during the remap process.
    6. For instruments with serial numbers 602697 and higher or 900568 and higher:
      1. Adjust the reagent pipettor nest to reagent pipettor.
        Note: Refer to theRS: Adjusting Reagent Pipettor Nest procedure.
      2. Align reagent pipettor to reagent pack.
        Note: Refer to the RP: Aligning Reagent Pipettor procedure.
    7. Perform the gripper alignments to the pipettor nests.
      Note: Refer to the RS: Aligning Reagent Gripper X- and Z-Axisprocedure.
    8. Remove the tape from the interlock switches.
    9. Install the cover on the reagent storage module (six 5/32-in hex screws).
  7. Close the SPU.
    The SPU is hinged on the left side. The right side is released and can be swung out from the instrument to approximately 45 degrees.
    Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    1. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end toward the instrument.
    2. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin to attach the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    3. Close the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    4. Insert the 3/16-in hex bolt.
    5. Tighten the bolt that holds the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument (locking screw).
    6. Close the upper left cover of the instrument.
      Note: Initialize the instrument after opening and closing the SPU, because a switch is actuated when the SPU is opened.
  8. Initialize the system with Remap selected.
  9. Using the reagent pack load tray, load all the reagent packs that were removed from the reagent storage module.
  10. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
