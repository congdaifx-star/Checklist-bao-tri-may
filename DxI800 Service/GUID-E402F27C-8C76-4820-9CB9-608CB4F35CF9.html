Removing Bulk Vessel Feeder

This procedure describes how to remove the bulk vessel feeder module.

Since the bulk vessel feeder is rather bulky and visually and physically obstructive, the bulk vessel feeder is designed for easy removal for servicing.

  1. Power off the upper electronics section by moving the upper switch on the power distribution PCB to the off position.
  2. Prepare the bulk feeder for removal.
    1. To disconnect the air supply tubing entering the manifold from the rear of the bulk feeder module, push the tubing toward the air fitting while pressing the release ring.
    2. Disconnect the electrical connectors from the bulk feeder interface PCB located on the front of the module. Both connectors are located near the left side of the PCB:
      • J16 - Elevator Motor Power Harness
      • J1 - Sensor Signals (ribbon cable)
    3. Remove the two disconnected plugs from the harness clamps on the right side of the module.
  3. Remove the bulk feeder module.
    1. Remove the two screws that fasten the upper support bracket to the bulk feeder.
      Note: Do not loosen or remove the two screws that attach the bracket to the instrument. Loosening or removing the bracket from the instrument will require adjusting the bulk feeder module.
    2. Locate and remove the large cap-screw located at the front and bottom of the bulk feeder module.
    3. Slide the module forward to move it out of its retaining bracket at the rear and bottom of the module.
    4. Carefully lift the module out of the instrument.
    5. Place the module so it stands securely in a vertical position.
      Caution: Place the hopper in a location where debris, dust, or other possible contaminants cannot enter the area containing the vessels. If necessary, cover the module to prevent contaminants from entering the hopper or place the vessels into a clean container to remove the vessels from the hopper.
