Troubleshooting Pump Errors - Sample Pump

Purpose

This procedure describes how to troubleshoot errors on the sample pump.

Troubleshooting Procedure

Caution:
  • Place a lint free towel under each reagent probe to catch the wash buffer that drains when the pump, valve, or other fluid components are removed.
  • Remove power to the components when swapping, removing, or installing.

Perform these steps to troubleshoot errors on the sample pump. Repeat the initial failing test after any major issues are identified and resolved.

Possible Issue Action Step
1 If the instrument is a new install, the piston and seal can be seized with dried wash buffer. Clean the pump.
Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure.
2 For Pressure Errors only: If the errors occur only with Daily Clean or Special Clean, Contrad or Citranox may be expired.
  1. Verify when the errors occur.
  2. Check the expiration dates on the Contrad and Citranox bottles. If the bottles are expired, then order new bottles.
  3. Make a new dilution of the fluid that the errors occur on:
    • Citranox: 1:5 dilution
    • Contrad: Full strength.
    • Methanol: 70%.
3 For Pressure Errors only: If the errors occur only with occasional patient samples, sample quality may be an issue.
  1. Verify when the errors occur.
  2. Check the sample quality.
  3. Work with the customer to address any issues that are identified.
Refer to these sample handling documents in the Access Applications Database:
  • Preanalytical Variables.
  • AccuTnI Support Portfolio.
4 Incorrect rack and tube combination being used
  1. In the Main Menu, select Configure > System Setup  > Rack ID Setup and verify the rack configuration in the user interface matches what the customer is using.
  2. Verify the correct sample cups are being used (for example, 0.5-mL cups and 2-mL cups should be Beckman Coulter Access 2 cups).
5 Wash buffer not being delivered Prime the sample pipettor 20 times and watch the fluid lines to verify wash buffer is being delivered and there are no air bubbles.
Note: Priming can resolve issues with a sticking pump or help clear an obstructed sample probe.
6 Upper wash buffer reservoir is empty. Verify that the upper wash buffer reservoir contains buffer. If the float sensor is stuck to the top of the reservoir, clean the float sensor with DI water and relaunch the AU.
A rare failure mode for pressure errors occurs when the float sensor on the upper wash buffer reservoir adheres to the top of the reservoir. If the error occurs often enough to catch regularly on priming, then prime to isolate the source to either the pump and valve or the tubing and probe:
  1. Position the pipettor over the wash tower.
  2. Place a lint-free tissue under the pipettor probe.
  3. Disconnect the union fitting (remove the clear fitting about 5 inches down from the fitting, not the white fitting) of the pipettor tubing and allow the tubing to drain.
  4. Remove the lint-free tissue.
  5. Place the upper tubing into a beaker to prevent wash buffer from spraying during priming.
  6. Prime the affected pipettor.
    • If the error continues, then troubleshoot for pressure sensor errors in this table.
    • If the error does not continue, then continue troubleshooting for damaged or missing parts in this table.
7 Damaged or missing parts
  1. Inspect the sample probe and replace it if:
    • Teflon tubing on probe tip is damaged.
    • probe tip is bent.
    • there are signs of gel or clot.
  2. Inspect the sample pipettor tubing and connections and replace them if:
    • red-and-white quick-disconnect fitting to the pump is damaged.
    • sample pipettor tubing is damaged.
    • Union fittings are damaged or cracked.
      Note: There are two union fittings per DxI instrument in the sample pipettor fluidics pathway: One fitting is closer to the sample pump pressure sensor, the other is closer to the sample pipettor probe.
8 Pressure sensor error
  1. Power down and reseat pressure sensor connections (interface board on front of fluidics reservoir).
  2. Calibrate the pressure sensor.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Calibrating Sample and Precision Pump Pressure Sensors procedure.
  3. Swap the pressure sensors, then recalibrate both sensors.
    Note: The sample pump pressure sensor is mounted with an extra plate in a different direction but is interchangeable with other pump pressure sensors.
9 Insufficient power supply voltages
Note: Power supply voltages that are outside of specifications may cause intermittent sample pump motion errors.
Verify power supply voltages.
Note: Refer to the Electronics: Adjusting Main Power Supply Voltage procedure.
10 Connection problems Reseat the MCC PCB, ribbon cables, and power harnesses. Refer to Cables and PCBs chart in the Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision) document.
11 Pump idler pulley is shattered or missing teeth Inspect the idler pulley in the pump. If teeth are missing from the idler pulley, replace the pulley. The pulley can be replaced with an idler pulley from a reagent pump. Disable the reagent pump until a replacement idler pulley can be obtained.

12 Loose or damaged valve Inspect the valve. If the valve is loose or damaged, replace it.
13 Loose end-cap Inspect the lower black end-cap.
  • If a torque wrench and the 1-1/16 inch socket tool are available, then secure the lower cap using these specifications. Instruments with:
    • Brown Delrin guide: Torque specification of 12 in-lb (1.4 N•m)
    • Beige or gray guide: Torque specification of 10 in-lb (1.1 N•m)
  • If the tools are not available, secure by hand tightening the lower cap on the bottom of the pump. Then use a wrench to rotate the black lower cap an additional ½ hex (30°) beyond hand tight.

14 Damaged belt or belt flag
  1. Inspect for belt damage. If any damage is found, replace the belt and clean the pump.
    Note: Refer to Fluidics: Installing Pump Belt and Tensioner Mounting Bracket and Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedures.
  2. Verify that the belt flag is not tilted and rubbing on the acrylic pump block. If the belt flag is tilted, replace the belt (if damaged). Verify that the home flag does not rotate to the side when securing the screw.
15 Damaged or missing o-rings Verify that these o-rings are undamaged and positioned correctly:
  • between the pump and fluidics reservoir
  • between the pump and pressure sensor
  • in the piston and seal.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure for pictures showing the positioning of the o-ring.
16 Other damaged or dirty components Inspect and clean or replace any damaged ribbon cables, power harnesses, or piston seals.
  • If the issues continue on a previous version pump (brown Delrin guide with one or two o-rings), then perform Fluidics: Installing Pump Reliability Components (Sample/Precision/Wash) procedure
  • If parts are not available for the mod or the pump is a new pump (beige or gray guide, two o-rings), then perform Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure.
