Troubleshooting Sample Storage

Purpose

This document contains information for troubleshooting hardware issues related to the sample storage module.

Troubleshooting can be separated into these three types of errors:
  • Sample vessel (SV) handling errors
  • Positioning errors
  • Temperature errors

Reference Drawing

Sample Vessel Handling Errors

Issue Possible Cause Troubleshooting Steps or Repair Method
Dropped SV on the sample storage module (possibly lying on the sample wheel). Incorrect alignments.
Note: Position errors that are not caused by incorrect alignments produce index or position error events.
Manually check the sample pick-and-place to sample storage module alignments.
Note: Do not assume that the alignments, manual or automatic, are correct.
Sample pick-and-place - SV not found or unexpected SV errors.
  • Missing vessel in supply shuttle.
  • Weak Hall effect sensor on sample pick-and-place.
  • Worn collet on sample pick-and-place collet.
  • Leaking air valve ejects or does not eject the RV.
  • Confirm that the RV supply shuttle has three RVs in it.
  • Replace the pick-and-place sensor PCB.
  • Troubleshoot or replace the collet or air valve.

Positioning Errors

Issue Possible Cause Troubleshooting Steps or Repair Method

Indexing Error

Move To Position Error

  • Worn or loose drive belt.
  • Seized shaft bearings.
  • Dirty or corroded index sensor.
  • Obstruction between sample wheel and cover.
  • Encoder failure.
  • Check for a worn or loose drive belt.
  • Inspect the drive belt area for an obstruction.
  • Confirm the smooth motion of the carousel.
  • Disassemble and clean or replace the index sensor.
  • Replace the defective bearings
  • Inspect the top of Sample Wheel for a vessel of other object.
  • Replace the encoder.

Temperature Errors

The Displayed Temperature is: Possible Cause Troubleshooting Steps or Repair Method
-21 °C (approximately)
  • Thermistor unplugged.
  • Break in thermistor circuit.
Confirm the thermistor connections. Connect another thermistor and confirm that the temperature status changes.
50 °C (approximately)
  • Thermistor shorted.
  • Short in thermistor circuit.
Unplug the thermistor and confirm that the temperature status changes to -21 °C.
Slightly high out-of-specification and the duty cycle of the Peltier is almost 100%.
  • Heat sink clogged with dust
  • Instrument air filter dirty.
  • Weak Peltier.
Clean the air filter. Clean the heat sink fins. If the temperature is still outside of the specification, replace the Peltier.
