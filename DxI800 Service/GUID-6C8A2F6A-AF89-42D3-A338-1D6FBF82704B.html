LEDs - PCB - Interface - Bulk Feeder

LED Color Function

D1

Green

RV Hopper Motor Home/Index.

Home: TP12 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

D2

Green

RV Escrow Chute Full.

Home: TP9 = 0V and D2 LED is On.

D3

Green

Lower Cylinder Extended.

Extended: TP3 = 0V and D3 LED is On. (uses RV Hopper Full data line)

D4

Green

RV Hopper Reserve (Full).

Retracted: TP4 = 0V and D4 LED is On.

D5

Green

RV Escapement Retracted.

Retracted: TP5 = 0V and D5 LED is On.

D6

Green

Upper Cylinder Extended (not used).

Retracted: TP11 = 0V and D6 LED is On. (RV in scoop position)

D9

Green

Upper Cylinder Retracted.

Retracted: TP8 = 0V and D9 LED is On.

D10

Green

Lower Cylinder Retracted.

Retracted: TP6 = 0V and D10 LED is On.
