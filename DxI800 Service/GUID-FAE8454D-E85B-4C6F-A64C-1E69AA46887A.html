LEDs - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Peristaltic and Vacuum Pump

  • These are the LEDs provided on this PCB.

LED

Color

Function

D1

Green

Sample Wash Tower Peri Pump.

Index/Home: TP2 = 5V and D1 LED is On.

D2

Green

Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 1.

Index/Home: TP3 = 5V and D2 LED is On.

D3

Green

Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 2.

Index/Home: TP4 = 5V and D3 LED is On.

D4

Green

Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 3.

Index/Home: TP5 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

D5

Green

Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 4.

Index/Home: TP6 = 5V and D5 LED is On.

D6

Green

Wash Buffer Transfer Peri Pump.

Index/Home: TP8 = 5V and D6 LED is On.

D7

Green

Waste Liquid Transfer Peri Pump.

Index/Home: TP7 = 5V and D7 LED is On.

D14

Green

Vacuum Pump 1.

On: TP9 = 24V and D14 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-10 to U3-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q1, J19-2 is grounded activating the pump.

D15

Green

Vacuum Pump 2.

On: TP10 = 24V and D15 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-11 to U3-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q2, J20-2 is grounded activating the pump.

D16

Green

Vacuum Pump 3.

On: TP11 = 24V and D16 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-12 to U2-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q3, J21-2 is grounded activating the pump.

D17

Green

Vacuum Pump 4.

On: TP12 = 24V and D17 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-13 to U2-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q4, J22-2 is grounded activating the pump.

D18

Green

Vacuum Pump 5 (not used).

On: TP13 = 24V and D18 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-14 to U1-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q5, J23-2 is grounded activating the pump.

D19

Green

Vacuum Pump 6 (not used).

On: TP14 = 24V and D19 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-15 to U1-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q6, J24-2 is grounded activating the pump.
