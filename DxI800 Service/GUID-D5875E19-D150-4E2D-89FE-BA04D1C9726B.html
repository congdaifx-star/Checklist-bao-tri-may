LEDs - PCB - Interface - Reagent Storage

LEDs

LED Color Function

D1

Green

Reagent gripper X/Z home.

Home: TP3 = 0V and D1 LED is on. Not home: TP3 = 5V.

D2

Green

Reagent pack gripper open.

Open: TP4 = 0V and D2 LED is on. Not open: TP4 = 5V.

D3

Green

Comm.

Controlled by software. D3 LED is on when communicating data.

D6

Green

Reagent pack gripper closed (on a pack)

Closed: TP7 = 0V and D6 LED is on. Not closed: TP7 = 5V.

D8

Green

Reagent storage door closed.

Closed: TP8 = 0V and LED D8 is off.

D11

Green

Reagent pack waste door (open) air valve.

Enable: TP11 = 5V, D11 LED is on, and LED on Air Valve is on.

5V control signal to Q1 gate grounds J24-2 to activate the air valve.

D16

Green

Reagent pack waste door closed. (J22-3)

Home: TP12 = 0V and D16 LED is on.

D17

Green

Reagent pack tray closed. (J23-3)

Home: TP13 = 0V and D17 LED is on.

D19

Green

Reagent pack tray open. (J26-3)

Home: TP17 = 0V and D19 LED is on.

D20

Green

Reagent pack gripper (close) air valve.

Enable: TP18 = 5V, D20 LED is on, and LED on air valve is on.

5V control signal to Q2 gate grounds J27-2 to activate the air valve.

Gripper status table

Note:
  • Each sensor has a status indicator LED.
  • The pack present and pack not present indicators are software logic states based on the status of the two sensors.
  • An intermittent or broken gripper sensor cable can cause gripper crashes when homing, pack not present in gripper errors, software lockups, and loss of communication errors.
  • If replacing, always replace both sensors.
