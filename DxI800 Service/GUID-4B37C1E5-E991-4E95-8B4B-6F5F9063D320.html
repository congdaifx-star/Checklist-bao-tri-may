Specs - Fuses

Purpose

This document provides specification information for all DxI replaceable fuses.

General Fuse Information

The instrument has two types of fuses:

  • Resettable: Opens and closes with overheating and cooling. If the initial fault is not corrected, it heats and opens again. This causes cycling of the power to the device that the fuse is protecting.
  • Replaceable: Requires replacement once it has exceeded its rated current.

Resettable Fuses

Resettable fuses are located on many DxI PCBs and are not serviceable. If a resettable fuse fails to close once it has opened, the PCB on which the fuse is located must be replaced.

Replaceable Fuses

Replaceable fuse locations:
  • Power distribution PCB (two fuses)
  • AC inlet (two fuses)
