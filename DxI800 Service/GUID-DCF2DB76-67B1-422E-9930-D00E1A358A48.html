Replacing Peltier Assembly

This procedure describes how to replace the Peltier assembly on the sample storage module.

Required Materials:
  • Torque wrench and torque wrench accessories
  • Thermal compound

Replace the Peltier assembly on the sample storage module when the cause of sample storage module temperature errors is determined to be Peltier failure. The symptoms of Peltier failure are sample storage temperatures with out-of-limits error or that are nearly out-of-limits.

Note: Before replacing the Peltier, verify that the thermistor is functioning and that the heat sink is free of excessive dust. If there is dust in the heat sink, clean the heat sink. Refer to the SS: Sample Storage Troubleshooting procedure.
  1. Remove the sample storage module.
    Note: Refer to the SS: Removing Sample Storage Module procedure.
  2. Disassemble the sample storage module.
    1. With the module upright, loosen the two set-screws on the drive motor positioning block.
    2. Push the drive motor toward the positioning block to release the tension on the drive belt, and then remove the belt.
    3. Invert the module, remove the three screws that attach the mounting plate to the legs, and then remove the main plate.
    4. Loosen the two set-screws that hold the pulley to the center shaft, and then remove the pulley from the shaft.
    5. Remove the two screws from the encoder cover, and then remove the cover.
    6. Remove the two screws that attach the encoder module to the base, and then move the module to the side.
      Note: Do not damage the encoder wheel.
    7. Loosen the small set-screw on the index wheel, and then lift the wheel off the shaft.
      Caution: To avoid damaging the index wheel:
      • Use sandpaper to remove any burrs on the shaft, so that the wheel slides off easily.
      • Push upward on the bottom of the wheel and do not pull directly on the wheel.
    8. Remove the two screws that attach the encoder base to the shaft housing, and then lift the base off the shaft.
    9. Remove the four screws that attach the air duct to the module, and then remove the duct.
  3. Remove the Peltier.
    1. Loosen the four screws that attach the heat sink to the cold plate.
    2. Remove the entire assembly as a unit: heat sink, foam insulator pad, mounting screws, flat washers, and step washers.
      When the heat sink is removed, the Peltier can stick to the cold plate or the heat sink.
    3. Remove the Peltier.
      Note: If the Peltier is stuck to the cold plate or heat sink, gently remove it. Do not damage the cold plate or heat sink surface that contacts the Peltier.
    4. Use paper towels to remove all of the thermal compound and debris from the heat sink and cold plate areas. If you are reusing the Peltier, clean all of the thermal compound off the edges of the Peltier.
  4. Install the Peltier.
    1. Apply a thin, even coat of the thermal compound (approximately 0.001 inch thick) to the cold side of the Peltier.
      Caution: When installing the Peltier, do not apply too much thermal compound to the cold side of the Peltier or allow the compound to get on the edges of the Peltier. Compound on the edges conducts heat from the cold side to the hot side and decreases the performance of the Peltier.
    2. Place the Peltier assembly onto the housing with one Peltier on each of the two cold plate areas, making sure that the Peltier hot side is up.
      Note: Identify the hot side by orienting the colored wire as shown.
    3. Install the foam insulator pad into the module.
    4. Apply a thin, even coat of thermal compound (approximately 0.001 inch thick) to the Peltier contact area of the heat sink.
      Caution: When installing the Peltier, do not apply too much thermal compound to the cold side of the Peltier or allow the compound to get on the edges of the Peltier. Compound on the edges conducts heat from the cold side to the hot side and decreases the performance of the Peltier.
    5. Place the heat sink over the Peltier assembly, aligning the screw holes in the heat sink with the holes in the housing.
      Note: Observe the correct orientation of the heat sink. The four threaded holes for attaching the air duct to the heat sink are on the end of the module farthest from the interface PCB.
    6. Insert the four heat sink stepped washers, four flat washers, and four screws into the holes in the heat sink. Tighten the screws finger-tight with the appropriate hex wrench using the sequence shown in the figure.
    7. Torque the four screws, in the same sequence as in the previous step to 5 in-lb, then to 10 in-lb, and then to 20 in-lb. Verify that all four screws are tightened to 20 in-lb.
      Caution: Over-tightening or under-tightening the mounting screws on the heat sink can cause premature failure of the Peltier assembly.
  5. Assemble the sample storage module.
    1. Install the air duct (four screws).
    2. Attach the encoder base to the air duct (two screws).
    3. Install the encoder index wheel onto the drive shaft and tighten the small set-screw.
      Note:
      • Do not push on the rim of the wheel. Push downward on the top of the wheel hub.
      • Set the height of the wheel so that it will be centered within the encoder.
      • Refer to the Electronics: Replacing Encoders procedure.
    4. Attach the encoder to the encoder base (two screws).
      Note: After tightening the screws, verify that the index wheel is centered in the encoder reader.
    5. Attach the cover to the encoder (two screws).
    6. Mount the pulley onto the drive shaft so that the two set-screws are aligned to the depressions in the shaft, and then tighten the set-screws.
      Caution: Confirm that the home sensor flag on the pulley is centered in the home sensor on the mounting plate. An incorrectly positioned flag causes motion errors that require the removal of the module to readjust the position of the pulley position.
    7. Mount the main plate to the three legs (three screws).
      Note: Confirm that the home sensor flag on the drive pulley does not interfere with the home sensor on the main plate.
    8. Install the drive belt on the motor and the pulley. Allow the spring-loaded motor base to tension the belt. Confirm that the belt is tensioned, and then invert the module and tighten the two set-screws on the motor base.
    9. Confirm that the wires do not interfere with the motion of the pulley or belt.
  6. Install the sample storage module and verify instrument operation.
    Note: Refer to the SS: Installing Sample Storage Module procedure.
