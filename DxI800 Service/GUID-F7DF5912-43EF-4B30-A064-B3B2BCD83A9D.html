J12 - PCB - Interface - Reagent Pipettor

  • The Reagent Pipettor 2 or 4 Y-Axis Home Sensor connects to J12.
J12 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

+5V_MCC4/5

5V

From J1 pin 2.

5V supplied by the MCC4 or MCC5 PCB.

2

GND_MCC4/5

0V

From J1 pin 1, or 9.

Ground. TP1.

3

Y-Axis Home

Reagent Pip 2/4

5V or 0V

To J1 pin 5.

Home: TP4 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

4

+5V_MCC4/5

Xmit LED Power

5V

From J1 pin 2.

5V supplied by the MCC4 or MCC5 PCB.

The sensor LED power is 5V supplied through a 221 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected pin 4 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

5

GND_MCC4/5

0V

From J1 pin 1 or 9.

Ground. TP1.
