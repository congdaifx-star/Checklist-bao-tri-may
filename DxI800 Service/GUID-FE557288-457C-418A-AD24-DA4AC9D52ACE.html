Aligning Wash Carousel Angular Offset

This procedure describes how to align the wash carousel angular offset. This manual analytical module manual alignments must be performed before the analytical module adjustments and the other analytical module alignments.

Required Materials:
  • Wash carousel locking pin
    Note: For information about the tool, refer to the AU: Wash Carousel Locking Tooldocument.
Pre-requisites:
  • Confirm that there are no vessels in the wash carousel.
  • Remove the analytical module covers, replace when all alignments are completed.
Warning: The photomultiplier tube (PMT) is a very sensitive photon measuring device. Any background or extraneous light coming into contact with a powered PMT can saturate the PMT and reduce the gain. This reduces the performance of the PMT, and it can take a long time for the PMT to recover or it might never recover fully. A magnetic field close to a powered PMT also reduces the performance of the PMT. The magnetic field causes the electrons to follow a curved path instead of moving in a straight line. When you are performing service in the area of the luminometer, observe the following rules.
  • Never shine a flashlight around the luminometer while the PMT is powered on.
  • Never bring a strong magnetic field close to the luminometer while the luminometer is powered on.
  • Before removing the covers on the analytical module, turn off the power to the PMT.
  • Cap the PMT whenever it is removed from the analytical module.
Caution:
  • Typically the angular offset alignment does not need to be changed from the factory setting. This alignment may required if the entire analytical module is replaced or if factory alignments are lost.
  • If the angular offset alignment is changed, then verify the dispense probe plate, aspirate probe plate, incubator pick-and-place, and wash pick-and-place mechanical adjustments to the wash carousel.
  1. Remove the power from the luminometer.
    1. Open the upper right front cover.
    2. Remove power from the luminometer high-voltage power supply using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
      Caution: To avoid electrical sparks and damage to the electronics, do not disconnect the connectors on the top of the luminometer. Also, if you disconnect the connectors, the instrument will not initialize.
    3. Disconnect the lower cable at the luminometer high-voltage power supply.
      Note: The connector is located on the lower right side of the luminometer for most instruments. Some older DxI 800 instruments may have the cable located at the lower left side of the luminometer.
    4. Apply power using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
  2. Remove the analytical module covers.
  3. Align wash carousel angular offset.
    1. Select Main Menu > Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
    2. Select Wash Carousel as the device and Angular Offset as the position, and then select Move to Position.
      The instrument moves the wash carousel to the current angular offset position. If the wash carousel fails to move the angular offset position, then a subsystem might be disabled. To resolve the problem:
      1. Exit Device Diagnostics.
      2. Shutdown and restart the DxI instrument. Refer to the Instrument: Shut Down and Restart Navigation procedure.
      3. Repeat the alignment.
    3. Insert the locking pin tool into the alignment hole near the luminometer.
      • If the tool drops completely down under its own weight, the alignment is correct.
      • If the tool does not drop completely down under its own weight, then use the directional keys to step the wash carousel until it drops down.
    4. Remove the locking pin tool.
    5. Select Alignments/Offsets > Preserve Offsets.
    6. Select Alignments/Offsets > Save Alignments.
  4. Install the analytical module covers.
  5. Restore the power to the luminometer after servicing the analytical module.
    1. Power off the luminometer high-voltage power supply using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    2. If the analytical module covers have been removed, replace the covers.
    3. Reconnect the lower cable at the luminometer high voltage power supply.
      Note: The connector is located on the lower right side of the luminometer for most instruments. Some older DxI 800 instruments may have the cable located at the lower left side of the luminometer.
    4. Apply power using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    5. Initialize the instrument.
    6. Ensure that all temperatures are stabilized and within specification.
  6. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
