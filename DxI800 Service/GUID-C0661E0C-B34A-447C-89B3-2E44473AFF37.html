Installing Mixer Motor Tensioner With Fixed Sensor Mount

This procedure describes how to install the mixer motor tensioner with a fixed sensor mount location. The new tensioner design was implemented in manufacturing in July 2016 as a reliability improvement to reduce sensor failures due to broken wires from vibration of the sensor when it is mounted directly to the tensioner. The new design has a fixed sensor mounting location on the mixer motor plate which eliminates vibration for the sensor.

Perform this procedure when:

  • the mixer speed sensor wires break.
  • the tensioner does not rotate smoothly.
  • upgrading to the new design to prevent future visits.
  1. Shut down the DxI instrument.
    1. Select Configure  > PC Admin > System Admin > Shutdown Instrument to exit the software in preparation for powering off the instrument.
    2. Enter the password Access2Support, and then select OK.
    3. At the pop-up You must now turn off the instrument, using the instrument power switch, select OK.
    4. After the instrument software has shut down, turn off the instrument at the power switch.
  2. Remove the mixer tensioner assembly.
    Note: It may be helpful to remove the probes for better access to the mixer tensioner and motor.
    1. Disconnect the mixer speed sensor from the J8 connector on the Upper Analytical Interface PCB.
    2. Remove the 7/64 inch shoulder screw that secures the tensioner to the dispense plate and remove the assembly from the instrument..
  3. Remove the two 9/64 inch screws that secure the mixer motor to the dispense plate and remove the motor.
    Note: The motor does not have to be fully removed from the instrument to add the new sensor mounting plate.
  4. Remove the mixer motor pulley from the motor shaft (two 1/16 inch set screws).
    Note: The pulley must be removed and re-mounted for use with the fixed sensor mounting plate.
  5. Remove the mixer motor from the mixer motor mounting plate (three 1.5mm metric screws).
  6. Assemble the mixer motor and fixed sensor.
    1. Attach the mixer motor to the fixed sensor mounting plate (three 1.5mm metric screws).
    2. Attach the mixer speed sensor to the bottom side of the mounting plate (two 1/16 inch screws).
    3. Attach the pulley to the motor shaft.
      1. Apply Loctite 222 (if available) to the set screws on the motor pulley, and thread the screws into the pulley.
      2. Align one set screw with the flat slot located on the motor shaft.
      3. Tighten the set screw until it lightly contacts the flat surface of the mixer motor shaft.
      4. Push the pulley down on the shaft towards the motor until the set screw stops at the top of the flat slot of the mixer motor shaft
      5. Tighten both set screws securely.
  7. Assemble and install the mixer tensioner.
    1. Place the spring in the tensioner bracket and insert the shoulder screw through the bracket holes and spring.
    2. Install the tentioner assembly on the dispense plate and ensure that spring is mounted on the correct side of the pin to provide tension to the belt. Tighten the shoulder screw (7/64 inch) to secure the tensioner assembly to the dispense plate.
  8. Attach the mixer motor and sensor to the dispense plate (two 9/64 inch screws).
    Note:
    • Ensure that the tensioner wheel does not touch any part of the sensor.
    • Move ejector plate cylinder up and down to verify mixer motor wire leads do not interfere with the cylinder.
  9. Route and connect the cable for the mixer motor sensor.
    1. Route sensor cable up to the ejector plate cylinder and through the cable clamp on top of the wash arm assembly. Secure the cable to the ejector plate cylinder with a cable tie.
    2. Connect the mixer speed sensor to the J8 connector on the Upper Analytical Interface PCB.
  10. Restart the instrument and verify system operation.
    1. Reinstall probes if they were previously removed.
    2. Power on the instrument and verify it initializes to ready. If initialization fails refer to the AU: Troubleshooting Mixer Errors procedure to troubleshoot.
    3. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document. Refer to Admin: Verification of Service Repair.
