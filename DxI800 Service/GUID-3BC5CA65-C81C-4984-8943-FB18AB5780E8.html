J3 / JP1 - Drawing - PCB - Ultrasonics

J3 / JP1

  • The ultrasonic transducer pipettor 1 or 3 connects to J3 (lower connector).
  • CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 1 and 2 (P/N 117793, Pipettor 1 and 2) connects J3 to J14 and J15 on the Pipettor Interface PCB.
  • CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 3 and 4 (P/N 117773, Pipettor 3 and 4) connects J3 to J14 and J15 on the Pipettor Interface PCB.
J3 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes
1 HIGH_LEG A 197 V AC

(33 V AC low power)

To the ultrasonic transducer.

Power output to the ultrasonic transducer.

2 GND 0 V

To the ultrasonic transducer.

Ground for the ultrasonic transducer through resistor R20 (4.75 ohms). R20 is bypassed by jumper at J2.

3 GND 0 V

To the ultrasonic transducer.

Ground for cable shield.

  • Use JP1 pins as test points for monitoring J3 signals.
J1 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes
1 HIGH_LEG A 197 V AC

(33 V AC low power)

Power output to the ultrasonic transducer.

2 LOW_LEG A 0 V Ground for T1 ultrasonic output transformer.
3 GND 0 V Ground for cable shield.
4 GND 0 V Ground for the ultrasonic transducer through resistor R20 (4.75 ohms).
