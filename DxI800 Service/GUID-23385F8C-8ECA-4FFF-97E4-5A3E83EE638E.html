Conn. Overview - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Substrate

These are the connectors on the Substrate Interface PCB.

#

Connection Notes

Function / Signals

J1

The Substrate Door 1 Down sensor connects to J1.

Door 1 closed: TP1 = 0V and D2 LED is On.

J2

J2 is not used.

N/A

J3

J3 is not used.

N/A

J4

The Substrate Door 1 Up sensor connects to J4.

Door 1 open: TP2 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

J5

The Substrate Bottle 1 In-Place sensor connects to J5.

Bottle 1 present: TP3 = 0V and D4 LED is On.

J6

The Substrate Pump Home sensor connects to J6.

Substrate Pump home: TP5 = 0V and D5 LED is On.

J7

The Substrate Door 2 Down sensor connects to J7.

Door 2 closed: TP4 = 0V and D3 LED is On.

J8

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SUBSTRATE (P/N 111421) connects J8 to J74 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The Substrate Heater Control, Pump Home, Bottle Present, Door Open/Closed, and Air Valve signals run through J8.

J9

The Substrate Door 2 Up sensor connects to J9.

Door 2 open: TP6 = 0V and D7 LED is On.

J10

The Substrate Bottle 2 In-Place sensor connects to J10.

Bottle present: TP7 = 5V and D6 LED is On.

J11

Substrate Door 2, Air Valve 2 connects to J11.

5V signal from J8-37 to Q6 grounds J11-2. Valve enabled: TP17 = 5V and D20 LED is On.

J12

The Substrate Heater connects to J12.

Pulsed 5V control signal to U1-1 turns Q10 Off and Q7 On grounding J12-2. Enabled: TP18 = 5V and D22 LED is On.

J13

The Substrate Dispense Valve connects to J13.

5V signal from J8-12 to Q1 grounds J13-2. Valve enabled: TP12 = 5V and D10 LED is On.

J14

The Substrate Door 1, Air Valve 1 connects to J14.

5V signal from J8-31 to Q2 grounds J14-2. Valve enabled: TP13 = 5V and D12 LED is On.

J15

The Substrate Door 1, Air Valve 2 connects to J15.

5V signal from J8-33 to Q3 grounds J15-2. Valve enabled: TP14 = 5V and D14 LED is On.

J16

The Substrate Pump motor connects to J16.

Motor drive signals.

J17

HARNESS, INTERFACE, SUBSTRATE (P/N 110983) connects J17 to JP63 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The drive signals for the Substrate Pump Motor run through J17.

J18

The Substrate Select Valve connects to J18.

5V signal from J8-26 to Q4 grounds J18-2. Valve enabled: TP15 = 5V and D16 LED is On.

J19

The Substrate Door 2, Air Valve 1 connects to J19.

5V signal from J8-35 to Q5 grounds J19-2. Valve enabled: TP16 = 5V and D18 LED is On.
