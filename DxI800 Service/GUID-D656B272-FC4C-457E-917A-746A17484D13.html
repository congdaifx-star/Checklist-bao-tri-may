Deinstall Quick Reference

Pupose

This procedure provides a quick overview of the deinstall process for a DxI instrument.

Overview

The DxI Service Manual provides four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. If an instrument is being moved within a building or temporarily stored, not all procedures are necessary. When performing the deinstallation procedures consider:
  • Shipping conditions
  • Instrument final destination
  • Environmental issues
Procedure Description When to Perform Required Supplies
1. Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry (Siphon Method)

or

Deinstall: Rinsing and Priming Dry (Prime Dry Method)

  • Special Clean
  • Fluidics flush with DI water
  • Fluidics priming dry
  • Deinstalling a DxI instrument
  • Preparing instrument for storage
  • Contrad
  • Citranox dilution (1 part Citranox to 4 parts of deionized water)
  • Methanol or ethanol dilution (7 parts methanol or ethanol and 3 parts Wash Buffer II)
  • DI water
  • Lint-free towels
  • Optional: Siphoning equipment (tubing and a pipet bulb or other suction device)
    Note: Siphoning equipment provides a faster alternative to priming the instrument dry. This equipment saves approximately 1 hour of priming time.
2. Deinstall: Decontaminating and Cleaning
  • Clean, empty, and disinfect a DxI instrument for deinstallation and shipment
  • Remove all customer information and patient data
  • Deinstalling a DxI
  • Preparing an instrument to ship to Chaska or another site
  • Touchscreen Monitor and PC Decontamination kit (refer to DxI Parts List for part numbers)
  • Lint-free towels
  • 0.5% solution of sodium hypochlorite. Prepared by mixing 1 part household bleach with 9 parts water. Use either regular or ultra bleach.
    Note: Refer to "Instrument Decontamination" in the North America Commercial Operations (NACO) Database for alternative decontaminants.
3. Deinstall: Securing Instrument Components
  • Secure instrument module components
  • Deinstalling a DxI
  • Preparing an instrument to ship to Chaska or another site
  • Tie-wraps
  • TaPE
  • Small piece of polyfoam or alternative packing material approximately 1 in x 1 in x 1/4 in
4. Deinstall: Crating
  • Crate instrument
  • Pack peripherals
  • Deinstalling a DxI
  • Preparing an instrument to ship to Chaska or another site
  • Instrument crate
  • Large pliers
  • Hammer
  • Large straight slot screwdriver or pry bar
  • Wrench, adjustable or 5/8-in open end
  • Boxes and packing material for peripherals
    Child Topics:
  • Rinsing and Priming Dry (Prime Dry Method)
    This procedure describes how to rinse and prime dry a DxI instrument. Rinsing and Priming Dry is the first of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  • Rinsing and Priming Dry (Siphon Method)
    This procedure describes how to rinse and prime dry a DxI instrument. Rinsing and Priming Dry is the first of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  • Decontaminating and Cleaning
    This procedure describes how to clean, empty, and disinfect a DxI instrument. Decontaminating and Cleaning is the second of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  • Securing Instrument Components
    This procedure describes how to prepare a DxI instrument for shipment. Securing Instrument Components is the third of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  • Crating
    This procedure describes how to crate a DxI instrument and peripherals for shipment. Crating is the fourth of four procedures for Deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  • Decontaminating Surfaces of Parts and Equipment
    This procedure describes how to decontaminate the surface of parts, components, or equipment. This procedure also lists the locations of other documents containing decontamination information and instructions.
