Test Points - PCB - RF Level Sense

TP Voltage / Signal Notes

1

24V

VIN. Power to this PCB from JP1-2.

2

24V

VIN_FILT. VIN filtered through FB10.

3

5V

5V from power converter U1 on this PCB.

4

12V

12V from power converter U2 on this PCB.

5

5V

5V_REF from power converter U3 on this PCB.

6

N/A

MCLR. Reset for U5 level sense chip.

7

5V or 0V

LVL_SENSE. Level sense signal.

When a surface has been detected: TP7 = 0V and D6 is on.

Note:
  • D6 is controlled by software and may not indicate the physical status of the hardware. Generally, the red LED D6 stays on until the level sense returns to ready, but the level sense output at TP7 may reset and not indicate a level sense while D6 is still on.
  • The level sense status is also indicated on the Sample Pipettor Interface PCB (Surface detected: TP2 = 0V, D1 is on).

8

0V

Ground from JP1-4 (MCC0).

N/A

RX_PIC. Receive from U5-18 to JP1-7.

N/A

TX_PIC. Transmit from U5-17 to JP1-9.

N/A

12

N/A

N/A

FREQ_ADJ. Probe frequency.

14

N/A

VCO_TTL. 5V to U7-5 VCO chip.

15

N/A

TANK_VREF. Tank circuit (probe oscillation) reference voltage.

16

N/A

Range

N/A

ANA_LEVEL. Probe oscillation voltage to U5-2. Raw level sense.

N/A

FREQ_ADJ_FILT. Used for monitoring probe frequency.

0V

Ground from JP1-4 (MCC0).
