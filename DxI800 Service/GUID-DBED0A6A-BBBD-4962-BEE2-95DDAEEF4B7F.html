Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - Reagent Pipettor

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, REAGENT PIPETTOR 1 and 2 (P/N 116221) connects J1 to J41 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, REAGENT PIPETTOR 3 and 4 (P/N 116231) connects J1 to J51 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 5V power and home sensor signals run through J1.

J2

HARNESS, INTERFACE, REAGENT PIP, (P/N 116363) connects J2 to MCC Backplane JP41 (Pipettor 1 and 2) or JP51 (Pipettor 3 and 4).

The Y and Z Axis motor drive signals from the MCC4 (Pipettor 1 and 2) or MCC5 (Pipettor 2 and 3) PCB run through J2.

The 24V power and the Ground to the PCB run through J2.

J4

CABLE, SERVICE LOOP (P/N 117782) connects J4 to Ultrasonic Transducer 1 or 3.

The Pipettor 1 or 3 Ultrasonic Transducer power signals run through J4.

The Pipettor 1 or 3 transducer heater power and control signals run through J4.

J5

CABLE, SERVICE LOOP (P/N 117782) connects J5 to Ultrasonic Transducer 2 or 4.

The Pipettor 2 or 4 Ultrasonic Transducer power signals run through J5.

The Pipettor 2 or 4 transducer heater power and control signals run through J5.

J6

The Pipettor 1 or 3 Z-Axis Motor connects to J6.

An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages. Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J7

The Pipettor 1 or 3 Y-Axis Motor connects to J7.

An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages. Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J8

The Pipettor 2 or 4 Z-Axis Motor connects to J8.

An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages. Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J9

The Pipettor 2 or 4 Y-Axis Motor connects to J9.

An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages. Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J10

The Pipettor 1 or 3 Z-Axis Home Sensor connects to J10.

Pipettor 1 or 3 Z-Axis Home: TP3 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

J11

The Pipettor 1 or 3 Y-Axis Home Sensor connects to J11.

Pipettor 1 or 3 Y-Axis Home: TP2 = 0V and D2 LED is On.

J12

The Pipettor 2 or 4 Y-Axis Home Sensor connects to J12.

Pipettor 2 or 4 Y-Axis Home: TP4 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

J13

The Pipettor 2 or 4 Z-Axis Home Sensor connects to J13.

Pipettor 2 or 4 Z-Axis Home: TP5 = 5V and D3 LED is On.

J14

CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 1 and 2 (P/N 117793, this is the longer cable) connects J14 to the Ultrasonic PCB J3.

CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 3 and 4 (P/N 117773) connects J14 to the Ultrasonic PCB J3.

The ultrasonic power from the Ultrasonic PCB to Pipettor 1 or 3 runs through J14.

J15

CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 1 and 2 (P/N 117793, this is the longer cable) connects J15 to the Ultrasonic PCB J6.

CABLE, ULTRASONIC SIGNAL 3 and 4 (P/N 117773) connects J15 to the Ultrasonic PCB J6.

The ultrasonic power from the Ultrasonic PCB to Pipettor 2 or 4 runs through J15.
