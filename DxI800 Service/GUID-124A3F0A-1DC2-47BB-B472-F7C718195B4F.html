Specs - Analytical Module Hardware

Purpose

This document provides the specifications for the analytical module and its components.

Wash Carousel (Ring)

Item Specification
Finish Anodized
Drive motor 0.9 degrees per step
Time required for one revolution of carousel 10.35 minutes
Number of RV locations (holes) 69
Length of one pitch 9 seconds
Magnetic particle separation time 54 seconds per wash cycle
Number of magnets 39 total
Magnets strength No less than 2500 gauss
Ring position encoding Within 3 half-steps with a full-step resolution of 0.010 inch per step
RV height during mixing Approximately 0.845 inch
Degrees between RV locations on wash carousel 5.2174 degrees
Incubation time from substrate dispense to read 6 minutes 12.4 seconds
Temperature 37 degrees ±0.2 °C

Incubator Wheel

Item Specification
Finish Anodized Aluminum
Number of RV locations (holes) 258
Rotation controlled by 0.9 degree stepper motor
Positioning accuracy of wash wheel Within 3 half-steps with a full-step resolution of 0.010 inch per step

Luminometer / LED

Item Specification
LED Count Range 1,000,000 ±20%
LED CVs <0.2%
Dark Counts <100 RLUs in Luminometer > Read screen

<400 RLUs when using the Luminometer > Dark Count Check screen.

Note: The dark counts reported in the Dark Count Check and System Check Reports are scaled by a factor of 4 while the Luminometer Read screen is a raw DAQC read and is not scaled.

The system will flag a dark count error if the scaled RLU is >600 during operation.

Maximum allowable crosstalk between adjacent vessels (From an adjacent vessel containing 50 µL of neat System Check Solution in 200 µL of substrate) Not greater than 400 (scaled RLUs) or 100 raw RLUs
Time between dark count reads Any empty position on the wheel
Time between LED reads One revolution of carousel (10.35 minutes)
PMT high voltage control value Max 4.00 V DC

Probes - Dispense

Item Specification
Material Anodized Aluminum
Inside Diameter 0.030 inch
Lead Screw Pitch 1 inch
Vertical Motion Controlled by 1.8 degree / step motor
Positioning Resolution Not greater than 0.005 inch per step
Probe Type Side Dispense

Probes - Aspirate

Item Specification
Material Non-Magnetic
Inside Diameter 0.050 inch
Lead Screw Pitch 1 inch
Vertical Motion Controlled by 1.8 degree / step motor
Positioning Resolution Not greater than 0.005 inch per step
Spring Compression 0.030 inch minimum when bottomed out in an RV

Probe Plate / Mixer

Item Specification
Finish Anodized Aluminum
Dispense Probe Plate Range of Motion Approximately 1-3/8 inch
Eject Plate Range of Motion Approximately 3/8 inch
Aspirate Probe Plate Range of Motion Approximately 4-3/8 inch
RV release from the mixer Air Cylinder Actuation of Ejector Plate
Mixing Sequence timing (In Seconds) CW 1.5, Pause 0.8, CCW 1.1, Pause 0.8, CW 1.1, Pause 0.8, CCW 1.1
Dispense 2 Mixer Pulley 30 tooth (2200 RPM)
Dispense 3 Mixer Pulley 30 tooth (2200 RPM)
Substrate Dispense Mix Pulley Early 24 tooth (originally was 22 tooth - changed by Wash Mod) (2750 RPM)
Mixer Motor Pulley 22 Tooth (3000 RPM)
Mixer Belt Speed 237.4 RPM when mixer is at 30,000 RPM
Tensioner Pulley 14 Tooth (4714 RPM)
