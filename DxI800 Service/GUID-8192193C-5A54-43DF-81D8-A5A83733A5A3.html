Aligning Supply Carriage

This procedure describes how to align the supply carriage to the bulk feeder. Because the supply carriage module is a secondary device for the sample pick-and-place alignment, the alignment is covered as a sample pick-and-place procedure.

Required Materials:
  • No special tools are required.
Pre-requisites:
  • All mechanical adjustments to the supply carriage must be completed prior to performing the software alignments.

The supply carriage must be able to receive vessels from the bulk feeder and deliver these vessels to the pickup location of the sample pick-and-place. Because the supply carriage module is a secondary device for the sample pick-and-place alignment, the alignment is covered as a sample pick-and-place procedure.

The manual alignments for this module include:
  • align supply carriage to bulk feeder
Changes to alignment offsets are written to a file:
  • when the alignments are saved when exiting the Alignment screen.
  • by selecting Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the upper left front cover.
    2. Insert an interlock bypass.
  2. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
  3. Align supply carriage to bulk vessel feeder position.
    1. Select Supply Carriage as the device and Bulk Feeder Align as the position.
    2. Select Home Device and then Move X/Y to Position.
    3. Manually remove all vessels from the supply carriage.
    4. Using the directional buttons on the screen, step the supply carriage to align the front of the carriage with the front of vessel guide.
      Note: The position of the carriage at the alignment point is slightly forward of its position for accepting a vessel while in operation.
      Note: If a new-style supply carriage (identified by a linear slide instead of a rod and bushing) is being aligned to an original-style bulk feeder, move the alignment an additional minus (down arrow) eight steps (towards the front of the instrument).
      Note: Refer to the Supply Carriage Module document for image to identify new- and original-style carriages.
    5. Select Alignments / Offsets > Preserve Offsets.
    6. Select Supply Carriage as the device and Bulk Feeder Dispense as the position.
    7. Select Move X/Y to Position.
    8. Select Dispense Vessel and observe the transition of the vessel into the supply carriage.
      • If the vessel drops into the carriage smoothly, then the alignment is correct.
      • If the vessel does not drop into the carriage smoothly, then the alignment is incorrect. Align or adjust the carriage position, as necessary.
  4. Initialize the instrument.
  5. Verification: Run the vessel exerciser to verify bulk vessel feeder operation.
    1. From the Device Diagnostics screen, select Exerciser.
    2. Run 50 reps of the Bulk Vessel Feeder.
    3. Select Cycle Vessels.
    4. Observe the transition of the vessel into the supply carriage.
      • If the vessel drops into the carriage smoothly, then the alignment is correct.
      • If the vessel does not drop into the carriage smoothly, then the alignment is incorrect. Align or adjust the carriage position, as necessary.
  6. Remove the interlock bypass and close the cover.
  7. Initialize the instrument.
