Adjusting Luminometer DCF

This procedure describes how to adjust the luminometer so that the drift correction factor (DCF) is within specification.

The luminometer output can drift slightly over time as the luminometer ages. Adjusting the DCF is essentially adjusting the luminometer sensitivity so that its output, when reading the light from the LED, matches the stored LED target. Failure of the reference LED, the luminometer, an associated cable, or the DAQC PCB can cause a DCF error.

Adjust the luminometer when the DCF exceeds the guideline range of 0.80 to 1.20, or a DCF error is posted in the event log.

Note:
  • A DCF error is generated when the DCF is greater than 1.7 or less than 0.6.
  • When a DCF error occurs during the system check, the system check report prints the last in-range DCF.
  • The DCF equals the stored LED target, divided by the LED output measurement, minus the cross-talk reading.
  • The DCF is applied to the raw relative light unit (RLU) counts to compensate for drift in the photo multiplier tube.
  1. Adjust the Drift Correction Factor.
    1. Close all instrument covers.
    2. Select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics  > Luminometer > Read .
    3. Verify that the RLU counts in the Current Mean are less than 50.
      With the LED off and all covers installed and closed, a mean RLU count of 10 or less is common.
    4. Select Enable LED.
    5. Place the cursor in the High Voltage Control window.
    6. Using the directional keys, adjust the control voltage so that the current mean RLU matches the LED target within 2%.
    7. Enter the high-voltage control voltage into the High Voltage Control window, using the directional keys or the Enter key.
    8. Select Cancel > Save High Voltage Control, and then select Yes at the Save new H. V. Control value pop-up. .
      Do not save the LED reading. If saving a new LED target, recalibrate the assay.
    9. Select Enable LED to power off the LED.
    10. Press the [Esc] key.
  2. Perform a system check to verify luminometer operation, DCF value, and substrate mean RLU.
    Note: The specification for the DCF is 0.8 to 1.2, and the substrate mean RLU is 5000 to 9000. If the substrate mean RLU is not within specification, perform troubleshooting. Refer to the Fluidics: Troubleshoot Substrate System procedure.
  3. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
  4. Remove all cover interlock switch bypass devices and close all instrument covers.
