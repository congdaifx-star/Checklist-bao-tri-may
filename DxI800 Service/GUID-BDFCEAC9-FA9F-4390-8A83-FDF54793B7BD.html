Specs - Powervar ABCDEF3000 UPS

Purpose

This document provides the specifications for the Powervar ABCDEF3000 UPS.

Notes

This UPS:
  • Provides backup power for the DxI instrument (analytical unit and PC).
  • Has zero transfer time when switching from line power to battery power.
  • Is available in NAO and International versions.
  • Is provided with a cord for use between the UPS and the wall outlet.
  • Ships from Intelligent Power Solutions, California for NAO. Ships from the UK for international.

Back-Up Time

Estimated backup time that is available from the batteries in the Powervar ABCDEF3000.

Note:
  • The exact back-up time depends on the condition of the batteries, amount of load, temperature, and other variables.
  • Contact the distributor for information on UPS models and battery expansion cabinets providing up to two hours of backup time.
Load Estimated Backup Time
DxI running samples 20 minutes.
Note: At 17 minutes of operation, a 2 to 3 minute run time remaining warning alarm is displayed.

Battery Life

The UPS contains sealed, maintenance-free lead acid batteries. The batteries will last for many years under normal conditions (minimum 3 to 5 years). If the UPS self-test indicates there is a battery issue, then the customer should contact Powervar Technical Support for assistance.
