J73 - PCB - MCC Backplane

  • The reagent and substrate BCR outputs, gripper open and closed, gripper and tray home, waste door open, and interface PCB data signals to the MCC Backplane PCB run through J73.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, REAGENT STORAGE connects J73 to J1 on the Reagent Storage Interface PCB.
Note: The connections for the colored status LEDs are not used. The colored status LEDs on the instrument front cover are connected to MCC Backplane PCB J97 and controlled by the MCC6 PCB.
