J3 - PCB - Interface - SPU User

  • The SPU on-load motor connects to J3.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.
