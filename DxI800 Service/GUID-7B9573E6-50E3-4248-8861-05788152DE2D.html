J62 - PCB - MCC Backplane

  • The Fluidics Interface PCB 5-V power, sample wash and precision pump and valve home sensor, peri-pump index sensor, sample pump solenoid valve, and buffer reservoir float switch signals run through J62.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, FLUIDICS connects J62 to J1 on the Fluidics Interface PCB.
