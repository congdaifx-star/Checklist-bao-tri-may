Conn. Overview - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Peristaltic and Vacuum Pump

  • These are the connectors on this PCB.

#

Connection Notes

Function / Signals

J1

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, PERI/VAC PUMP (P/N 116181) connects J1 to J31 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The Vacuum Pump 1, 2, 3, and 4 enable signals and the Peristaltic Pump Home/Index sensor signals to MCC3 run through J1.

J2

HARNESS, INTERFACE, PERISTALTIC & VACUUM PUMP (P/N 116323) connects J2 to JP32 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The harness also connects MCC Backplane JP31 to Peri / Vac Pump I/F J3.

The Wash Tower 1, 2, 3, and 4 Peri Pump stepper motor drive signals from MCC3 run through J2.

Notes:

  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.

J3

HARNESS, INTERFACE, PERISTALTIC & VACUUM PUMP (P/N 116323) connects J3 to JP31 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The harness also connects MCC Backplane JP32 to Peri / Vac Pump I/F J2.

The Liquid Waste Transfer, Wash Buffer Transfer, and Sample Wash Tower Peri Pump motor drive signals from MCC3 run through J3.

J4

HARNESS, INTERFACE POWER (P/N 116473) connects J4 to J1 on the Power Distribution PCB.

The harness also connects J1 to:

  • Analytic Power I/F PCB J3.
  • Sample Storage PELTIER.
  • Lower Cabinet I/F PCB J2.

The 24V power from the Power Distribution PCB runs through J4.

J5

The Sample Wash Tower Peri Pump Index/Home sensor connects to J5.

Index/Home: TP2 = 5V and D1 LED is On.

J6

The Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 1 Index/Home sensor connects to J6.

Index/Home: TP3 = 5V and D2 LED is On.

J7

The Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 2 Index/Home sensor connects to J7.

Index/Home: TP4 = 5V and D3 LED is On.

J8

The Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 3 Index/Home sensor connects to J8.

(not used on DxI 600)

Index/Home: TP5 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

J9

The Reagent Wash Tower Peri Pump 4 Index/Home sensor connects to J9.

(not used on DxI 600)

Index/Home: TP6 = 5V and D5 LED is On.

J10

The Sample Wash Tower Peri Pump motor connects to J10.

Stepper motor drive signals.

J11

The Wash Tower 1 Peri Pump motor connects to J11.

Stepper motor drive signals.

J12

The Wash Tower 2 Peri Pump motor connects to J12.

Stepper motor drive signals.

J13

The Wash Tower 3 Peri Pump motor connects to J13.

(not used on DxI 600)

Stepper motor drive signals.

J14

The Wash Tower 4 Peri Pump motor connects to J14.

(not used on DxI 600)

Stepper motor drive signals.

J15

The Wash Buffer Transfer Peri Pump Index/Home sensor connects to J15.

Index/Home: TP8 = 5V and D6 LED is On.

J16

The Waste Liquid Transfer Peri Pump Index/Home sensor connects to J16.

Index/Home: TP7 = 5V and D7 LED is On.

J17

The Wash Buffer Transfer Peri Pump motor connects to J17.

Stepper motor drive signals.

J18

The Waste Transfer Peri Pump motor connects to J18.

Stepper motor drive signals.

J19

Vacuum Pump 1 connects to J19.

On: TP9 = 24V and D14 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-10 to U3-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q1, J19-2 is grounded activating the pump.

J20

Vacuum Pump 2 connects to J20.

On: TP10 = 24V and D15 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-11 to U3-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q2, J20-2 is grounded activating the pump.

J21

Vacuum Pump 3 connects to J21.

On: TP11 = 24V and D16 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-12 to U2-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q3, J21-2 is grounded activating the pump.

J22

Vacuum Pump 4 connects to J22.

On: TP12 = 24V and D17 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-13 to U2-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q4, J22-2 is grounded activating the pump.

J23

Vacuum Pump 5 (not used) connects to J23.

On: TP13 = 24V and D18 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-14 to U1-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q5, J23-2 is grounded activating the pump.

J24

Vacuum Pump 6 (not used) connects to J24.

On: TP14 = 24V and D19 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-15 to U1-1 applies 24V to the gate of Q6, J24-2 is grounded activating the pump.
