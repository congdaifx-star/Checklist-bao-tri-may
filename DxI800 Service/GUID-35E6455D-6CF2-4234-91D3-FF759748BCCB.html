Volume Check Loading and Unloading

Purpose

This document provides detailed loading information for any of the volume checks.

General Information

Note: The numbers assigned to the sample wheel in this drawing are for reference only. These numbers are not the numbers assigned by the software.

Hole locations and rows as viewed from the rear of the instrument after selecting Start Test OR after the end of the test. Double and single dots in the positions shown indicate that the wheel is ready to be loaded or unloaded.

Important factors for loading vessels into the sample wheel when it is requested are:
  • Regardless of which type of probe is selected, vessels must be loaded starting at location 1 in the sample wheel, the farthest hole to the right at end of row 1 (as viewed from the rear of the instrument).
  • Vessels must be loaded from right to left from the rear of the instrument after selecting Start Test.
  • If performing the verification using the gravimetric method, then number and weigh the vessels before beginning.
  • If performing the verification using the visual method, then you do not need to number or weigh the vessels.

Dispense Probe Volume Check - Loading

  • Any combination of the three dispense probes can be selected.
  • Each dispense probe that is tested requires ten vessels.
  • Delivery volumes are 150 µL for the QS Probe (Dispense 1) and 500 µL each for Probe 2 and Probe 3.
  • Regardless of how many tests are selected, always load the vessels right to left beginning in location 1.

Reagent Pipettor Volume Check - Loading

  • Each additional pipettor or volume test requires that nine vessels be loaded (up to a maximum of 72 vessels).
  • The total number of vessels required (VR) equals the number of pipettors (Pip) selected (1-4) times the number of volumes (Vol) selected (1 or 2) x 9. VR = Pip x Vol x 9.
  • The instrument always performs the Low Volume tests (50 µL) first when both Low and High Volume tests (200 µL) are requested.
  • The DxI 600 uses only two pipettors, thus only half as many holes are used for loading a full-load test.

The drawings show the loading group locations for a DxI 800 and DxI 600. Some or all of the locations can be used, depending on the number of tests requested, but vessels are loaded sequentially by group number, right to left, starting with location 1. Each group is a series of nine vessels, all of which are repetitions of any one test.
