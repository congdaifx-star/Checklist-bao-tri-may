Cleaning Deionizer Emitters

This procedure describes how to clean the emitter points in the deionizer blower located at the top of the hopper on the bulk feeder.

Required Materials:
  • Cloth swabs (the polyester cloth type is recommended)
  • Isopropanol alcohol prep pads or 50% water / 50% isopropanol alcohol (IPA) cleaning solution (a higher percentage of IPA is acceptable)
  • Paper towels
Clean the deionizer emitter points:
  • when the emitter points are dirty.
  • as instructed in the PM procedure.

The cleaning interval recommended by the manufacturer is every six months; however, the cleaning interval also depends on the operating environment. Some instruments can require more frequent cleaning.

Note: Not all instruments have a deionizer.
Caution: Do not let dust or debris fall into the hopper while cleaning the emitter points on the deionizer.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Remove power from the deionizer blower by disconnecting the cable from the green terminal block of the deionizer.
      Caution: Allow 1 minute for the high voltage power supply to discharge after disconnecting power.
    2. Confirm that there is at least two inches of open space below the deionizer blower. If there is less than two inches of open space, then create two inches of open space by removing vessels from the hopper. Place the vessels in a clean container.
  2. Clean the deionizer emitter.
    Caution: Do not remove the emitters from the blower. Do not bend or move the emitter points during the cleaning process.
    Note: The emitter points are accessible from the top side of the deionizer blower (the same side as the green terminal block).
    1. Moisten a cloth swab using either an IPA prep pad or 50% IPA cleaning solution.
    2. Reach in under the blower with a paper towel, and hold the towel there to catch any dust that falls out of the blower during cleaning.
    3. Locate the emitter points, and gently wipe them to remove all the dust.
    4. Confirm that, after cleaning, the emitters point to the center of the blower and are on the same horizontal plane.
  3. Remove dust from the blower that was loosened during cleaning.
    Warning: Ensure that two minutes has passed since the emitter points were cleaned. This allows adequate time for the cleaning fluid to completely evaporate prior to applying power.
    1. Holding a clean towel under the deionizer, apply power to the deionizer blower by connecting the cable to the green terminal block of the deionizer.
    2. Allow the blower to run for a few seconds to blow any loose dust into the towel.
    3. Carefully remove the towel.
  4. Replace any vessels that were removed from the hopper.
  5. Verify operation of the deionizer.
    1. Confirm that the deionizer blower is running and that the green LED on the front side of the blower is lit.
      If the blower is not running, confirm that the power cable for the deionizer is connected correctly at both ends.
    2. Run the vessel exerciser.
      1. With the instrument in the Ready mode, from the Main Menu select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics.
      2. Select Exerciser.
      3. Run the bulk vessel feeder exerciser 20 cycles.
      4. Monitor the bulk feeder module and event log for any issues.
      5. If an error is reported, then troubleshoot, repair, and retest.
  6. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
