Adjusting Luminometer Substrate RLU

This procedure describes how to adjust the luminometer so that the RLU for substrate is within specification.

Adjust the luminometer and LED target when the substrate RLUs are out of specification (5000 to 9000 RLU) during the system check, and none of these conditions exist:
  • Contamination
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Decontaminating Substrate procedure.
  • Dilution immediately following decontamination
  • Incorrect dispensed substrate volume (substrate valve, loose fittings, worn pump belt or seals, or damaged probe)
  • Incorrect substrate storage and handling (contaminated, hot, cold, equilibrated, or radioactive)
  • Incorrect temperature
  • Stray light (dark counts)
Caution: Do not adjust the system to compensate for high or low substrate RLUs without confirming that the substrate system is OK.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the upper right instrument cover.
    2. Bypass the cover safety switch.
    3. Initialize the system, if it is in Not Ready mode.
  2. Adjust the luminometer substrate RLU.
    Adjust the instrument so that substrate RLUs are closer to the center of the 5000 to 9000 RLU specification.
    Note:
    • Ensure that the temperature of the incubator wheel and the wash carousel are within specification.
    • Assay recalibration is required after performing this procedure.
    1. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics  > Luminometer  > Load / Read Vessel.
    2. Load a reaction vessel (RV) containing 200 µL of substrate, as directed, and close the instrument covers.
    3. Allow the substrate sample RLU output to stabilize for six minutes.
      Note:
      • Hand pipetting the substrate sample helps rule out contamination and dispense volume issues.
      • Use substrate that has been allowed to equilibrate, unopened, at room temperature (15 to 30 °C) for 18 hours.
      • The normal incubation time between substrate dispense and read is six minutes.
      • The wash carousel load position for the vessel is just to the right of the D1 dispense probe.
    4. Enter the high-voltage control voltage into the High Voltage Control window, using the directional keys or the Enter key to complete the number entry. Adjust the control voltage so that the RLU displayed in the Current Counts window is approximately 1750.
      Note:
      • All instrument covers must be in place and closed, so that stray light does not affect the reading.
      • An RLU of 1750 normally produces a substrate mean RLU of 7000.
      • Result RLU = (4 x (current RLU - dark counts) x the DCF).
    5. Select Cancel, and remove the RV as directed.
    6. Select Save High Voltage Control, and then select Yes at the Save new H. V. Control value pop-up.
    7. Select Read > Enable LED, and allow the LED output to stabilize until the current %CV is less than 0.2%.
    8. Select Cancel.
      Note: Leave the LED enabled so that the current mean is still present.
    9. Select Save LED Reading, and then select Yes to the Save new LED Reading pop-up.
      Warning: All customer assays must be calibrated after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED target.
    10. Select Enable LED to turn the LED power off.
    11. Press the [Esc] key.
  3. Verify the instrument and luminometer meet specification.
    1. Perform a system check to verify luminometer operation.
      The specification for the DCF is 0.8 to 1.2, and for the substrate mean RLU is 5000 to 9000.
      Note: If the substrate mean RLU is not within specification, refer to the troubleshooting procedures.
    2. Calibrate all customer assays and run a customer QC with results within customer limits.
      Caution: Calibrate customer assays after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED target.
  4. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
  5. Remove all cover interlock switch bypass devices and close all instrument covers.
