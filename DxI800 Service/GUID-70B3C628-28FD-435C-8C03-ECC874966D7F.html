Crating

This procedure describes how to crate a DxI instrument and peripherals for shipment. Crating is the fourth of four procedures for Deinstalling a DxI instrument. Perform this procedure when preparing an instrument for storage or deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.

This procedure is the last of four procedures for deinstalling a DxI. All four procedures are summarized in the Deinstall Quick Reference. If an instrument is being moved within a building or temporarily stored, not all procedures are necessary. When performing the deinstallation procedures, consider:
  • Moving and shipping conditions
  • Final destination
  • Environmental issues
Perform this procedure when:
  • Preparing an instrument for storage.
  • Deinstalling an instrument to be shipped to another site or returned to Chaska.
  1. Crate the instrument by performing the steps in the uncrating procedure in reverse order.
    Note: Refer to the Installing: Uncrating from Cardboard Crate or Installing: Uncrating from Wooden Crate procedure.
    Note: Remove all carrier and ship-to labels from the crate.
  2. Crate the peripherals.
    1. If the instrument will be shipped to Chaska, discard the mouse pad and Beckman Coulter manuals.
    2. If there are no other DxI or Access instruments onsite, then discard these items:
      • Racks
      • Reaction vessels
      • Sample containers
      • Biohazard waste bags
      • Consumables, wash buffer
      • Reagents, and substrate
    3. Package components for shipment. Use strong boxes and packing materials to pack each item safely.
      • Cart
      • Beckman Coulter manuals (if not discarded)
      • Monitor
      • Keyboard
      • Mouse
      • Printer
      • UPS console
      • Barcode reader of console PC
      • All associated cabling with the peripherals
      • UPS instrument
      • Ethernet cable to back of instrument
      • Power cords (printer, PC, and AU)
  3. Return the instrument and peripherals.
    Note: Refer to these procedures for shipping instructions.
    • For NAO, refer to the Instrument Preparation for Commercial Shipment in the North America Commercial Operations (NACO) Database procedure.
    • For OUS, refer to the Returned Materials Authorization (RMA) Process.
    • For Clinical Studies, contact Systems Technical Support.
