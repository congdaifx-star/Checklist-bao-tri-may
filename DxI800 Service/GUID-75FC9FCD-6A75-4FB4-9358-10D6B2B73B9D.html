J1 - PCB - Interface - Lower Cabinet

  • The 5V power and home sensor signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, LOWER CABINET connects J1 to J61 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
J1 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

GND_MCC3

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-1.

Ground from MCC backplane. GND_PLANE

2

+5V_MCC3

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-2.

5V power from the MCC3 PCB.

3

Wash Buffer 1 Select

0V or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-3 then from J30-A21 (MCC3).

Selects valve for front Wash Buffer supply box.

In the Bubble Trap style PCB WB1 selects valve for the Front or rear Buffer Box.

4

Wash Buffer 2 Select

0V or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-4 then from J30-A22 (MCC3).

Selects valve for rear Wash Buffer supply box.

In the Bubble Trap style PCB WB2 selects WB Flow Occlusion/Pressure Sensor or WB Bubble Detector.

5

N/C

0V

Not connected.

6

N/C

0V

Not connected.

7

N/C

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-7.

Not connected. Grounded at MCC Backplane. GND_PLANE

8

N/C

0V

Not connected.

9

Waste Bottle 1 Select

0V or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-9 then from J30-A23 (MCC3).

Selects valve for front Liquid Waste bottle.

In the Bubble Trap style PCB Waste1 selects valve for the Front or Rear Waste Bottle.

10

Waste Bottle 2 Select

0V or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-10 then from J30-A24 (MCC3)

Selects valve for rear Liquid Waste bottle.

In the Bubble Trap style PCB Waste2 selects WB Supply box front or rear weight sensor.

11

N/C

0V

Not connected.

12

N/C

0V

Not connected.

13

Waste Bag In Place

0 or 5V

To MCC Backplane J61-13.

Solid waste compartment door closed: TP3 =0V and D1 LED is On.

14

Waste Bag Emptied

0 or 5V

To MCC Backplane J61-14.

Green button in the solid waste compartment pressed: TP7 =0V and D8 LED is On.

15

Liquid Waste Full

0 or 5V

To MCC Backplane J61-15.

Intermediate Waste Bottle float at full position: 0V and D3 LED is On.

16

Liquid Waste Empty

0 or 5V

To MCC Backplane J61-16.

Intermediate Waste Bottle float at empty position: 0V and D2 LED is On.

17

Compressor Enable

0 or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-17 then from J60-A17 (MCC6).

5V control signal to Q1 grounds J8-2.

Compressor 1 On: TP5 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

5V control signal to Q3 grounds J10-2.

Compressor 2 On: TP9 = 5V and D10 LED is On.

18

Air Bleed Valve Enable

(Moisture Bleed)

0 or 5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J6-18 then from J60-A18 (MCC6).

5V control signal to Q2 grounds J9-2.

Valve On: TP6 = 5V and D5 LED is On.

19

GND_MCC6

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-19.

Ground from MCC backplane. GND_PLANE

20

+5V_MCC6

5V

From MCC Backplane PCB J61-20.

5V power from the MCC6 PCB.
