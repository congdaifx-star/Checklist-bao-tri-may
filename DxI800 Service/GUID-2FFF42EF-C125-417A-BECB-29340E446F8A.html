J70 - PCB - MCC Backplane

  • The MCC7 PCB connects to J70 (3 rows of 32 pins, pin A1 is at the top left).
Motors:
  • Reagent Storage (Gripper) X and Z
  • Reagent Pack Tray
  • Incubator Wheel
  • Wash Carousel
  • Dispense Arm Z
  • Aspirate Arm Z
