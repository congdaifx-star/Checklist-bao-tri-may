Lubricants

Caution: Tri-Flow is not approved for use on DxI or Access instruments.
Table 45: The only lubricants to be used with DxI instruments (approved by Chaska Engineering)
Name Image Color Size Notes
Anderol 465®

Light Amber 2-ounce wide neck polypropylene bottle with white screw cap. Synthetic oil. ISO grade 68 diester-based lubricant intended to give extended lubrication over wide temperature ranges, resist gumming and speed fluctuations, and minimize wear. Classified as not hazardous by OSHA regulations in the USA, the WHMIS in Canada, the NOM-018-STPS-2000 in Mexico and Brazil NBR 14725:2001
3-IN-ONE® Oil

Amber 3-ounce plastic squirt container with spout. Multipurpose oil that lubricates, cleans, and prevents rust. Contains no acids. Will not gum, corrode, or turn rancid. Insoluble in water. Percent volatile is nil.
No. 2 Lithium Grease

Amber or Dark Green 5-gram clear plastic tube with screw
Caution: Use only green or amber grease in the reagent storage module. Blue grease was available form 2010 to 2011, but it has a higher viscosity at cold temperatures creating a potential for reagent storage motion errors.
Silicone Lubricant (Auto Gloss)

Clear 0.5-gram clear plastic tube with screw Sigma Aldrich Co. DMPS2C, Dimethylpolysiloxane fluid. Used on early dispense chutes (non-nickel plated) and on orienters.
Magnalube G®

Green 0.75-ounce metal squeeze tube with screw cover. Contains TEFLON®. Deposits a fine protective, lubricating film on metal parts. Unaffected by corrosive moisture, does not migrate from hot surfaces, is water resistant, stable at high temperatures, resists oil separation, and is chemically and mechanically stable. Effectively lubricates at temperatures of -50 °F to 600 °F.

Wash Assembly Lubrication

These lubrication points for the improved wash assembly (there should not be any original wash assemblies in any instruments).

Caution: Do not lubricate the lead screws on the redesigned wash arm assembly. The lead screws are Teflon-coated and lubrication causes them to swell and bind.
Area Component Frequency Type Instructions REF No.
Wash Assembly Ejector Plate Rod (1) PMB No. 2 Lithium Grease Apply a light coat of lubricant to the rod above and below the ball bushing guide. Work the lubricant in by manually exercising the ejector plate. 7A
Wash Assembly Mixer Idler Pulley If Necessary (Do Not Perform during PMs) Magnalube G®
Caution:
  • Do not perform during a PM. For emergency cases only.
  • Do not allow lubricant to contact the belt or any area of the pulley that contacts the belt.

Remove the pulley, clean and lubricate the pulley shaft, and reassemble using the replacement clip.
