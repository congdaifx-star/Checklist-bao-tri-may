J19 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Peristaltic and Vacuum Pump

  • Vacuum Pump 1 connects to J19.

J19

Description

Signal / Voltage

To / From / Notes

1

+24V_POWER

24V

From J4-1 or 3.

24V from the Power distribution PCB through F1 (1.35A).

2

Vacuum Pump 1

24V (Off)

0V (On)

To Vacuum Pump 1 through Q1.

On: TP9 = 24V and D14 LED is On.

When the 5V control signal from J1-10 to U3-3 applies 24V to the gate of Q1, J19-2 is grounded activating the pump.
