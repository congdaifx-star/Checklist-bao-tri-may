Test Points - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Vacuum / Pressure Sensor

  • These are the test points on this PCB.
Note: The pressure sensor output is typically 0V to -20mV in standby, -0.4V at 30mmHg (typical with vacuum and peri pumps running), -2V at 150mmHg (Aspirate probe wash), and -4V at 300mmHg (tubing clamped, vacuum pump running).
