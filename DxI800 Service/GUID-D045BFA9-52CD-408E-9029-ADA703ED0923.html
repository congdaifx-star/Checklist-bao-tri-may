Specs - Instrument and Peripherals Electrical

Purpose

This document provides the electrical specifications for the DxI instrument and peripherals.

Overview

At times, customers and regulatory agencies require specifications for the DxI and its related peripherals. This document provides a source for the specifications most often requested from STS Chaska.

Instrument

These electrical specifications are for the UniCel DxI 600 and 800 instruments.

Parameter Specification
Line Voltage (nominal) 220 V AC
Line Voltage (max / min) (based on the instrument power supply and ability to operate) 180 to 264 V AC
Power Supply Output Power (Capability) 1750 Watts at 180 to 264 V AC (47 to 63 Hz)
Power Supply - Power Factor 0.99
Nominal Current Draw 5 Amps
Max Current Draw 10 Amps
Fuses 10 Amps
Power Consumed 1100 Volt Amps
Power 1100 Watts
Power 3753 BTU/hr
