J2 - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

  • The 24V power and stepper motor drive signals from the MCC2 PCB run through J2.
  • The HARNESS, INTERFACE, BULK FEEDER/CARRIAGE /P&P (P/N 116333) connects the Sample Pick and Place Interface PCB J2 to JP23 on the MCC backplane.
  • The HARNESS, INTERFACE, INCUBATE P&P (P/N 116343) connects the Incubator Pick and Place Interface PCB J2 to JP21 on the MCC backplane.
  • The HARNESS, INTERFACE, WASH P&P (P/N 116353) connects the Wash Pick and Place Interface PCB J2 to JP22 on the MCC backplane.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.
