Aligning Bulk Vessel Feeder

This procedure describes how to align the elevator scoops to the ejector cylinders in the bulk feeder module.

Required Tools:
  • White or green vessel-shaped alignment tool

Alignment of the bulk vessel feeder is independent of any other DxI instrument module or component alignment.

The bulk feeder module must be able to deliver vessels to the supply carriage continually without errors. Because this module is a secondary device for the supply carriage alignment, the alignment is described as a supply carriage alignment procedure. The internal alignment for the bulk feeder module aligns the elevator scoops to the ejector cylinders.

The alignments for this module include:
  • Align elevator scoops to ejector cylinders
Changes to alignment offsets are written to a file:
  • when the alignments are saved when exiting the Alignment screen.
  • by selecting Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
Warning: Bulk feeder movements are quick, full-motor strength motions. To prevent injury, keep your hands and fingers clear of moving parts when motor movements are being performed.
  1. Prepare to align the elevator scoops to the ejector cylinders.
    Note: If the bulk feeder module was replaced recently, then enter the offsets from the data sheet into the specific.ini file as an alignment starting point and proceed to the "Align Elevator Scoops to Ejector Cylinder" step.
    1. Attach a hemostat to the bulk vessel feeder air supply line to remove air pressure to the module.
      Caution: To prevent damage to the air supply line, clamp the line just enough to stop the flow of air through it.
    2. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
  2. Align elevator scoops to ejector cylinder position.
    1. Select Bulk Vessel Feeder as the primary device.
    2. Select the Pusher button on the Alignments screen, and then cycle the lower ejector cylinder enough times to remove the air pressure.
    3. Select Vessel Eject as the secondary device.
    4. Starting from the top vessel pusher, remove the vessels from the scoops.
    5. Select Home Device > Move to Position.
    6. Verify that the hopper is half full of vessels.
    7. Place the vessel-shaped alignment tool into the scoop at the lower ejector cylinder location.
      The open end of the tool should be towards the cylinder.
    8. Manually slide the ejector cylinder into the alignment tool just far enough to prevent the cylinder from bottoming out in the tool.
    9. Using the up and down directional keys to move the scoop, determine an offset range where the pusher does not lift up or press down on the tool.
      Each step down with the directional key moves the scoop down approximately 0.5 inch and then up into the new offset point. This is done to allow for the backlash in the chain and drive.
    10. Determine the center of the alignment range from the previous step and write it down.
    11. Remove the alignment tool.
    12. Select Home Device ten times.
      Remove the vessels from the scoops to prevent them from falling behind the instrument.
    13. Repeat the alignment for four more elevator scoops.
    14. Determine the average of the five alignment positions and save this average as the alignment offset.
    15. Select Alignments/Offsets > Preserve Offsets.
    16. Verify the offset position on a minimum of five more scoop positions.
    17. Remove the clamp from the air line.
  3. Verify the proper operation of the bulk vessel feeder.
    1. From the Device Diagnostics screen, select Exerciser.
    2. Run 200 replicates of Bulk Vessel Feeder.
    3. Select Cycle Vessels.
    4. Verify the following:
      • Vessels are moved out of the scoops correctly.
      • Vessels are oriented in the dispense chute correctly.
      • No errors occur.
    5. If the exerciser routine does not complete successfully or meet the requirements for the exerciser routine, then repeat the alignment procedure and the verification procedure.
  4. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
