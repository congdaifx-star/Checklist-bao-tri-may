Replacing Substrate Cap Gasket

This procedure describes how to replace the substrate cap assembly gasket when the gasket is damaged or a replacement gasket is called out during a PM procedure.

Perform this procedure when:
  • a gasket is damaged.
  • a replacement is called out during a PM procedure.
  1. Access the substrate cap gaskets.
    1. Open the main upper right cover.
    2. Remove the top air line from one of the substrate loader air cylinders to release air pressure from the system.
    3. Reattach the air line.
    4. Locate the six screws on the top plate of the substrate loader, and then loosen the screws two turns.
      Note: It is not necessary to remove the screws.
    5. Lift each load door to the up position. Leave the substrate bottles in place.
    6. Remove the two screws that attach the substrate doors to the substrate loader.
    7. Slide one door to the top, and spread the side walls enough to remove the door.
    8. Remove the second substrate door.
    9. Remove both LED masks by removing the two screws in each mask.
  2. Replace the substrate cap gaskets.
    1. Locate and remove the foam gaskets from the substrate supply caps.
      Caution: Do not touch the draw tube with the gasket or your fingers.
    2. Carefully slide the new gaskets into position. Confirm that each gasket is seated in the cap slot.
      Note: A small allen wrench can be used to push the replacement gasket into the slot.
    3. Remove both substrate bottles and discard them.
    4. Use a dry absorbent tissue to wipe down both draw tubes.
    5. Reattach the two LED masks.
    6. Spread the side walls to insert and attach each substrate door.
    7. Tighten the six screws on the top plate of the substrate loader.
    8. Close the main upper right cover.
  3. Initialize the instrument.
  4. Verify the proper operation of the substrate loader.
    1. Load and scan new substrate bottles onto the system.
    2. Prime the substrate 15 cycles.
    3. Run the substrate portion of a system check and verify that the results are within specification.
