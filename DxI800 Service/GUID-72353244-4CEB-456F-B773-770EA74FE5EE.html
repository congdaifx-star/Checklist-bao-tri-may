Adjusting Instrument to LAS Track

This procedure describes how to adjust the DxI instrument to the LAS track position when attaching the DxI instrument to the LAS track after separation for service or during initial installation.

  1. Adjust the height of the instrument.
    1. If necessary, fine-tune the alignments by physically adjusting the position of the DxI instrument.
      This position must meet the following requirements:
      • The DxI instrument is parallel to and level with the connection unit.
      • The sample probe is centered left-to-right (x-axis) and plumb (z-axis) with the auto-aliquot tube.
      • The SPOTB housing does not block the path of tubes running through it.

      The DxI instrument is not secured to the connection unit bracket (no screws are provided). The bracket serves only as a spacing guide.

    2. Rotate the four adjustable feet under the DxI instrument to achieve a separation of approximately 7- 7/16 inches between the bottom of the pipettor gantry and the top of the LAS track.
      Note:
      • Adjusting the DxI instrument to raise the wheels off the floor by about 1/8 inch is sufficient typically.
      • It is important also to level the DxI instrument front to back and side to side.
      • Mark the position of the feet on the floor to aid alignment following service in the future.
  2. For DxA automation track adjustments, see the procedure for aligning the sample pipettor of the DxA analyzer to the DxA transport in the DxA service manual for more information.
