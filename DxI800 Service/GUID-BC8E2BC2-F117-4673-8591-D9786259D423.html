Troubleshooting Monitor

Purpose

This document provides information for troubleshooting video monitor issues.

When to Perform

Troubleshoot whenever a monitor issue is encountered.

Manufacturer Notes

Manufacturers of monitors and related equipment used on the DxI:
  • NEC® - Brand name of monitor.
  • 3M® - Manufacturer of touchscreen component (DTx® installs).
  • DTx® - Supplier of peripherals (monitors, PCs, etc.) for Beckman Coulter.

Troubleshooting

Known monitor issues and the solutions are given in the following table.

Issue Possible Cause Corrective Action Additional Information
Erratic cursor on the screen or the cursor seems to be stuck in one spot. Internal defect. Replace the monitor (not field repairable). Failing screens exhibit a honeycomb pattern, best viewed with the screen powered off. Disconnect the touchscreen cable to gain mouse control.
Power button not responsive
  • The power switch on the back, side, or bottom of the monitor is in the Off position.
  • Vacation switch in wrong position.
  • Monitor reset actuated.
  • Position the power switch to On.
  • Position vacation switch to Off.
  • Unplug the power cord of the monitor from the AC outlet to power off and reset the monitor.
Move the power switch to On then press the power button on the front of the monitor.

The vacation switch overrides the monitor power on/off switch. This switch is located halfway up the right rear side of the monitor.

No display A: Signal cable not completely connected.

B: Signal cable pins bent or pushed in.

C: Front power switch or PC power switch not On.

A: Confirm that the signal cable is completely connected at both ends.

B: Straighten pins or replace cable or monitor (if cable is hard wired).

C: Position switches to On.
