UPS-AU Source and Service

This document provides information for the uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) used with the DxI instrument analytical unit.

Purpose

This document provides:
  • Part number
  • Supplier information
  • Servicing information
for the uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) used with the DxI instrument analytical unit.

UPS Service

Warranty support for the Toshiba UPS is provided through the UPS supplier. Do not have the UPS returned to Chaska.

To obtain parts or service for the Toshiba UPS:
  • Call 800-231-1412 or 713-466-0277.
  • Then ask for Field Service.
Have this information available:
  • Model number.
  • Serial number.
  • Installation date.
  • Application.
