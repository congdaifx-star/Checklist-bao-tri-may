Pipettor Matching - DxI 800

Sample Pipettor

The sample pipettor aspirates 3 SVs from each cup.
  • The first 2 SV volumes are 555 µL for a total of 9 tests.
  • The third SV aspiration is for 445 µL for a total of 7 tests.
