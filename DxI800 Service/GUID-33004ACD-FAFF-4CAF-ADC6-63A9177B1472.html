Removing Reagent Pipettor

This procedure describes how to remove the reagent pipettor.

Remove the reagent pipettor when a reagent pipettor requires service or part replacement.

Note: Reagent pipettors must be removed in pairs because of the design of the module. Remove pipettors: 1 and 2 or 3 and 4.
Note: DxI 600 instruments do not have pipettors 3 and 4 installed.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. From the rear of the instrument, remove the center gray rear cover and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    2. Remove the upper rear cover, and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    3. Open the upper left cover.
    4. Insert a cover interlock bypass.
    5. Initialize the system.
  2. Drain the tubing.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices  > Peristaltic and Vacuum Pumps.
    2. Enable the vacuum pump for the pipettor being serviced.
    3. Verify that the pipettor is directly over the wash tower.
    4. Remove the fitting from the pipettor precision pump and allow the wash buffer to drain into the wash tower.
      Caution: When draining wash buffer from the tubing, do not let fluid leak onto the instrument or any of the instrument components. When disconnecting the tubing, hold a dry towel at the in-line fitting.
    5. After the tubing is drained, disable the vacuum pump and exit the Digital Devices screen.
  3. Remove the cover interlock bypass.
  4. Remove power from the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  5. Remove the reagent pipettors.
    1. From the rear of the instrument remove these connectors from the appropriate reagent pipettor interface PCB:
      Note: The interface PCB for either pair of reagent pipettors is identical in layout.
      • J1 - Ribbon Cable
      • J2 - Reagent Pipettor Motor
      • J14 - Ultrasonics Cable
      • J15 - Ultrasonics Cable
    2. Slide the pipettor carriage fully forward on the gantry.
      Caution: Verify that the reagent pipettors are raised fully to the z-home position.
    3. Slide the sample pick- and-place carriage fully left to keep it from interfering.
    4. Remove the two screws that secure the reagent pipettor to the tower.
    5. Pull the reagent pipettor from the locating pins on the tower, and slide the pipettor module forward.
      Note: If the removed gantry has a Y axis linear slide there will be a shim present on the tower locating pins.

      Caution: Use care in handling the module. Avoid grasping the assembly by the guide rods or any other fragile component.
    6. Carefully remove the pipettor out through the front of the instrument.
