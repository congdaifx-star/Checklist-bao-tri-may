Accessing Parts and Assemblies

The instrument functions as a stand-alone instrument, as part of a workstation with multiple instruments connected side by side, or with an automation line behind it. When the instrument is located close to a wall, automation line, or another instrument, the instrument might need to be moved to access the rear of the instrument for servicing.

Note: In some locations, DxI instruments might be secured to the floor with seismic mounts. To move the instrument, it must be separated from the floor mounts.

Upper Front Covers

Two separate hinged covers cover the upper front of the instrument. Lift the covers together or separately to open. No special fasteners are used when the covers are open or closed. In the up position, an air cylinder supports each cover.

Opening these covers during operation requires the insertion of an interlock bypass tool into the interlock switch. This bypass tool is the same H-shaped plastic tool used in Access 2 instruments.

Side Panels

The instrument side panels are not designed to be removed.

Upper Front Covers

The gray panels on the back of the upper front covers can be removed (as shown in this photo) to improve access to the card cages when the covers are open. This enables easier troubleshooting by allowing the technician to view the instrument front to rear without having to open and close covers.

SPU Covers

The tinted SPU covers are interlocked to prevent personal injury to users. These covers can be opened when the system is in the ready mode.
