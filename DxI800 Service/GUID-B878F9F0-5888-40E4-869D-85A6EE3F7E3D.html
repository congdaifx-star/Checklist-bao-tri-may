Rear Connectors - Card Cage - Upper Left - Layout

Connectors

The cables on the rear of the upper left card cage:
  • are not on earlier instruments.
  • eliminate the difficulty due to limited space for Field Service making connections to the left side of the card cage .
  • enable Field Service to connect a mouse, keyboard, and monitor to the instrument easily for diagnosis.
  • can be added if not present. Refer to the DxI Parts List and search for Cable Kit, Video, Mouse, Keyboard.
Connector Connects to: Function:

J10

Luminometer HVPS

Communication of HVDAC and HVMON signals with the DACQ PCB and provides +24 V DC to the luminometer HVPS (originating from +24V_DAQ/LUMI J7 Power Distribution Interface PCB).

J11

Not used

COM2

COM B/2 on the rear panel

Serial communication with external device to CPU PCB Com2 port.

J14

J7 on the Power Distribution PCB

Provides +24V_DAQ/LUMI power for luminometer and status signals for all +24 V DC power supplies to DACQ PCB.

J15

J1 on the Temperature Interface PCB

Provides the status of the temperature sensors from the incubator, wash wheel, substrate, reagent coolers (three), reagent storage air, and sample storage to the DACQ PCB.

J16

JP8 on the Pressure Sensor Interface PCB (above reagent pipettors)

Provides the status of the upper cabinet temperature and the pressure sensors for the sample probe, aspirate probe 1, the four reagent pipettors, and the substrate bubble detector to the DACQ PCB. It also provides +12 V DC and -12 V DC for the Pressure Connector PCB.

J17

JP1 on the Pressure Sensor (Vacuums) Interface PCB (in the fluids drawer)

Provides the status of the lower cabinet temperature and the five wash tower vacuum sensors and the aspirate probe collar vacuum sensor to the DACQ PCB. It also provides +12 V DC and -12 V DC for the Pressure Sensor Interface PCB.

J18

J3 on the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB

Provides the status of the air pressure and wash buffer bottle and waste bottle weight sensors to the DACQ PCB. It also provides +12 V DC and -12 V DC for the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB.
