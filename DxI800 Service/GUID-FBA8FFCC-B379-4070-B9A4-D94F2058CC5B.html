Replacing Sample Storage Module Thermistor

This procedure describes how to replace the thermistor on the sample storage module.

Connect a test thermistor to the interface PCB to confirm that the thermistor is defective before replacing the thermistor. The thermistor on the sample storage module is sealed against moisture. Thermistors that are not sealed can fail prematurely.

Do not interchange the DxI sample storage thermistors and the Access sample and reagent module thermistors.

  1. Remove the sample storage module.
    Note: Refer to the SS: Removing Sample Storage Moduleprocedure.
  2. Remove the thermistor.
    1. Disconnect the thermistor connector from J11 near the lower right corner of the interface PCB.
    2. Remove the thermistor cable from any harness clamps or cable-ties.
    3. Remove the foam plug from the thermistor access hole in the module.
    4. Use the slotted nut-driver (specialty tool) to rotate the thermistor counter-clockwise until it is completely free of the module.
  3. Install the thermistor.
    1. Remove the spacer washer from the old thermistor, place the washer onto the new thermistor, and then apply a small amount of thermal compound to the threads.
    2. Place the thermistor into position and start threading the thermistor into the mounting hole by-hand, rotating the thermistor and cable clockwise one or two turns.
    3. Place the slotted nut-driver over the thermistor and cable, and rotate the nut-driver clockwise until the thermistor is seated fully in the mounting hole.
    4. Place the foam plug back into the thermistor access hole in the module.
    5. Route the thermistor cable through any harness clamps, and replace any wire-ties that were cut when the thermistor was removed.
    6. Connect the thermistor to J11 near the lower right corner of the interface PCB.
  4. Install the sample storage module and verify instrument performance.
    Note: Refer to the SS: Installing Sample Storage Module procedure.
