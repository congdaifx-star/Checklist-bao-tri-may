Conn Overview - PCB - Interface - Reagent Storage

Connectors

Note:
  • The motors are controlled by the MCC7 PCB.
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.
# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, REAGENT STORAGE connects J1 to J73 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The reagent and substrate BCR outputs, the gripper open and closed, the gripper and tray home, the waste door open, and the Interface PCB Data signals to the MCC Backplane PCB run through J1.

J2

HARNESS, INTERFACE, REAGENT STORAGE connects J2 to JP72 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The reagent gripper X/Z-axis and reagent load tray stepper motor drive signals run through J2.

J3

CABLE, REAGENT STORE AIR TEMPERATURE EXT connects to J3 to J9 on the Temperature Interface PCB.

The reagent air temperature signal runs through J3.

J4

The reagent air temperature thermistor connects to J4.

Reagent cooler air temperature 0.3 to 0.4 V (0.39 V @ 6 °C).

J5

CABLE, REAGENT FAN POWER connects J5 to J14 on the Power Distribution PCB.

The 24 V power from the Power Distribution PCB runs through J5.

J6

Reagent fan 1 connects to J6.

Reagent fan 1 (24 V).

J7

Reagent fan 2 connects to J7.

Reagent fan 2 (24 V).

J8

The bar code window heater connects to J8.

Bar code window heater (24 V).

J9

Reagent fan 3 connects to J9.

Reagent fan 3 (24 V).

J10

The reagent gripper X-axis encoder connects to J10.

X-axis encoder signal.

J11

The reagent gripper Z-axis encoder connects to J11.

Z-axis encoder signal.

J12

Sensor, Home, Reagent Gripper connects to J12.

The reagent pack gripper X/Z-axis home Hall effect sensor.

To J1-3 and 4.

Home: TP3 = 0 V and D1 LED is On. Not home: TP3 = 5 V.

J13

J13 is a series of four flat test pads. No connector or raised test points are present.

The reagent gripper X-axis encoder signals are present on J13.

J14

J14 is a series of four flat test pads. No connector or raised test points are present.

The reagent gripper Z-axis encoder signals are present on J14.

J15

The Sensor, Status, Gripper connects to J15.

Reagent pack gripper open Hall effect sensor.

The signal goes to J1-10.

Open: TP4 = 0 V and D2 LED is On. Not open: TP4 = 5 V.

J16

The Sensor, Status, Gripper connects to J16.

Reagent pack gripper closed Hall effect sensor.

The signal goes to J1-9.

Closed: TP7 = 0 V and D6 LED is On. Not closed: TP7 = 5 V.

J17

HARNESS, REAGENT DOOR SWITCH connects J17 to the door switch SENSOR, DOOR, HONEYWELL IDMI.

The reagent storage module (front) door closed signals run through J17.

Closed: TP8 = 0 V and LED D8 is Off.

J18

J18 (not used) is a PIC programming port.

Programming port.

J19

The reagent load tray stepper motor connects to J19.

Stepper motor drive signal.

J20

The reagent gripper Z-axis stepper motor connects to J20.

Stepper motor drive signal.

J21

The reagent gripper X-axis stepper motor connects to J21.

Stepper motor drive signal.

J22

The reagent pack waste door closed sensor (Hall effect magnetic sensor included as part of the waste door air cylinder) connects to J22.

Reagent pack waste door opened and closed.

Closed / Home: TP12 = 0 V and D16 LED is On.

J23

The reagent pack tray out sensor connects to J23.

Pack tray out - open, end of travel.

Tray out: TP13 = 0V and D17 LED is On.

J24

The reagent pack waste door air valve connects to J24.

Reagent pack waste door (open) air valve.

Enable: TP11 = 5V, D11 LED is On, and LED on air valve is on.

5V control signal to Q1 gate grounds J24-2 to activate the air valve.

J25

HARNESS, REAGENT DOOR LOCK SWITCH connects SENSOR, DOOR, HONEYWELL IDMI to J25.

The reagent pack load tray cover closed signal runs through J25.

The harness connects to the lower two pins of the switch.

The switch provides 24 V to K1 relay enabling the reagent load tray motor and to Q4 gate, grounding (door closed) signal to J1-7.

Normally closed. (Open = 0V, Closed = 24V)

J26

The reagent pack tray in home sensor connects to J26.

Pack tray in - closed, home.

Tray in, home: TP17 = 0V and D19 LED is On.

J27

The reagent pack gripper close air valve connects to J27.

Gripper close - enable: TP18 = 5V, D20 LED is On, and LED on air valve is on.

5V control signal to Q2 gate grounds J27-2 to activate the air valve.

J28

The Reagent storage bar code reader READER, BARCODE connects to J28 (D-sub 15 pin).

Reagent storage bar code reader signals, in and out.

The interface cable is part of the BCR assembly.

J29

CABLE, RIBBON, LEXAN STATUS PANEL connects J29 to J1 on the Lexan status panel.

The Lexan status display panel connects to J29.

J30

CABLE, RIBBON, WAND, SUBSTRATE connects the READER, SUBSTRATE BARCODE GUN to J30.

Substrate bar code reader (handheld IT3800).

J31

Reagent fan 4 (not used) connects to J31.

Reagent fan 4 (24 V).

J32

REAGENT_LOAD_LED (not used)

J32 is not used. The reagent load status LED connects to J3 on the Lexan Panel Interface PCB.
