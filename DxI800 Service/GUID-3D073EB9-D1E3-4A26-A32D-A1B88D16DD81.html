J1 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Peristaltic and Vacuum Pump

  • The Vacuum Pump 1, 2, 3, and 4 enable signals and the Peristaltic Pump Home/Index sensor signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, PERI/VAC PUMP (P/N 116181) connects J1 to J31 (MCC3) on the MCC Backplane PCB.
