Adjusting Vessel Dispenser

This procedure describes how to mechanically adjust the vessel dispenser block and the vessel dispenser position sensor when the vessel dispenser air cylinder is replaced or has errors dispensing vessels.

Required Materials:
  • There are no special tools for this procedure

Perform this procedure when the vessel dispenser air cylinder is replaced or has errors dispensing vessels.

  1. Adjust the vessel dispenser.
    1. Verify that the dispenser air cylinder is in the normal, retracted position.
    2. Determine if the current adjustment is correct. The end of the dispenser block should be aligned flush with the rear of the vessel output guide where the vessel dispenser block comes through.
      • If the dispenser block is flush with the guide, the adjustment is correct.
      • If the dispenser block is not flush, the adjustment is incorrect. Continue with the adjustment of the vessel dispenser block.
    3. Remove the air cylinder from the dispenser (2 screws).
    4. Loosen the locking nut on the air cylinder shaft.
    5. Rotate the end of the shaft in the appropriate direction to correct the dispenser block alignment.
    6. Tighten the locking nut.
    7. Reassemble the air cylinder and the dispenser block into the dispenser housing (2 screws).
      Note: Orient the dispenser block with the large opening farthest to the rear of the instrument facing upward.
    8. To verify this adjustment, go back to the beginning of this step and determine if the adjustment is correct.
  2. Adjust the position sensor on the vessel dispenser.
    1. Verify that the dispense air cylinder is in the normal retracted position.
    2. Push the dispense block slightly to the rear of the instrument until the LED on the position sensor goes off.
    3. Determine if the current adjustment is correct.
      • If LED on the position sensor goes off at 1/8 inch travel, the adjustment is correct.
      • If LED on the position sensor does not go off at 1/8 inch travel, the adjustment is incorrect. Continue with the adjustment of the vessel dispenser position sensor.
      The position sensor window of detection is approximately 1/4 inch wide. Adjustment to 1/8 inch provides positive detection of the position, without the risk of the piston traveling through the sensor and losing detection at the fully-retracted position.
    4. Loosen the screw that holds the sensor clamp to the air cylinder shaft
    5. Move the sensor in a direction to correct the adjustment.
    6. Tighten the screw on the sensor.
    7. To verify this adjustment, go back to the beginning of this step and determine if the adjustment is correct.
  3. Verify the operation of the vessel dispenser.
    1. From the Device Diagnostics screen, select Exerciser.
    2. Run 50 replicates.
    3. Select Cycle Vessels.
  4. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
