Dilution Assay Test Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Dilution Assay Ratio Problems

Ratio Possible Causes Troubleshooting/Repair
Greater than 300
Note: A ratio greater than 300 indicates over diluted sample.

Low sample delivery.

Excessive diluent volume.

  • Check or adjust the reagent pipettor z-alignments.
  • Inspect precision pump belt for broken teeth or stretched belt.
  • Inspect precision pump seals.
  • Inspect or replace the precision pump return spring. Check that the washer is present below the spring.
  • Verify the black end cap on the pump bottom is secure. Specific end cap torque values:
    • Pump reliability components (light tan piston/seal guide) 10 in-lb
    • Previous version (brown Delrin guide) 12 in-lb
Less than 205
Note: A ratio less than 205 indicates under diluted sample.
Low diluent delivery.
  • Verify the precision pump low-resolution/high volume dispense check (200 µL).
    Note: Refer to the Tests: Volume Check procedure.
  • Inspect the precision pump motor, belt, and drive for a possible backlash problem, loose belt coupling screw, or broken teeth.
  • Ultrasonics mixing.

Troubleshooting Dilution Assay CV Problems

Ratio Possible Causes Troubleshooting/Repair
The LumiAP CV is high RV Pipetting precision is poor.
  • Remove the RV spring finger from the vessel positioner assembly near the luminometer, and install the vessel positioner assembly.
  • Reagent pipettor valve
    • Clean wash buffer residue
    • Check for worn or chipped components
  • Reagent pipettor pump belt
  • Ceramic piston and seal: Clean and reposition
  • Precision Pump o-rings (missing or worn)
    • Between pressure transducer and buffer reservoir
    • Between pressure transducer and pump housing
  • Reagent pipettor probe
  • Reagent pipettor alignments
  • Fluidics tubing from pump to probe
    • Cracked or small cuts causing leakage
    • Leaking tubing fitting at transducer
    • Quick-disconnect fitting cracked or loose
    • Clear in-line fitting leaking
    • Tubing flares damaged

The dTest CV is high or

The dAP CV is high

Dilution pipetting is poor.

Carryover from the LumiAP assay is occurring.

Pipettor adjustment/alignment to reagent carriage.

  • Precision pump or seals or o-rings
  • Precision valve
  • Reagent pipettor probe
  • Pipettor adjustment or alignments
  • Reagent carriage adjustment: Disable the pipettor y-motor and verify the pipettor x-alignment tracks through the center of all four RV slots.
    Note: Refer to the CA: Adjusting Reagent Carriage procedure.
  • Ultrasonics
  • Wash tower
  • Splashing onto Substrate Probe
  • Luminometer
  • Precision Pump o-rings (missing or worn)
    • Between pressure transducer and buffer reservoir
    • Between pressure transducer and pump housing
  • Fluidics tubing from pump to probe
    • Cracked or small cuts causing leakage
    • Leaking tubing fitting at transducer
    • Quick-disconnect fitting cracked or loose
    • Clear in-line fitting leaking
    • Tubing flares damaged
Either mean is high or low

The preparation of the 1:201 or 1:501 System Check solution may be inaccurate.

The Luminometer response may be outside the norm.

Systematic over/under pipetting pickup or delivery.

  • Verify 1:201 and 1:501 system check solutions.
  • Verify sample is system check (not HS system check)
  • Precision pump
  • Precision valve
  • Primary probe
  • Pipettor alignment
  • Ultrasonics
  • Wash tower

  • Luminometer

LumiAP CV high across multiple pipettors

Sample pipettor delivering bubbles.

Substrate delivery issue.

  • Remove the RV spring finger from the vessel positioner assembly near the luminometer, and install the vessel positioner assembly.
  • Sample pipettor valve
  • Sample pump belt
  • sample pump o-rings
    • Between pressure transducer and buffer reservoir
    • Between pressure transducer and pump housing
  • Sample pump quick-disconnect fitting
  • Fluidics tubing from sample pump to probe - Cracked or small cuts causing leakage.
  • Substrate volume checks
