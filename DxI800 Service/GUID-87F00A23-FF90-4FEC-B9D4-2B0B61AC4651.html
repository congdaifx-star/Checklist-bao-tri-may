Installing Seismic Kit

This procedure provides instructions for installing a seismic kit to fasten the DxI instrument to the floor of the laboratory.

Refer to LX Seismic Mounting Procedure.pdf for installing the mounting hardware. This PDF can be detached and printed or emailed to any California customer who is requesting this information.

Once installed and firmly attached to the floor, this kit complies with the 1995 California Uniform Building Code as it pertains to seismic safety. It does not comply with seismic safety codes from Japan, Alaska, or any other location outside of California. This kit prevents the instrument from rolling in an earthquake and damaging laboratory equipment or instrumentation, or injuring lab personnel.

Mounting Choices:

The seismic engineer has determined that this product must be bolted to secure floor braces using all four corners or two opposing corners.

Customer laboratory or seismic engineer responsibilities:
  1. Install the seismic floor mounting brackets including determining:
    • if any additional holes are needed in the seismic floor mounting braces.
    • fasteners used to affix the brackets to the floor.
    • selecting one of these mounting options:
      • four corners
      • two corners—Front left and right rear
      • two corners— Front right and left rear
  2. Have the correct number of braces correctly secured to the floor before the arrival of the DxI. The Beckman Coulter service technician must bolt the instrument to the braces that have been attached to the floor.
