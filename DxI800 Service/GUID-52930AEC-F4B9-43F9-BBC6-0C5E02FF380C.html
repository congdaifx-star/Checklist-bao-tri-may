Replacing Wash Arm

This procedure describes how to replace the wash arm. The analytical module must be removed before replacing the wash arm.

The analytical module must be removed before replacing the wash arm and installed after replacing the wash arm.
Note:
  • Refer to the AU: Removing Analytical Module procedure.
  • Refer to the AU: Installing Analytical Module procedure.
Warning: Potentially, the magnets in the analytical module are strong enough to interfere with medical devices, ferromagnetic implants, or loose metal objects in close proximity to the magnetic field. Consider the following:
  • Persons with medical devices or metal implants should not hold the wash arm side of the analytical module close to their body.
  • Consult your physician or your medical device manual for more information.
  • The magnetic field near the analytical module was measured:
    • near the wash arm: 26.4 mT.
    • one foot from the wash arm: 0.12 mT.
  1. Remove the wash arm from the analytical module.
    1. Remove the connectors from the analytical module interface PCB that is located at the rear of the analytical module.
      • J12 - Aspirate Arm Z-Motor
      • J10 - Dispense Arm Z-Motor
    2. Remove both harnesses from the plastic clamp that is located under the analytical module.
    3. Carefully tilt the analytical module on its side, and then remove the screw securing the wash arm to the bottom of the analytical module.
      It is a 5/32-inch hex screw that is located next to the wash carousel motor.
    4. Remove the screws securing the wash arm slide mechanism (two 3/16-inch hex screws).
    5. Slide the wash arm away from the analytical module.
    6. Remove the mounting screw that is located next to the slide guide rod (5/32-inch hex screw).
    7. Pull the wash arm away from the analytical module.
      Note:
      • The wash arm is pinned, and it might be necessary to move the wash arm back and forth to free it from the analytical module.
      • Do not hook any wires, cables, or tubing when removing the wash arm.
  2. Install replacement wash arm on the analytical module.
    1. Remove any packing material particles or dust from the replacement wash arm, especially on the lead screws and guide rods.
    2. Install the selector valve manifold on the eject plate to the back of the wash arm (two 3/32-inch hex screws).
    3. Insert the pneumatics tubing into the pneumatics connectors.
    4. Attach the plastic clamp to the top of the wash arm (5/64-inch hex screw).
    5. Remove the screws securing the wash arm slide mechanism (two 3/16-inch hex screws).
    6. Slide the wash arm slide mechanism away from the mounting position.
    7. Position the wash arm over the guide pins on the analytical module, and then push the wash arm into place.
    8. Insert and tighten the mounting screw that is located next to the slide guide rod (5/32-inch hex screw).
    9. Carefully tilt the analytical module on its side, and then insert and tighten the screw securing the wash arm to the bottom of the analytical module.
      It is a 5/32-inch hex screw that is located next to the wash carousel motor.
    10. Route the two harnesses through the plastic clamp under the analytical module.
    11. Connect the connectors from the analytical module interface PCB that is located at the rear of the analytical module.
      • J12 - Aspirate Arm Z-Motor
      • J10 - Dispense Arm Z-Motor
