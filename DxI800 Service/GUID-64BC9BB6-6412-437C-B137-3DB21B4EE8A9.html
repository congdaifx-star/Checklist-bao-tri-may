Reagent Pack and SPU Onload Microscan MS-3 Readers

The specifications for both Microscan MS-3 readers are identical.

Parameter Specification
Type Class II
Power 5 VDC + or - 5%, 200 mV p-p max ripple, 260 mA @ 5 VDC (typical)
Communications RS-422 / RS- 485 (RS-232)
Connection 15 Pin D-Sub HD Socket Connector
Scan Width Angle > 70 degrees, + or -50 degrees pitch, + or - 40 degrees skew
Read Range 1.7 - 4.1" (7.5 mil C39)
Scan Width 1.8" Left. 2.0 Right @ 2.0" from reader window (6.6 mil C128)
Scan Rate 800 scans / second
Code Types C 128, C93

The specifications for both Microscan 710 readers are identical.

Density Low
Scan Rate 500 per second
Communications RS-422 / 485
Connection 15 Pin D-Sub HD with 22 in permanent cable
Read Range 2.5 in to 3.75 in
