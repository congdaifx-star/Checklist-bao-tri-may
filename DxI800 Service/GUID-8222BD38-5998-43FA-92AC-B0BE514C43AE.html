Adjusting Ultrasonics CnS Transducer Temperature

This procedure describes how to adjust the temperature of the ultrasonics CnS transducer on the DxI instrument reagent pipettors.

Required Material:
  • Beckman Coulter approved multimeter for use on the Access Product line.
  • Fluke Model 80TK temperature adaptor with thermocouple or an alternate Beckman Coulter approved thermocouple type temperature meter.

Prerequisites:

Before performing this adjustment verify that:
  • the instrument is powered up for a minimum of 15 minutes.
  • the transducer heater is connected and operational.
  • there is fluid in the tubing and transducer housing (prime if necessary).
Adjust the temperature of the ultrasonics CnS transducer on the DxI instrument reagent pipettors:
  • after replacing the ultrasonic CnS transducer, pipettor interface PCB, or reagent pipettor module.
  • during the PM procedures.
The transducer temperature:
  • affects the displacement and magnetic particle suspension on the main pipettor tip.
  • is directly related to the amount of current sent through the heater by the heater controller.
  • is not monitored by system software. To view the temperature, you must use an external temperature measuring device.
  1. Adjust the temperature of the ultrasonic transducer.
    Note: If an accurate measurement cannot be obtained from the transducer, another option is to drain the pipettor, remove the CnS connector, and then measure the temperature on the transducer thread.
    1. Attach the 80 TK thermocouple device to the multimeter.
    2. Move each gantry forward and upward as far as possible to improve accessibility to the transducers.
    3. Place the tip of the thermocouple onto the transducer supply tubing fitting and housing.
      Caution: Do not contact the thermocouple tip while holding it in position.
      Caution: Do not place the thermocouple in contact with the heater.

      A thermocouple can be formed into a curve as shown here. Attach it to something solid nearby, causing it to be 'spring-loaded' against the surface being measured. The thermocouple wire, not the tip, can be clamped with a small clamp or tie-wrap to something solid to eliminate the need to hold it in position manually.

    4. Monitor the temperature reading, note the temperature reading after it has stabilized for 60 seconds, and adjust the temperature, if necessary.
      • If the temperature is 37 °C ±2 °C, then no adjustment is necessary and this procedure is complete.
      • If the temperature is not 37 °C ±2 °C, then
        1. Adjust the temperature by rotating the appropriate adjustment potentiometer.
          Note: Moving the adjustment potentiometer one turn clockwise increases the temperature by approximately 1 °C.
          Caution: Lowering the temperature requires more recovery time than raising the temperature.
        2. Allow the temperature to stabilize for ten minutes, then repeat the step to take a second reading. If necessary, repeat until the temperature is within specification.
    5. Remove the thermocouple and tools from the instrument.
  2. If the CnS connector was removed to measure the temperature, verify that the CnS connector has been installed.
  3. Verify the proper operation of the heater and ultrasonics.
    • If the temperature was adjusted by 5 °C or less, then verification is not required.
    • If the temperature was adjusted by more than 5 °C, then proceed with verification.
      1. Calibrate the transducer.
      2. Perform QC and verify that the results are within customer specifications.
      3. If QC fails, then perform assay calibrations and repeat QC.
