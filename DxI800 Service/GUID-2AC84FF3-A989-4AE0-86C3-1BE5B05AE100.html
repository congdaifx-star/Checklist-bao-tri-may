Removing Reagent Storage Module

This procedure describes how to remove the reagent storage module to access the reagent cooler.

Remove the reagent storage module to access the reagent cooler.

  1. Unload and refrigerate the reagent packs.
    1. Select Supplies > Reagent Supplies > Unload all Reagent Packs.
    2. When the load tray opens with packs, remove them.
    3. Place the removed packs into refrigerated storage.
  2. Home all devices by selecting Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Home All.
  3. Shut down the DxI instrument.
    1. Select Configure  > PC Admin > System Admin > Shutdown Instrument to exit the software in preparation for powering off the instrument.
    2. Enter the password Access2Support, and then select OK.
    3. At the pop-up You must now turn off the instrument, using the instrument power switch, select OK.
    4. After the instrument software has shut down, turn off the instrument at the power switch.
  4. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Disconnect the instrument power cord from the power source.
    2. Open the upper left and upper right front covers.
    3. Prepare a clean area for the reagent storage module to be placed after it is removed from the instrument.
  5. Detach the reagent storage module.
    1. Open the solid waste door, and remove the solid waste container.
    2. Remove the two screws that secure the left end of the reagent storage module.
      The screws are located on the overhead surface of the solid waste compartment.
  6. For instruments with Magnetek power supplies, remove the power conditioner module.
    1. Open the lower right access door and remove the power supply cover (five screws).
    2. Remove the cover on the power conditioner (two screws).
    3. Disconnect the two power connectors from the power conditioner.
    4. Remove the front mounting screw from the power conditioner.
    5. Slide the power conditioner partially out of the instrument. Remove the mounting screw for the green ground wire that is mounted on the left side of the power conditioner.
    6. Remove the power conditioner from the instrument.
    7. Remove the two screws securing the right end of the reagent storage module.
      These screws are located on the overhead surface of the power supply compartment.
  7. Open the SPU.
    1. Open the upper left cover of the instrument.
    2. Loosen the locking screw.
      The locking screw prevents the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument. It is not necessary to remove the screw because the SPU plate is slotted.
    3. Remove the 3/16-in hex bolt located near the right end of the SPU cover.
    4. Open the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    5. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin that holds the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    6. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end away from the instrument.
      Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    7. (For China and Canada Only.) On the front facing the edge of the Framus assembly, install the Chinese and French labels adjacent to the existing English label.
      Note: The labels are in the DxI Installation Kit.

  8. Remove the main front cover (Framus).
    1. Remove the two screws holding the right end of the main front cover (Framus) to the instrument.
      The Framus is the instrument skin and frame located mostly to the right of the SPU.
      Note: DxI 800 only: Early instruments also have two screws holding the left end of the Framus to the instrument. If the two screws are there, loosen them. The left end of the Framus is slotted for removal.
    2. DxI 800 only: Loosen the four captive screws mounting the substrate loader module to the Framus. Pull the captive pin on the module left side, and slide the module upward so that the pin locks into the hole provided on the front of the instrument.
    3. DxI 800 only: Remove the triangular-shaped support plate at the center of the Framus (four screws).
      The mounting holes are slotted to simplify removal.
    4. Lift the Framus up slightly, and then remove these connectors from the reagent storage interface PCB:
      • J25 - Reagent Load Door
      • J30 - Bar Code Reader
      • DxI 800 only: J29 - Ribbon Cable
    5. Disconnect the two SPU interlock switch wires from the terminals on the under side of the Framus.
    6. Remove the Framus from the instrument.
      Note: To disengage the cover latch on the reagent load tray and remove the Framus, operate the release lever under the load tray inside the lower right access door.
  9. Disconnect the reagent storage module.
    1. Remove the screw that attaches the reagent storage module ground strap to the instrument base plate.
      The ground screw is located to the right and slightly behind the luminometer.
    2. Disconnect these cables from the reagent storage module interface PCB.
      The reagent storage interface PCB is mounted on top of the reagent storage module.
      • J1 - Ribbon Cable
      • J2 - Motor Drive Harness
      • J3 - Reagent Cooler
      • J5 - 24 V DC Supply
    3. Disconnect the air supply line by pushing in on the locking collar and pulling the tube out of the fitting.
  10. Remove the reagent storage module.
    Caution: When removing or installing the reagent storage module, do not damage the bottom of the waste chute door, the load tray motor pulley, the load tray drive pulley, or the load tray home sensor and wires.
    1. Confirm that all cabling is free from the module.
    2. Lift the module upward until all four feet on the module are disengaged from the detents.
    3. While lifting the module, slowly pull the module away from the instrument and then remove the module.
    4. Carefully place the bottom surface of the module down onto the location prepared for it.
