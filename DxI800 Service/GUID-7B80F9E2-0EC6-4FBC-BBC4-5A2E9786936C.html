J2 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Peristaltic and Vacuum Pump

  • The Wash Tower 1, 2, 3, and 4 Peri Pump motor drive signals run through J2.
  • HARNESS, INTERFACE, PERISTALTIC & VACUUM PUMP (P/N 116323) connects J2 to JP32 (MCC3) on the MCC Backplane PCB.
  • The harness also connects MCC Backplane JP31 to Peri / Vac Pump I/F J3.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.
