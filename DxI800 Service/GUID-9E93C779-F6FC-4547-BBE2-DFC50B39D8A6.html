Removing Particle Wash Aspirate Peristaltic Pump

This procedure describes how to remove the particle wash aspirate peristaltic pump from the instrument.

Perform this procedure when it is necessary to:
  • replace the index sensor.
  • replace the pump head.
  • replace the particle wash aspirate peristaltic pump.
  • clean the peristaltic pump thoroughly.

These critical warnings apply to the particle wash aspirate pump cartridges on all DxI instruments.

Warning: The position of the cassettes is critical for correct pump operation in the old-style particle wash aspirate pumps. The positions are numbered 1 to 3 beginning at the motor end. Maintain this orientation. Number existing cartridges in fielded instruments using a permanent marker.
Warning: Cassettes are not identical. The dimensions of each cassette can vary slightly from one cartridge to another. Once a cassette is installed in a pump and is functioning correctly, it is matched to the pump.
  • Do not move cassettes to a different position within the pump.
  • Do not move cassettes to different pump.
  • Do not replace cassettes with another cassette of the same style.

Replace cassettes by installing a replacement pump head.

Warning: Do not place a redesigned lever guard from a pump head with factory-numbered cassettes, on a peristaltic pump with an original lever guard. The width of the slot is narrower on the redesigned lever guard. The narrower slot could allow a cassette to 'click' one or more positions tighter or looser, which could cause tubing damage or loss of pumping action.
Caution: Use caution when replacing tubing. Do not twist the tubing in the pump once it is replaced.
Caution: If a pump has no issues related to the cartridges, then leave the cartridges where they are and number the cartridges using the above numbering scheme.
  1. Confirm that the three pump cartridges are numbered.

    If the pump cartridges are not numbered and they will be reinstalled, use a permanent marker to number the cartridges 1 through 3 starting at the motor end.

  2. Remove power using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  3. Remove the peristaltic pump.
    1. Disconnect the J20 (Aspirate Pump Motor) and J21 (Aspirate Pump Index) connectors from the fluidics interface PCB.
    2. Remove the tubing from the fitting.
      Caution: To prevent the possibility of damage caused by fluid leaks when removing the tubing, remove all aspirate probes from the aspirate probe plate and place them in a beaker before performing this step.
      Note: Refer to the reference drawing when performing this step.
      • If the pump manifold is in location A, remove the tubing from fitting 4 on the fluid outlet manifold.
      • If the pump manifold is in location B, remove the tubing from fittings 1, 2, and 3 on the fluid outlet manifold.
      Note: The pump shown includes a lever guard of original design. The pump being removed might have a redesigned lever guard.
    3. Disconnect the three peristaltic pump tubes from the in-line fittings on the short tubes that connect to the rear of the probe connection panel.
    4. Remove the two peristaltic pump mounting screws.
    5. Remove the pump from the instrument.
