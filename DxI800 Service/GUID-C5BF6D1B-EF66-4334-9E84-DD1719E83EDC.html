Installing External Drain Kit

This procedure describes how to install an external drain kit.

Required Materials:

The part number for the kit is found in the Peripherals (All) and Kits Section of the Parts List.

The external drain fitting in the pneumatic cabinet door is installed in DxI instrument manufacturing. If this fitting is broken, then refer to the Baseplate / Chassis / Final Parts List.

Kit, Waste Drain, External Notes
fitting, insert, 90-degree, quick-disconnect, shutoff Male fitting that connects to quick-disconnect
protective bracket, waste fitting With biohazard warning label.
cable tie For securing waste tubing to bracket
tubing (25 ft), 1/8-in inner diameter To be cut to correct length depending on proximity to drain
Optional Parts to Order Separately if Needed:
  • Parts required for older DxI 800 instruments without option for house air line connection:
    • Cover, rear lower right
    • Kit, house air connect
  • Parts required if connecting to an EMS (effluent management system):
    • Tubing (0.5 ft), 1/2-in inner diameter
    • Tubing (0.5 ft), 1/4-in inner diameter
    • Union fitting (1/2 in to 1/4 in)
    • Union fitting (1/8 in to 1/4 in)

Install an external drain kit when a customer wants liquid waste to flow directly into a drain rather than into the on-board liquid waste bottles.

Warning: Do not install the drain kit in customer sites with lead, brass, tin, copper, or zinc plumbing. Sodium azide can react with these materials to form highly explosive metal azides.
  1. Confirm that the instrument is in the Ready mode.
  2. Remove the lower right rear cover.
  3. Flush the tubing leading to the liquid waste bottles to prevent liquid waste from dripping while installing the drain kit.
    1. Select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices > Peristaltic and Vacuum Pumps.
    2. Clear the check box for Liquid Waste Reservoir Auto Control On.
    3. Select OK at the warning prompt.
    4. Select the check box for Liquid Waste Peristaltic On.
    5. Run the liquid waste peri-pump until all the liquid is flushed out of the tubing to the input side of the selector valve manifold for the liquid waste bottles.
    6. Select Done.
  4. Install the external drain kit
    1. Empty, thoroughly rinse, and dry both liquid waste bottles.
      Note: Leave the bottles in position in case the customer must revert to intermediate waste containers (for example, during lab construction).
    2. Disconnect the tubing from the input side of the selector valve manifold located directly behind the waste buckets.
      Note: Do not discard the tubing in case the customer must revert to intermediate waste containers.
    3. Remove the tubing from the service loop chain and connect it to the fitting inside the lower right rear cover.
    4. Replace the lower right rear cover.
      Caution: When installing the cover, confirm that the tube is not pinched or kinked.
    5. Insert the quick-disconnect fitting from the drain kit into the fitting on the lower right rear cover.
    6. Select Configure > System Setup  > Supplies Setup.
    7. Clear the check box for Use Internal Containers.
  5. Route the drain tubing to a sanitary drain.
    1. Route the new waste tubing and verify the tubing meets the following guidelines.
      • The tubing cannot be longer than 25 feet.
      • The tubing can be shortened as required.
      • The tubing can be raised to an elevation of 4 feet above the floor.
    2. Confirm that the waste drain tubing is pushed into the drain past the sanitary drain trap and that the waste drain tubing is not restricted.
      • If the tubing is not pushed past the trap, noise and foam come from the drain.
      • If the drain trap has a clean out plug, the tubing can catch on it. Ask for assistance from maintenance personnel to insert the drain tubing past the sanitary drain trap.
    3. Have the customer verify daily that waste fluid is flowing smoothly out of the drain line.
  6. Install the protective bracket on the waste fitting.
    1. Remove the lower right rear cover.
    2. Loosen the retaining nut on the fitting to the house air supply line. Slide the protective bracket around the fitting.
      Note:
      • If the lower right rear cover does not have an air supply fitting, order the fitting kit (P/N A21110).
      • If the cover does not have a hole for the house air fitting, order a replacement cover (P/N 119955) and the fitting kit (some early model DxI instruments do not have the house air line fitting option).
    3. Replace the lower right rear cover, and use the lower right screw to fasten the protective bracket to the cover.
      Caution: When installing the cover, confirm that the tube is not pinched or kinked.
    4. Use the cable-tie to secure the waste tubing to the protective bracket and eliminate stress on the fitting.
  7. Prime to engage the liquid and verify that the waste fluid flows smoothly from the waste tubing to the drain.
    • For the DxI 600 instrument: 100 primes on all reagent pipettors.
    • For the DxI 800 instrument: 50 primes on all reagent pipettors.
