Replacing Reagent Storage Interface PCB

This procedure describes how to replace the reagent storage module interface PCB.

  1. Home the reagent gripper and the reagent load tray.
  2. Shut down the DxI instrument.
    1. Select Configure  > PC Admin > System Admin > Shutdown Instrument to exit the software in preparation for powering off the instrument.
    2. Enter the password Access2Support, and then select OK.
    3. At the pop-up You must now turn off the instrument, using the instrument power switch, select OK.
    4. After the instrument software has shut down, turn off the instrument at the power switch.
  3. Open the SPU.
    1. Open the upper left cover of the instrument.
    2. Loosen the locking screw.
      The locking screw prevents the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument. It is not necessary to remove the screw because the SPU plate is slotted.
    3. Remove the 3/16-in hex bolt located near the right end of the SPU cover.
    4. Open the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    5. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin that holds the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    6. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end away from the instrument.
      Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    7. (For China and Canada Only.) On the front facing the edge of the Framus assembly, install the Chinese and French labels adjacent to the existing English label.
      Note: The labels are in the DxI Installation Kit.

  4. Remove the main front cover (Framus).
    1. Remove the two screws holding the right end of the main front cover (Framus) to the instrument.
      The Framus is the instrument skin and frame located mostly to the right of the SPU.
      Note: DxI 800 only: Early instruments also have two screws holding the left end of the Framus to the instrument. If the two screws are there, loosen them. The left end of the Framus is slotted for removal.
    2. DxI 800 only: Loosen the four captive screws mounting the substrate loader module to the Framus. Pull the captive pin on the module left side, and slide the module upward so that the pin locks into the hole provided on the front of the instrument.
    3. DxI 800 only: Remove the triangular-shaped support plate at the center of the Framus (four screws).
      The mounting holes are slotted to simplify removal.
    4. Lift the Framus up slightly, and then remove these connectors from the reagent storage interface PCB:
      • J25 - Reagent Load Door
      • J30 - Bar Code Reader
      • DxI 800 only: J29 - Ribbon Cable
    5. Disconnect the two SPU interlock switch wires from the terminals on the under side of the Framus.
    6. Remove the Framus from the instrument.
      Note: To disengage the cover latch on the reagent load tray and remove the Framus, operate the release lever under the load tray inside the lower right access door.
  5. Replace the reagent storage interface PCB.
    1. Label and remove all the connections from the reagent storage interface PCB.
      Caution: To prevent damage to other components from connections at the wrong position, ensure that all connections are labeled correctly.
    2. Remove the interface PCB from the reagent storage module (four screws).
    3. Install the replacement interface PCB onto the reagent storage module (four screws).
    4. Install the connectors that were labeled and removed.
  6. Install the main front cover (Framus).
    1. Place the Framus on the instrument and mount it to the right end (two screws).
      Note:
      • To disengage the cover latch on the reagent load tray when installing the Framus, operate the release lever located under the reagent load tray inside the lower right access door.
      • DxI 800 only: Early instruments also have two screws that hold the left end of the Framus to the instrument. If the instrument has these screws, install them to mount the Framus on the left end.
    2. DxI 800 only: Move the substrate loader from the hanging position on the front of the instrument, down onto the Framus. Tighten the four captive screws that mount the substrate loader to the Framus.
    3. DxI 800 only: Mount the triangular-shaped support plate at the center of the Framus (four screws).
  7. Close the SPU.
    The SPU is hinged on the left side. The right side is released and can be swung out from the instrument to approximately 45 degrees.
    Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    1. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end toward the instrument.
    2. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin to attach the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    3. Close the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    4. Insert the 3/16-in hex bolt.
    5. Tighten the bolt that holds the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument (locking screw).
    6. Close the upper left cover of the instrument.
      Note: Initialize the instrument after opening and closing the SPU, because a switch is actuated when the SPU is opened.
  8. Initialize the replacement PCB.
    1. Ensure that all covers are closed.
    2. Apply power to the instrument.
    3. Open a desktop sharing between the console PC and the AU.

      For Windows XP System with Software 5.2 and below:

      • Press the [Windows] key, and then select Start  > Programs > Administrative Tools > Remote Desktop Connection to AU PC.

      For Windows 8 System with Software 5.3.1 and above:

      • Press the Windows key, select the Remote Desktop Connection icon or type remote and open the Remote Desktop Connection program.
    4. Select the TestReagentIF.exe shortcut on the AU desktop to open the Test Reagent program.
      Note: If the TestReagentIF.exe shortcut is not shown on the AU desktop, then navigate to Local Disk (C:) or AU_PC > AU to locate and open the application.
    5. In Test Reagent, select Connect.
    6. When connected, select Zero X > Zero Z > Clear Status to reset the reagent storage interface PCB.
    7. Exit the Test Reagent program.
    8. Initialize the system with Remap selected.
    9. Confirm that there are no reagent storage error messages posted to the Event Log.
  9. Run QC, and verify that QC results are within laboratory specifications.
  10. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
