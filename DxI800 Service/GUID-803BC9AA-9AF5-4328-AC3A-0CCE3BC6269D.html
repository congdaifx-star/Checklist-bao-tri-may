J1 - PCB - Interface - Temperature Sensor

  • The Incubator, Wash Wheel, Substrate, Reagent Coolers (3), Reagent Storage Air, and Sample Storage temperature signals run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, TEMPERATURE, DSUB connects J1 to J15 on rear of the Upper Left Card Cage.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J15 to J4 on the DAQC PCB.
Note:
  • The normal temperature sensor output is approximately 0.39 V @ 3 °C, 0.64 V @ 27 °C, 0.80 V @ 37 °C.
  • When the sensor is disconnected the output = 0 V and the Temp display = - 21 °C.
