Ref - Switches

Purpose

This document provides reference information for all non-PCB mounted switches contained in a DxI. The document is intended to provide a source of cross-reference information.

DxI Switches

There are seven different types of switches in the DxI. Some are used in more than one location The total quantity of switches is ten. Most of these switches are not in CARES.
