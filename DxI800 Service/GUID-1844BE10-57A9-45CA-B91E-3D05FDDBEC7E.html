Aligning Bulk Vessel Feeder II

This procedure describes how to align the bulk feeder II module.

Required Tools:
  • Forceps or tweezers

Alignment of the bulk vessel feeder is independent of any other DxI instrument module or component alignment.

The bulk feeder module
  • interacts with the supply carriage.
  • must be able to deliver vessels continually to the supply carriage without errors.
The internal alignments for this module include:
  • elevator scoops to RV pusher (manual alignment).
  • RV pusher to orienter (manual alignment).

The supply carriage is aligned to bulk feeder. Refer to the CA: Aligning Supply Carriage procedure.

Changes to alignment offsets are written to a file:
  • when the alignments are saved when exiting the Alignment screen and selecting Yes at the message Do you want to save alignments?
  • by selecting Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
Warning: Bulk feeder movements are quick, full-motor strength motions. To prevent injury, keep your hands and fingers clear of moving parts when motor movements are being performed.
Caution: The orienter cover is critical for the bulk feeder operation. Always install the orienter cover and screws before general operation.
  1. Align elevator scoops to RV pusher position.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
    2. Remove the two countersunk screws from the top of the orienter cover, and then remove the orienter cover.
      Note: Once the orienter cover is removed, it is much easier to see the scoop to RV pusher orientation from the right side of the module.
    3. Select Bulk Vessel Feeder as the primary device.
    4. Select RV Elevator as the position.
    5. Remove any vessels from the scoops closest to the RV pusher.
    6. Select Home Device.
    7. Verify that the Home Current Position is 30. Confirm that the cutout of the scoop is centered relative to the RV pusher and the orienter.
      Note: Most bulk feeder II elevators are not expected to require alignment adjustment after following the Bulk Feeder II Installation procedure, which sets the Index Offset to 30 in the specific.ini file.
    8. Verify the RV elevator alignment.
      1. Select the Pusher button on the Alignment screen to extend the RV pusher through the scoop opening. Select the Pusher button again to retract the pusher. When the pusher extends or retracts, it should not come in contact with the scoop or cause the scoop to move.
      2. If the pusher contacts the elevator scoop when it extends or retracts, use the left and right directional keys or on-screen buttons to adjust the RV elevator alignment offset until there is no contact.
      3. Select Alignments/Offsets > Preserve Offsets.
    9. Verify the RV elevator alignment on a least three more scoops. For each additional scoop position checked:
      Caution:
      • Confirm that the piston is retracted before moving the elevator.
      • To prevent vessels from falling behind the bulk feeder and into the instrument, remove any vessels from the scoops.
      1. Select Home Device ten times to move the elevator ten scoop positions, and then verify the alignment of the next scoop.
      2. Repeat the Verify the RV elevator alignment step.
      Note: Verifying the alignment on three more scoops, ensures that scoop alignments are being verified at different parts of the scoop drive chain.
    10. Reinstall the orienter cover and screws, unless immediately aligning RV pusher to orienter.
      Caution: The orienter cover is critical for the bulk feeder operation. Always install the orienter cover and screws before general operation.
  2. Align the RV pusher to orienter.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
    2. Remove the two countersunk screws from the top of the orienter cover, and then remove the orienter cover.
      Note: Once the orienter cover is removed, it is much easier to view the scoop-to-RV pusher orientation from the right side of the module.
    3. Select Bulk Vessel Feeder as the primary device.
    4. Select RV Pusher as the position.
    5. Select Home Device.
    6. Remove any vessels from the scoops closest to the RV pusher.
    7. Select Move to Position to extend the RV pusher over the orienter rail.
      Note: In the extended position, the end of the RV pusher is nearly even with the end of the orienter rail.
    8. Use a forceps or tweezers to hold an RV at the top, and without letting the RV go, allow the RV to move vertically down through the gap between the end of the RV pusher and the right side of the orienter opening. Use the directional keys or buttons to change the RV pusher alignment offset until the vertical movement of the RV is restricted to between the end of the RV pusher and the right side of the orienter opening.
      Caution: Do not drop RVs through the opening in the orienter, unless the dispense chute was emptied. Extra RVs in the orienter can cause a jam. If you drop an RV into a full dispense chute, manually actuate the vessel dispenser block back-and-forth once to remove the RV.
      Note: The vertical restriction of the RV through the orienter opening is at the ring of the RV.
    9. Use the directional keys or buttons to change the RV pusher alignment offset by five steps to the left (away from the orienter opening).
    10. Select Alignments / Offsets > Preserve Offsets .
    11. Select Home Device to home the pusher.
    12. To verify the alignment, select Move to Position and ensure that an RV can move vertically through the orienter opening unrestricted.
      Note: The unrestricted movement of the RV through the orienter opening includes the ring of the RV.
    13. Select Home Device to home the pusher.
    14. Reinstall the orienter cover and screws.
      Caution: The orienter cover is critical for the bulk feeder operation. Always install the orienter cover and screws before general operation.
  3. Verify the proper operation of the bulk vessel feeder.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
    2. During the sample route exerciser routine, ensure that:
      • Vessels are moved out of the scoops and onto the orienter rails correctly.
      • Vessels are oriented correctly when dispensed into the RV supply carriage.
      • No errors occur.
    3. If the exerciser routine does not complete successfully or meet the requirements for the exerciser routine, then repeat the alignment procedure and the verification procedure.
    4. Remove any cover interlock switch bypass devices and close the upper left and right front instrument covers.
