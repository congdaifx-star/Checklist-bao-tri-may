Determining Substrate Bottle Change During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to determine when the substrate bottle changed.

Knowing if QSB flags correlate to bottle changes can help identify a failing bottle selector valve or broken substrate straw.

  1. Perform a Diagnostic Copy to Disk.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Copy to Disk.
    2. Enter an appropriate Filename and StartDate.
    3. Insert a floppy disk into the floppy drive.
    4. Select OK.
  2. Open the csv file on a laptop using Microsoft Excel.
    1. Use My Computer or Windows Explorer to access the floppy drive on the laptop.
    2. Right click on the file.
    3. Select Open With > Microsoft Office Excel.
  3. View the column for Substrate Serial Number (far right side of the file) and look for a change in the substrate serial number.
    The change in serial number indicates the bottle change occurred.
    1. Sort the csv file by the Completion Time column.
    2. Locate the change in substrate serial number.
    3. Count 30 cells down from the serial number and highlight this row.
    4. Look for the Test Flag column, and then look for QSB flags occurring near this row.
    5. Check several substrate bottle changes.
      • If the dispense errors or QSB flags are correlating to substrate bottle changes, then troubleshoot the bottle select valve and the tubing connections to the valve.
      • If the dispense errors or QSB flags occur prior to the bottle change and are resolved with the new bottle, then inspect the substrate draw straws for a broken straw.
      Note:
      • The first 30 reps after the serial number change are from the previous bottle. Approximately 30 dispenses must occur before the new substrate is pulled through the entire substrate system.
      • One substrate prime dispenses a similar volume to four normal substrate dispenses. These primes are not included in the Copy to Disk file.
