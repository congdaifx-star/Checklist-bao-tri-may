Carryover Test Troubleshooting

Interpreting Failed Test Results

Use this table to focus repair efforts. Repeat Carryover Test to verify repairs.

If... Then carryover is indicated on... Possible causes:
Three reps of all failed cups have similar RLUs Sample probe Sample probe or wash tower:
  • x-, y-, and z-alignments at wash tower. Check for excessive play in drive belts. Confirm probe is perpendicular at top and bottom of wash nozzle (check for bent probe).
  • Damaged, defective, or non-cleaned new sample probe.
  • Flat peri-pump tubing for wash tower spray.
  • Clogged wash tower nozzle or tubing.
  • Wash tower vacuum low or blocked.
Note: Perform Special Clean whenever new sample probe is installed. Special Clean removes any greasy residues.
All failures are on one pipettor

AND

Three reps do not have similar RLUs (first rep is high)...

Reagent probe of pipettor that showed failures Pipettor reagent probe or wash tower:
  • Verify sample probe x-, y-, and z-alignments to wash tower. Check for excessive play in drive belts, and verify probe is perpendicular at top and bottom of wash nozzle (check for bent probe).
  • Confirm that HS System Check was not used in place of Field Service System Check.
  • Check for worn or flat wash tower peri-pump tubing.
  • Damaged or defective sample probe.
  • Check for clogged wash tower nozzle or tubing.
  • Check for low or blocked wash tower vacuum.
  • Check pipettor fluidic parts (o-rings, fittings, tubing).

Failures are on multiple pipettors

AND

Some have three reps that do not have similar RLUs (first reps high)...

Combination of two (or more) probes

If all three reps are above cutoff on multiple pipettors, then sample probe is involved.

Indicated probes or wash towers:
  • All items listed above for all indicated probes.
  • Review Confirming Alignments and Cleanliness section.
  • Verify sample was System Check and not HS System Check.

Failures are distributed randomly to various pipettors

AND

RLUs are very high, usually on first reps...

Substrate probe (by splashing)

If the splash is very consistent, then failures may show consistently on all pipettors.

Substrate dispense problems, causing sample contents to splash onto substrate probe:
  • Dispense probe plate to wash wheel alignments.
  • Mixer collet mounting screw interference.
  • Damaged or defective substrate probe.
