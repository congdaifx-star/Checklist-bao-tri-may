J2 - PCB - Interface - Sample Pipettor

  • The RF level sense and Z-axis home signals run through J2.
  • The service loop CABLE, RIBBON (P/N 109293) connects Sample Pipettor Interface PCB J2 to JP1 on the RF Level Sense PCB.
J2 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

GND_MCC0

0V

From J1 pin 1, 11, 12, or 19 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 1.

Ground. TP3.

2

N/C

0V

Not Connected.

3

+5V_LOCAL

5V

From U2 switching regulator to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 3 (Not connected). TP8.

4

GND_MCC0

0V

From J1 pin 1, 11, 12, or 19 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 4.

Ground. TP3.

5

Level Sense

5V or 0V

From RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 5 to optical isolator U1 pin 4, and then to J1 pin 6.

Surface detected: TP2 = 0V and D1 is on.

6

Z - Axis Home

5V or 0V

From RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 6 to J1 pin 5.

Home: TP1 = 0V and D2 is on.

7

RF Level Data from Host (+)

N/A

From J1 pin 13 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 7.

Data to RF Level Sense PCB.

8

RF Level Data from Host (-)

N/A

From J1 pin 14 to RF Level PCB J1 pin 8.

Ground for data signal.

9

RF Level Data to Host (+)

N/A

From RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 9 to J1 pin 15.

Data from RF Level Sense PCB.

10

RF Level Data to Host (-)

N/A

From J1 pin 16 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 10.

Ground for data signal.

11

+5V_MCC0

5V

From J1 pin 2, 10 or 20 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 11. TP7.

12

GND_MCC0

0V

From J1 pin 1, 11, 12, or 19 to RF Level Sense PCB J1 pin 12.

Ground. TP3.
