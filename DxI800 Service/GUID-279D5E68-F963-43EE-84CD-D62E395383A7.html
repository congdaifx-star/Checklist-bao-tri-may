Ref - Consumable Usage

Purpose

This document provides calculated usage quantities for DxI consumables and the standard shipment quantities.

Supply Usage - Calculated Average Quantities

Note:
  • The reagent pack estimates are based on DxI 600 usage of 75,000 tests per year and DxI 800 usage of 120,000 tests per year.
  • If the system is idle, then the sample pipettor primes or washes each time a rack or puck is presented to the system. The prime occurs in preparation to aspirate a sample, even if there is no sample requested for the rack or puck. This prime can increase buffer usage, especially on automation systems where single samples load infrequently.

Supply Usage - One-Step and Two-Step Assays

Note: The amount of wash buffer used to clean the sample probe for tests depends on the amount of sample drawn from the sample tube.
  • If the amount of sample drawn from the sample tube is less than 200 µL, 5 mL of wash buffer is used to wash the interior of the probe.
  • If the amount of sample drawn from the sample tube is greater than 200 µL, 10 mL of wash buffer is used to wash the interior of the probe.

Waste Generated

The maximum waste that can be generated in three hours:
  • DxI 800: 3600 RVs and 73 packs.
  • DxI 600: 1800 RVs and 61 packs.
Note: The calculations for maximum waste assume:
  • Reagent storage starts with 50 packs with one test remaining per pack.
  • All selected tests use three RVs (one SV, one DV, and one RV).
  • Maximum throughput of 400 tests per hour for the DxI 800 and 200 tests per hour for the DxI 600.
