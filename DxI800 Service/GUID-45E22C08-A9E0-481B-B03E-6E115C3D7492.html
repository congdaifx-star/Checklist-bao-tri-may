Setting Bulk Feeder to Safe State

This procedure describes how to bring the bulk feeder system to a known vessel state to resolve most bulk feeder issues and reset the system.

Caution: Confirm that the MCC power is off before performing any of the steps in this procedure.
  1. Clear all jams and remove all loose vessels.
    1. Manually clear any vessels jammed in the top of the bulk feeder.
    2. Remove any dropped or misplaced vessels on the instrument baseplate. Closely examine the:
      • Supply carriage (remove all vessels manually).
      • Supply carriage motion path, especially at the belt.
      • Sample wheel (only remove vessels that are on their side). Manually rotate the wheel if necessary
      • Sample pick-and-place collet (confirm that it can hold a vessel securely).
    3. Confirm that the electrical connections on the bulk feeder interface board are connected before proceeding.
  2. Remove any vessels that are inverted in the dispense chute.
    1. Examine the dispense chute for inverted vessels.
    2. If there are inverted vessels, remove them by sliding the wooden end of a cotton swab into the slot in the dispense chute and lifting the vessels up and out.
  3. Clean the vessels from the upper scoops.
    1. Manually remove all vessels from the top front four bulk feeder scoops to prevent issues with scoops that are moved upward during the initialization process.
    2. Install or check the installation of the orienter cover. Confirm that the cover is installed correctly.
      Note: Early orienter covers could be installed backwards. New bulk feeder covers cannot be installed backwards.
    3. Verify that there are no other loose vessels in the immediate area of the bulk feeder, including any scoop on the upper backside.
  4. Restore DC Power using the toggle switch on Power Distribution PCB Cover.
  5. Allow the instrument to reinitialize.
    Caution: When performing this step, do not select Toss All Samples.
  6. If instrument initialization fails, then perform an instrument shutdown. Select Configure > PC Admin > Instrument Admin > Shut Down, and then reboot the instrument.
  7. Run the instrument and confirm that the bulk feeder is delivering vessels correctly.
