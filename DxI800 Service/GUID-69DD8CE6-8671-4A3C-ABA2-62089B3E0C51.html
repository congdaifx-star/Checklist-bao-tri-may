Obtaining DxI Parts After Hours

Pupose

This document provides instructions for field service personnel to obtain DxI parts after normal working hours, and instructions for STS to assist in obtaining parts after normal working hours.

When to Perform

Perform this procedure when parts are needed after normal working hours and after making these attempts to obtain parts:
  • Contact other local field support personnel
  • Contact the Parts Depot in Memphis, TN

After Hours Definition

After hours for the Chaska site:
  • Weekdays: 5:30 p.m. - 9:00 p.m. CST
  • Weekends: 8:00 am - 5:00 p.m. CST
  • Holidays: 8:00 am - 5:00 p.m. CST
Note:
  • If a request is received after 6:00 PM CST, parts are not guaranteed to ship at night because the parts must be at the Minneapolis airport two hours before the flight.
  • If parts arrive at the airport and no flights are available that night, the parts will be flown out the next AM.
  • After hours parts procurement through Chaska is only available during the times listed.

Options

Two options are available for obtaining DxI parts:
  • Call the After Hours Call Center (ACC) in Florida and inform the ACC person of the need (the procedure is documented in the NAO - D Procedure Database).
  • Call the STS phone and inform the on-call STS person of the need.

Required Information

The ACC/STS Chaska must provide the on-call parts person with specific information, thus the FSE must be prepared to provide:
  • all information in the parts request form (Admin: Parts Request Form).
  • any other special instructions or information.
