J3 - PCB - Data Acquisition (DAQC)

  • Obstruction detection pressure signals from the Pressure Sensor Interface PCB run through J3.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J3 to J17 at the top of the Upper Left Card Cage.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, PRESSURE connects J17 to JP8 on the Pressure Sensor I/F PCB.
Note:
  • All ground pins on the J3 connector are tied together on the Pressure Sensor Interface PCB.
  • The pin number assignments going from J3 on the DAQC PCB to JP8 on the Pressure Interface PCB are interleaved due to the way pins are numbered on a ribbon cable and a D-sub connector cable.
