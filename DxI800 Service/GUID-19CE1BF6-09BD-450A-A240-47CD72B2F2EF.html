PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

Purpose

This document provides these details for the Pick and Place Interface PCB:
  • Drawings.
  • Details (Test Points, LEDs, Connector Overview, Connector Pin-outs).
  • Schematics.
  • Location.

Drawings

Use these drawings to identify connectors and test points on this PCB.

Note: This view is of the connector side of the PCB which normally faces the back of the instrument. TP3, TP6, and TP10 are on this side of the PCB and the remaining test points are on the other side of the PCB.

Details

The Pick and Place Interface PCB connects to these devices:
  • Eject sensors (SV, RV, DV).
  • Air valves for ejectors (SV, RV, DV).
  • Motors (X/Y/Z).
  • Home sensors (X/Y/Z).
  • MCC Backplane / MCC2 PCB.
Note: The Pick and Place Interface PCB is used for all the pick and place assemblies (Incubator, Wash, and Sample).
