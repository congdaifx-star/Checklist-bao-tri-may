J11 - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

  • The DV Eject Air Valve connects to J11.
  • The signal to energize the air valve comes from the MCC2 PCB through J1 pin 14.
Note: The J11 connector is only used on the Sample Pick and Place.
J11 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

+24V_Power

24V

To the DV ejector air valve (+) from J1-9 & 11.

24V power for the air valve.

2

DV Ejector

24V (normal)

0V (active)

To the DV ejector air valve (-).

From J1-14.

When the signal at TP10 goes to 5V, Q3 grounds the low side of the DV ejector air valve (J11-2) to energize the valve.
