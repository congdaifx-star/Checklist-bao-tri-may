Adjusting Incubator Pick-and-Place

This procedure describes how to mechanically adjust the position of the incubator pick-and-place to the wash carousel, incubator cover, waste tube, and vertical home position prior to performing any alignments on this module.

Required Materials:
  • Wash carousel locking pin
  • Pick-and-place auto-align tool
  • Retractable tape measure or ruler
Prerequisites:
  • The wash carousel angular offset alignment must be performed prior to incubator pick-and-place adjustments.
Note: Refer to the AU: Aligning Wash Carousel Angular Offset procedure.
Adjustments for this module include:
  • adjust incubator pick-and-place to incubator cover position
  • adjust incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel position
  • adjust incubator pick-and-place to vertical home position
  • adjust incubator pick-and-place to waste tube position
Warning: The photomultiplier tube (PMT) is a very sensitive photon measuring device. Any background or extraneous light coming into contact with a powered PMT can saturate the PMT and reduce the gain. This reduces the performance of the PMT, and it can take a long time for the PMT to recover or it might never recover fully. A magnetic field close to a powered PMT also reduces the performance of the PMT. The magnetic field causes the electrons to follow a curved path instead of moving in a straight line. When you are performing service in the area of the luminometer, observe the following rules.
  • Never shine a flashlight around the luminometer while the PMT is powered on.
  • Never bring a strong magnetic field close to the luminometer while the luminometer is powered on.
  • Before removing the covers on the analytical module, turn off the power to the PMT.
  • Cap the PMT whenever it is removed from the analytical module.
Note: Normally the incubator pick-and-place is removed by removing only the gantry and not the towers, thus it is rare that mechanical adjustment will be required.
  1. Remove the incubator cover.
  2. Select Main Menu > Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
  3. Adjust incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel position and incubator positions.

    If you determine that the adjustment is correct for the incubator pick-and-place, you may not have to complete this entire step.

    1. Select Wash Carousel as the device and Angular Offset as the position.
    2. Select Home Device > Move to Position, and insert the wash carousel locking pin.
    3. Select Incubator P&P as the device, and then select Motors > Disable Both Motors.
    4. Install the pick-and-place auto-align tool onto the incubator pick-and-place collet.
    5. Manually move the carriage with the tool towards the right end of its travel, then downward using the index wheel on the end of the upper gantry rod.
    6. Determine if the adjustment to the wash carousel is correct (the tool is centered).
      • If the tool IS centered in the wash-out position of the wash carousel, the adjustment is correct.
        1. Remove the wash carousel locking pin and replace the incubator cover.
        2. Manually move the carriage with the tool to the right end of its travel, then downward using the index wheel on the end of the upper gantry rod.
        3. Determine if the adjustment to the incubator cover is correct.
          • If the tool is centered in the incubator cover slot, the adjustment of the incubator pick-and-place to incubator cover is correct and this adjustment is complete.
          • If the tool is not centered in the incubator cover slot, the adjustment is incorrect.

            Adjust the incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel and incubator positions.

      • If the tool is NOT centered in the wash out position of the wash carousel, the adjustment is incorrect.

        Adjust the incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel and incubator positions.

    7. To adjust the incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel and incubator positions:
      1. Slightly loosen the four screws at each of the two mounting towers that secure the incubator pick-and-place to the base plate. The towers should be moveable, with little or no slop.
      2. Move the incubator pick-and-place assembly forward and rearward until the alignment tool is centered in the wash out position of the wash carousel. The pick-and-place auto-align tool needs to move cleanly in the vertical travel into the wash carousel hole.
      3. Adjust the incubator pick-and-place assembly so that the distances from the back edge of the baseplate to the back edge of each tower are within 1/8 in of each other. Maintain the incubator pick-and-place to wash carousel alignment while adjusting the incubator pick-and-place assembly.
      4. Tighten the screws at each of the mounting towers.
      5. To verify this adjustment, repeat the adjustment from the beginning.
  4. Adjust incubator pick-and-place to waste tube position.

    If you determine that the adjustment is correct for the incubator pick-and-place, you may not have to complete this entire step.

    1. Move the incubator pick-and-place head until the alignment tool is over the waste tube.
      Note: The waste tube in the DxI 600 instrument is located between reagent carriages 2 and 3. The waste tube in the DxI 800 instrument is located between reagent carriages 3 and 4.
    2. Determine if the adjustment is correct.
      • If the tool is centered in the waste tube, the adjustment is correct and this step is complete.
      • If the tool is not centered in the waste tube, continue with the adjustment.
    3. Loosen the adjustment screw that secures the waste tube to the instrument.
    4. Move the waste tube forward and rearward until the adjustment tool is centered in the waste tube.
    5. Tighten the adjustment screw.
    6. To verify this adjustment, go back to the beginning of this step and determine if the adjustment is correct.
  5. Complete the adjustments.
    1. Remove the alignment tool from the incubator P&P and the wash carousel.
    2. Select Incubator P&P as the device, and then select Motors > Enable All.
  6. Adjust pick-and-place to z-home position.
    1. Select the pick-and-place type being serviced as the device option, and then select Motors > Disable Both Motors.
    2. Move the pick-and-place carriage assembly to the uppermost position.
    3. Select the appropriate pick-and-place as the device option, and then select Motors Enable All.
    4. Loosen the index wheel attachment screw at the end of the upper gantry rod.
    5. Rotate the index wheel to a position where the square hole in the wheel is centered in the sensor.
    6. Tighten the index wheel attachment screw.
  7. If any adjustments were made, perform the incubator pick-and-place alignments.
    Note: Refer to the P&P: Aligning Pick-and-Place Module procedure.
