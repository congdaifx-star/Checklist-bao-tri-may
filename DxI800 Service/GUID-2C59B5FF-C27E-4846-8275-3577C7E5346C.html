Installing Silicone Sealant During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to install silicone sealant when troubleshooting the analytical module.

Install silicone sealant.
Note:
  • If a spill occurs in the analytical module, wash buffer can leak through a hole and onto the wash carousel motor, corroding the encoder.
  • The hole is in the bottom of the analytical module and fits a screw that attaches the wash arm to the analytical module. The hole is located directly above the wash carousel motor.
  • In DxI 800 systems above serial number 600750 and DxI 600 systems, manufacturing installs a longer screw for the wash arm to prevent this issue.
  • In older instruments, the screw is too short to enclose the hole and prevent the wash buffer from migrating through to the motor.
  • If the wash carousel must be removed, fill the hole with GE silicone sealant 162 (PN 7310A).
  1. Place the GE silicone sealant 162 (PN 7310A) in the half-screw indentation where the wash arm attaches to the analytical module. Use a lint-free swab with enough silicone sealant to fill the half-screw indentation only. Do not use excessive silicone sealant.
  2. Wait 30 minutes for the silicone sealant to set, and then reassemble the module.
  3. Manually rotate the wash wheel in the analytical module and verify that movement is smooth.
