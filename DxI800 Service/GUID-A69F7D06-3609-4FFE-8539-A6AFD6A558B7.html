Replacing Peristaltic Pump Head

This procedure describes how to replace the pump head on the particle wash aspirate peristaltic pump.

Perform this procedure when pump head replacement is required because of:
  • a cartridge failure. (Cartridges are not available separately. Gear heads, with numbered cartridges, must be ordered and installed.)
  • pump head failure.

These critical warnings apply to particle wash aspirate pump cartridges in all DxI instruments.

Warning: The position of the cassettes is critical for correct pump operation in the old-style particle wash aspirate pumps. The positions are numbered 1 to 3 beginning at the motor end. Maintain this orientation. Number existing cartridges in fielded instruments using a permanent marker.
Warning: Cassettes are not identical. The dimensions of each cassette can vary slightly from one cartridge to another. Once a cassette is installed in a pump and is functioning correctly, it is matched to the pump.
  • Do not move cassettes to a different position within the pump.
  • Do not move cassettes to different pump.
  • Do not replace cassettes with another cassette of the same style.

Replace cassettes by installing a replacement pump head.

Warning: Do not place a redesigned lever guard from a pump head with factory-numbered cassettes, on a peristaltic pump with an original lever guard. The width of the slot is narrower on the redesigned lever guard. The narrower slot could allow a cassette to 'click' one or more positions tighter or looser, which could cause tubing damage or loss of pumping action.
Caution: Use caution when replacing tubing. Do not twist the tubing in the pump once it is replaced.
Caution: If a pump has no issues related to the cartridges, then leave the cartridges where they are and number the cartridges using the above numbering scheme.
  1. Remove the peristaltic pump.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Removing Particle Wash Aspirate Peristaltic Pump procedure.
  2. Remove the pump head from the peristaltic pump.
    1. Loosen the two screws on the lever guard slightly, and then remove the lever guard.
    2. Hold the motor in your right hand and push up on tab A with your right thumb.
    3. While pushing tab A, push down on tab B by the optical sensor leads.
    4. With both tabs depressed, rotate the pump head counterclockwise 1/8 turn.
    5. Lift the pump head and remove it from the motor mounting plate.
  3. Install the pump head onto the peristaltic pump.
    Caution: When installing a new pump head on the aspirate probe peristaltic pump, do not remove the cartridges from the new pump head before assembly. The position of the cartridges is used to aid orientation of the head during mounting.
    1. Position the shaft on the motor so that the tab on the shaft aligns with a line from the left front screw to the right rear screw. Position the motor assembly shaft as shown.
    2. Position the slot on the pump head shaft so that the slot points to the location shown in the drawing.
    3. Position the pump head on the motor as shown.
    4. Once the two shafts engage, the pump head drops downward. Rotate the pump head 1/8 turn clockwise until the tabs click into place, indicating the pump head is locked into position.
  4. Perform quality checks.
    1. Confirm the cartridge locking levers are above the mounting screws on the lever guard, when the pump head is locked into the assembled position.
    2. Confirm the location of the orange label on the top of the replacement pump head. If the lever guard cannot be installed over the levers, the pump head has been installed incorrectly. If installed incorrectly, disassemble the pump head and repeat the assembly process.
    3. Verify that there are numbers on the cartridges and that they are still legible. If the numbers are not legible, then renumber them using a permanent marker.
    4. Press the orange MOD sticker, to verify it is applied to the pump head fully.
    5. Reattach the lever guard.
  5. Install the pump.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Installing Particle Wash Aspirate Peristaltic Pump procedure.
