Conn. Overview - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Pressure Sensor

  • These are the connectors present on the Pressure Sensor PCB.
  • All the pressure sensors connected to this PCB are SENSOR, PRESSURE, FLUID.

#

Connection Notes

Function / Signals

JP1

The Sample Pump pressure sensor connects to JP1.

Pressure Sensor 1. TP6.

Sample Pump pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP2

The Precision Pump 1 pressure sensor connects to JP2.

Pressure Sensor 2. TP7.

Precision Pump 1 pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP3

The Precision Pump 2 pressure sensor connects to JP3.

Pressure Sensor 3. TP8.

Precision Pump 2 pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP4

The Precision Pump 3 pressure sensor connects to JP4.

Pressure Sensor 4. TP9.

Precision Pump 3 pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP5

The Precision Pump 4 pressure sensor connects to JP5.

Pressure Sensor 5. TP10.

Precision Pump 4 pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP6

The Aspirate Probe 1 Wash pressure sensor connects to JP6.

Pressure Sensor 6. TP11.

Aspirate Probe Wash pressure sensor output (-0.2V in Ready state).

JP7

The Substrate Bubble Detector connects to JP7.

Substrate Bubble Detector PCB output.

When the Substrate bubble detector has:

  • Air in the tube (LED at detector is On): TP12 = 5 V (4.5.to 5V)
  • Fluid in the tube (LED at detector is Off): TP12 = 0 V (<100mV)

JP8

CABLE, INTERFACE, PRESSURE connects JP8 to J17 at the top of the Upper Left Card Cage.

CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J17 to J3 on the DAQC PCB.

The signals for the Pipettor Pressure Sensors, Substrate Bubble Detector, and Upper Cabinet air temperature to the DAQC PCB run through JP8.

The + 12V, -12V power to this PCB, and the substrate bubble detector also run through JP8.

JP9

The THERMISTOR, CASE TEMPERATURE, ASSY connects to JP9.

The Upper Case Temperature signal is sent to DACQ PCB J3-23 and U21-11.

The thermistor output is approximately 0.64V at 27C.

when the thermistor is disconnected the output = 0V and Temp display = - 21C.
