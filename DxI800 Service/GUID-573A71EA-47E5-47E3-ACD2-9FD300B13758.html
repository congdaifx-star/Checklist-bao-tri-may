Adjusting Analytical Module

This procedure describes how to adjust the analytical module mechanically before aligning the analytical module probe plate.

Required Materials:
  • Wash carousel locking pin (1)
  • Dispense probe plate adjustment tool (2)
  • Aspirate probe plate alignment tool (3)
Pre-requisites:
  • Perform the wash carousel angular offset alignment before the probe plate adjustments in this procedure.
    Note: Refer to the AU: Aligning Wash Carousel Angular Offset procedure.
The mechanical adjustments for this module include:
  • Adjust dispense probe plate to wash carousel
  • Adjust aspirate probe plate to wash carousel
Warning: The photomultiplier tube (PMT) is a very sensitive photon measuring device. Any background or extraneous light coming into contact with a powered PMT can saturate the PMT and reduce the gain. This reduces the performance of the PMT, and it can take a long time for the PMT to recover or it might never recover fully. A magnetic field close to a powered PMT also reduces the performance of the PMT. The magnetic field causes the electrons to follow a curved path instead of moving in a straight line. When you are performing service in the area of the luminometer, observe the following rules.
  • Never shine a flashlight around the luminometer while the PMT is powered on.
  • Never bring a strong magnetic field close to the luminometer while the luminometer is powered on.
  • Before removing the covers on the analytical module, turn off the power to the PMT.
  • Cap the PMT whenever it is removed from the analytical module.
Warning:
  • Devices are powered on during these adjustment procedures. To prevent damage to the probes, do not move the wash carousel with probe plates down or force probes into alignment tools.
  • Dispense plate adjustments must be completed or verified before aspirate plate adjustments can be done.
  • Any change in dispense plate adjustments requires verification of aspirate plate adjustments.
  1. Prepare the analytical module for adjustment.
    1. Remove power from the luminometer.
      Caution: Completion of this step is very important to prevent damage to the luminometer.
      Note: Refer to the AU: Removing Power from Luminometer procedure.
    2. If using the older dispense probe plate adjustment tools, remove the substrate probe and place it in a location where it cannot be contaminated or damaged.
      Note: The newer tools are hollow, to allow the substrate probe to pass through.
    3. Remove the analytical module cover.
    4. Verify that the two screws securing the wash assembly slide are installed and tightened. If the attachment screws are not tightened, slide the wash assembly toward the analytical module until it reaches the stops, and then tighten the screws.
    5. Remove all three aspirate probes from the aspirate probe plate and place them in a location where they cannot be damaged.
      Do not remove the aspirate probe connections.
      Note: The aspirate probes are removed to prevent any binding issues when performing the dispense probe plate adjustment.
    6. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
    7. Select Wash Carousel as the device and Angular Offset as the position, and then select Move to Position.
      The instrument moves the wash carousel to the current angular offset position.
  2. Install the adjustment tools.
    1. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device and Both Plates Up as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
      The instrument moves the probe plates to the up position.
    2. Insert the wash carousel locking tool into the alignment hole near them luminometer.
      Note: If the alignment tool does not fall cleanly into the hole, then perform the wash carousel angular offset alignment.
    3. Install the two dispense probe plate adjustment tools into the wash carousel. Place one tool under the mixer for dispense probe 2 and one tool under the mixer for the substrate probe.
    4. Install the three aspirate probe plate adjustment tools into the wash carousel, one under each aspirate position.
  3. Adjust dispense probe plate to wash carousel position.
    1. Select Motors > Disable Z Motor, to disable the aspirate probe plate.
    2. Gently press down on the center of the aspirate probe plate near the drive shaft, to lower the plate approximately 1 inch (2.5 cm).
      Do not push the aspirate probes into the adjustment tools. The probes should be above the tools.
    3. Select Dispense Probe Plate as the device. At the Do you want to enable the motor of the primary device? message, select No.
      Do not enable the aspirate z-motor.
    4. SelectMotors > Disable Z Motor to disable the dispense probe plate.
    5. Use a 3/32-inch hex driver to rotate the screw on top of the dispense plate and raise and lower the dispense plate in and out of the adjustment tools.
    6. If the mixer housings are not centered in the adjustment tools and the tools lift when the dispense plate is raised, loosen the three screws securing the dispense probe plate, adjust the plate as needed, and tighten the screws.
      To assist with a difficult alignment consider these techniques:
      • Clean the exterior of the spinner gripper and mixer with alcohol and allow to dry.
      • Use three dispense probe plate adjustment tools if available. If only two tools are available, then consider moving one tool to the D3 position to confirm or assist with adjustments.
      • When securing the dispense plate screws, lightly snug up the button-head screw first then the other two hex-screws. Tighten the three screws in the same order.
      • Slightly loosen the dispense plate screws to allow plate movement without tilting. Lower the plate into the adjustment tools. Secure the dispense plate screws.
    7. Select Motors  > Enable Z Motor to enable the dispense probe plate.
    8. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device.
    9. Select Motors > Enable Z Motor to enable the aspirate probe plate.
    10. Select Both Plates Up as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
      The probe plates will move to the up position to allow more clearance for the next step.
    11. Remove the two dispense probe plate adjustment tools.
    12. Select Home to home the probe plates.
  4. Adjust aspirate probe plate to wash carousel position.
    Caution: Adjust the aspirate probe plate after adjustments to the dispense probe plates have been completed.
    1. Install the three aspirate probes in the aspirate probe plate.
    2. Select Motors  > Disable Z Motor, to disable the aspirate probe plate.
    3. Use a 3/32-inch hex driver to rotate the screw on top of the aspirate plate and raise and lower the dispense plate in and out of the adjustment tools.
    4. Observing the three aspirate probe plate adjustment tools and the aspirate probes as they enter the tools, continue to lower the probes to the bottom of the tools and raise the probe plate.
    5. If the aspirate probes are not centered in the adjustment tools and the tools lift when the aspirate probes are raised, then loosen the three screws securing the aspirate probe plate, lower the plate so that all three probes are to the bottom of the tools, and tighten the screws.
      To assist with a difficult alignment, consider the following techniques:
      • Clean the exterior of the aspirate probe with an alcohol pad and allow the probe to dry.
      • Remove the aspirate probes from the aspirate arm, and confirm that the aspirate probe slides cleanly into the aspirate probe plate alignment tool. This technique can help eliminate issues with bent probes.
      • When securing the aspirate plate screws, lightly snug-up the center screw first, then the two other screws. Tighten all three screws in the same order.
      • Slightly loosen the aspirate plate screws to allow plate movement without tilting. Lower the plate into the aspirate probe plate adjustment tools. Secure the dispense plate screws.
    6. To verify that the probe is not bent, raise the aspirate plate and rotate each probe while observing the probe tip relative to the center of the tool. Replace the probes as needed.
  5. Remove the adjustment tools.
    1. Select Motors  > Enable Z Motor, to enable the aspirate probe plate.
    2. Select Both Plates Up as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
      The instrument moves the probe plates to the up position.
    3. Select Wash Carousel as the device, and then select Motors  > Disable X/Y Motor, to disable the wash carousel.
    4. Remove the wash carousel locking tool.
    5. Remove the three aspirate probe plate adjustment tools from the carousel.
      Note: Manually rotate the carousel to access the tools.
    6. Select Motors  > Enable X/Y Motor, to enable the wash carousel.
    7. Select Home, to home the carousel.
    8. Select Angular Offset as the position, and then select Move to Position, to move to the current angular offset position.
  6. Verify the aspirate probe deflection.
    1. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device, and then select Home to home the probe plates.
    2. Select RV Bottom as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
    3. Gently lift and release each aspirate probe, verifying that the probes move smoothly and snap into position when released.
      • If the probes do not move smoothly or do not snap into position when released, place the three aspirate probe plate adjustment tools into the wash carousel and repeat the aspirate probe plate to wash carousel adjustment.
      • If only aspirate probe 1 does not snap into place, replace the duck-bill valve, and retest.
    4. Select Dispense Probe Plate as the device and RV Engaged as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
    5. Gently lift and release each aspirate probe, verifying that the probes move smoothly and snap into position when released.
      • If the probes do not move smoothly or do not snap into position when released, place the three aspirate probe plate adjustment tools into the wash carousel and repeat the aspirate probe plate to wash carousel adjustment.
      • If only aspirate probe 1 does not snap into place, replace the duck-bill valve, and retest.
    6. Select Home to home the probe plates.
  7. Complete the adjustment process.
    1. Verify that all adjustment tools and RVs are removed from the wash carousel.
    2. If the substrate probe was removed, then install the probe.
    3. Perform all analytical module alignments.
      Note: Refer to the AU: Aligning the Analytical Module procedure.
  8. Verify probe plate adjustments.
    1. From the Device Diagnostics screen, select Exerciser.
    2. Run 40 replicates of either Reaction Route with Mixer enabled.
    3. Select Cycle Vessels.
    4. Verify that:
      • The vessels do not slip down into the collets.
      • The vessels do not make a squealing noise.
      • The piston height of all three mixer-spinner-gripper pistons relative to the eject plate remains constant and does not rub against the bottom of the eject plate.
    Note: If a problem is encountered, contact Technical Support for assistance.
  9. Install the analytical module cover.
  10. Restore the power to the luminometer after servicing the analytical module.
    1. Power off the luminometer high-voltage power supply using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    2. If the analytical module covers have been removed, replace the covers.
    3. Reconnect the lower cable at the luminometer high voltage power supply.
      Note: The connector is located on the lower right side of the luminometer for most instruments. Some older DxI 800 instruments may have the cable located at the lower left side of the luminometer.
    4. Apply power using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    5. Initialize the instrument.
    6. Ensure that all temperatures are stabilized and within specification.
  11. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
