Sample Pipettor Level Sense Test Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting

If Then
  • Wet Failure Count equals 0

and

  • Wet Z-step Range is less than 40

and

  • Wet %CV is less than 1.0%
Wet Test passes.
  • Wet Failure Count is greater than 0

or

  • Wet Z-step Range is greater than or equal to 40

or

  • Wet %CV is greater than or equal to 1.0%
Wet Test fails. Check:
  • Z-step range on the report to determine the height at which the failure occurred.
  • Alignment of sample pipettor to sample cup.
  • Alignment of sample pipettor to wash tower.
  • Sample pipettor supply tubing for kinks or cracks.
  • Sample probe for damage or defects.
  • Sample pump.
  • Sample pump seals.
  • Sample pump valves.
  • RF level sense PCB.
  • Vacuum to make sure that fluid is drawn off the probe after exiting the wash tower.
  • Sample cup and rack combination.
  • Rack configuration in the user interface.
Dry Test failure count equals 0 Dry Test passes.
Dry Test failure count is greater than 0 Dry Test fails. Check:
  • Z-step range on the report to determine the height at which the failure occurred.
  • Alignment of sample pipettor to sample cup.
  • Alignment of sample pipettor to wash tower.
  • Sample pump.
  • Sample pump seals.
  • Sample valve.
  • Sample pipettor supply tubing for kinks or cracks.
  • Sample probe for damage or defects.
  • Vacuum at the wash tower.
    Note: Refer to the Pneumatics: Troubleshoot Vacuum Failure procedure.
  • Electrical noise level.
  • RF level sense PCB.
  • Sample pipettor ground cable terminals to make sure that the copper surface is against the metal. Refer to Mod 6385 - Sample Pipettor - Ground Cable - Inspect/Modify .
  • Sample cup and rack combination.
  • Rack configuration in the user interface.
