Automated Carryover Test Troubleshooting

Sample Aspiration During the Automated Carryover Test

Rack 1 Rack 2 Rack 3
Position 1
  • 3 reps wash buffer for baseline and 2 reps of AP10 for system delay (1 SV)
  • 12 reps wash buffer for reagent pipettor 1 (1 SV)
  • 12 reps wash buffer for reagent pipettor 2 (1 SV)
  • 12 reps wash buffer for reagent pipettor 3 (1 SV)
  • 12 reps wash buffer for reagent pipettor 4 (1 SV)
  • 4 reps system check for reagent pipettor 1 and 1 rep of DilTest (1 SV)
  • 4 reps system check for reagent pipettor 3 and 1 rep of DilTest (1 SV)
Position 2
  • Empty
  • 3 reps wash buffer for sample pipettor
  • 3 reps wash buffer for sample pipettor
Position 3
  • Empty
  • 4 reps system check for reagent pipettor 2 and 1 rep of DilTest (1 SV)
  • 4 reps system check for reagent pipettor 4 and 1 rep of DilTest (1 SV)
Position 4
  • Empty
  • 3 reps wash buffer for sample pipettor
  • 3 reps wash buffer for sample pipettor

Sample Processing During the Automated Carryover Test

Steps Sample Pipettor Aspiration Sample Processing
1

Rack 1, Position 1: (1 SV of wash buffer is aspirated for baseline and AP 10)

  • Randomly selected reagent pipettor aliquots 4 RVs from wash buffer SV (1 RV for AP10 and 3 RVs for baseline).
AP10:
  • One rep of AP10 runs through system.
  • A 3-minute delay begins after AP10 rep starts.
  • Delay guarantees that any sample running on system finishes before carryover test starts.
  • Result not used in any calculations.
Baseline:
  • Baseline RVs transfer to wash wheel.
  • Luminometer reads baseline RVs.
2 Rack 1, Position 1: (4 SVs of wash buffer are aspirated, 1 SV for each reagent pipettor)
  • Cold sample SVs held in sample wheel until all sample pipettor wash buffer reps are aspirated and moved to wash wheel (refer to Step 6).
  • SVs have not been exposed to hot sample.
3 Rack 2, Position 1: (1 SV of system check is aspirated for reagent pipettor 1)
  • Hot sample SV held in sample wheel until all sample pipettor wash buffer reps are aspirated and moved to wash wheel (refer to Step 6).
4 Rack 2, Position 2: (1 SV of wash buffer is aspirated for sample pipettor)
  • Reagent pipettor aliquots 3 RVs from wash buffer SV.
  • RVs transferred to wash wheel.
  • Luminometer reads the RVs.
Note: Reps used to determine presence of sample probe carryover.
5 Steps 3 and Step 4 repeated for remaining racks and positions following same pattern of hot and cold samples.
Note: System Check SVs reserved for remaining reagent pipettors in sequential order.
6
  1. All reagent pipettors simultaneously:
    • Aliquot RV from System Check SV. RV tossed as possible and not processed.
    • Aliquot 3 RVs from wash buffer SV. RVs transferred to wash wheel and read by luminometer.
  2. Cycle is repeated 3 more times per pipettor for total of 4 cycles.
Note: Replicates used to determine presence of reagent probe carryover.
7
  • After aspirating all carryover replicates, instrument performs 1 rep of DilTest from each System Check SVs.
  • RVs transferred to wash wheel.
  • Replicates are last four samples of carryover routine.
Note: Replicates used to determine presence of reagent probe carryover.
8
  • A 3-minute delay forced after last DilTest replicate starts.
  • One replicate of AP10 starts after delay.
  • Delay guarantees that no new samples start before carryover test finishes.
  • Result not used in any calculations.

Interpreting Failed Results

Use this table to troubleshoot failed carryover tests. Repeat the carryover test to verify repairs.

If carryover failed with this description... then... Possible Causes and Troubleshooting Actions
QNS Flag One or more samples failed with a QNS flag or level sense error.
  • Review volume of wash buffer in rack 1, position 1.
    Note: instrument pipettes 53 reps plus dead volume out of this cup for total volume of 1,745 µL. To avoid QNS errors, this cup must be filled with 2 mL.
  • Verify rack or cup configuration.
    Note: Refer to Ref - Sample Racks and Containers.
    Note: Do not use reflex racks configured to draw reserve volume (standard or flexible) for automated carryover. To use these reflex racks, change configuration to No Reserve in Rack ID Setup before use.
  • Verify approved Beckman Coulter 2-mL cup is being used. Non-approved cups can have different dimensions and can cause QNS errors.
  • Inspect sample probe for damage.
  • Verify sample probe is not leaking. Check for loose or leaking fittings between sample pump and probe.
  • Check sample pipettor z-alignment.
    Note: Refer to SP: Aligning Sample Pipettor procedure.
  • Perform sample pipettor level sense test and troubleshoot failures.
    Note: Refer to SP: Performing SPU RF Level Sense Test procedure.
  • Clean or replace sample pump. It could be overdrawing fluid and causing QNS error during final reps.
    Note: Refer to Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision) procedure.
System Flag One or more samples failed with a system flag.
  • Review Event Log for reason for flag, address issue, and repeat carryover procedure.
Baseline Baseline failed to meet the specification of <9000.
  • Wash buffer could be contaminated. Use different source of buffer to perform carryover procedure.
  • Substrate could be contaminated. Review system check substrate mean to rule out substrate contamination.
  • Dark counts could be elevated. Perform dark count check.
    Note: Refer to AU: Performing Dark Count Read.
Sample Pipettor Carryover is indicated on the sample pipettor.
  • Verify sample probe x-, y-, and z-alignments to wash tower. Check for excessive play in drive belts, and verify probe is perpendicular at top and bottom of wash nozzle (check for bent probe).
  • Confirm HS System Check was not used instead of Field Service System Check.
  • Check for worn or flat wash tower peri-pump tubing.
  • Check for defective or damaged sample probe.
  • Check for clogged wash tower nozzle or tubing.
  • Check for low or blocked wash tower vacuum.
  • Check pipettor fluidic parts (o-rings, fittings, tubing).
Note: Perform Special Clean whenever new sample probe is installed (to remove residues on probe).
Pipettor 1, 2, 3, 4 or Multiple Pipettors Carryover is indicated on the reagent probe of the pipettor with failures.
  • Verify reagent probe x-, y-, and z-alignments to wash tower. Check for excessive play in drive belts, and verify probe is perpendicular at top and bottom of wash nozzle (check for bent probe).
  • Confirm HS System Check was not used instead of Field Service System Check.
  • Check for damaged or defective probes. Worn or pitted probes are common symptom of probes past their PM cycle.
  • Check for worn or flat wash tower peri-pump tubing.
  • Check for clogged wash tower nozzle or tubing.
  • Check for low or blocked wash tower vacuum.
  • Check pipettor fluidic parts (o-rings, fittings, tubing).
Access System Check Solution not confirmed. The system could not confirm that system check solution was loaded in cups 1 and 3 on racks 2 and 3.
  • Review Test Results screen for Dil-Test samples.
    • If Dil-Test samples fail on only one pipettor, then troubleshoot that pipettor.
    • If Dil-Test samples fail across all four pipettors, then verify that neat system check solution was placed into cups 1 and 3 on racks 2 and 3.
    • If Dil-Test samples fail, verify that 1:501 system check solution was not used.
