Reliability Component Replacement Guidelines

When to Perform

Use these guidelines to determine which parts to replace. For more detailed troubleshooting, refer to Fluidics: Troubleshooting Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision).

Note: Original pumps are on non-upgraded DxI 800 instruments with SN 601418 and lower and DxI 600 instruments with SN 900085 and lower. The new pump reliability components can be distinguished visually by a gray belt, gray dot belt flag, lighter colored PEEK piston/seal/guide, two lower pump o-rings, and the tensioner mounting bracket.
Issue/Upgrade Order These Parts Details
Sample pump failure or upgrade on instrument with original sample pump.
  • A58781 - Sample Pump Kit
These parts are Mod 10043. Document mod completion after installation of parts.
Precision pump failure or upgrade on instrument with original precision pump.
  • A58782 - Urethane Pump Belt and Belt Clip
  • A58783 - Piston/Seal/Guide Set and O-rings
  • Each kit includes the parts to upgrade a single pump. If multiple pumps will be upgraded, then order multiple kits.
  • This kit includes an extra o-ring to replace the upper pump o-ring.
  • If the piston/seal is not available, then replace the belt.
Wash pump failure or upgrade on instrument with original wash pump.
  • A58781 - Sample Pump Kit
  • Each kit includes the parts to upgrade a single pump.
  • The Sample Pump Kit also works for a wash pump upgrade.
  • If multiple pumps will be upgraded, then order multiple kits.
  • If the piston/seal is not available, then replace the belt.
Any pump failure on pump reliability components:
  • instrument with Mod 10043 completed.
  • DxI 800 instrument with SN 601419 and above.
  • DxI 600 instrument with SN 900085 and above.
  • instrument with upgraded pump parts.
  • A58782 - Urethane Pump Belt and Belt Clip
Inspect the belt for damage, and replace the belt if damage is found.
Consider additional root causes for failure besides the pump.
Note: Refer to Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision) procedure.
Upgrading all pumps from original parts. DxI 800 instrument or DxC 880i instrument
  • Order 7 of A58782 - Urethane Pump Belt and Belt Clip
  • Order 7 of A58783 - Piston/Seal/Guide Set and O-rings
  • Order 7 of A52044 - Motor Mount Arm Tensioning
OR
  • Order 1 of A58784 - Instrument Upgrade Kit
  • Order 7 of A58783 - Piston/Seal/Guide Set and O-rings
Upgrading all pumps from original parts. DxI 600 instrument
  • Order 5 of A58782 - Urethane Pump Belt and Belt Clip
  • Order 5 of A58783 - Piston/Seal/Guide Set and O-rings
  • Order 5 of A52044 - Motor Mount Arm Tensioning

Part Pump Used In Benefit Notes
Urethane Gray Belt and Gray Dot Belt Flag Sample

Precision

Wash

Long-term reliability
  • The new urethane gray belt is only compatible with the new gray dot belt flag.
    • Do not use the new urethane gray belt with an original black "no dot" flag.
    • Do not use the original black belt with a new gray dot flag.
  • The new urethane belt cannot be ordered separately from the gray dot flag.
  • This belt is not used in the substrate pump. The substrate pump belt is a different size.
