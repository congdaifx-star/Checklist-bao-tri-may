LEDs - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Substrate

The LEDs on this PCB are visible from either side of the PCB.

LED

Color

Function

D1

Green

Substrate Door 1 Up. Door 1 open: TP2 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

D2

Green

Substrate Door 1 Down. Door 1 closed: TP1 = 0V and D2 LED is On.

D3

Green

Substrate Door 2 Down. Door 2 closed: TP4 = 0V and D3 LED is On.

D4

Green

Substrate Bottle 1 In-Place. Bottle present: TP3 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

D5

Green

Substrate pump home. Home: TP5 = 0V and D5 LED is On.

D6

Green

Substrate Bottle 2 In-Place. Bottle present: TP7 = 5V and D6 LED is On.

D7

Green

Substrate Door 2 up. Door 2 open: TP6 = 0V and D7 LED is On.

D8

Green

Substrate Door 2 Light. Enabled: TP19 = 5V and D8 LED is On.

D9

Green

Substrate Door 1 Light. Enabled: TP20 = 5V and D9 LED is On.

D10

Green

Substrate Dispense Valve. Enabled: TP12 = 5V and D10 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-12 to Q1 grounds J13-2.

D12

Green

Substrate Door 1, Valve 1. Enabled: TP13 = 5V and D12 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-31 to Q2 grounds J14-2.

D14

Green

Substrate Door 1, Valve 2. Enabled: TP14 = 5V and D14 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-33 to Q3 grounds J15-2.

D16

Green

Substrate Select Valve. Enabled. TP15 = 5V and D16 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-13 to Q4 grounds J18-2.

D18

Green

Substrate Door 2, Valve 1. Enabled: TP16 = 5V and D18 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-35 to Q5 grounds J19-2.

D20

Green

Substrate Door 2, Valve 2. Enabled: TP17 = 5V and D20 LED is On. 5V signal from J8-37 to Q6 grounds J11-2.

D22

Green

Substrate Heater PWM. (Pulsed 5V control signal) Enabled: TP18 = 5V and D22 LED is On. 5V signal to U1 turns (Q10 off ) Q7 on grounding J12-2.
