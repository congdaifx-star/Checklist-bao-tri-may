Specs - Sample Storage Module

Component Specifications

Component Description
Drive Belt
  • Spring-loaded automatic tensioner.
Peltier
  • Powered by a 24 V DC signal that is cycled on and off (duty cycle).
  • PWM drivers on power distribution PCB control power to the Peltiers assemblies.
  • Temperature maintained at 4 to 10 °C.
Stepper Motor
  • 24 V stepper pulses from MCC0 PCB.
  • 0.8 degrees per motor step.
  • Half-step mode.
  • Run / Hold Current = 1.5 A / 0.3 A
  • Speed = 4800
  • Capable of moving wheel to a specified vessel location within 0.7 seconds.
Storage Wheel
  • Storage capacity of 288 sample vessels.
  • Capable of moving to a specified vessel location within 0.7 seconds.
  • Four locations marked with dots for service reference.
