Pressure System Troubleshooting

Purpose

This document provides troubleshooting information for pressure system failures.

Failure Causes

Symptom Possible Cause Resolution
The compressor runs at an abnormally high duty cycle (50% or higher when instrument is not running).
Software controls the air pressure:
  • 32.0 psi - Power on compressors.
  • 35.0 psi - Power off compressors.
Leak in the pressure system.

Compressor is leaking internally because of a failed diaphragm.

Repair the leak by tightening fittings and securely attaching tubing to fittings.
Repair the compressor.
Note: Refer to the Pneumatics: Repairing or Cleaning KNF Compressor procedure.
Install the House Air Kit.
Note: Refer to the Pneumatics: Installing House Air Kit procedure.
After connecting to house air, the internal compressors will run only when the house air supply falls below 32 psi. This improves the pressure system reliability.
Compressor noisy Compressor bearing failure Replace the compressor.
Low compressor pressure The pressure does not reach or maintain a minimum of 32 psi with the compressor set to run continuously.

A missing or worn out check valve between the compressor and the air canister causes the compressor to stall until the pressure drops below 20 psi.

Check for leaks, mainly at air fittings that have screw-on connections (bulk feeder ejectors, reagent waste door, eject plate).

Check or replace the in-line check valve between the compressor and the air reservoir.

System fails to initialize

Insufficient air pressure. A pressure of 32 psi is required.

Loss of power to the compressor.

Compressor motor failure.

Check for 24 V DC on the compressor power connector.

Check for air leaks

Confirm that the air-bleed valve is not connected to one of the compressor power connections on the I/F PCB.

System air pressure not reached within time limit error and the system fails to initialize The condensation relief (condensation release-air bleed-moisture bleed) valve is not working.

The system will not initialize unless the Condensation Relief valve function is confirmed by the system air pressure dropping below 32 psi when the valve is cycled during the initialization routine.

Check that the valve is connected to J9 on the lower cabinet interface board and is not connected to one or the compressor power connectors.

Confirm that the valve operates and is not clogged.

The voltage monitored in TestAnalog on channel 58 (Air Pressure) drops at an approximate rate of 1 volt/second when MoistureBleedOn is selected in OutbitTest.

Note: On Windows 8 System with Software 5.3.1 and above, the Test - Analog data channels are not displayed properly on the desktop sharing screen. To open and view the data channels properly, connect the monitor, mouse, and keyboard to the AU side.
Fluid buildup in the air accumulator canister Moisture bleed valve clogged. Check the moisture bleed valve tubing and function.
Compressor cycling when covers opened When opening instrument covers turns off MCC power, 5V_ENCODER BACKUP power from the storage capacitor on the reagent storage module I/F PCB feeds back through the LED panel PCB to 5V_MCC6. The 5-V power is flashed on and off once per second by the process that checks the status of the reagent storage cover closed switch. This feedback can cause the MCC6 LEDs to flash and can also cause the moisture bleed valve and air compressor to cycle on and off. This behavior can be considered normal and goes away when the MCC power is restored. It is more likely to be visible on systems with newer (red LED) MCC PCBs. Installation of a new version LED panel PCB (not released) corrects this issue.

Viewing Pressure, Duty Cycle, and Voltage

Use one of these three methods to view the air pressure and duty cycle.

Note: Before viewing the pressure with any method, confirm that the compressors do not run continuously. This issue must be corrected before taking an air pressure reading.
View the pressure and test the compressed air system using Steps
User Interface
  1. If the compressors are running, allow them to pump until they power off, then check the pressure. If the pumps never power off (maximum duty cycle), there is a major air leak at a fitting or a tubing, or a defective sensor or pump.

    Power Off Specification: 35 to 36 psi, duty cycle no higher than 50%.

    Note: The range of 35 to 36 psi is normal and acceptable because the sensor signal has a short software read delay.
  2. Determine if the pressure meets specification.
    • If the pressure reaches the required level, the system is verified and this test is complete. Proceed to the Verification Section.
    • If the pressure does not reach the required level, proceed to the next step to determine the cause.
  3. Check for air leaks by monitoring the pressure level for one minute and confirm that the compressors do not start during that time.
    • If the off time is one minute or more, the compressed air system is not leaking. This check is complete.
    • If the off time is less than one minute, there is an air leak that must be located and repaired.

    Pressure guideline: The pumps must remain off for one minute or more between on cycles.

  4. Pinch off all pressure lines at a location that is beyond the pressure sensor, then recheck.
    • If the pump remains off more than one minute, the pump or output connection is not leaking. This check is complete.
    • If the pump remains off more than one minute, there is an air leak that must be located and repaired.
  5. Check all fitting tubing connections for leaks. Confirm that:
    • the threads on every fitting are sealed.
    • every fitting and tubing connection is sealed.
    • tubing is not cut or cracked, allowing air to escape.
    Note: The substrate bottle selector valve can be the source of an air leak.
  6. If an issue is located, repair or replace the defective component and retest the pressure level and pump off duration again.
  7. If no issue is located, retest.
Pressure Icon on the Bottom of the Task Bar of the AU
Note:
  • It is best to perform this check when the instrument is at its most active state.
  • The system air consumption volume affects the duty cycle of the compressors.
  • The duty cycle indicates how hard the compressor is working to maintain system pressure.
For Windows XP System with Software 5.2 and below:
  1. Press the [Windows] key, and then select Start  > Programs > Administrative Tools > Remote Desktop Connection to AU PC to start desktop sharing.
  2. Configure the screen to enable viewing the Bottom Task Bar.
  3. Hold the mouse pointer over the pressure control icon (located in the tool caddy in the lower right corner of the screen). Note the system pressure and duty cycle.

    Guidelines: Duty cycle (%) and pressure level (psi)

    • 3% to 50% - Instrument is maintaining pressure, especially when the instrument has been active for a minimum of 10 to 15 minutes. No action required.
    • 50% to 75% - Instrument is having difficulty maintaining adequate pressure. Monitor and determine if it can recover when processing slows down. Troubleshoot and repair.
    • 75% or Higher - Instrument is not maintaining adequate pressure. Troubleshoot and repair.
    Note: KNF compressors normally run at 10% when new (2.5 minutes between on times).

For Windows 8 System with Software 5.3.1 and above:

  1. Press the [Windows] key, select Remote Desktop Connection icon or type remote and open the program to start desktop sharing.
  2. Configure the screen to enable viewing the Bottom Task Bar.
  3. Hold the mouse pointer over the pressure control icon (located in the tool caddy in the lower right corner of the screen). Note the system pressure and duty cycle.

    Guidelines: Duty cycle (%) and pressure level (psi)

    • 3% to 50% - Instrument is maintaining pressure, especially when the instrument has been active for a minimum of 10 to 15 minutes. No action required.
    • 50% to 75% - Instrument is having difficulty maintaining adequate pressure. Monitor and determine if it can recover when processing slows down. Troubleshoot and repair.
    • 75% or Higher - Instrument is not maintaining adequate pressure. Troubleshoot and repair.
    Note: KNF compressors normally run at 10% when new (2.5 minutes between on times).
testanalog.exe
For Windows XP System with Software 5.2 and below:
  1. Press the [Windows] key, and then select Start  > Programs > Administrative Tools > Remote Desktop Connection to AU PC to start desktop sharing.
  2. Navigate to and open the C:\AU\TestAnalog.exe program on the AU.
  3. Navigate to and open the C:\AU\OutbitTest.exe program on the AU.
  4. Power on the compressor by selecting CompressorOn and then toggling the bit in OutbitTest.exe.
  5. Channel 58 should reach 7 volts (42 psi = 7 volts × 6 psi/volt).
    Note: If necessary, adjust the pressure relief valve to allow the pressure indicator to reach 7 volts (42 psi).
  6. Using a watch with a second hand, power off the compressor using OutBitTest.exe. Note the pressure drop over time.

    The pressure should not drop more than 0.5 volts in 1 minute. Pressure drops greater than 0.5 volts indicate a leak.

  7. With a voltage of a minimum of 6 on channel 58, open the condensation relief valve (select MoistureBleedOn and toggle the bit in OutbitTest.exe) while monitoring the voltage.
    Note: The voltage drops at an approximate rate of 1 volt/second.
  8. Close the moisture relief valve (using OutbitTest.exe) and power on the compressor while noting the time.

    Channel 58 should reach 7 volts on channel 58 within 2 minutes.

  9. Close the test applications when finished and relaunch the AUObj.exe.
  10. Exit the AU and restart to get the pressure control started again.

For Windows 8 System with Software 5.3.1 and above:

Note: On Windows 8 System with Software 5.3.1 and above, the Test - Analog data channels are not displayed properly on the desktop sharing screen. To open and view the data channels properly, connect the monitor, mouse, and keyboard to the AU side.
  1. Connect the monitor, mouse, and keyboard to the AU side.
  2. Navigate to and open the C:\AU\TestAnalog.exe program on the AU.
  3. Navigate to and open the C:\AU\OutbitTest.exe program on the AU.
  4. Power on the compressor by selecting CompressorOn and then toggling the bit in OutbitTest.exe.
  5. Channel 58 should reach 7 volts (42 psi = 7 volts × 6 psi/volt).

  7. With a voltage of a minimum of 6 on channel 58, open the condensation relief valve (select MoistureBleedOn and toggle the bit in OutbitTest.exe) while monitoring the voltage.
    Note: The voltage drops at an approximate rate of 1 volt/second.
  8. Close the moisture relief valve (using OutbitTest.exe) and power on the compressor while noting the time.

    Channel 58 should reach 7 volts on channel 58 within 2 minutes.

  9. Close the test applications when finished and relaunch the AUObj.exe.
