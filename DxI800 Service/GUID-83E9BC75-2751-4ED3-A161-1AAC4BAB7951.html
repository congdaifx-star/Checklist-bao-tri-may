J5 - PCB - Interface - Dual Gantry

  • The CTA P&P Z Axis Home sensor connects to J5.
J5 Description Signal / Voltage To/ From / Notes

1

+5V_MCC1

5V

From J1-2.

5V supplied by the MCC1 PCB.

2

GND_MCC1

0V

From J1-1.

Ground. TP1.

3

Z Axis Home

5V or 0V

To J1-18.

CTA P&P is at Z Home: LED D7 = On, TP5 = 5V.

4

+5V_MCC1

Xmit LED Power

5V

From J1-2.

5V is supplied by the MCC1 PCB.

The sensor LED power is 5V supplied through a 221 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected pin 4 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

5

GND_MCC1

0V

From J1-1.

Ground. TP1.
