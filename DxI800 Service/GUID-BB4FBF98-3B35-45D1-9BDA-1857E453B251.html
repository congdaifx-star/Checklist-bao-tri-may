Removing Sample Pick-and-Place

This procedure describes how to remove the sample pick-and-place module.

Perform this procedure when service access to the sample pick-and-place is insufficient.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Remove the incubator pick-and-place.
      Note: Refer to the P&P: Removing Incubator Pick-and-Place procedure.
      Note: Confirm that power has been removed from the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
    2. Remove the connectors from the sample pick-and-place interface PCB.
      • J1 - Ribbon Cable
      • J2 - Sample P&P Motor
    3. Remove the air line from the sample pick-and-place air valve.
      Note: Push in on the locking ring and pull out on the tube to remove it from the sample pick-and-place air valve.
    4. Manually move the pick-and-place carriage to the far right.
    5. Move the z-carriage to a position that is one inch below its full upward position.
  2. Remove the sample pick-and-place gantry.
    1. Loosen the two captive screws holding the gantry to the towers.
    2. Firmly grasp the main bracket assembly with both hands and wiggle it forward off the two locator pins.
      Caution: Use care in handling the module. Avoid grasping the assembly by the guide rods or any other fragile area.
    3. Once the assembly is off the locator pins, lift it upward and 'roll' the top toward the rear of the instrument.
      Caution: Do not force the module. There is sufficient clearance for removing it.
    4. Keep rolling and lifting the module until it is free of the instrument.
    5. Take the removed sample pick-and-place gantry to a clean work area and lay it down with its PCB side upward.
  3. Remove the pick-and-place mounting towers.
    Caution: Do not remove the towers from the base plate (if at all possible). Removing the towers makes it much more difficult to adjust and align the sample pipettor module after it is installed. If replacing a pick-and-place, then remove the new gantry from the towers and install it on the mounting towers currently in place.
    1. To remove the towers, mark the locations of the towers on the instrument base plate using a sharp object or pen.
    2. Remove the Temperature Sensor Interface PCB cover to access the connectors.
    3. Remove these connectors from the Temperature Sensor Interface PCB:
      • J1 - Ribbon Cable
      • J2 - Incubator
      • J3 - Wash Wheel
      • J4 - Substrate Heater
      • J6 - Reagent Cooler 1
      • J7 - Reagent Cooler 2
      • J8 - Reagent Cooler 3
      • J9 - Reagent Air
      • J10 - Sample Cooler
    4. Remove the screws holding each tower to the instrument base plate.
    5. Remove the towers from the instrument.
