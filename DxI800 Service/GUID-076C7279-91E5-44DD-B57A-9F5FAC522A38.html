Ref - Sensors - Optical / Hall /Tx-Rx

Purpose

This document provides reference information for the optical, inductive, and Hall Effect sensors used in a DxI. The document provides cross-reference information for service personnel to determine the sensor that can be used as a temporary substitute for a defective, out-of-stock sensor.

General Information

Three basic types of sensors are in the UniCel DxI:
  • Optical (light beam transmit and receive)
  • Hall Effect or inductive - Detects the presence of a metal object without physical contact.
  • Inductive - An electromechanical device in which the magnetic characteristics of its electric circuit change in response to the motion of an object.

Optical Sensor Body Styles

Although there are a few unique body styles, most of the light beam transmit and receive sensors are one of three body styles.
