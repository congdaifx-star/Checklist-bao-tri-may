Confirming Contamination During Substrate Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to troubleshoot substrate contamination issues that may cause elevated RLUs in the substrate portion of the System Check test.

Caution: Do not adjust the system to compensate for high or low substrate RLUs without confirming that the substrate system is functioning properly.
Substrate RLUs are affected by:
  • Contamination.
  • Dilution immediately after decontamination.
  • Dispense volume (substrate valve, loose fittings, worn pump belt or seals, damaged probe).
  • Luminometer sensitivity adjustment (LED target value).
  • Substrate storage and handling (contaminated, hot, cold, equilibrated, radioactive).
  • Lot-to-lot variability in the substrate (refer to QC release values).
  • Temperature.
  • Stray light ( increases dark counts).
  • D1, D2, and D3 probes not properly dispensing wash buffer (causes high Washed System Check RLUs ).
  • Peri-pump not aspirating (tubing, pump, or aspirate probes) may cause low Washed System Check RLUs and low patient results.
  1. Obtain several vessels of system-dispensed substrate.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Subsystem Diagnostics > Volume Checks.
    2. Select Substrate Probe > Start Test.
    3. Load ten vessels into the highlighted locations on the drawing.
      Note:
      • The positions used are normally under the module cover, which may need to be removed.
      • The two-dot load area will be at the front until you select Start, and then the two-dot load area rotates to the rear.
      • The system will dispense 200 µl of substrate into the loaded vessels and then put the loaded vessels back on the sample storage wheel.
    4. Select OK to begin the test.
    5. Manually remove several of the substrate vessels, and then select Done.
      This will cause the sample pick-and-place to discard the remainder of the substrate vessels and the user interface to exit the Volume Check window.
  2. Measure the RLU output of the system-dispensed substrate and hand-dispensed substrate.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Luminometer > Load/Read Vessel.
    2. Place one of the system-dispensed substrate vessels into the wash carousel at the wash pick-and-place location, and then select OK.
    3. Allow the sample to incubate for six minutes, and then record the RLU value.
      Note: The RLU readings displayed in Luminometer Diagnostics are raw RLUs and will be approximately 1/4 of the result RLUs. The approximate, normal value for substrate RLUs is 1800 to 2200.
    4. Select Done, and remove the substrate vessel from the wash carousel.
    5. Hand-dispense 200 µl of fresh, equilibrated substrate from a new substrate bottle into an RV.
    6. Select Load/Read Vessel, place the hand-dispensed vessel into the wash carousel at the wash pick-and-place location, and select OK.
    7. Allow the sample to incubate for six minutes, and then record the RLU value.
    8. Select Done, and remove the substrate vessel from the wash carousel.
  3. Compare the RLU reading from the system-dispensed substrate to the RLU reading from the hand-dispensed substrate to determine if the substrate system is contaminated.
    If values are Then contamination is Action Reference Document
    more than 200 RLUs apart. confirmed Decontaminate the substrate system. Fluidics: Decontaminating Substrate
    within 200 RLUs not confirmed Perform a Dark Count Read.

    If the Dark Count Read passes, then adjust the Luminometer RLU sensitivity.
