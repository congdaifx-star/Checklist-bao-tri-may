Troubleshooting Carriage Issues

Purpose

This document provides information on troubleshooting issues related to carriage assemblies.

When to Perform

Troubleshoot carriages when there are:
  • Carriage motion errors.
  • Incorrect positioning issues.
  • Unexplainable sample or incubator pick-and-place errors.

Causes and Resolutions

Known causes and resolutions for motion errors and incorrect positioning issues are shown in the table in this section.

Note: Carriage errors can cause:
  • Pick-and-place errors.
  • Reagent pipettor errors.
  • Missing vessels in the supply carriage.
Possible Cause Resolution
Vessel:
  • in the carriage path.
  • jammed between the belt and pulley.
Remove all vessels found anywhere around the carriage.
The supply carriage strikes the bottom of the bulk feeder Readjust the carriage to the bulk vessel feeder.
Note: Refer to the CARR: Adjusting Supply Carriage procedure.
Vessel backed up in the DV waste chute Clear the vessels from the chute. Verify that a vessel in the DV position in the reagent carriage ejects correctly and falls into the DV waste chute when the carriage is moved rearward.
Flag or pin rubbing the carriage home sensor on the underside of the carriage Adjust the home sensor.
Note: Refer to the CARR: Adjusting Home Sensor During Troubleshooting procedure.
Sticky guide rod Clean and lubricate the carriage guide rod.
Note: Refer to Instrument: Lubrication procedure.
Corroded guide rod Remove corrosion or replace the carriage.
Inoperative drive motor Replace the carriage drive motor.
Loose or missing spring pin securing the motor pulley to the drive motor Replace the carriage drive motor.
Defective drive belt (damaged) Replace the drive belt.
