Certifications

This document provides a copy of the DxI 800 and DxI 600 certifications.

Purpose

Certifications available for the DxI systems include:
  • Certificate of Compliance
  • EU Declaration of Conformity
  • Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE)
  • Waste Water Analysis.
