Deionizer Installation - Main Aspects

This is an overview of the main aspects of the deionizer installation:
  • Purpose: The deionizer kit was created as a field service MOD to deal with instruments that appear to have excessive static in the bulk vessel feeder module and the vessels in it.
  • Manufacturing Cut-In: Instrument manufacturing will not cut this MOD into production instruments; however, the bulk feeder interface PCB, which is required for this MOD, will be cut into all manufactured instruments.
  • Location: The deionizer is installed near the ejector cylinders and pushes air downward.
  • Cost: The cost of this MOD is significant, so the benefits should be compared to the cost of the service calls to the account prior to installing it.
  • Alarm Feature: The deionizer contains an alarm LED that indicates a failure of the high-voltage circuitry.
  • Terminology: The blower is actually an ionizer that creates and sends positive and negative ions into the hopper. Static positive and negative charges on the vessels are contacted by the blower air, reducing the charge on them. Because the function of the ionizer is to deionize it is called a deionizer.
  • Maintenance: Emitter point cleaning is necessary.
