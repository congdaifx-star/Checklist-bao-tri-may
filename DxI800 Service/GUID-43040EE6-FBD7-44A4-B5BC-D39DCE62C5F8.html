Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - SPU

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SPU (P/N 116131) connects J1 to J01 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 5V power, bar code reader, and home sensor signals run through J1.

J2

HARNESS, SAMPLE HANDLING, (P/N 116313) connects J2 to JP01 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The harness also connects MCC Backplane:
  • JP02 to Sam Storage I/F PCB J2
  • JP01 to Sam Pip I/F PCB J2 and J3

The 24V power and SPU motor drive signals from the MCC0 PCB run through J2.

J3

The Stat/Routine User Panel connects to J3.

On-load axis home: TP8 = 0V and D8 LED is on.

J4

The LED source for the Rack In Presentation Area sensor connects to J4.

The LED power is 5V supplied through a 68 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected, pin 1 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

J5

The detector for the Rack In Presentation Area sensor connects to J5.

Rack present: TP6 = 0V and D1 LED is on.

J6

The Off-Load Axis Home sensor connects to J6.

Home: TP3 = 0V and D2 LED is on.

J7

The Presentation Axis Home sensor connects to J7.

Home: TP4 = 0V and D3 LED is on.

J8

The Off-Load Queue Full sensor connects to J8.

Rack present: TP5 = 0V and D4 LED is on.

J9

The Rack At Rail End Position sensor connects to J9.

Rack present: TP7 = 0V and D5 LED is on.

J10

The LED source for the Rack Off-Load Tray Full sensor connects to J10.

The LED power is 5V supplied through a 68 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected, pin 1 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

J11

The On-Load Axis motor connects to J11.

An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages. Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J12

The Off-Load Axis motor connects to J12

Stepper motor drive signal/voltages.

J13

The Presentation Axis motor connects to J13.

Stepper motor drive signal/voltages.

J14

The Sample Bar Code reader connects to J14.

Serial data from BCR.

J15

The detector for the Rack In Presentation Area sensor connects to J15.

When a rack is present: TP9 = 0V and D6 LED is on.

J16

The Off-Load Cover Interlock Switch connects to J16.

Switch closed: TP14 = 24V and D16 LED is on.

The switch provides power to relay K2 that enables the off-load axis motor.

J17

The SPU On-Load Cover Interlock Switch connects to J17.

Switch closed: TP13 = 24V and D11 LED is on.

The switch provides power to relay K1 that enables the SPU on-load axis motor, K3 that enables the off-load axis motor, and K4 that enables the presentation tray motor.

J18

The Sample Pip Z Interlock Relay (not used) connects to J18.

When the SPU on-load switch is closed (TP13 = 24V and D11 LED is on), 24V power is supplied to J18 pin 1 to energize relay (not used) that enables the sample pipettor axis Z motor.
