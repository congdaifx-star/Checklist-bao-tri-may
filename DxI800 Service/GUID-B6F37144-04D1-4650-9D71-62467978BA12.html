Removing Sample Pipettor

This procedure describes how to remove the sample pipettor module for repair or replacement.

  1. Open the covers.
    1. Remove the gray center rear cover and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    2. Remove the upper rear cover and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    3. Open the upper left front cover.
  2. Drain the tubing.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices  > Peristaltic and Vacuum Pumps.
    2. Enable the vacuum pump for the pipettor being serviced.
    3. Verify that the pipettor is directly over the wash tower.
    4. Remove the fitting from the pipettor precision pump and allow the wash buffer to drain into the wash tower.
      Caution: When draining wash buffer from the tubing, do not let fluid leak onto the instrument or any of the instrument components. When disconnecting the tubing, hold a dry towel at the in-line fitting.
    5. After the tubing is drained, disable the vacuum pump and exit the Digital Devices screen.
  3. Remove the cover interlock bypass.
  4. Power off the switch for the power distribution PCB labeled all DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
  5. Remove the sample pipettor.
    1. From the front of the instrument, disconnect the connectors from the sample pipettor interface PCB.
      • J1-Ribbon Cable
      • J3-Sample Pipettor Motor
    2. Slide the pipettor carriage fully forward on the gantry, so that the screws securing the carriage to the baseplate can be accessed.
    3. Loosen the two captive screws that hold the gantry to the tower.
    4. Firmly grasp the gantry with both hands and remove the gantry from the towers.
      Caution: Use care in handling the module. Avoid grasping the assembly by the guide rods or any other fragile component.
      Two locating pins hold the gantry in place when the captive screws are removed.
    5. Once the assembly is off the locator pins, remove the assembly out the front of the instrument.
      Caution: Do not force the module. There is sufficient clearance for removing it.
    6. If the towers must be removed, mark the tower locations on the baseplate with a pen. Remove the screws securing each tower to the baseplate, and then remove the towers from the instrument.
      Caution: Do not remove the towers from the base plate (if at all possible). Removing the towers makes it much more difficult to adjust and align the sample pipettor module after it is installed.
