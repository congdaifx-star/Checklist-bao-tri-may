Adjusting Reagent Carriage

This procedure describes how to mechanically adjust the reagent carriage to the reagent pipettor prior to performing any alignments on this module.

Required Materials:
  • there are no special tools required

The reagent carriage interacts with the reagent pipettor, the incubator pick-and-place, and the sample pick-and-place. The mechanical adjustment should be checked or performed prior to setting the alignments of the interacting assemblies in the software.

The mechanical adjustments for this module include:
  • adjust reagent carriage to reagent pipettor
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Open the instrument main upper left and right covers.
    2. Power off the motors using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  2. Adjust reagent carriage to reagent pipettor position.
    1. Move the reagent carriage to the front of its travel, and then lower the pipettor probe down to the carriage surface.
    2. Loosen the three bolts securing the reagent carriage mounting plate to the instrument baseplate.
    3. Move the pipettor probe from the front of its travel to the rear of its travel.
    4. Adjust the position of the carriage assembly so that the pipettor probe is centered along the line of carriage vessel positions when the probe is moved.
    5. Tighten the three bolts securing the reagent carriage mounting plate to the instrument baseplate.
      Note: The three bolts should be spaced evenly in the slots. This indicates the carriage is straight.
    6. Recheck the adjustment of the reagent carriage to the reagent pipettor probe.
  3. Power on the motors using the switch on the power distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  4. If an adjustment is made to a reagent carriage, align the intersecting devices.
    Note: Refer to the appropriate procedure:
    • P&P: Adjusting Sample Pick-and-Place
    • P&P: Adjusting Incubator Pick-and-Place
    • RP: Adjusting Reagent Pipettor
