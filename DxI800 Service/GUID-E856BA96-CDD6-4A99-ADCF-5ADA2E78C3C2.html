Installing LAS EMI Modifications

This procedure describes how to install the EMI modifications on DxI instruments with serial numbers below 600133 that will be connected to a lab automation system (LAS).

Required Materials: All the required parts are included in the LAS Option Kit.

Perform this procedure only if the DxI 800 serial number is below 600133 and if it will be connected to a lab automation system (LAS).

Note: All DxI 600 instruments and all DxI 800 instruments with serial numbers 600133 and above have had the EMI modifications installed in manufacturing.
  1. Remove the paint from the rear covers.
    1. Remove the panel from the upper rear cover of the instrument.
    2. Remove both the upper rear and center rear gray covers.
    3. Using an emery cloth or sandpaper, remove the paint from each of the ten holes on the outside of the upper rear cover.
      Note: Remove enough paint from each hole so that the diameter of the removed paint is slightly larger than the head of the mounting screw.
    4. Remove the paint from each of the four mounting holes on the inside of the upper rear cover.
      Note: Remove enough paint from each hole so that the diameter of the removed paint is slightly larger than the head of the mounting screw.
    5. Remove the paint from the screw hole on the inside of the center rear gray cover,
      Note: Remove enough paint from the hole so that the diameter of the removed paint is slightly larger than the head of the mounting screw.
    6. Install the upper rear and center rear gray covers.
  2. Install the ferrite beads on the cables on the right side of the upper left card cage.
    The ferrite beads are included in the LAS install kit.
    1. Install a black ferrite bead approximately 1 in (2.54 cm) from the connector of the luminometer control cable.
      Note: The luminometer control cable is the top left cable connected to the DAQC PCB.
    2. Install a white ferrite bead approximately 1 in (2.54 cm) from the connector of the luminometer signal cable.
      Note: The luminometer signal cable is the top right cable connected to the DAQC PCB.
  3. Install a white ferrite bead approximately 1 in (2.54 cm) from the connector of the luminometer HVPS cable on the side of the luminometer.
  4. Complete the installation. Refer to the LAS: Installing DxI Instrument into LAS procedure. All verification testing will be completed during the installation procedure.
