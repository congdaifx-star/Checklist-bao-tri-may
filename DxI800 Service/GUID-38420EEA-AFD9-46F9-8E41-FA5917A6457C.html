JP8 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Pressure Sensor

  • The signals for the Pipettor Pressure Sensors, Substrate Bubble Detector, and Upper Cabinet air temperature to the DAQC PCB run through JP8.
  • The + 12V, -12V power to this PCB, and the substrate bubble detector also run through JP8.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, PRESSURE connects JP8 to J17 at the top of the Upper Left Card Cage.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, PARALLEL PORT connects J17 to J3 on the DAQC PCB.
Note: All ground pins on the JP8 connector are tied together on this PCB. The JP8 connector shell is grounded by its connection to the Upper Left Card Cage.
