SP: Cleaning PFA Sample Probe

This procedure describes how to clean the sample probe.

Perform this procedure when the sample probe has become bent, damaged, or dirty due to a probe crash or by entering sample tube gel.

Required Materials
  • Proper hand, eye, and facial protection.
  • 70% ethanol or methanol solution.
    Caution: Ethanol and methanol are extremely flammable. Do not use near heat or flame. Do not ingest. Avoid contact with eyes, skin, and clothing. Use with adequate ventilation.
  • Alcohol wipe.
  • Kimwipe or lint-free tissue.
  • Empty beaker or container.
  • Syringe and syringe fitting assembly.
Caution: Handle the sample probe with care to avoid damage to the probe tip.
Note: Refer to SP: Replacing PFA Sample Probe: procedure for removing and reinstalling the sample probe.
  1. Open the upper left main cover and remove sample probe from housing to gain better access to probe tip.
  2. Wipe probe exterior with alcohol wipe to clean tip and remove any residual gel.
  3. If gel may have been aspirated, soak entire sample probe in 70% methanol or ethanol in empty probe beaker or container (i.e. sample, aspirate, dispense, or substrate probe) for 5-10 minutes to attempt to loosen any gel inside the probe.
  4. Remove sample probe from alcohol and wipe with kimwipe or lint-free tissue.
  5. Attach the syringe fitting assembly to the syringe.

  6. Attach tubing from aspirate probe cleaning kit over the sample probe tip.
  7. Place other end of sample probe into a beaker of methanol and draw methanol through sample probe into the syringe, aspirating any obstructions in sample probe into the syringe. Do not flush methanol from the tip through the top of the probe in order to avoid forcing any gel further into the probe. Repeat 2-3 times.

  8. Reattach sample probe to housing and close the upper left main cover.
  9. Initialize system and prime sample pipettor 10 cycles.
  10. Run Carryover Test. Refer to Tests: Automated Carryover Test procedure.
    Note: If carryover test fails, refer to Tests: Automated Carryover Test Troubleshooting document.
