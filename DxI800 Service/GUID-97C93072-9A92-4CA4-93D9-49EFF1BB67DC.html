Installing Bulk Feeder

This procedure describes how to install the bulk vessel feeder module into the instrument.

Prerequisites:

Inspect the area where the bulk feeder module will be installed. It should is clean and free of salts, vessels, dust and debris. Ensure that the cables, wires and air lines that will be connected to the bulk feeder module will be accessible after the module is installed.

  1. Install the bulk feeder.
    1. Lift the module into position in the instrument.
    2. Slide the module rearward until the rear 'foot' is securely and completely under the hold down bracket and the front locating pin is positioned inside the second hole from the front of the foot.
    3. Insert and tighten the large cap-screw and washer at the front and bottom of the bulk feeder module.
    4. Insert and tighten the two screws that attach the upper support bracket to the module.
  2. Attach the main air supply tubing to the air manifold on the bulk feeder module by inserting the tubing into the fitting and then lightly pulling on it to ensure it is in position.
    If the tubing will not seat securely in the fitting, clip off a short amount of tubing to create a smooth surface for insertion into the fitting.
  3. Route the ribbon cable and wiring harness through the harness clamps on the left side of the module, and then plug the connectors into J1 and J16 on the bulk feeder interface PCB.
  4. Move the upper switch on the power distribution PCB to the on position.
  5. Verify that the adjustment between the dispenser and the supply carriage is correct.
    Note: Refer to the CA: Adjusting Supply Carriage procedure.
  6. Initialize the system.
  7. Perform a minimum of 20 lumblanks and verify that the bulk feeder reliably delivers vessels to the supply carriage.
