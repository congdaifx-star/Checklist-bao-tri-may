Troubleshooting Pump Errors - Wash Pump

Purpose

This procedure describes how to troubleshoot errors on the wash pump.

Troubleshooting Procedure

Caution:
  • Place a lint free towel under each reagent probe to catch the wash buffer that drains when the pump, valve, or other fluid components are removed.
  • Remove power to the components when swapping, removing, or installing.

Perform these steps to troubleshoot errors on the wash pump. Repeat the initial failing test after any major issues are identified and resolved.

Step Possible Issue Action Step
1 If the instrument is a new install, the piston and seal can be seized with dried wash buffer. Clean the pump.
Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure.
2 Wash buffer not being delivered Prime the dispense probes and aspirate probe 1. Watch the fluid lines to verify wash buffer is being delivered and there are no air bubbles.
3 Upper wash buffer reservoir is empty. Verify that the upper wash buffer reservoir contains buffer. If the float sensor is stuck to the top of the reservoir, clean the float sensor with DI water and relaunch the AU.
A rare failure mode for pressure errors occurs when the float sensor on the upper wash buffer reservoir adheres to the top of the reservoir. If the error occurs often enough to catch regularly on priming, then prime to isolate the source to either the pump and valve or the tubing and probe:
  1. Position the pipettor over the wash tower.
  2. Place a lint-free tissue under the probes:
    • For Wash Pump 1: Dispense Probe 1 and 2.
    • For Wash Pump 2: Aspirate Probe 1 and Dispense Probe 3
  3. Disconnect the union fitting from the fittings that route to:
    • Wash Pump 1: Dispense Probe 1 and 2.
    • Wash Pump 2: Dispense Probe 3 and Aspirate Probe 1 wash (located close to the aspirate Peristaltic Pump) .
  4. Remove the lint-free tissue.
  5. Place the upper tubing into a beaker to prevent wash buffer from spraying during priming.
  6. Prime the dispense probes and aspirate probe 1.
    • If the error continues, then troubleshoot the valve.
    • If the error does not continue, then continue troubleshooting for damaged or missing parts in this table.
4 Damaged or missing parts
  • O-rings
    • between the pump and fluidics reservoir.
    • between the pump and pressure sensor.
  • Red-and-white quick-disconnect fittings:
    • Upper wash buffer reservoir labeled APW
    • Front side of the Wash Pump 2 pressure sensor block (mounted to the right side of the wash pump).
    • Back side of the Wash Pump 2 pressure sensor.
  • Tubing or connectors.
  • Aspirate probe.
  • Dispense probe
  • For pressure error only:
    • Duck-bill valve
    • Vacuum Pump 3
    • Peristaltic pump.
      Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pump (Peristaltic) procedure.
5

Valve problems

  1. Check for valve problems.
    • Correct orientation of the valve in the home position.
    • Correct position of the rotor. Remove and reinstall the rotor if necessary. The correct precision valve rotor position:
    • Correct orientation of the stator. Rotate the stator, if necessary.
    • Dried wash buffer in the valve. Clean if necessary.
      Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Cleaning and Maintaining Precision and Wash Valves procedure.
    • Sticking valve cap. Lubricate if necessary.
      Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Lubricating Ceramic Valve Cap procedure.
    • Loose electrical connections. Reseat the valve electrical connections. Refer to the Cables and PCBs chart to determine which cables and PCBs to reseat.
6 Pressure sensor error
  1. Power down and reseat pressure sensor connections (interface board on front of fluidics reservoir).
  2. Calibrate the pressure sensor.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Calibrating Sample and Precision Pump Pressure Sensors procedure.
  3. Swap the pressure sensors, then recalibrate both sensors.
    Caution: The wash pump pressure sensor does not require calibration. However, the other swapped pressure sensor does require a new calibration.
7 Connection problems Reseat the MCC PCB, ribbon cables, and power harnesses. Refer to Cables and PCBs chart.
8 Problem is not the pump Verify that the issue is the pump.
  1. Swap the wash pumps temporarily.
  2. If the issue stays on the original wash circuit, then troubleshoot the fluidics lines and electronics for this pump circuit.
    Note: Refer to Cables and PCBs chart to determine which cables and PCBs to reseat.
  3. If the issue moves with the pump, then continue troubleshooting this pump.
9 Loose end-cap Inspect the end-cap.
  • If a torque wrench and the 1-1/16 inch socket tool are available, then secure the lower cap using these specifications. Instruments with:
    • Brown Delrin guide: Torque specification of 12 in-lb (1.4 N•m)
    • Beige or gray guide: Torque specification of 10 in-lb (1.1 N•m)
  • If the tools are not available, secure by hand tightening the lower cap on the bottom of the pump. Then use a wrench to rotate the black lower cap an additional ½ hex (30°) beyond hand tight.

10 Damaged belt or belt flag
  1. Inspect for belt damage. If any damage is found, replace the belt and clean the pump.
    Note: Refer to Fluidics: Installing Pump Belt and Tensioner Mounting Bracket and Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedures.
  2. Verify that the belt flag is not tilted and rubbing on the acrylic pump block. If the belt flag is tilted, replace the belt (if damaged). Verify that the home flag does not rotate to the side when securing the screw.
11 Damaged or missing o-rings Verify that these o-rings are undamaged and positioned correctly:
  • between the pump and fluidics reservoir
  • between the pump and pressure sensor
  • in the piston and seal.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure for pictures showing the positioning of the o-ring.
12 Dirty or damaged piston guide, piston, or seals. Inspect and clean or replace dirty or damaged piston guide, piston, or seals.
  • If the issues continue on a previous version pump (brown Delrin guide with one or two o-rings), then perform Fluidics: Installing Pump Reliability Components (Sample/Precision/Wash) procedure
  • If parts are not available for the mod or the pump is a new pump (beige or gray guide, two o-rings), then perform Fluidics: Cleaning Pumps (Precision/Sample/Wash) procedure.
