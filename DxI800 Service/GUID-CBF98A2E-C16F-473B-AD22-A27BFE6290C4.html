Inspecting Substrate Loader During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to inspect the substrate loader for loose or missing parts during substrate system troubleshooting.

  1. Prepare the substrate loader.
    1. Remove power by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
    2. Remove the top air line from one of the substrate loader air cylinders to release air pressure from the system.
    3. Reattach the air line.
    4. Loosen, but do not remove, the six screws on the top plate of the substrate loader by two turns.
    5. Remove the two screws that attach the substrate doors to the substrate loader.
    6. Lift one door and spread the side walls enough to remove the door. Leave the substrate bottles in place.
    7. Lift the second door and spread the side walls enough to remove the door. Leave the substrate bottles in place.
    8. Remove both LED masks by removing the two screws in each.
  2. Inspect the substrate loader.
    1. Verify that the gasket is on the substrate cap.
    2. Verify that both the front and rear springs are under the cap bracket.
      Caution: A small number of new installs have found that the front and rear springs were installed incorrectly (above the cap bracket) on the substrate cap bracket screws. Confirm that the springs are installed correctly (under the cap bracket).
    3. Reattach the two LED masks.
    4. Spread the side walls to insert and attach each substrate door.
    5. Tighten the six screws on the top plate of the substrate loader.
    6. Restore power by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
