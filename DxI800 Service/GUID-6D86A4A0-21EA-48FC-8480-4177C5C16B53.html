LEDs - PCB - Interface - Lower Cabinet

Note: The silk-screen for the functions of D2 and D3 are reversed on all versions of this PCB. The actual functions are shown in the table.
LED Color Function

D1

Green

Waste Bag present.

Solid Waste Compartment door is closed: TP3 = 0V and D1 LED is On.

D2

Green

Liquid Waste Container Full Status (The PCB silk-screening is reversed between D2 & D3)

Float at the Full position: D2 LED is On.

D3

Green

Liquid Waste Container Empty Status (The PCB silk-screening is reversed between D2 & D3)

Float at the Empty position: D3 LED is On.

D4

Green

Air Compressor 1.5V control signal from J1-17 to Q1 grounds J8-2.

Compressor On: TP5 = 5V and D4 LED is On.

D5

Green

Air (Moisture) Bleed Valve. 5V control signal from J1-18 to Q2 grounds J9-2.

Valve On: TP6 = 5V and D5 LED is On.

D8

Green

Waste Bag emptied PB.

Green button in the Solid Waste Compartment pressed: TP7 = 0V and D8 LED is On.

D10

Green

Air Compressor 2. 5V control signal from J1-17 to Q3 grounds J10-2.

Compressor On: TP9 = 5V and D10 LED is On.
