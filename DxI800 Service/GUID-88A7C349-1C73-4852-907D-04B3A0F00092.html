Assembling Cart A25791

This procedure describes how to assemble the cart used to hold the peripherals for both the UniCel DxI 600 instrument and the UniCel DxI 800 instrument.

Use the correct procedure for your cart. There are two other peripheral carts with different assembly procedures:
  • C43878 Cart (new).
  • A25791 cart (old).
  • A96829 cart (older).
  1. Assemble the peripherals cart.
    1. Remove the cart from the shipping box and pallet.


    2. Remove the monitor arm and keyboard tray from the accessories box.
    3. Route the monitor cables (serial, video, power) through the monitor arm before the arm is mounted to the cart.


      Note: Verify that the cables reach the intended location of the PC. Some cables might not reach to some locations.
    4. Remove the bolt from the monitor arm, insert the monitor arm into the cart, then secure the arm using the bolt.


      Note: Finished cart dimensions:
      • Shelf 25.66 in X 25.12 in
      • Height 42 in
      • Wheels 3.25 in
      • Monitor arm 12.4 in
    5. Mount the keyboard tray onto the keyboard tray arm (using four screws).
      The keyboard tray mouse holder is optional. Normally the mouse is placed on the top shelf of the cart.
    6. Remove the rear rubber bumper from the keyboard tray bracket on the bottom of the upper cart tray. Slide the keyboard tray arm onto the bracket from the rear, and then reattach the bumper.


    7. Remove the two screws from the bottom right corner of the upper cart tray. Mount the bar code reader bracket to the cart using the removed screws, and then mount the bar code reader holder to the bracket.


      Note: The bracket can be mounted on the left side of the cart. Mounting screws for the optional B33252 automation bracket are attached to the right cart post.
  2. After assembling the cart, attach the monitor to the tilt arm and assemble the peripherals on the cart.
    Note: Refer to the Installing: Assembling Peripherals procedure.
