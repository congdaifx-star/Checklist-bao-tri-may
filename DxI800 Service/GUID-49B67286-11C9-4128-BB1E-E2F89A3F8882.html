Testing Analytical Module Encoders During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to troubleshoot encoder errors on the wash carousel and incubator carousel encoders.

This chart lists the encoder count increment for the incubator and wash wheel encoder.

Device Encoder Count Increment per CW/CCW movement

Incubator Encoder

150 ±2 steps

Wash Wheel Encoder

168 ±2 steps

Determine if the motor encoder is working correctly.
  1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
  2. Select the Device to troubleshoot.
  3. Select Home Device.
    Note: The encoder count should be 0.
  4. Select Advance CW several times, and then select Advance CCW several times.
    Note: The encoder count should increment with every move, see the device specifications for the exact count.
  5. If the encoder counts are erratic, then troubleshoot the encoder by removing power to all the motors and switching the two encoder connections on the analytical module PCB.
    Switching the encoder connections allows the encoder count to be read on a different channel. Either replace the encoder or troubleshoot the PCBs and cables. Be sure to connect the encoder cables to the correct connections when troubleshooting is complete.
  6. Restore power to the motors.
  7. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
  8. Select the Device to troubleshoot.
  9. Select Home Device > Advance CW.
  10. Select the opposite device (either wash wheel or incubator,) and verify the encoder count is reporting the increment count listed in the encoder increment table.

    Example: If troubleshooting the wash carousel encoder, then switch the encoder connection with the incubator encoder. From the alignments screen, select the wash carousel, home the wash carousel, advance CW, select incubator wheel as the device, and review the encoder count.

  11. Repeat the steps several times to confirm that the encoder counts are erratic.
    Caution: Be sure to connect the encoder cables to the correct connections when troubleshooting is complete.
    If the encoder count is then
    Erratic
    1. Verify that the drive motor, pulley, and gear are not loose or damaged.
    2. Replace the encoder.
    Not erratic Troubleshoot and reseat the analytical interface PCB connections, cable connections from the analytical interface PCB to J 71 on the MCC backplane, cable connections from J2 on the MCC backplane to J2 on the COMC PCB (furthest ribbon cable to the right).
