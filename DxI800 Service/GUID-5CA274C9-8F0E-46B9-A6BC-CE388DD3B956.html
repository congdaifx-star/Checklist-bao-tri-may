Removing Precision or Sample Pump

This procedure describes how to remove either a precision pump or a sample pump when a pump or valve error occurs.

This procedure provides instructions for removing any of these pumps from the fluids reservoir:
  • Precision Pump
  • Sample Pump
Perform this procedure:
  • If pump errors occur.
  • If valve errors occur.
  1. Open the main upper left cover and insert an interlock switch bypass.
  2. Drain the tubing.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices  > Peristaltic and Vacuum Pumps.
    2. Enable the vacuum pump for the pump being serviced.
    3. Verify that the pipettor is directly over the wash tower.
    4. Disconnect the fitting from the pressure sensor, and allow the wash buffer to drain into the wash tower.
    5. After the tubing is drained, disable the vacuum pump and exit the Digital Devices screen.
  3. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Remove the interlock bypass.
    2. Remove power from the motors using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
    3. Locate the pump connectors (by number) on the fluidics interface PCB located above and behind the pumps.
    4. Disconnect the connectors from the fluidics interface PCB.
      • If removing a sample pump, then disconnect:
        • J6 - Pump Power
        • J22 - Valve Power
        • J27 - Pump Sensor
      • If removing a precision pump, then disconnect:
        • Valve Power
        • Pump Power
        • Valve Sensor
        • Pump Sensor
        Note: Mark at least one sensor connector and one power connector with the correct J number for correct placement when the pump is reinstalled.
    5. Disconnect the pressure sensor cable on the pressure sensor interface PCB (located on the front of the reservoir).
  4. Remove the pump.
    1. Remove the two hex-screws that hold the pump assembly to the reservoir.
      Note: These screws are toward the front of the assembly, by the pressure sensor, and enter the housing vertically.
    2. Lift the assembly straight up until the valve motor clears the reservoir, then tip the valve end of the assembly forward.
      Caution: Do not damage the fluidics interface PCB with the pump assembly.
      Caution: If while removing the sample pump the pump catches on the upper left card cage cables, then remove reagent pump 1 and slide the sample pump assembly to the right.
    3. While holding the pump at an angle, lift it upward and forward out of the instrument.
      Caution: There are o-rings are on the bottom of the pump assembly.
