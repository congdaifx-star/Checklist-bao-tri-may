Tool Kits: Sample Rack Gauge

The Sample Rack Gauge, P/N B, is a specific tool measure the alignment tab on the back of the sample rack.
Tool Name (CARES) Tool Name (STS) Part Number Details
Kit, Gauge, Go-Nogo, Sample rack Kit, Sample Rack Gauge B36560

Use: Measure the alignment tab on the back of the DxI sample rack.

When to use: If the instrument has a lot if intermittent SPU rack jams with no root cause found with the hardware then measure the sample racks as detailed below.

Note: All sample rack colors should be checked for correct fit with the gauge.
  1. Hold rack in one hand the rack gauge in the other.
    Note: Hold the gauge with the notch on top.
  2. Hold the gauge at an angle and slide the lower portion of the gauge under the rack guide.
  3. Tilt the gauge toward the tab on the rack.
  4. The notch in the tool should easily slide past the tab on the rack.
  5. If the tool does not slide over the tab then the rack could rub on the sample presentation plate. See pictures below.
  6. If the tool fits over the rack tab then place the checked rack indicator label on the rack to indicate it has been checked.
  7. If the tool does not fit over the rack tab then replace the input rack.
