Performing Dark Count Read

This procedure describes how to perform a dark count read.

Warning: The photomultiplier tube (PMT) is a very sensitive photon measuring device. Any background or extraneous light coming into contact with a powered PMT can saturate the PMT and reduce the gain. This reduces the performance of the PMT, and it can take a long time for the PMT to recover or it might never recover fully. A magnetic field close to a powered PMT also reduces the performance of the PMT. The magnetic field causes the electrons to follow a curved path instead of moving in a straight line. When you are performing service in the area of the luminometer, observe the following rules.
  • Never shine a flashlight around the luminometer while the PMT is powered on.
  • Never bring a strong magnetic field close to the luminometer while the luminometer is powered on.
  • Before removing the covers on the analytical module, turn off the power to the PMT.
  • Cap the PMT whenever it is removed from the analytical module.
Parameter Specifications
Software Dark Count Read <600 RLUs. The system will post a dark count error if the scaled RLU is >600 during operation.
STS Dark Count Read

<100 RLUs in the Luminometer > Read screen.

< 400 RLUs in the Luminometer > Dark Count Check > screen.

Note: The dark counts reported in the Dark Count Check and System Check Report are scaled by a factor of 4 while the Luminometer Read screen is a raw DAQC read and is not scaled.
Perform a dark count read.
  1. From the Main Menu select, Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Luminometer > Dark Count Read.
  2. Select Print Results > Start Test.
    Note: The LED must be off during this test.
  3. Allow the test to complete and the results to print.
  4. Evaluate the results, using the specifications.
  5. Determine if:
    • The dark counts meet the software specification.
    • The dark counts meet the STS criteria.
    • The dark counts are elevated at all 69 wash carousel positions
    • The dark counts are elevated at one or only a few wash carousel positions.
