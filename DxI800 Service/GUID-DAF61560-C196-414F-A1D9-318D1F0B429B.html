Contacting the Global Product Technical Support (GPTS) Department in Chaska, Minnesota

This topic includes instructions for and information about contacting the Global Product Technical Support (GPTS) department in Chaska, Minnesota.

Before You Contact GPTS

Before you contact GPTS, you must:
  • Contact the Engineering Technical Support Organization (ETSO) at 1-800-248-0859. See Contacting the Engineering Technical Support Organization (ETSO) for more information.
  • Follow the local escalation process.

Support Hours

Contact Hours

8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Central Time

Monday through Friday

Contact Process

You have the following options for contacting GPTS:
  • Submit a GPTS Inquiry at the following link:
    • https://danaher.sharepoint.com/sites/bec_service/gpts/SitePages/GPTS-Technical-Requests-Submission-Form.aspx
  • File an L1 escalation through ServiceMax.
