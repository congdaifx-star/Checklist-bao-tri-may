Pipettor Matching Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Run Matching

If Then
CV >2.5% on more than one pipettor Check the following areas for issues.
  • RV spring finger—Remove the RV spring finger from the vessel positioner and reinstall the vessel positioner.
  • Sample pipettor (over dispensing and diluting an RV)
  • Dispense probe plate adjustment or alignment
  • Ejector plate
  • Mixer-gripper-spinner
  • Substrate dispense
  • Pick-and-place alignment
CV >2.5% within one pipettor Check the following areas for issues.
  • Troubleshoot as with an Access precision problem in the unwashed portion of the system check
  • Pick-and-place alignment
  • Sample pipettor (over dispensing and diluting an RV)
more than two reps were eliminated Check the following areas for issues.
  • Sample pipettor
  • Tubing, valve
  • Precision pump
PCF >1.15 Troubleshoot the suspect pipettor like an Access precision problem in the unwashed portion of the system check.

Troubleshooting Verify Matching

If Then
CV >2.5% on more than one pipettor Check the following areas for issues.
  • RV spring finger—Remove the RV Spring Finger from the vessel positioner and reinstall the vessel positioner.
  • Sample pipettor (over dispensing and diluting an RV)
  • Dispense probe plate adjustment or alignment
  • Ejector plate
  • Mixer-gripper-spinner
  • Substrate dispense
  • Pick-and-place alignment
CV >2.5% within one pipettor Check the following areas for issues.
  • Troubleshoot as with an Access precision problem in the unwashed portion of the system check
  • Pick-and-place alignments
  • Sample pipettor (over dispensing and diluting an RV)
more than two reps were eliminated from any pipettor (within pipettor) Check the following areas for issues.
  • Sample pipettor
  • Tubing, valve
  • Precision pump
%Difference >1.50 Perform a dTest/dAP.
Note: Refer to theTests: Performing Dilution Assays procedure.
If the test Then
passes
  1. Perform Run Matching
  2. Perform Low Volume Matching
fails
  • Inspect the pump belt
  • Inspect the flare fittings
  • Disassemble and clean the upper and lower pistons
  • Verify the pump spring is not broken

Troubleshooting Low Volume Matching

If Then
more than four reps eliminated from any pipettor for one or more pipettors Check the following areas for issues.
  • Sample pipettor
  • Tubing, valve
  • Pump
  • Reagent pipettor ultrasonics and alignments
reps more than 4 SD from the mean or CV >7.0% for any pipettor (within pipettor) Check the following areas for issues.
  • Affected reagent pipettor (drip back, wash tower malfunction, and so on)
mean RLU <500,000 or >2,500,000 for one or more pipettors Check the following areas for issues.
  • Troubleshoot like a precision or accuracy problem in the unwashed portion of system check.
  • 1:501 dilution for expiration or dilution error.
