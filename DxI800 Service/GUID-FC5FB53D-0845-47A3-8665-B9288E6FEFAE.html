Removing Sample or Reagent Carriage

This procedure describes how to remove the supply or reagent carriages from the instrument.

Perform this procedure when a supply or reagent carriage is replaced or repaired.
  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Remove the center gray rear cover and upper rear covers.
    2. Open the upper left and right front covers.
    3. Remove power from the motors using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
  2. Remove the sample or reagent carriage.
    1. Disconnect the electrical connectors from the carriage I/F PCB for the carriage being removed.
      Carriage Sensor Cable Connection Power Cable Connection
      Supply (DxI 600 and 800) J3 J8
      Reagent 1 (DxI 600 and DxI 800) J4 J9
      Reagent 2 (DxI 600 and DxI 800) J5 J10
      Reagent 3 (DxI 800 only) J6 J11
      Reagent 4 (DxI 800 only) J7 J12
      Note: Remove the cable-ties.
    2. Remove the screws.
      Note: Fielded instruments can have one of two versions of the carriage. The newest version has a linear slide and the original carriage design does not. For information on how to distinguish between carriage versions, refer to the Carriage OP: Reagent Carriage document. The mounting plate must be replaced when switching between carriage versions because the mounting pins in the different carriage versions are in different locations.
      • If replacing the carriage with the same carriage version, then remove the two screws securing the carriage to the mounting bracket.
      • If replacing the carriage with a different carriage version, then remove the three screws securing the mounting bracket to the carriage stand.
    3. Lift the carriage out of the rear of the instrument.
