Specs - Power Cord

NEMA Designations

Caution: Do not connect the instrument to a 110 V AC source. The internal line conditioner will overheat.

Power Circuits

The NEMA designations for the power cord and receptacle for the DxI are:
  • Plug - NEMA L6-20P
  • Receptacle - NEMA L6-20R.

Both plug and receptacle are twist-lock type devices, 208 to 240 V AC, 20 A.
