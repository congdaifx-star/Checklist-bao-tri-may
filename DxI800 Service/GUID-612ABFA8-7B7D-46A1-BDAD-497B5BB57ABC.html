Installing Sample Pipettor

This procedure describes how to install a new sample pipettor module when the old sample pipettor module has been removed for repair or is being replaced.

  1. Prepare the instrument.
    1. Remove the gray center rear cover and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    2. Remove the upper rear cover and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
    3. Open the upper left front cover.
    4. Power off the switch for the power distribution PCB labeled all DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
  2. Install the sample pipettor.
    1. Move the pipettor carriage all the way to the front of the pipettor assembly and then slide the pipettor into the instrument.
    2. If replacing a pipettor, remove the new gantry from the towers and install it on the mounting towers that are already in place.
      Caution: Do not remove the towers from the base plate (if at all possible). Removing the towers makes it much more difficult to adjust and align the sample pipettor module after it is installed.
      • If installing a sample pipettor without mounting towers,
        Caution: If the home sensor was routed through the opening in the front tower previously, then route the home sensor through the opening in the front tower before placing the sample pipettor in place.
        1. Align the gantry mounting holes with the tower locating pins.
        2. Push the gantry onto the pins.
        3. Tighten the two captive screws that hold the gantry to the tower.
      • If installing a sample pipettor with mounting towers, mount the towers (with the gantry attached) to the instrument base plate using the mounting screws.
        Note: The location of the sample pipettor towers may have been marked on the instrument base plate.
        Caution: If the tower locations were not marked on the base plate, adjustments and alignments are required.
    3. Reconnect the connectors to the sample pipettor interface PCB.
      • J1- Ribbon Cable
      • J3- Sample Pipettor Motor
    4. Reconnect the pipettor supply tubing connector at the in-line fitting.
  3. Initialize the system.
    1. Insert the interlock bypass.
    2. Power on the switch for the power distribution PCB labeled all DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    3. Initialize the instrument.
  4. Verify operation of the sample pipettor.
    1. Confirm the sample pipettor alignments to all intersecting devices. Align and adjust as needed.
      Note: If the sample pipettor is connected to an LAS, then align to the track also.
    2. Verify that all fluid lines have been reconnected and prime the pipettor ten times. Check for leaks.
    3. Perform ten reps each of the RF level sense test for wet and dry modes.
      • If the sample pipettor is not connected to LAS, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics  > RF Level Sense > SPU.
      • If the sample pipettor is connected to LAS, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics  > RF Level Sense > LAS.
    4. Remove the interlock bypass and close the covers.
    5. Initialize the instrument.
    6. Perform the sample pipettor carryover test, and verify that all results are within the pass criteria.
      Note: Refer to Instrument / System > Tests - System > Carryover Test.
