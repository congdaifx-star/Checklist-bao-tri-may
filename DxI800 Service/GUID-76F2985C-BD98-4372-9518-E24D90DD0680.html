Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - Analytical Module

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals
J1 CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, ANALYTIC POWER (P/N 116271) connects J1 to J71 (MCC7) on the MCC Backplane PCB. The Wash Wheel and Incubator Wheel Heater PWM, Home Sensor, and Encoder Sensor signals run through J1.
J2 HARNESS, INTERFACE, ANALYTICAL POWER (P/N 116393) connects J2 to JP71 on the MCC Backplane PCB. The Aspirate Arm, Dispense Arm, Wash Wheel, and Incubator stepper motor drive signals run through J2.
J3 HARNESS, ANALYTICAL MODULE POWER (P/N 110972) connects J3 to J8 on the Power Distribution PCB. The 24V power from the Power Distribution PCB runs through J3.
J4 The Thermal Cutout Switch for the 3 heaters connects to J4. 24V power to the heaters.

The 3 heaters are wired in series with the Thermal Cutout Switch.

J5 The Wash Bracket Heater connects to J5. 24V power from the thermal cutout to the heaters (D2 LED = On).

The 3 heaters are wired in series.

J6 The Magnet Side Area Heater connects to J6. The 3 heaters are wired in series.
J7 The Substrate Incubate Side Area Heater connects to J7. The WW Heater PWM+ signal (TP3 ) activates Q1 to ground J7-2.

The 3 heaters are wired in series.

The WW Heater PWM signal originates at COMC PCB J2-39.

J8 The Baseplate Thermal Cutout Switch and the Baseplate Heater connect to J8.
  • The Incubator Heater PWM+ signal (TP4 ) activates Q2 to ground J8-4.
  • The power to the heater runs through the Thermal Cutout Switch.
  • The Incubator Heater PWM signal originates at COMC PCB J2-41.
J9 The Incubator Wheel stepper motor connects to J9. Stepper motor drive signals.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.
J10 The Dispense Arm Z stepper motor connects to J10. Stepper motor drive signals.
J11 The Wash Wheel stepper motor connects to J11. Stepper motor drive signals.
J12 The Aspirate Arm Z stepper motor connects to J12. Stepper motor drive signals.
J13 The Incubator Home sensor connects to J13. Home: TP5 = 0V and D7 LED is On.
J14 The Wash Wheel Home sensor connects to J14. Home: TP7 = 0V and D6 LED is On.
J19 The Incubator Encoder connects to J19. Encoder data.
J20 The Wash Wheel Encoder connects to J20. Encoder data.
