JP01 - PCB - MCC Backplane

  • The SPU Interface PCB 24-V power and SPU motor drive signals run through JP01. (MCC0)
  • HARNESS, SAMPLE HANDLING connects JP01 to J2 on the SPU Interface PCB.
The harness also connects:
  • JP01 to Sample Pipettor I/F PCB J2 and J3
  • JP02 to Sample Storage I/F PCB J2.
Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of 4 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.
