J8 - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

  • The Triple Hall Effect Sensor PCB P/N 11021E or Single Hall Effect Sensor PCB P/N 11023D connects to J8 through the service loop cable P/N 109262.
Note: The wires exit the 6 pin service loop connector from the side that faces upward when connected to the interface PCB and the connector is also keyed for polarity.
J8 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

SV Ejector Sense

5V or 0V

From Triple Hall Effect Sensor PCB J1-1 to U1B-3 then from U1B-4 to J1-15.

Ejector down / vessel not present: TP8 = 0V and D5 LED is On.

2

+5V_MCC2

5V

From J1 pin 2, 8, or 20 then from MCC Backplane J21, J22, or J23 pin 2, 8, or 20 (MCC2 PCB).

3

GND_MCC2

0V

From J1 pin 1, 12, or 19 then from MCC Backplane J21, J22, or J23 pin 1, 12, or 19 (MCC2 PCB).

4

RV Ejector Sense

5V or 0V

From Triple or Single Hall Effect Sensor PCB J1-4 to U1A-1 then from U1A-2 to J1-6.

Ejector down / vessel not present: TP7 = 0V and D4 LED is On.

5

DV Ejector Sense

5V or 0V

From Triple Hall Effect Sensor PCB J1-5 to U1C-5 then from U1C-6 to J1-16.

Ejector down / vessel not present: TP9 = 0V and D6 LED is On.

6

N/C

0V

Not connected.
