Assembling Cart C43878

This procedure describes how to assemble peripherals cart used with the UniCel DxI 600 instrument and the UniCel DxI 800 instrument.

Cart dimensions:
  • 21" wide X 26" deep.
  • Top Shelf Height 40".
Use the correct procedure for your cart. There are two older peripheral carts with different assembly procedures:
  • C43878 Cart (new).
  • A25791 cart (old).
  • A96829 cart (older).
  1. Remove the cart from the shipping box and pallet.

  2. Attach the monitor to the tilt arm and assemble the peripherals onto the cart.
    Note: There are Velcro straps that can be used for attaching the cables to the monitor arm, and double sided Velcro strips that can be used for mounting the PC, the CD ROM, and the keyboard to the cart.

    Note: Refer to Installing: Assembling Peripherals procedure for cable connection details.
