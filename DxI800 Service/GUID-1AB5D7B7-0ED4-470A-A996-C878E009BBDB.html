Field Service Tests

These assays are for field troubleshooting.

Note: All field assays are one step with one incubation pitch.
Test ID or APF Process Sample Aspirated Sample + Buffer Delivered to RV Ultrasonic Mix RV Wash Used In
905 Lum Blank Dispenses 200 µL of substrate into an SV. 0 0 no 0 System Check: substrate
906 Lum Wash Picks up 165 µL of sample and dispenses 150 µL into an RV. 165 150 no 1 System Check: washed
907 Lumi AP Picks up 55 µL of sample and dispenses 50 µL into an RV. 55 50 no 0 System Check: unwashed
909 AP10 Picks up 10 µL of sample and dispenses 50 µL (with chase) into an RV. 10 50 no 0 Instrument Manufacturing
910 AP25 Picks up 25 µL of sample and dispenses 50 µL (with chase) into an RV. 25 50 no 0
  • Instrument Manufacturing
  • Automated Carryover
935 dAP Performs a 1:24 dilution with an ultrasonic mix in the DV. Aspirates 20 µL and delivers 480 µL (with chase) to RV1. Aspirates 55 µL from RV1 and delivers 50 µL to RV2. 20 480 yes 0 dTest/dAP - Dilution Assays with Ultrasonic Mix
936 dTest Performs a 1:400 dilution like dil hCG with an ultrasonic mix. Aspirates 10 µL and delivers 480 µL (with chase) to RV1, then aspirates 6 µL from RV1 and delivers 50 µL (with chase) to RV2. 10 480 yes 0 dTest/dAP - Dilution Assays with Ultrasonic Mix
961 HSNoFoam Aspirates 185 µL of sample and delivers 165 µL (no chase) into an RV. 185 165 no 1 HS System Check Service Assay and Macro
962 HSFoam Aspirates 185 µL of sample and delivers 165 µL (no chase) into an RV. The RV is mixed at high power. 185 165 yes 1 HS System Check Service Assay and Macro
971 LumiAPY Runs the LumiAp test with a Y command. 55 50 no 0 Pipettor Matching

Manufacturing Tests

These assays are primarily for instrument manufacturing and development use and are placed here for reference.

Note: All the listed assays are one step with one incubation pitch except:
  • LWashInc = 52 pitches
  • Wakeup and Utility = 0 pitches
Test ID or APF Process Sample Aspirated Sample + Buffer Delivered to RV 1 Ultrasonic Mix RV Wash Used In
901 DilAP Dispenses 50 µL of a 1:24.5 dilution into 200 µL of substrate. 20 480 no 0
  • Instrument Development
  • Manufacturing Final Test.
902 LumiAP4 Picks up 25 µL of sample and dispenses 50 µL (with chase) into a single RV four separate times. 25 50 no 0 Level Sense Testing
903 Particle Retension Mixes particles, picks up 100 µL, and dispenses 200 µL (with chase). 0 0 no 1 Instrument Development
912 USMIX Mixes particles, picks up 55 µL, and dispenses 50 µL into an RV that is discarded. 0 0 no 0 Instrument Development
913 PipCo Picks up 55 µL of sample and dispenses 50 µL into an RV. 55 50 no 0 NA
914 LWashInc Picks up 165 µL of sample and dispenses 150 µL into an RV. 165 150 no 1 Not used on DxI
915 NGWash Adds 150 µL of wash buffer. 0 0 no 1 NA
919 PREC250 Makes 1:50 dilution in RV1, does not make a dilution in RV2, incubates RV2 one pitch, and discards RV2. 5 250 no 0 Gravimetric procedure
923 PrbWash Performs a full probe wash for each rep requested. Incubates empty RV for one pitch and discards. 0 0 no 0 Gravimetric procedure
927 CLEAN Performs a full probe wash for each rep requested. Does not wash RV, but adds substrate. 0 0 no 0 Similar to Lumblank.
933 WakeUp Performs a full probe wash for each rep requested. Washes empty RV and adds substrate. 0 0 no 1 N/A for DxI
940 Utility Performs a full probe wash for each rep requested. Washes empty RV and adds substrate. 0 0 no 1 N/A for DxI
941 LWInc52 Picks up 165 µL of sample and dispenses 350 µL (with chase) into an RV. 165 350 no 1 Not used on DxI
942 LWashSon Picks up 165 µL of sample and dispense 350 µL (with chase) into an RV. RV is mixed at high power. 165 350 yes 0 Not used on DxI. Obsolete with the transition to Wash Buffer II.
945 Grav 400 Verifies reagent pipettor delivery via gravimetrics. 450 no no 0 NA
