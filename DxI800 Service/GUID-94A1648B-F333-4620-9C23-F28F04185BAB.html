Test Points - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

Note: Test points 3, 6, and 10 are located on the component side of the PCB and the other test points are located on the opposite side of the PCB.
TP Voltage / Signal Notes

1

0V

Ground.

2

5V or 0V

X/Y-Axis Home.

Home position: TP2 = 0V and D2 LED is on.

3

5V or 0V

RV Ejector Air Valve.

Air valve activated: TP3 = 5V and LED on the valve is on.

4

5V or 0V

X/Y-Axis End.

End of Travel position: TP4 = 0V and D1 LED is on.

The X/Y - Axis End of Travel signal is only used on the Sample Pick and Place.

5

5V or 0V

Z-Axis Home.

Home position: TP5 = 5V and D7 LED is on.

6

5V or 0V

SV Ejector Air Valve.

Air valve activated: TP6 = 5V and LED on the valve is on.

7

5V or 0V

RV Ejector sensor.

RV ejector is down: TP7 = 0V and D4 LED is on.

8

5V or 0V

SV Ejector sensor.

SV ejector is down: TP8 = 0V and D5 LED is on.

9

5V or 0V

DV Ejector sensor.

DV ejector is down: TP9 = 0V and D6 LED is on.

10

5V or 0V

DV Ejector Air Valve.

Air valve activated: TP10 = 5V and LED on the valve is on.
