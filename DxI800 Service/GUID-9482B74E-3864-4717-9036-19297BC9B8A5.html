Specs - Toshiba 1400SE UPS

Purpose

This document provides the specifications for the Toshiba 1400SE Plus 2.4 kVA HV UPS.

Notes

This UPS:
  • Is available in NAO and international versions.
  • Is provided with a cord for use between the UPS and the wall outlet.
  • Provides backup power for the DxI instrument.
  • Ships from Intelligent Power Solutions, California for NAO. Ships from the UK for international.
