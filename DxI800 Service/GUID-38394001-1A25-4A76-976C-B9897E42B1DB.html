Drawings

Details

The Vacuum / Pressure Sensor Interface PCB connects to these devices:
  • Wash Tower external flow monitor vacuum/pressure sensors.
  • Aspirate Probe Wash Manifold vacuum/pressure sensor.
  • Lower Cabinet Temperature sensor.
  • DAQC PCB.
