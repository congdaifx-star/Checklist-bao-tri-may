J4 - PCB - Communications (COMC)

  • The signals from the MCC Backplane for the Sample Bar Code Reader, Sample Wheel Encoder, Sample Cooler PWM, RF Level Sense, and MCC0, 1, 2, 3 PCBs run through J4.
  • CABLE, RIBBON, MCC BACKPLANE, J4 (P/N 116101) connects J4 to J1 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
