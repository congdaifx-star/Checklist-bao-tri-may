Replacing Vessel Load Door

This procedure describes how to remove and replace the vessel load door.

Required Materials
  • Vessel load door kit (vessel load door, redesigned hinges, hinge extension arms, rubber bumpers, force distribution bracket, inserts, and screws)

Two part kits are available for the upper left (UL) cover area. Refer to the parts list for part numbers.

  • Upper left cover kit (upper left cover with pre-assembled vessel load door)
    Note: For instructions on replacing the upper left cover, refer to the Instrument: Replacing Upper Left Main Cover procedure.
  • Vessel load door kit (vessel load door, redesigned hinges, hinge extension arms, rubber bumpers, force distribution bracket, inserts, and screws)
The vessel load door:
  • is minimally preassembled.
  • does not require that parts be removed from the original cover assembly.
  • can be installed on any upper left cover.
  • can be installed proactively to prevent the possibility of a failure on the upper left cover insert.
Caution: Never install vessel doors or vessel door hardware that does not include the redesigned and added parts. Failure to use the correct door hardware can cause failure of the upper left cover inserts.
  1. Prepare for replacement.
    1. Remove the vessel load door kit from the box.
    2. Inspect the vessel load door and door hardware for damage.
    3. Turn off the power to instrument motors by turning off the power distribution PCB switch.
      The power distribution PCB switch is labeled All DC Power (except CPU and reagent coolers).
    4. Cover the reaction vessels in the bulk vessel feeder.
      Covering the vessels prevents:
      • contamination of the vessels.
      • hardware from falling into the vessels or hopper, if the hardware is dropped.
  2. Remove the vessel load door and door hardware.
    Note: The main upper left cover is not removed during this procedure.
    1. Close the upper left cover and open the vessel load door.
    2. Remove the screws (two on each end) that attach the two hinge extension arms to the upper left cover (four 3/32-in hex screws).
    3. Open the UL cover and remove the screws numbered 1, 2, and 3. Remove the hinge extension arm from the coupler block (9/64-inch hex screw).
      Caution: Do not drop any screws or hardware into the instrument.
    4. Remove the coupler block.
    5. Remove the screws numbered 4 and 5, and then remove the hinge from the cover.
    6. Repeat the hinge and hinge hardware removal steps for the other hinge (3/32-in hex screw).
    7. Close the upper left cover.
    8. Remove the hinge extension arms.
      Caution: Do not scratch the upper left cover. Protect the cover when removing the hinge extension arm.
      1. Before proceeding, place a soft cloth or towel on the cover.

      2. Position the hinge extension arm for removal. Move it in the direction of the arrows until the right angle corner is centered in the opening and the upper end is against the protective padding.
      3. Pull the hinge extension arm out of the slot from the outside of the cover.
        Note: It may be necessary to open the cover and tap the end of the hinge extension through the slots.
      4. Repeat the hinge extension arm removal steps on the other hinge extension arm.
  3. Install the vessel load door kit.
    1. Insert one hinge extension arm.
      Place the vessel load door end of the arm directly onto the padding and push or tap the hinge extensions through the slot. Repeat for the other hinge extension arm.
      Note: The arms are identical so either arm can be inserted into either slot.
    2. Open the upper left cover.
    3. Place the load distribution bracket over the replacement hinge. Attach the hinge to the cover using the two long screws included in the kit (two 3/32-in screws). Repeat for the other hinge.
      Caution: When attaching the hinge mounting screws, snug the screws only. Overtightening these screws can pull the metal inserts out of the upper left cover.
      Note: The pre-installed plastic inserts in the hinges must face the inner surface of the upper left cover.
    4. Attach the rubber bumper to the coupler block, so that the outer edge touches the corner where the metal changes angle. Attach the hinge arm and the hinge extension arm to the coupler block.
      Note: It might be necessary to change the angle of the hinge arm for the parts to be assembled. The hinge arm is spring-loaded and can be moved manually.
    5. Close the upper left cover.
    6. Attach the vessel load door to the hinge extension arms (four 3/32-in hex screws).
  4. If needed, adjust the vessel load door.
    Note: This adjustment can be time-consuming because there are several variables that cause the door hinge extension arms to be misaligned with the upper left cover slots.
    1. First, determine if adjustment is needed. Open and close the door. If a hinge extension arm rubs on an upper left cover slot, then the vessel load door must be adjusted.
    2. Determine:
      • which hinge extension arm is rubbing.
      • which side the arm rubs.
      • where the rubbing is the worst.
    3. Hold the door with one hand and push or pull the hinge extension arm at the point where the rubbing is worst while holding the plastic door to counteract the push force.
    4. Open and close the door. Check for rubbing and adjust again, if necessary.
  5. Power on the instrument motors by turning on the power distribution PCB switch.
  6. Verify the hinge extension arms on the vessel load door do not rub on the upper left cover slots.
