J8 - Drawing - PCB - Interface - Substrate

The Substrate Heater Control, Pump Home, Bottle Present, Door Open/Closed, and Air Valve signals run through J8. CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SUBSTRATE (P/N 111421) connects J8 to J74 on the MCC Backplane.

J8

Description

Signal / Voltage

To / From / Notes

1

SUBSTRATE HEATER PWM+

5V or 0V (Pulsed 5V control signal)

From MCC Backplane PCB J74-1 to U1-1. Power for the Substrate Heater, pulse with modulated. 5V signal to U1-1 turns Q10 Off and Q7 On grounding J12-2. Enabled: TP18 = 5V and D22 LED is On.

2

SUBSTRATE HEATER PWM-

0V

From MCC Backplane PCB J74-2 to U1-2. Ground for the Substrate Heater.
