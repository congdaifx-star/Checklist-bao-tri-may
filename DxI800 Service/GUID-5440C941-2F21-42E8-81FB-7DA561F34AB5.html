Installing Gripper Sensors (Rectangular from Rectangular)

  1. To install rectangular sensors to replace the rectangular sensors that were removed, perform these steps.
    1. Install the sensor cables into the guide chains, and snap the hinged top of each guide chain link closed.
      Note:
      • For identification, mark one replacement sensor cable J15 on both ends and the other cable J16 on both ends.
      • Extend the sensors out of the guide chain close to the gripper end the approximate distance noted during removal.
    2. Install the blue (lower) and black (upper) air lines into the gripper air fittings.
    3. Install the sensor cables into the guide chains, and snap the hinged top of each guide chain link closed.
      Note: Extend the sensors out of the guide chain by the gripper end the approximate distance noted during removal.
    4. Mount the brackets.
      • Z-guide chain moving-end mounting bracket to the gantry close to the gripper (one 3/32-in screw).
      • X-guide chain moving-end mounting bracket to the bottom of the gantry (one 3/32-in screw).
      • Z-guide chain fixed-end mounting bracket to the gantry (two 3/32-in screws).
      • X-guide chain fixed-end mounting bracket to the base (two 3/32-in screws).
    5. Mount the sensors and the sensor mounting bracket onto the bottom of the gripper with the LED side of the sensor facing downward (two screws).
      • Push the sensors forward until they contact the front edge of the sensor bracket, then tighten the 2 screws to secure the sensor bracket to gripper mounting bracket.
    6. Insert the sensor wires into the wire harness mounts.
    7. Pull the sensor wires up through the opening in the top of the reagent storage module.
    8. Connect these connectors on the interface PCB.
      The correct connector for each sensor will be tested after powering on the instrument.
      • J15 Left Gripper Sensor
      • J16 Right Gripper Sensor
    9. Move the gripper to the home position at the lower left corner of the module.
  2. Prepare to test gripper sensor function.
    1. Reinstall the gripper assembly.
    2. Manually move the gripper to the home position.
    3. Enable all motors.
  3. Check the gripper status in the user interface. The LED on each sensor as well as the D2 (left) and D6 (right) LEDs on the reagent storage interface PCB should match the user interface status. The gripper can be manually toggled to confirm all 4 positions.
    Note: Open cover switches disable power to the gripper sensors and motors. The reagent storage module cover switch disables the gripper motors, but does not affect the sensor operation.

    • If the physical state of the gripper does not match the user interface status, the sensors may be plugged into the wrong connectors on the interface PCB. Reverse the J15 and J16 connectors on the interface PCB.
    • If the gripper LEDs do not function, check the SPU closed switch and the two main cover switches.
  4. Install the main front cover (Framus).
    1. Place the Framus on the instrument and mount it to the right end (two screws).
      Note:
      • To disengage the cover latch on the reagent load tray when installing the Framus, operate the release lever located under the reagent load tray inside the lower right access door.
      • DxI 800 only: Early instruments also have two screws that hold the left end of the Framus to the instrument. If the instrument has these screws, install them to mount the Framus on the left end.
    2. DxI 800 only: Move the substrate loader from the hanging position on the front of the instrument, down onto the Framus. Tighten the four captive screws that mount the substrate loader to the Framus.
    3. DxI 800 only: Mount the triangular-shaped support plate at the center of the Framus (four screws).
  5. Mount the covers.
    1. Install the cover on the reagent storage module (six 5/32-in screws).
    2. Install the lower panel on the bottom of the SPU (four thumb-screws).
  6. Close the SPU.
    The SPU is hinged on the left side. The right side is released and can be swung out from the instrument to approximately 45 degrees.
    Warning: The SPU off-load area slides forward and rearward as the SPU is swung in and out. To prevent damage to the SPU or the off-load area, confirm that nothing obstructs the movement of the off-load area as the SPU is moved.
    1. Grasping the SPU near the right end, swing the right end toward the instrument.
    2. Pull the spring-loaded captive pin to attach the panel (located under the SPU) to the frame.
    3. Close the lower right access panel on the front of the instrument.
    4. Insert the 3/16-in hex bolt.
    5. Tighten the bolt that holds the SPU from swinging outward from the instrument (locking screw).
    6. Close the upper left cover of the instrument.
      Note: Initialize the instrument after opening and closing the SPU, because a switch is actuated when the SPU is opened.
  7. Initialize the system.
    1. Confirm that all cover switches are in the closed position.
    2. Initialize the system with Remap selected.
    3. If initialization fails because of an error communicating with the reagent storage module (ReagentSupply encoder is NAK), then:
      1. Press the [Windows] key, and then select Start  > Programs > Administrative Tools > Remote Desktop Connection to AU PC to start desktop sharing.
      2. Select the TestReagentIF.exe shortcut on the AU desktop to open the Test Reagent program.
      3. In Test Reagent, select Connect.
      4. When connected, select Zero X > Zero Z > Clear Status to reset the reagent storage interface PCB.
      5. Exit the Test Reagent program.
      6. Return to the user interface and initialize the instrument with Remap selected.
      Note: If encoder memory loss occurs following instrument power down for less than 24 hours, then replace the reagent storage module interface PCB at the next opportunity.
  8. Use the reagent load tray to load the removed reagent packs.
    Note: The system will not load expired packs or packs that appear to be on-board.
  9. Initialize the system with Remap selected.
    Note: A final remap with reagent packs on-board verifies the operation of the gripper.
  10. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
