Adjusting Instrument Level

This procedure describes how to adjust the DxI instrument to an approximately level position when there is an uneven floor or a high or low spot on the floor where an instrument foot rests.

In most installations there is no need to adjust the instrument to level because most floors are nearly level.
Adjust the instrument level.
  1. Place a small level on the surface of the instrument near the substrate loaders.
  2. Check the level and determine if one end of the instrument is lower than the other end.
  3. Loosen the lock nuts on the two leveling feet at the lowest end of the instrument.
  4. Using an adjustable wrench, rotate each of these two leveling feet in a direction that raises the instrument off the floor.

  5. Check for level.
  6. Check that all four leveling feet are on the floor. If a leveling foot is off the floor, rotate the foot until it is slightly past the point where it touches the floor.
  7. Recheck for level.
  8. Tighten the locking nut on any feet that were adjusted.
  9. Remove the level from the instrument.
All 4 casters on the instrument are swivel type. It may require 2 persons to move the instrument in areas that are restricted in space.
