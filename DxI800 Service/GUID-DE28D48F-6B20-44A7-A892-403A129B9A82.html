Ultrasonics Level Sense Test Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Wet and Dry Test

If Then Reference these procedures
Wet test failure count equals 0

and

Wet z-step range is less than or equal to 40

Wet test passes.
If during normal operation, the instrument continues to have Reagent Pipettor detected fluid at unexpected height errors across multiple pipettors, then troubleshoot possible causes:
  • Reagent pipettor probes, especially if a PMB is past due. Worn probe tips can cause intermittent level sense errors.
  • Sample pipettor and sample pump.
  • UCTA aliquot (probe, pump, tubing), if the instrument is an integrated DxI.
Fluidics: Troubleshooting Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision)
Wet test failure count greater than 0 Wet test fails.
Troubleshoot possible causes:
  • Circuitry failure.
RP: Troubleshooting Ultrasonics
Wet z-step range is greater than 40 Wet test fails.
Troubleshoot possible causes:
  • Reagent pipettor probes, especially if a PMB is past due. Worn probe tips can cause intermittent level sense errors.
  • Fluid detection or a false level sense.
  • Reagent pipettor to carriage alignment.
  • Fluid leak in pipettor tubing. Disable pipettor y- and z-motor in Device Diagnostics screen. Move the pipettor and watch the probe tip for drip to form or increase.
  • Fluid leak at fitting connections.
  • Fluid leak at o-rings
    • between pump and fluidics reservoir.
    • between pressure sensor and pump.
  • For instruments at high elevation, consider the high elevation configuration.
    Note: Examples of high elevations sites: Mexico City, Santa Fe, NM, and Salt Lake City, UT.
