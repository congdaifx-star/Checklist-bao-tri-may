J13 - PCB - Interface - Analytical Module

  • The Incubator Home sensor connects to J13.
J13 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

+5V_MCC7

5V

From J1-2.

5V power from MCC7 PCB. TP6.

White wire.

2

GND_MCC7

0V

From J1-1, 13, or 19.

Ground from MCC7 PCB. TP8.

Green wire.

3

Incubator - Home

5V or 0V

To J1-3.

Home: TP5 = 0V and D7 LED is On.

Blue wire.

4

+5V_MCC7

Xmit LED Power

5V

From J1-2.

5V is supplied by the MCC7 PCB.

The sensor LED power is 5V supplied through a 221 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected, pin 4 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

Red wire.

5

GND_MCC7

0V

From J1-1, 13, or 19.

Ground from MCC7 PCB. TP8.

Black wire.

Typical home sensor. Pin 3 (TP = 0.7V flag in sensor, 4.8V flag open or sensor disconnected)
