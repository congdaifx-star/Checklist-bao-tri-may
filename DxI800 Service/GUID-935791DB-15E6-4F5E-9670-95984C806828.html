Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - Bulk Feeder

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

09-011616-2 CBL,RIBBN,BULK FEDR I/F (P/N 116162) connects J1 to J11 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 5V power, the Air Valve activation, The Air Cylinder Extended/Retracted, the RV Hopper Motor Home/Index, and the Hopper Reserve sensor signals run through J1.

J3

The Emitter LED for the RV Hopper Reserve (Full) Sensor connects to J3.

Light from the Emitter LED crosses the Bulk Feeder Hopper to the Detector. The Hopper Reserve sensor is activated when an RV blocks the light.

J4

The Lower Cylinder Extended Sensor connects to J4.

This is the Hall Effect magnetic sensor clamped to the right end of the Lower Air Cylinder. It is active, LED on the sensor is On, when the air cylinder is extended and no RV is present. An RV in the scoop will keep the cylinder from fully extending so that the sensor does not activate.

J5

The detector for the RV Hopper Reserve (Full) Sensor connects to J5.

Light from the Emitter LED crosses the Bulk Feeder Hopper to the Detector. The Hopper Reserve sensor is activated when an RV blocks the light. This resets the RV supply count to 1000.

J6

The RV Dispense Cylinder Retracted Sensor connects to J6.

This is the Hall Effect magnetic sensor clamped to the front end of the Escapement (RV dispenser) Air Cylinder. It is active, LED on the sensor is On, when the air cylinder is retracted.

J7

The RV Dispense Cylinder Air Valve connects to J7.

The LED on the Air Valve is On when the Air Valve is activated. Valve activation should extend the air cylinder. Pressing the orange button on the air valve will manually activate it.

J8

The Lower Cylinder Retracted Sensor connects to J8.

This is the Hall Effect magnetic sensor clamped to the left end of the Lower Air Cylinder. It is active, LED on the sensor is On, when the air cylinder is retracted.

J9

The Upper Cylinder Retracted Sensor connects to J9.

This is the Hall Effect magnetic sensor clamped to the left end of the Upper Air Cylinder. It is active, LED on the sensor is On, when the air cylinder is retracted.

J10

The Lower Cylinder Air Valve connects to J10.

The LED on the Air Valve is On when the Air Valve is activated. Valve activation should extend the air cylinder. Pressing the orange button on the air valve will manually activate it.

J11

The RV Escrow Full Sensor connects to J11.

This sensor detects an RV at the top of the Escrow Chute (Chute Full), which directs the bulk feeder to start or stop feeding RVs into the Chute.

J12

The Upper Cylinder Extended Sensor (not used) connects to J12.

Not used.

Extended: TP11 = 0V and D6 LED is On. (RV in upper scoop position)

J13

The RV Hopper Motor Index Sensor connects to J13.

This sensor controls positioning of the RV feed chain scoops.

J14

The Upper Cylinder Air Valve connects to J14.

The LED on the Air Valve is On when the Air Valve is activated. Valve activation should extend the air cylinder. Pressing the orange button on the air valve will manually activate it.

J15

The Bulk Feeder Hopper Motor connects to J15.

Stepper motor drive signal.

Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+ / A- or B+ / B- pins for each motor.

J16

HARNESS, INTERFACE, BULK FEEDER/CARRIAGE /P&P (P/N 116333) connects J16 to JP11 on the MCC Backplane PCB.

The 24V power and the Bulk Feeder Hopper Motor drive signals run through J16. The harness also connects:
  • MCC Backplane PCB JP23 to Sample P&P I/F J2.
  • MCC Backplane PCB JP12 to Carriage I/F J2.
