Motion Tests Overview

General

Motion tests include:
  • Moving to specific position—A one time move from one location to another
  • Cycling— Repeated moves from one location to another.
  • Exercising—A series multiple, sequential moves. One device begins the series of moves and other devices continue the movement. In a DxI instrument, the exercisers all involve placing a vessel into the supply carriage and moving the vessel through various areas the system.
Note: For instructions on how to perform a motion test, refer to the Tests: Motion Test procedure.

Exerciser Screen

Function Keys on Exerciser Screen

Refer to the reference numbers on the figure of the Exerciser Screen.

Reference Number Function Key Name Operation Performed Notes
1 Cycle Vessels (changes to Stop Vessels after selecting) Begins cycling the selected devices.
1 Stop Vessels (selectable only after Cycle Vessels is selected) Stops vessels where they are. No cleanout is performed. User must initialize the system, which cleans out all vessels, before running any tests.
2 Cancel Vessels Stops cycling the selected devices. Performs a cleanout of all vessels that were placed into the system by the exerciser.
3 Cycle Reagent Cycles the reagent gripper
4 Drop Packs Cycles the pack disposal door by removing and dropping all the packs in the reagent storage module. If this test is run, all in-use packs are removed. Do not load packs for this test. The test uses existing packs.
5 Done Exit the Exerciser screen
6 Help Provides more information regarding motion testing.

E Mixer a vessel to lift and spin in the analytical module (only when Reaction Vessel is selected) Same as describe in the reaction route, but adds a lift and spin in the mixer area. Mix, lift, and spin operations.
F Reagent Storage the reagent gripper to move packs within the reagent storage module. Gripper moves. Gripper routes for this test are dependent on the locations of the reagent packs.
In general,
  1. The gripper moves to reagent pipettor 1 (RP1), looks for a pack, and takes the pack from RP1.
  2. Moves the pack to nest 1.
  3. Moves the pack out of nest 1.
  4. Moves the pack to RP1.
  5. Moves the pack out of RP1 to nest 2.
  6. Moves the pack to RP1.
This process continues until the gripper reaches a nest with a known reagent pack (pack 2). The gripper leaves the first reagent pack in the nest before the nest containing pack 2. The gripper takes pack 2 to RP1, and then cycles pack 2 between the next empty nests and RP1. This process continues until another occupied nest is encountered. Once the gripper has moved packs between RP1 and all the nests, it moves to RP2 and repeats all of the moves for RP2. The gripper then repeats the process for RP3 and RP4.
Note:
  • If there are no packs in the module, then this test cannot run.
  • If there are no packs located in the reagent pipetting positions, then this test cannot run.
  • It can take up to an hour to run this entire test.
Reagent gripper operation.

Number of Cycles

Although 999 reps can be entered for any device, do not enter more than ten cycles. The bulk vessel feeder can supply enough vessels for ten or fewer cycles. As the tests are performed, the Cycles Completed box displays the number of cycles remaining.
