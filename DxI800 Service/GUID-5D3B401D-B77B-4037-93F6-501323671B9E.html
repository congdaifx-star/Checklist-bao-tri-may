Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - Sample Pipettor

Connectors

# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SAMPLE PIPETTOR (P/N 116141) connects Sample Pipettor Interface PCB J1 to J03 on the MCC backplane (MCC0).

Ribbon cable connection to MCC0.
  • The logic signals from the interface PCB to the MCC0 PCB run through J1.
  • The signals are pipettor Y- and Z-home, end-of-travel, and RF level sense active.
  • The 24V and 5V power to the Interface PCB from the MCC0 PCB also run through J1.

J2

The service loop CABLE, RIBBON (P/N 109293) connects Sample Pipettor Interface PCB J2 to JP1 on the RF Level Sense PCB.

Service loop ribbon cable connection.
  • The Z-axis home and RF level sense signals run through J2.
  • The 5V power to the RF Level Sense PCB also runs through J1.

J3

The HARNESS, SAMPLE HANDLING (P/N 116313) connects the Sample Pipettor Interface PCB J3 to JP02 on the MCC backplane.

The harness also connects MCC Backplane JP01 to the SPU Interface PCB J2, and JP02 to the Sample Storage I/F PCB J2.

Harness connection for power and motor drive signals from MCC0.
  • The 24V power and stepper motor drive signals from the MCC0 PCB run through J2.
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J5

The Y-axis home sensor connects to J5.

Y-axis home sensor connector.
  • Pin 3 (blue wire) is the home signal.
  • At home TP4 = 0V and D4 LED is on.

J6

The Y-axis stepper motor connects to J6.

Y-axis stepper motor connector.
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J8

The Y-axis end-of-travel sensor connects to J8.

Y-axis end-of-travel sensor connector.
  • Pin 3 (blue wire) is the home signal.
  • At home TP6 = 0V and D3 LED is on.

J9

The Z-axis stepper motor connects to J9.

Z-axis stepper motor connector.
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z-axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by an ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.
