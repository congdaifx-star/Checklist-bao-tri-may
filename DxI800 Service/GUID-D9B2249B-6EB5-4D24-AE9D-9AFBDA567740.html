Fluidics: Replacing the Bubble Trap Assembly

This procedure describes how to replace or install the bubble trap assembly.

Caution: Use proper PPE when performing this procedure.
  1. Remove the bubble trap assembly.
    1. Shut down the instrument.
      1. From the Main Menu, select Configure > PC Admin > System Admin > Shut Down Instrument.
      2. Enter password "Access2Support" or "Access2Admin" and select OK.
      3. Wait for instrument to shut down.
      4. Turn off the instrument power switch.
    2. Remove the lower right rear cover of the instrument and place it in a location where it is safe from damage.
      Note: If the instrument is using an external house air system or a drain system, be careful not to damage the fittings and tubing attached to the cover.

    3. Locate the bubble trap assembly.
      Note: The bubble trap assembly is attached to the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB mounted on the left side of the lower rear right wall.

    4. Gently move the Intermediate Waste Jar out and put it to the side to gain access to the PCB.

    5. Gently push or move the Accumulator Jar to the right side to gain more access to the PCB.
      Note: For the DxI 600 instruments, the Accumulator Jar does not need to be moved to gain more access to the PCB.
    6. Remove the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB cover from the four plastic standoffs and set to the side.
    7. Check the revision of the PCB and make sure to include it in the service record.

    8. Clamp the small bubble trap out Y tubing, gently remove the plastic clamp or zip-tie securing the bubble trap vacuum sensor tubing, then disconnect the tubing from the PCB vacuum sensor connector U10/PS2 and the tubing from the bubble trap out top small fitting.

      Note: Handle the tubing with care to avoid dropping fluid on any electronic connectors or components.
    9. Clamp the big bubble trap out tubing, gently disconnect the tubing from the bubble trap out big fitting.

      Note: Handle the tubing with care to avoid dropping fluid on any electronic connectors or components.
    10. Clamp the bubble trap in tubing from the bottom of the bubble trap assembly, disconnect the tubing from the bottom bubble trap in fitting.

      Note: Handle the tubing with care to avoid dropping fluid on any electronic connectors or components.
    11. Unscrew the three screws securing the bubble trap assembly to the PCB, then remove the bubble trap assembly.
  2. Install the bubble trap assembly.
    1. Place the bubble trap assembly to the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB, ensure the bubble trap block is flush with the bubble detector sensor U8, and screw in the three screws securing the bubble trap assembly in place.

    2. Connect bubble trap vacuum sensor tubing to the PCB vacuum sensor connector U10/PS2 and secure the tubing with a plastic clamp or zip-tie.
    3. Connect the two bubble trap out/top tubing and bubble trap in/bottom tubing to the bubble trap assembly fittings.
  3. Turn on the instrument.
  4. If the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB is revision AC and higher, then calibrate the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB. Refer to the Fluidics: Calibrating the Lower Cabinet Interface PCB procedure.
  5. Put the Accumulator Jar and Intermediate Waste Jar back in place.
  6. Prime and check for leaks.
  7. Calibrate the Wash Buffer Bubble Detector Sensor. Refer to Fluidics: Calibrating Wash Buffer Supply Bubble Detector procedure.
  8. Reinstall the lower right rear cover of the instrument.
  9. Run System Check. Refer to Verification of Service Repairs: Pneumatics document.
