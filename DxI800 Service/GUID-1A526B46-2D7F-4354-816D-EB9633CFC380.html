HS System Check Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting Failed Results

Note:
  • Troubleshooting is organized by module with the most frequent root causes and simple components to check listed first.
  • The most common HS System Check failure root causes are peristaltic pump tubing and aspirate probes.
  • If the failures are intermittent or if there is only one outlier causing the failure, it might be necessary to repeat this protocol more than once to determine the exact root of failure.
Failing Assay Module Troubleshoot
HS No Foam Failed (splashing) Aspiration
  • Inspect and replace the duck-bill valve.
  • Clean or replace the aspirate probes.
  • Check the peristaltic tubing and replace if damaged.
  • Perform the peri-pump flow test.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pump (Peristaltic) procedure.
  • Test the vacuum system.
    Note: Refer to Fluidics: Troubleshoot Vacuum and Wash Buffer Systems procedure.
Incubator and wash pick-and-place (due to splashing during placement)
  • Check pick-and-place alignments.
    Note: Refer to this procedure: P&P: Aligning Pick-and-Place Module
  • Inspect the pick-and-place piston and collets for deposits and damage. Clean or repair as needed.
  • Check the secondary air pressure.
    Note: Refer to the Pneumatics: Adjusting Pressure Regulator procedure.
Analytical module wash arm
  • Check for a loose or broken eject plate.
  • Check for air pressure leaks to the eject plate cylinder.
  • Check wash arm adjustments and alignments.
    Note: Refer to these procedures:AU: Adjusting Analytical ModuleAU: Aligning Analytical Module
  • Check mixer-gripper-spinner function (broken fingers, excessive play in pulleys, binding).
Fluid dispense Wash pump buffer dispense:
  • Perform dispense probe volume checks.
    Note: Refer to the Tests: Volume Check procedure.
  • If volume checks indicate an issue, then clean and inspect the wash pumps and inspect the dispense probes.
  • Check fittings, dispense probe tubing, and clean the wash valve.
HS foam failed (poor aspiration) Supplies Confirm that HS System Check was used. System Check solution (P/N A21537 or 81910) causes failures in HS Foam.
Aspiration
  • Inspect and replace the duck-bill valve.
  • Clean or replace the aspirate probes.
  • Check the peristaltic tubing and replace it, if damaged.
  • Perform the peri-pump flow test.
    Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Troubleshoot Pumps (Sample/Wash/Precision) procedure.
  • Test the vacuum system.
    Note: Refer to Fluidics: Troubleshoot Vacuum and Wash Buffer Systems procedure.
  • Check for back-pressure (creating poor aspiration).
  • Check for kinked, clogged, or constricted tubing in the waste lines.
    Note: Refer to the document "Diagram - Fluidics System"
  • DxI 800 only: Check for clogged bubble-burster.
Back-pressure symptoms: lots of foam in the solid waste, a bulging intermediate waste container, or leaking at the waste fittings.
Analytical module wash arm
  • Check wash arm adjustments and alignments.
    Note: Refer to these procedures:AU: Adjusting Analytical ModuleAU: Aligning Analytical Module
  • Check mixer-gripper-spinner function (broken fingers, excessive play in pulleys, binding).
Reagent pipettors
Reagent pipettor alignments to the carriage (due to splashing when performing the ultrasonics mix to create the foam).
Note: Refer to the Reagent Carriage section of the RP: Aligning Reagent Pipettor procedure.
Note: The Performance macro can be used to sort by pipettor.
Fluid dispense

Wash pump buffer dispense:

  • Perform dispense and aspirate probe volume checks.
    Note: Refer to Tests: Volume Check.
  • If volume checks indicate an issue, then clean and inspect the wash pumps and inspect the dispense probes.
  • Check fittings and tubing, and clean wash valve.
Incubator and wash pick-and-place (due to splashing during placement)
  • Check pick-and-place alignments.
    Note: Refer to this procedure: P&P: Aligning Pick-and-Place Module
  • Inspect the pick-and-place piston and collets for deposits and damage. Clean or repair as needed.
  • Check the secondary air pressure.
    Note: Refer to the Pneumatics: Adjusting Pressure Regulator procedure.
