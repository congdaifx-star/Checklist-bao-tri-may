Removing Sample Storage Module

This procedure describes how to remove the sample storage module from the instrument.

  1. Disconnect the module.
    1. Open the instrument front covers.
    2. Remove power from the motors by using the switch on the Power Distribution PCB labeled "All DC power (except CPU and reagent coolers)".
    3. Remove the interface PCB cover at the front of the sample storage module
    4. Remove these connectors from the sample storage module interface PCB:
      • J1 - MCC PCB (gray, 20-pin, ribbon cable)
      • J2 - Motor Drive cable (purple, black, and gray, 10-pin)
      • J3 - Power cable (purple and black, 4-pin)
      • J10 - Thermistor cable (silver, 2-pin)
  2. Remove the module.
    1. Remove the upper and center rear instrument covers.
      Note: The upper rear cover has four screws (remove). The center rear cover has one screw (remove) and three snap retainers.
    2. Remove the rightmost lower cover bracket (two screws).
      Note: Mark the location of the bracket on the base using a pen or sharp object to aid alignment during reassembly.
    3. Remove the top cover from the sample storage module.
    4. Remove the reservoir vent and the two drain tubes on the fluids tray drain tubes from the hole in the instrument base.
      Note: These tubes crossover behind the module and feed into the solid waste container along with the module drain tube.
    5. Disconnect the drain tube on the sample storage module from the connector on the instrument base.
    6. Disconnect the Peltier power connector from the harness near the rear of the module (black and purple).
    7. Remove the two screws that attach the sample storage module to the instrument base.
    8. Place paper towels on the work area where the sample storage module will be placed to capture the residual moisture from inside the carousel.
    9. Slide the module rearward until it is clear of the hold-down bracket.
    10. Remove the module from the instrument, invert the module over a sink to drain any condensation, and then place the inverted module on the paper towels.
