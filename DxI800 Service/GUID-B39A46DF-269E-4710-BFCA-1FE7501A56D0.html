Parts and Configuration Cut-In Log

Purpose

This document provides a list of some of the instrument configuration changes for DxI 600 and DxI 800. Due to continuous improvement in the DxI design, device and component variations will be seen in field instruments. These design changes are typically invisible to our customers, but being aware of them can aid the field in troubleshooting instrument issues.
