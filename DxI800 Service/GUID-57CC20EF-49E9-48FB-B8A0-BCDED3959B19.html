J31 - PCB - MCC Backplane

  • The vacuum pump 1, 2, 3, and 4 enable signals and the peristaltic pump home and index sensor signals run through J31.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, PERI/VAC PUMP connects J31 to J1 on the Peristaltic Pump / Vacuum Pump Interface PCB.
