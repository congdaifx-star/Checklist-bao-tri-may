Replacing Reagent Cooler

This procedure describes how to replace the reagent cooler.

The reagent cooler is also called the Peltier cooling module. This module includes three separate reagent coolers, and each cooler can be replaced individually. After the reagent storage module is removed, you will see the reagent cooling module. The entire reagent cooling module must be removed to replace the individual coolers. Refer to the RS: Reagent Storage Operating Principles document for more details.

  1. Remove the reagent storage module.
    Note: Refer to the RS: Removing Reagent Storage Module procedure.
  2. Replace the reagent cooler.
    1. Remove the cover on the reagent temperature interface board, and unplug the thermistor connection for the coolers.
      The interface PCB is located behind the reagent storage module and to the right of the gantry for pipettor 4.
      Note: Mark the connectors for identification during reassembly.
      • J6 - Reagent cooler 1
      • J7 - Reagent cooler 2
      • J8 - Reagent cooler 3
    2. Disconnect all cooler power connections.
      Note: Mark the connectors for identification during reassembly.
    3. Remove the cooler base from the instrument frame (six screws).
    4. Remove the support bracket from the cooler mounting bracket (four screws).
    5. Remove the four screws holding the cooler to the mounting bracket.
      One screw in each corner of the cooler.
    6. Remove the cooler, pulling the thermistor wire and power wire through the grommet.
      Caution: When removing the cooler, verify that the shims are not left on the bracket. The new style cooler does not require these shims. If the shims are left on the bracket, then the reagent Peltier will not seal adequately around the bracket, resulting in excess moisture in the reagent storage module and a temperature at or above 10 °C.
    7. Replace the cooler, installing the four screws that secure the cooler to the mounting plate and threading the thermistor cable and power cable through the grommet.
    8. Reinstall the support bracket to the mounting plate (four screws).
    9. Reinstall the mounting plate to the instrument (six screws).
    10. Reconnect the thermistors to the reagent temperature interface board, and then reconnect the power to the cooler.
  3. Install the reagent storage module.
    Note: Refer to the RS: Installing Reagent Storage Module procedure.
    Leave the reagent storage module cover off to perform the alignments.
  4. Verify operation of the replaced cooler.
    1. Verify alignments of the reagent pipettors to reagents packs.
      Note: Refer to the RP: Aligning Reagent Pipettor procedure.
    2. Install the cover on the reagent storage module.
    3. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics  > Analog Devices  > Analog Data, and verify reagent cooler temperatures are in specification.
      It can take 20 to 30 minutes for temperatures to come into range.
