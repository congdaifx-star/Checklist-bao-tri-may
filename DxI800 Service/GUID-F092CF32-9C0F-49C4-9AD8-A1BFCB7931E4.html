Verifying RV Ejection During Troubleshooting

This procedure describes how to verify RV ejection when troubleshooting the analytical unit.

  1. Prepare the instrument to eject RVs.
    1. Remove the left half of the analytical module cover.
    2. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics > Alignments.
    3. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device and RV Bottom as the position.
    4. Select Alignments/Offsets > Semi-Auto Alignment > OK.
      The instrument will automatically load three RVs into the wash wheel.
    5. When the Cancel button becomes active, select Cancel > OK.
    6. Select Wash Carousel as the device, and then select Home.
    7. Select Advance CW ten times (to move the RVs underneath the spin grips).
    8. Visually confirm that the RVs are underneath the spin grips. If the RVs are not underneath the gripper-spinner, move CW or CCW to align the RVs underneath the gripper-spinner.
  2. Verify RV ejection.
    1. Select Dispense Probe Plate as the device and RV Engaged as the position.
    2. Select Home > Move to Z Position while watching the eject plate and wash arm for RV ejection. Verify that the eject plate ejects the RVs from the gripper-spinner each time you select Home.
    3. Repeat the Home > Move to Z Position sequence while watching the eject plate and wash arm for RV ejection.
