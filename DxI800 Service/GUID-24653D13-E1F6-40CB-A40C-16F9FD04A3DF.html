Redistributing Toner

This procedure describes how to redistribute the toner in the printer cartridge.

When toner is low or faded or light areas appear on the printed page, the cartridge may be made useful for a short time by redistributing the toner in the cartridge. It may be possible to complete printing of a current print job.
Note:
  • This is a very short-term fix to be used only to complete a current print job of a few pages.
  • When toner requires distribution, it is time to install a replacement cartridge.
  • Some resistance will be encountered when opening the toner door.
  • Opening the toner door lifts the toner cartridge for removal.
Redistribute the toner.
  1. Open the toner door on the printer and remove the toner cartridge.
    Caution: To prevent damage, do not expose the toner cartridge to light. Cover the toner cartridge with a sheet of paper.
  2. Gently shake the toner cartridge from side to side.
    Caution: If toner gets on your clothing, wipe the toner off with a dry cloth and wash the clothing in cold water. Hot water sets the toner into the fabric.
  3. Reinsert the toner cartridge into the printer, and close the toner door.
    If the print is still light, install a new toner cartridge.
    Note: Refer to the following for instructions.
    • Peripherals: Replacing Printer Toner (HP 1200)
    • Peripherals: Replacing Printer Toner (Lexmark)
    • Peripherals: Replacing Printer Toner (Okidata).
