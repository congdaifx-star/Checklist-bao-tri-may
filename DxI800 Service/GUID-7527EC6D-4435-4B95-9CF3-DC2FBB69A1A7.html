J5 - PCB - Interface - Carriage Assemblies

  • The RV Carriage 2 Home Sensor connects to J5.
J5 Description Signal / Voltage To / From / Notes

1

+5V_MCC1

5V

From J1 pin 2 or 10.

5V supplied by the MCC1 PCB.

2

GND_MCC1

0V

From J1 pin 1 or 9.

Ground. TP1.

3

RV Carriage 2 Home

0V or 5V

To J1 pin 4.

Home: TP4 = 0V and D3 LED is On.

4

+5V_MCC1

Xmit LED Power

5V

From J1 pin 2 or 10.

5V supplied by the MCC1 PCB.

The sensor LED power is 5V supplied through a 221 ohm resistor. When the sensor is connected pin 4 of the connector will be at 0.7V.

5

GND_MCC1

0V

From J1 pin 1 or 9.

Ground. TP1.
