Cleaning PC Tape Drive

This procedure describes how to clean the PC tape drive.

Clean the PC tape drive when:
  • the tape drive has over eight hours of tape activity or approximately once per month. In dirty or high humidity environments, clean the tape drive more often.
  • the tape drive does not move the tape past the head correctly.
  • the tape head or capstan appears dirty when inspected.
  • error messages occur indicating the internal tape drive parts might be dirty.

Two brands of the Travan cleaning cartridges are in circulation. Either type works; however, the instructions for use included with each brand vary slightly. Disregard the instructions included with the cartridges, and use the instructions in this document.

Care and storage requirements for the cleaning cartridge:
  • Always store the cartridge in the plastic case.
  • Avoid exposing the cartridge to direct sunlight.
  • Store the cartridge vertically.
  • Always inspect the cartridge before using it. Do not use a damaged cartridge.
  • Handle the cleaning cartridge only by the edges of the plastic housing. Do not touch the cleaning cloth or roller in the cartridge.
Clean the tape drive.
Caution: Never use a swab to clean the tape drive.
  1. Confirm that the PC is powered on.
  2. If a tape is in the tape drive, eject it by selecting System Admin > PC Admin > Eject Tape.
    Note: Eject means that the tape is rewound to its beginning and the head is pulled away from the tape. Eject does not mean that the tape cartridge pops out of the drive.
  3. After Remove Tape is displayed on the screen (approximately 45 seconds), remove the tape from the drive.
  4. Insert the cleaning cartridge into the tape drive with the metal plate on the cartridge facing downward.
    Note: The cleaning process begins automatically once the cartridge is inserted. The LED on the front of the tape drive flashes during the cleaning process. The cleaning is complete when the LED no longer flashes. It takes approximately 20 seconds for this process.
  5. Wait for the cleaning process to complete, then remove the cleaning cartridge.
    Caution: Do not use Eject Tape when using the cleaning cartridge. Doing so will rewind the cleaning media.
  6. In the area of the cartridge label to be used for marking the number of uses, mark that it has been used.
  7. Place the cleaning cartridge into the protective case.
