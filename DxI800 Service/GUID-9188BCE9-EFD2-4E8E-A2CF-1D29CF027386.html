J1 - PCB - Interface - Reagent Storage

  • The reagent and substrate BCR outputs, the gripper open and closed, the gripper and tray home, the waste door open, and the Interface PCB Data signals to the MCC Backplane PCB run through J1.
  • CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, REAGENT STORAGE connects J1 to J73 on the MCC Backplane PCB.
Note: The connections referenced on this connector for the colored status LEDs are not used. The colored status LEDs on the instrument front cover are connected to MCC Backplane PCB J97 and controlled by the MCC6 PCB.
