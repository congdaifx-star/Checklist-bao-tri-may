Aligning Analytical Module

This procedure describes how to align the analytical module.

Required Materials:
  • Locking pin tool
    Note: For information about the tool, refer to AU: Wash Carousel Locking Tool document.
Prerequisites:
  • Complete the probe plate adjustments to the analytical module before performing the probe plate alignments.
    Note: Refer to the AU: Adjusting Analytical Module procedure.
  • Confirm that there are no vessels in the wash carousel.

The analytical module interacts with the dispense probe plate and the aspirate probe plate. The mixer collets on the dispense probe plate must be able to pick up reaction vessels from the wash carousel for mixing. The aspirate probe plate must be aligned with the RV to aspirate fluids from the RV. The analytical module interacts externally with the wash pick-and-place and the incubator pick-and-place. These alignment procedures are provided in the pick-and-place alignment procedures.

The analytical module alignment includes the following alignments:
  • Automatic alignment—dispense probe plate to RV-engaged position
  • Semi-automatic alignment—aspirate probe plate to RV-bottom position
  • Manual alignments—wash carousel angular offset, and aspirate probe plate to RV-bottom position
Note: Changes to alignment offsets are written to file:
  • When the alignments are saved while exiting the Alignment screen.
  • By selecting Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
  1. Verify the wash carousel angular offset alignment before performing the other alignments.
    Note: Refer to theAU: Aligning Wash Carousel Angular Offset procedure.
  2. Align dispense probe plate to RV-engaged position.
    1. Verify that the analytical module covers are installed.
      Note: The covers do not interfere with the alignment, but this step ensures the instrument alignment is performed in the same configuration as the instrument runs.
    2. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics  > Alignments.
    3. Select Dispense Probe Plate as the device and RV Engaged as the position.
    4. Select Alignments / Offsets  > Auto Align Selected.
      The instrument homes the probe plates and moves the carousel into position.
    5. Verify that there are no vessels in the wash carousel, then select OK.
      The dispense plate moves down until the drive motor stalls.
    6. Select Save Offset and Exit.
      The instrument homes the probe plates and moves the wash carousel to the angular offset position.
    7. Select Home Device > Move Z to position.
    8. Select Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
  3. Align aspirate probe plate to RV-bottom position (semi-auto alignment).
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics  > Alignments.
    2. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device and RV Bottom as the position, and then select Alignments / Offsets  > Semi-Auto Alignment.
      The instrument homes the probe plates and moves the carousel into position.
    3. Manually lower the aspirate probe plate down completely by gently pressing the center of the plate close to the drive shaft.
      Caution: To avoid damaging the probe or causing inaccurate alignments, do not use excessive force when depressing the plate.
      Caution: Do not kink or otherwise damage the aspirate and dispense tubing.
    4. Select OK.
      The instrument homes the aspirate probe plate.
    5. Select OK to verify the alignment.
    6. Select Home Device > Move Z to position.
    7. Using the directional keys, step the plate up or down until any one of the aspirate probes reaches full deflection.
      Caution: To prevent cutting any material from the RV and lodging the material in the aspirate probes, do not drive the probe into the RV too far.
    8. Step the plate up four steps from the full-deflection position.
    9. Select Alignments / Offsets  > Preserve Offset  > Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments > Home Device > Remove Vessels.
  4. Align aspirate probe plate to RV-bottom position (manual alignment).
    Note: Perform the aspirate probe plate to RV-bottom position semi-auto alignment before performing this procedure.
    1. From the Main Menu, select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics > Mechanics  > Alignments.
    2. Select Aspirate Probe Plate as the device and Both Plates Up as the position, and then select Move Z to position.
      The instrument moves the probe plates to the up position.
    3. Place an empty vessel under each aspirate probe position in the wash carousel.
    4. Select RV Bottom as the position, and then select Move Z to position.
      The instrument moves the aspirate plate to the current RV bottom alignment position.
    5. Using the directional keys, step the plate down until any one of the aspirate probes reaches full deflection.
      Caution: To prevent cutting any material from the RV and lodging the material in the aspirate probes, do not drive the probe into the RV too far.
    6. Using the directional keys, step the plate up four steps from the full-deflection position.
    7. Select Alignments / Offsets  > Preserve Offset  > Alignments / Offsets > Save Alignments.
    8. Select Both Plates Up as the position, and then select Move Z to Position.
    9. Remove the three empty vessels from the wash carousel.
  5. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
