Verification of Service Repair

Purpose

This document provides requirements and recommendations for verification of proper instrument operation after a service repair, installation, or preventive maintenance.

When to Perform

Perform the appropriate section of this procedure after completing a service repair, preventive maintenance, or installation to determine the required verification testing needed.

Quality Control Statement

Beckman Coulter recommends customers run quality control (QC) samples after any unscheduled or scheduled maintenance or service and after successfully installing new software. Quality control materials simulate the characteristics of patient samples and are essential for monitoring the system performance of immunochemical assays. The verifications recommended in this procedure demonstrate instrument repair and functionality to specification but they do not supersede Beckman Coulter's recommended guidelines for QC as stated in the assay directional inserts, Operator's Guide, and Help system. Each laboratory determines the QC rules to apply and the course of action to take when a result violates a rule.

Assay Specific Issue

Use this table to determine the required verification for an assay specific performance issue (In the absence of a mechanical issue).

Issue Service Tasks Minimum Verification Required Data to be Collected
Calibration Failure None Passing Calibration
  • Assay calibration print out
Imprecision None Precision Run CV Within Assay Insert Claims
  • Precision calculation print out
Inaccurate Result None Known Sample or Control Recovers Within Expected Range
  • QC report print out

Select the hardware module that was serviced to determine the verification testing required for the repair.
