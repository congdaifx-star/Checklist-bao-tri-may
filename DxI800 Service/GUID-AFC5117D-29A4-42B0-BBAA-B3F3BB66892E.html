Conn. Overview - PCB - Interface - Pick and Place

Connectors

  • The MCC2 PCB operates all the pick and place devices.
  • A Pick and Place Interface PCB is located on each pick and place gantry on the opposite end from the motors.
# Connection Notes Function / Signals

J1

The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, SAMPLE P&P (P/N 116191) connects the Sample P&P Interface PCB J1 to J21 on the MCC backplane.

The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, INCUBATE P&P (P/N 116201) connects the Incubator P&P Interface PCB J1 to J22 on the MCC backplane.

The CABLE, INTERFACE, RIBBON, WASH P&P (P/N 116211) connects the Wash P&P Interface PCB J1 to J23 on the MCC backplane.

The logic signals from the interface PCB to the MCC2 PCB run through J1.

The signals are:
  • X/Y Home.
  • Z Home.
  • End of Travel.
  • Eject.
  • Ejector Sense.
  • PCB Revision.

The 24V and 5V power to the Interface PCB from the MCC2 PCB also run through J1.

J2

The HARNESS, INTERFACE, BULK FEEDER/CARRIAGE /P&P (P/N 116333) connects the Sample P&P Interface PCB J2 to JP23 on the MCC backplane.

The HARNESS, INTERFACE, INCUBATE P&P (P/N 116343) connects the Incubator P&P Interface PCB J2 to JP21 on the MCC backplane.

The HARNESS, INTERFACE, WASH P&P (P/N 116353) connects the Wash P&P Interface PCB J2 to JP22 on the MCC backplane.

The 24V power and stepper motor drive signals from the MCC2 PCB run through J2.

Note:
  • An oscilloscope is required to view the X/Y/Z Axis stepper motor drive signal/voltages.
  • Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J3

The X/Y - Axis Home Sensor connects to J3.

X/Y - Axis Home.

J4

The X/Y - Axis End of Travel Sensor connects to J4.

X/Y - Axis End of Travel.

The J4 connector is only used on the Sample Pick and Place.

J5

The Z - Axis Home Sensor connects to J5.

Z - Axis Home.

J6

The X/Y - Axis Stepper Motor connects to J6.

Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J7

The Z - Axis Stepper Motor connects to J7.

Motor/harness continuity can be verified by ohm meter reading of ~2 ohms between the A+/A- or B+/B- pins for each motor.

J8

The TRIPLE HALL EFFECT SENSOR PCB (P/N 11021E) or SINGLE HALL EFFECT SENSOR PCB (P/N 11023D) connects to J8 through the service loop CABLE RIBBON (P/N 109262).

The wires exit the 6 pin service loop connector from the side that faces upward when connected to J8 on the interface PCB.

The service loop connector is also keyed for polarity.

J9

The RV Eject Air Valve connects to J9.

The signal to energize the RV eject air valve comes from the MCC2 PCB through J1 pin 7. When the signal at TP3 goes to 5V, Q1 grounds the low side (pin 2) of the RV eject air valve to energize the valve (the indicator LED on the valve is on).

J10

The SV Eject Air Valve connects to J10.

The signal to energize the SV eject air valve comes from the MCC2 PCB through J1 pin 13. When the signal at TP6 goes to 5V, Q2 grounds the low side (pin 2) of the SV eject air valve to energize the valve (the indicator LED on the valve is on).

The J10 connector is only used on the Sample Pick and Place Interface PCB.

J11

The DV Eject Air Valve connects to J11.

The signal to energize the DV eject air valve comes from the MCC2 PCB through J1 pin 14. When the signal at TP10 goes to 5V, Q3 grounds the low side of the DV eject air valve (pin 2) to energize the valve (the indicator LED on the valve is on).

The J11 connector is only used on the Sample Pick and Place Interface PCB.
