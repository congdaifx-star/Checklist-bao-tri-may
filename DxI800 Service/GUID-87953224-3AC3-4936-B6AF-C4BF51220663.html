Replacing LED

This procedure describes how to replace the LED assembly.

Acronyms in this document:
  • DCF = Drift Correction Factor
  • RLU = Relative Light Unit
  • LED = Light Emitting Diode
  • PMT = Photo Multiplier Tube
  • HV = High Voltage
Warning: The photomultiplier tube (PMT) is a very sensitive photon measuring device. Any background or extraneous light coming into contact with a powered PMT can saturate the PMT and reduce the gain. This reduces the performance of the PMT, and it can take a long time for the PMT to recover or it might never recover fully. A magnetic field close to a powered PMT also reduces the performance of the PMT. The magnetic field causes the electrons to follow a curved path instead of moving in a straight line. When you are performing service in the area of the luminometer, observe the following rules.
  • Never shine a flashlight around the luminometer while the PMT is powered on.
  • Never bring a strong magnetic field close to the luminometer while the luminometer is powered on.
  • Before removing the covers on the analytical module, turn off the power to the PMT.
  • Cap the PMT whenever it is removed from the analytical module.
  1. Shut down the DxI instrument.
    1. Select Configure  > PC Admin > System Admin > Shutdown Instrument to exit the software in preparation for powering off the instrument.
    2. Enter the password Access2Support, and then select OK.
    3. At the pop-up You must now turn off the instrument, using the instrument power switch, select OK.
    4. After the instrument software has shut down, turn off the instrument at the power switch.
  2. Remove the LED assembly.
    1. Remove the substrate probe from the dispense plate.
    2. Before moving the LED assembly away from the analytical module, verify that the aspirate and dispense plates are in the up position.
      The probe plates drops when the holding current is removed.
    3. Remove the two lower screws that attach the wash arm to the mounting bracket, and then slide the wash arm as far from the analytical module as possible.
    4. Remove both halves of the analytical module cover.
    5. Remove the spring tensioner bracket (two screws).
      Caution: To prevent damaging or bending the tensioning spring, handle the bracket carefully.
    6. Lift the wash carousel up, and then slide the wash carousel horizontally to access to the LED assembly.
      Note: It is not necessary to remove the wash carousel from the instrument completely.
    7. Disconnect the LED connector (brown/yellow/black wires) under the analytical module by the luminometer.
    8. Remove the LED assembly (one screw), pulling the LED wires and connector through the hole in the bottom of the analytical module.
  3. Install the LED assembly.
    Warning: Ensure that there is a light seal on the replacement LED assembly. Do not touch the filter.
    1. Install the replacement LED reference assembly (one screw), and route the LED connector and wires through the hole in the bottom of the analytical module.
    2. Connect the LED connector (brown/yellow/black wires).
    3. Install the wash carousel into the analytical module. To correctly seat the carousel, pull the spring-loaded tensioner pin for the idler bearing located by the drive gear. Confirm that the carousel is seated correctly and rotates freely.
    4. Install the spring tensioner bracket on top of the LED assembly (two screws), and confirm that the bracket does not rub on the wash carousel.
    5. Install the covers on the analytical module, and confirm that the spring tensioner bracket does not interfere with the covers.
    6. Slide the wash arm fully toward the analytical module and secure it with the two mounting screws.
      Caution: Before moving the wash arm toward the analytical module, verify that the aspirate and dispense plates are in the up position.
    7. Install the substrate probe.
    8. Close all covers.
    9. Power on the instrument.
    10. Initialize the instrument.
  4. Adjust the luminometer.
    Warning: All customer assays must be calibrated after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED target.
    1. Select Diagnostics  > Device Diagnostics  > Luminometer > Read .
    2. Confirm that the Dark Counts are less than 100 by viewing the RLU counts in the Current Counts field.
      Note:
      • Dark Counts is a term that refers to the RLU counts read from the luminometer when all covers are closed, there is no sample present, and the LED reference is Off.
    3. Select Enable LED.
    4. Allow the LED output to stabilize until the Current %CV is less than 0.2%.
      Note: A RLU count of approximately 1 million is common when reading the LED.
    5. Select Cancel.
      Note: Leave the LED enabled so that the Current Mean is displayed.
    6. Select Save LED Reading, and then select Yes at the Save new LED Reading pop-up.
      Warning: All customer assays must be calibrated after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED target.
    7. Select Enable LED to power off the LED.
    8. Press the [Esc] key.
  5. Verify the operation of the instrument.
    1. Verify temperatures are stable and within specification.
    2. Observe the wash area during priming.
      1. Run four cycles of substrate probe prime and ten cycles of dispense probe prime.
      2. Observe the wash area. Verify that:
        • fluid is not leaking.
        • tubing is not kinked or pinched.
        • vessel handling is free of issues.
    3. Perform a system check to verify luminometer operation.
      Note: The specification for the DCF is 0.8 to 1.2 and the substrate mean RLU is 5000 to 9000.
      If the substrate mean RLU is not within specification:
      • Evaluate the system for substrate contamination.
        Note: Refer to the Fluidics: Decontaminating Substrate procedure for details.
      • If the substrate is not contaminated, readjust the luminometer.
        Note: Refer to the AU: Adjusting Luminometer Substrate RLU procedure.
    4. Print a new Alignment Report and file it in the Maintenance Log Book.
    5. Calibrate all customer assays.
      Warning: All customer assays must be calibrated after replacing the luminometer or saving a new LED target.
  6. Verify system operation.
    1. Perform system verification as stated in the Verification of Service Repair document .
      Refer to the Admin: Verification of Service Repair document.
