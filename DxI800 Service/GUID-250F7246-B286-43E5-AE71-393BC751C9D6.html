Troubleshooting Ejector Plate Errors

Purpose

This document provides information on troubleshooting the ejector plate.

When To Perform

Perform this procedure when the Event Log reports Vessel did not eject.

Causes of Ejector Plate Errors

The troubleshooting in this document is separated into these two main causes of ejector plate errors:
  • Sensor Errors
  • Motion Errors

Determining the Source of the Error

  1. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices > Digital Outputs.
  2. Select MixerArmEjector.
  3. Select Toggle Output Value.
  4. Use the results below to determine the source of the error.
    If the ejector plate And the Output Value Then
    moves to the eject position, turns green, the ejector plate is working properly.
    moves to the eject position, does not change state, the issue is a sensor error.
    does not move to the eject position, does not change state, the issue is a motion error.

Sensor Errors

Sensor errors are characterized by the Output Value, as reported in Digital Outputs, not changing state when the ejector plate moves from the home position to the eject position. Determine the most likely area of failure in the table and perform the troubleshooting and repairs shown for that area.

Area Troubleshoot Repair
Optical Sensor The sensor input signal is on J7 Pin 2 (TP20) of the Upper Interface PCB.
Ejector Plate UP:
  • TP20 = 5 VDC (TP11 = GND)
  • D2 = Off
Ejector Plate DOWN:
  • TP20 = 0 VDC (TP11 = GND)
  • D2 = On
Replace Optical Sensor
Analytical Module Interface (Upper) PCB DC Voltage Supply to Optical Sensor— J7 Pin 1 should always read approximately 5 VDC with respect to TP11 (Ground).

Signal to Ejector Plate Pneumatic Valve—

Ejector Plate UP:
  • TP17 = 0 VDC (TP4 = GND)
  • D9 = Off
Ejector Plate DOWN:
  • TP17 = 5 VDC (TP4 = GND)
  • D9 = On
Note: If the voltages at TP17 are correct then check the pneumatic valve.
Local Power—
  • TP4 = GND
  • TP2 = 5 VDC
  • TP3 = 12 VDC
Replace Analytical Module Interface PCB
Analytical Module Interface (Upper) PCB
  • TP4 = GND
  • TP1 = 24 VDC
Troubleshoot power distribution board and/or wiring to Analytic Module Interface PCB.
Wiring/cables Inspect all cables and wires for cuts or other damage. Sensor power (5 VDC) and ground are supplied through the ribbon cable. The sensor signal travels out of the Interface PCB on J1 Pin 3 through the ribbon cable to J72 pin 3 on the MCC backplane. Replace wiring or cables as necessary.
Bad solder joints on PCBs Visually verify the solder connection on MCC #7 header Pin A8. Verify DC voltage on MCC #7 header Pin A8 is toggling with the sensor. The signal travels to MCC #7 through Pin A8 of the MCC connector; as you face the MCC backplane PCB, pin A8 is on the 3rd row (far right) of the connector, 8 pins from the top. Replace the PCB.
Sensor flag Inspect the flag to determine if the flag is missing or damaged Replace the sensor flag.

Motion Errors

Motion errors are characterized by the ejector plate not moving from the home position to the eject position when Toggle Output Value is toggled from the Digital Outputs screen.

Area Troubleshoot Repair
Air supply line Inspect air supply line for kinks or leaks. Replace the air lines.
Air valve
  1. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices > Digital Outputs.
  2. Select MixerArmEjector.
  3. Select Toggle Output Value.The LED should illuminate when the ejector is in the eject position. The air line can be unplugged from the piston to determine if the valve is switching properly.
Replace the air valve.
Air disconnects Inspect the quick disconnects at the air valve or the piston to make sure they are not loose. Tighten the connections.
Ejector plate bearings Disconnect the piston and move the ejector plate up and down. It should move freely. Inspect the guide shaft and bearings for wear. Replace the guide shafts or bearings.
Ejector plate piston
  1. Remove the ejector plate piston from the ejector plate assembly.
  2. Verify the air lines are not kinked or blocked and the air pressure is good.
  3. Select Diagnostics > Device Diagnostics > Digital Devices > Digital Outputs.
  4. Select MixerArmEjector.
  5. Select Toggle Output Value The piston should move in and out.
    Caution: If the piston is good, it should move in and out with a good amount of force.
Replace the ejector plate piston.
