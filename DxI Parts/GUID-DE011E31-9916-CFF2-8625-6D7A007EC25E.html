Bubble Trap, Wash Buffer

A34001

A34001

Acrylic bubble trap mounts on Lower Cabinet interface board. Comes with two M6 x 3/16" barb fittings A30298. Does not come with the 1/4-28 x 1/8" fitting A30347. A30347 is not an orderable part. Contact Tech Support for assistance.

9 PCB, Lower Cabinet WBD I/F C30751 C30751

Located on the left side panel in the lower rear cabinet. Near the intermediate waste container. PCB doesn't include the bubble trap (A34001) or bracket. Replaces B00347.

  • For instrument serial # 600841 and below that does not have the wash buffer flow monitoring tubing installed, move the two jumpers from the pins labeled Normal onto the Bypassed pins (C and D) at the top left corner of the PCB.
