Pick and Place Assembly, Sample DxI 800 ROHS C57471 C57471

F/S, DXI 800 X-AXIS LINEAR SLIDE SAMPLE PNP (new version) with new x-axis tensioner and x-motor.

New version cut-in:
  • DxI 800: 608946 to 608951, 608982 and higher
Previous version cut-in:
  • DxI 800: 608089 to 608125, 608128 to 608139, 608148 to 608276, 608336, 608344, 608346-608347, 608349-608377, 608379-608391, 608393-608394
If not replacing the entire gantry, previous version gantry can be upgraded to the new version and require installing the new x-axis tensioner and x-motor:
  • C80677 FS, Sample 800 PnP X-Idler Kit
  • C80675 FS, Sample PnP X-Motor, Motor Mount & Cable Kit

New x-motor operates at 2.0 Amps. See 16.3.2 Installing Sample Pick-and-Place procedure or 16.1.6 Configuring Pick and Place Gantries X-Motors Run Current to Operate at 2.0 Amps procedure in the DxI Service Manual for more details.

Replaces A12332 at stockout.
