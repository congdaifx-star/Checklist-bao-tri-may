New Notification System for Diagnostic Service Documentation

A new diagnostics support content notification system will be introduced in early September 2019 to replace the current Technical Bulletin notification system. ACTION IS REQUIRED for users to subscribe to product specific notifications, otherwise no notifications will be received. See General Technical Bulletin SB_G2019034 - Subscribing to New Weekly Documentation Notifications for details on the new process and instructions on how to subscribe. The new notification system will be a weekly notification including updates to any of the following content:
  • Technical Bulletins
  • Service Modification (Mod) Procedures
  • Service and Application Manuals
  • Parts Lists

New Style Sheet - Updated Appearance to Support Manuals and Parts Lists

A new Style Sheet used for HTML Support Manuals and Parts Lists has been rolled out beginning late June 2019. Users will notice a slightly different appearance to the manuals but the functionality and structure shall remain the same. A General Technical Bulletin SB_G2019033 New Appearance to Service Manuals and Parts Lists was released to outline the details. Updates include:
  • Improved embedded videos
  • Expandable/collapsible topics via "Show More"/"Show Less"
  • Improved compatibility with tablet devices
