Wash Arm and Carousel

Illustrated Parts List

This document contains parts for the Access 2 Analytical Module, Wash Arm and Carousel assembly including:
  • Wash carousel assembly
  • Mixer motor and components
  • Wash arm
  • Heaters
Note: MFG reverted back to old or original wash carousel design (cut in date 2/1/2023 and serial number 574130 or higher).
